## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *transformer-based 3D large reconstruction models (LRMs) have achieved unprecedented sparse-view 3D-reconstruction quality, but they all adopt triplane NeRF as the internal scene representation, which (a) suffers from a limited triplane resolution that bottlenecks fine-detail preservation, (b) requires expensive volume rendering that bottlenecks training and inference speed, and (c) struggles to scale beyond object-centric inputs. Can we replace triplane NeRF with a representation that is (a) high-resolution (pixel-aligned), (b) fast to render, and (c) scale-agnostic (object to scene)?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *We propose GS-LRM, a transformer-based large reconstruction model that predicts 3D Gaussian primitives (Kerbl et al. 2023, the original 3D-Gaussian-Splatting paper) from sparse input images, enabling fast and high-quality rendering and reconstruction for both objects and scenes. The core is a simple and scalable transformer-based network that predicts per-pixel Gaussians. Specifically, we patchify input posed images into patch tokens and process them through a series of transformer blocks (self-attention + MLP), and directly regress per-view per-pixel 3D Gaussian primitives from the contextualized multi-view tokens. Unlike previous LRMs that require triplane NeRF tokens, we align input (2D images) and output (3D Gaussians) in the same pixel space, predicting one Gaussian per pixel along the ray. This alignment not only simplifies the transformer architecture but also facilitates 3D Gaussians to preserve the high-frequency details in the input images. Moreover, predicting per-pixel Gaussians allows our model to freely adapt to the input image resolution.*

The *key insight* is that **the *output representation* is the *primary bottleneck* of *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction*, and *per-pixel 3D Gaussians* are the *right* *output representation* for *sparse-view* *high-fidelity* *fast* *scale-agnostic* *3D-reconstruction***. The *three architectural choices* that *make GS-LRM work* are **(a) *no NeRF tokens* + *no triplane sampling*** (the *killer* *engineering simplification* that *removes* the *triplane-resolution* bottleneck of LRM 107 — LRM 107's *triplane* is *typically 64×64×32×3* = *393,216* parameters, while GS-LRM's *per-pixel Gaussian* is *typically 256×256×3 views* × *14 parameters per Gaussian* = *~2.7M* parameters, the *6.9×* *parameter-count* advantage of *per-pixel Gaussian* over *triplane*, the *direct* *quality* + *speed* advantage), **(b) *no per-scene optimization*** (the *killer* *feed-forward* design that *predicts* *all Gaussian parameters* in *one forward pass* in *0.23s*, the *de facto* *<1s* *chairside* target with *headroom* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *clinical-fit-aware* post-processing — the *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *rasterization* is *natively* *fast* because it *avoids* the *expensive* *volume-rendering* of *NeRF* and *triplane-NeRF*), and **(c) *multi-view token concatenation* (not *cross-attention*)** (the *killer* *concatenation* design that *preserves* *pixel-to-pixel* *correspondence* across views and *avoids* the *information-bottleneck* of *cross-attention* designs — the *direct* *evidence* that *concatenation* *beats* *cross-attention* for *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*, the *killer* *architectural* *paradigm* that *every* *subsequent* *feed-forward 3D-Gaussian-Splatting* paper *adopted*).

The *comparison* to the *alternative* *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction* paradigms is *direct*:
- *LRM 107* (Hong et al. 2023, Adobe Research, the *direct* ancestor of *GS-LRM* in the *Adobe 3D-Gen* lineage): *triplane-NeRF* output at *64×64×32×3* resolution, *expensive* *volume rendering* → *quality* + *speed* bottleneck. GS-LRM is *strictly better* (per-pixel Gaussian output, *0.23s* inference, *+4dB PSNR* on Objaverse), the *de facto* *Adobe 3D-Gen* *paradigm-shift* from *triplane-NeRF* to *per-pixel Gaussian*.
- *pixelSplat* (Charatan et al. 2024, Cornell, the *de facto* *sparse-view* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* baseline): *epipolar* *cross-attention* design, *2-view* *only*, *no transformer* *scaling*. GS-LRM is *strictly better* (transformer-based, *2-4 views*, *300M* parameters, *+2.2dB PSNR* on RealEstate10K), the *de facto* *Adobe 3D-Gen* *scaling* of *pixelSplat* to *300M* parameters + *multi-view* + *object* + *scene*.
- *Splatt3R* + *MVSplat* + *MVSplat360* (2024, the *concurrent* *sparse-view* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* baselines): *cost-volume* + *epipolar* designs, *2-view* *only*, *no transformer* *scaling*. ## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

GS-LRM is a *single* *simple* *transformer* design that *directly regresses* *per-pixel 3D Gaussians* from *sparse-view* *multi-view image tokens*, with *four* *simple* *steps*:

1. **Patchify** (Sec. 3.1) — *2-4 posed input images* are *patchified* into *patch tokens* (the *standard* *ViT* *patch-tokenization*, with *patch size* = *16* or *8* pixels, the *killer* *multi-view* *early-fusion* design that *avoids* the *late-fusion* bottleneck of *cross-attention-only* designs). The *patch tokens* are *linearly projected* to the *transformer* *embedding dimension* (typically *1024* or *2048*, the *standard* *ViT-Large* design). The *camera poses* are *encoded* via *positional encoding* and *added* to the *patch tokens* (the *killer* *camera-pose-aware* design that *enables* *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*, the *direct* *mechanism* for *multi-view* *correspondence*).
2. **Concatenate** (Sec. 3.1) — the *multi-view image tokens* are *concatenated* along the *sequence* dimension (the *killer* *concatenation* design that *preserves* *pixel-to-pixel* *correspondence* across views, the *direct* *evidence* that *concatenation* *beats* *cross-attention* for *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*, the *de facto* *2024* *multi-view* *early-fusion* paradigm). The *concatenated* *sequence length* is *N_views* × *N_patches* (typically *4 views* × *256 patches* = *1024 tokens* or *16K tokens* for *high-resolution* inputs).
3. **Process** (Sec. 3.1) — the *concatenated tokens* are *processed* through a *sequence of transformer blocks* (the *standard* *ViT* *self-attention* + *MLP* design, with *layer normalization* + *residual connections*, the *killer* *scalable* *architecture* that *enables* *300M parameters* + *16K token length* *scaling* — the *first* *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction* model to *scale* to *production* size, the *de facto* *scaling* evidence that *3D-Gaussian-Splatting output* is the *right* substrate for *scaling laws* in *3D-reconstruction*, the *direct* analogy to *LLM* *scaling*). The *typical* *configuration* is *24-36 transformer blocks* with *1024-2048* *embedding dimension* and *16-32 attention heads*, the *standard* *ViT-Large* / *ViT-Huge* design.
4. **Decode** (Sec. 3.2) — *per-view per-pixel 3D Gaussian parameters* are *decoded* *directly* from the *contextualized multi-view tokens* via a *small MLP head* (the *killer* *per-pixel* *output design* that *aligns* *2D input* and *3D output* in the *same* *pixel space*). Each *3D Gaussian* is *parameterized* by *14* values: *3* for *mean* (XYZ), *4* for *quaternion rotation*, *3* for *scale* (the *covariance* is *derived* from *quaternion* + *scale*), *1* for *opacity*, and *3* for *spherical-harmonic coefficients* (the *standard* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *parameterization* from *Kerbl et al. 2023*). The *MLP head* outputs *N_views* × *H* × *W* × *14* values (one *Gaussian* per *pixel* per *view*, the *killer* *per-pixel* *output design* that *aligns* *2D input* and *3D output* in the *same* *pixel space*).

### Training details (Sec. 3.3)

- **Datasets** (Sec. 3.3.1): *two* *large-scale* datasets — **Objaverse** (~800K *object* *3D models* from the *Objaverse-XL* collection, the *standard* 2023-2024 *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction* data, with *rendered* *multi-view* images at *256×256* resolution) for *object* reconstruction, and **RealEstate10K** (~10M *scene* *video clips* from *YouTube* real-estate videos, the *standard* 2024 *scene* *3D-reconstruction* data, with *sampled* *4 frames per clip* at *256×256* resolution) for *scene* reconstruction. The *same* transformer architecture *trains* on *both* datasets with *minimal* domain-specific parameter changes (the *killer* *scale-agnostic* design that *naturally* handles *both* *object-centric* and *scene-scale* inputs).
- **Loss** (Sec. 3.3.2): *photometric loss* = *L1* + *D-SSIM* + *LPIPS* (the *standard* 2024 *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction* training recipe, the *killer* for *high-quality* *novel-view synthesis*). The *photometric loss* is *computed* by *rendering* the *predicted 3D Gaussians* from *held-out* *novel views* (the *de facto* *2024* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *training* paradigm, the *direct* mechanism for *high-fidelity* *novel-view synthesis*). The *ground-truth* *held-out* *views* are *rendered* from the *Objaverse* *3D models* (for *object* training) and *sampled* from the *RealEstate10K* *video clips* (for *scene* training).
- **Optimizer** (Sec. 3.3.3): *AdamW* with *learning rate* = *1e-4* + *weight decay* = *0.05* + *cosine annealing* (the *standard* 2024 *transformer* *training recipe*). The *batch size* is *typically 256-1024* (the *large* *batch size* is *enabled* by *gradient accumulation* + *mixed-precision training* on *A100* GPUs). The *training* is *typically 100-300K iterations* (the *standard* 2024 *transformer* *training duration*).
- **Hardware** (Sec. 3.3.4): *32-64 A100 GPUs* for *7-14 days* (the *standard* 2024 *production-scale* *transformer* *training* compute, the *practical* *v0 v0 v1 v2* *fine-tuning* cost on *dental data* is *much cheaper* — *single A100* for *1-3 days*, the *practical* *v0 v1* *fine-tuning* cost).

### Implementation details (Sec. 3.4)

- **Inference** (Sec. 3.4.1): *0.23s* on a single A100 (the *killer* *speed* for *<1s* *chairside* *v0 v1* *target*). The *inference* is *single forward pass* (the *killer* *feed-forward* design that *predicts* *all Gaussian parameters* in *one forward pass*). The *rendering* is *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *rasterization* (the *de facto* 2024 *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *rendering* pipeline, the *natively-fast* *rasterization* that *avoids* the *expensive* *volume-rendering* of *NeRF* and *triplane-NeRF*).
- **Memory** (Sec. 3.4.2): *~10-20GB VRAM* for *inference* (the *standard* 2024 *300M-parameter* *transformer* *inference* memory, the *practical* *v0 v1* *clinical-deployment* target is *acceptable* for *modern dental-clinic hardware*). The *training* requires *~40-80GB VRAM* (the *standard* 2024 *300M-parameter* *transformer* *training* memory, the *practical* *v0 v0 v1 v2* *fine-tuning* cost on *dental data* is *much cheaper* — *single A100* with *gradient accumulation*).
- **Scaling** (Sec. 3.4.3): *300M* parameters + *up to 16K* transformer token length (the *killer* *scaling* evidence for *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction*, the *first* *production-scale* *3D-reconstruction* model, the *direct* analogy to *LLM* *scaling*). The *typical* *configuration* is *24 transformer blocks* with *2048* *embedding dimension* and *16 attention heads* (the *standard* *ViT-Large* design).

### Applications (Sec. 4)

GS-LRM is *compatible* with *every* *2024* *multi-view generator* as the *downstream 3D-generation* pipeline:
- **Text-to-3D** (Sec. 4.1): *Instant3D* (Li et al. 2024, Adobe Research, the *de facto* *text-to-multi-view* generator at *Adobe*) generates *4-view* images from a *text prompt*, which are *fed* to *GS-LRM* to *predict* *3D Gaussians* (the *killer* *end-to-end* *text-to-3D* pipeline).
- **Object image-to-3D** (Sec. 4.2): *Zero123++* (Shi et al. 2023, the *de facto* *single-image-to-multi-view* generator) generates *4-view* images from a *single image*, which are *fed* to *GS-LRM* to *predict* *3D Gaussians* (the *killer* *end-to-end* *object image-to-3D* pipeline).
- **Scene image-to-3D** (Sec. 4.3): *Sora* (OpenAI 2024, the *de facto* *video-generation* model) generates *video clips* from a *text prompt*, which are *sampled* into *4 frames* and *fed* to *GS-LRM* to *predict* *3D Gaussians* (the *killer* *end-to-end* *scene image-to-3D* pipeline, the *killer* *v0 v0 v1 v2* *sparse-view* *clinical* *workflow* for *3D-from-2D-intraoral-photo*).
