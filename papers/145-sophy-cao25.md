# Paper 145 — SOPHY: Generating Simulation-Ready Objects with PHYsical Materials

## TL;DR

**A joint shape + texture + physical-material latent diffusion model by Junyi Cao (Technical University of Crete + UMass Amherst) and Evangelos Kalogerakis (UMass Amherst), WACV 2026, arXiv:2504.12684 (v1 17 Apr 2025 → v3 11 Aug 2025, 5,604 KB, cs.GR + cs.CV), ~5 GS citations as of 2026-06-11, ERC-funded (No. 101124742), no GitHub code release as of this reading (the WACV 2026 poster #4 only links the project page xjay18.github.io/SOPHY_page, no public repo at github.com/JunyiCao/SOPHY which 404s as of 2026-06-11).** The killer architecture is a **3DShape2VecSet-pretrained VAE** (initialized from ShapeNet 55K-shape pretrain for the shared cross-attention + self-attention layers) with **three sub-codes** (shape s, color c, material m; each M=512 × C0/3=8) and **cross-attention bridges** that model the natural "shape → color → material" dependency (`{f_c} = CA(f_c, f_s)`, `{f_m} = CA(f_m, [f_s, f_c])` — material is conditioned on BOTH shape and color). The per-point material vector is **9 entries** (5 scalars + 1 learnable 4-dim behavior embedding): log Young's modulus (E), Poisson's ratio (ν), log yield stress (σ), friction angle (ϕ), density (ρ), and material behavior type (pure elastic / plastic with softening / plastic without softening / granular). The VAE is then used as the latent space for an **EDM-style diffusion** (3,000 epochs, 18 sampling steps) with **DINOv2-ViT-B/14 image conditioning** OR **CLIP-ViT-L/14 text conditioning** via cross-attention. Trained on a **custom 3,004-object / 15,575-part / 12-category dataset** curated from 3DCoMPaT200 (12 categories: bag, bed, chair, crib, hat, headband, loveseat, pillow, planter, sofa, teddy bear, vase — specifically deformable/articulated objects, NOT cabinets/faucets), annotated by a **two-round VLM (ChatGPT-4o) + iterative expert verification** pipeline: VLM proposes material category + parameters, then 5 mechanical engineering grads verify the resulting MPM simulation and re-query the VLM until plausible. **THE KILLER PRACTICAL RECIPE IS THE VLM-GUIDED ANNOTATION PIPELINE** — the same H5 mechanism v0 needs to add physical material properties to 3DTeethSeg22 + ToSynFCD (since neither has Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, yield stress, friction angle, or density annotations). The killer 4D generation results are **VideoPhy z-score 0.43 image-to-4D / 0.76 text-to-4D**, beating all 4 baselines (DG4D -0.28, Free4D -0.21, B.Dec -0.15, B.Perc 0.21 image / STAG4D -1.23, Free4D 0.07, B.Dec -0.18, B.Perc 0.58 text) and matching the best baseline CLIP score (0.77 image, 0.18 text), in **5 min per video** (10× faster than DG4D's 10 min, 14.6× faster than Free4D's 73 min). The killer auto-encoding numbers are **material behavior classification accuracy 93.55% (vs B.Dec 71.04%, +22.5 pts) and Sim-CD 8.72×10⁻³ (vs B.Dec 53.84×10⁻³, 6.2× reduction)**, the direct H1 evidence that joint shape+material modeling > cascade. ## Research Question

**Q:** Can we train a *single* generative model that *jointly* synthesizes (a) 3D shape geometry, (b) RGB albedo texture, AND (c) physical material properties (Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, yield stress, friction angle, density, material behavior type — the 6 parameters used by the Material Point Method MPM simulator) — from a single image OR text prompt — so that the generated object can be *directly* imported into a physics simulator (Taichi MPM, Bullet, MuJoCo) and exhibit *plausible* physical behavior (deform, break, bounce) — by (1) building a *physics-aware VAE* that compresses (position, color, material) per point into three disentangled sub-codes with cross-attention bridges, (2) training a *latent EDM diffusion* on this VAE's latent space with DINOv2 image / CLIP text conditioning, and (3) annotating a *3K-object / 15.5K-part dataset* with the 6 material parameters via a *VLM-guided + expert-verified* semi-automatic pipeline (using MPM simulation as the verification oracle)?

**Their answer:** **Yes — joint shape+texture+material modeling is *strictly better* than cascade (B.Dec) or perception-based post-hoc estimation (B.Perc) on all metrics**, with the biggest wins on **material behavior classification (+22.5 pts)** and **simulated Chamfer distance (6.2× lower)**. The 4 key insights are: **(a) joint modeling captures the natural shape-material correlation** (e.g., "thin cantilever-shaped chair bases are usually metal"; "thick block-like backrests are usually fabric" — a thin metal cantilever would NEVER have fabric material, but a cascade model would happily predict such inconsistent combinations); **(b) three sub-codes with cross-attention bridges** model the natural pipeline "shape → color → material" (color depends on geometry, material depends on both geometry and color); **(c) MPM simulation is the *right* verification oracle** for material-property VLM annotations (the 5 mechanical engineering grads iteratively re-queried the VLM based on the simulation video, and 3 rounds of feedback were enough to converge to plausible materials for ~80% of test cases); **(d) 18-step EDM sampling in latent space** is *much* faster than 3DGS-based 4D generation methods (DG4D 10 min, Free4D 73 min, SOPHY 5 min) while producing more physically plausible 4D videos (VideoPhy z-score 0.43 image / 0.76 text, vs DG4D -0.28 / STAG4D -1.23).

## Method

### Architecture (3DShape2VecSet-pretrained joint shape-texture-material VAE + EDM diffusion)

**Stage 1: Physics-aware VAE**
- **Backbone:** 3DShape2VecSet (Zhang 2023, ICML) — the canonical 2023 3D latent diffusion backbone that uses cross-attention encoder + self-attention decoder
- **Input representation:** N=2048 surface point cloud P, each point augmented with (3D position, RGB color, 9-dim material vector): `x_j = (p_j, c_j, m_j)`, where `m_j = (log E_j, ν_j, log σ_j, ϕ_j, ρ_j, μ_j^4-dim)` (log scale for E and σ because of the 10⁶ range from soft rubber to stiff metal)
- **Encoder:** M=512 FPS sub-sample of P → cross-attention to N=2048 dense points → 512-dim latent codes per point
- **Latent compression:** 2 FC layers project each latent code to mean and variance (C0=24, with 8 dims for each of shape, color, material sub-codes); reparameterization sampling; project back via FC
- **Decoder:** three sub-branches with cross-attention bridges:
  - `{f_s^(l)} = SelfAttn({f_s^(l-1)})` — shape code is independent
  - `{f_c^(l)} = CA({f_c^(l-1)}, {f_s^(l-1)})` — color code attends to shape
  - `{f_m^(l)} = CA({f_m^(l-1)}, [f_s^(l-1), f_c^(l-1)])` — material code attends to BOTH shape and color
- **Decoder output per query point q:** occupancy (FC + interpolation over shape sub-codes), color (FC + interpolation over color sub-codes, [0,1] normalized), material (FC + interpolation over material sub-codes, log for E/σ, sigmoid × π/2 for ϕ, softmax for behavior)
- **Pretraining init:** cross-attention weights on point positions + self-attention weights on shape sub-codes + occupancy decoder weights initialized from **3DShape2VecSet ShapeNet 55K-pretrain** (the direct H5 mechanism — even though SOPHY's own dataset is small 3K, the *geometry-only* pretrain transfers well to the joint shape+material task)
- **VAE loss:** weighted sum of occupancy BCE (1.0) + color L1 (0.1) + log-E L1 (0.01) + ν L1 (0.1) + log-σ L1 (0.01) + ϕ L1 (0.1) + ρ L1 (0.1) + behavior CE (0.1) + KL reg (0.001), for 500 epochs batch 256

**Stage 2: Latent EDM diffusion**
- **Backbone:** EDM (Karras 2022) on the VAE's 512×24 latent codes
- **Conditioning:** DINOv2-ViT-B/14 features (image) or CLIP-ViT-L/14 features (text), injected via cross-attention
- **Denoiser architecture:** alternating self-attention (SA) + cross-attention (CA) layers
- **Loss:** L_edm = E[||D(Ẑ + N, σ_t, c) - Ẑ||²] with EDM noise schedule
- **Training:** 3,000 epochs, batch 256, lr linearly up to 2e-4 over 200 epochs then cosine decay to 1e-6
- **Sampling:** 18 denoising steps (very fast)
- **Hardware:** 2 NVIDIA L40s GPUs (the *de facto* 2024-2026 academic-gen budget)

**★ THE KILLER PRACTICAL CONTRIBUTION: VLM-guided material annotation pipeline**
1. Pick 12 categories from 3DCoMPaT200 with non-rigid behavior (bag, bed, chair, crib, hat, headband, loveseat, pillow, planter, sofa, teddy bear, vase)
2. For each part: 2-round VLM (ChatGPT-4o) prompt → round 1 = identify fine-grained material category (e.g., "LDPE plastic" not just "plastic"); round 2 = predict 6 MPM parameters based on (category, renderings, color)
3. MPM simulation of 5 test scenarios (drop, throw, tilt, drag, wind) — render video with diffrast (Laine 2020) + 3DGS (Kerbl 2023)
4. 5 mechanical engineering grads review video; if implausible, provide free-text feedback ("the headband is too stiff"), re-query VLM with feedback, re-simulate, iterate
5. 5 mechanical engineering grads reach consensus → store as final annotation
6. **3,004 objects / 15,575 parts annotated in 12 categories** (3-month expert effort, faster than full manual annotation which would take 2-3 years for this scale)

**★ THE KILLER DATASET INNOVATION: per-point material with 9-dim vector**
- Most material-aware 3D datasets (3DCoMPaT200, ABO, Matsynth) annotate materials *per part* (one material label per semantic part)
- SOPHY annotates materials *per point* with 9-dim continuous vector (E, ν, σ, ϕ, ρ, 4-dim behavior) — this is *necessary* because in real-world objects, materials are often *heterogeneous within a part* (e.g., a chair cushion has gradient foam density, not uniform)
- The label-propagation trick: 3DCoMPaT200 part labels → 5-NN majority vote for interior points → 9-dim material per point (the right way to go from sparse part-level annotations to dense point-level supervision)

### PhysX-style joint sim-ready output
- After sampling latent codes, decode to occupancy on 3D grid → Marching Cubes mesh
- Decode color and material at densely sampled surface points
- Output: (mesh, per-vertex color, per-vertex 9-dim material vector) — directly importable into Taichi MPM, Bullet, MuJoCo (via custom material plugin)
- Apply Paint3D (Zeng 2024) post-processing for higher-resolution 2D texture (UV unwrap → bake vertex colors → 2D texture refinement, ~1 min per object)

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage VAE + flow-matching is the right architecture for joint multi-attribute prediction):** **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT in the 145-paper reading list (tied with 142 PhysX-3D, 143 PhysX-Anything, 144 PhysX-Omni)** — SOPHY is *literally* a 2-stage VAE-then-EDM-diffusion architecture for joint shape+texture+material prediction, and the *killer* evidence is the +22.5 pt material behavior accuracy over the B.Dec cascade baseline. The disentangled sub-codes (shape, color, material) with cross-attention bridges are the *exact* H1 mechanism for *joint* multi-attribute prediction — the cross-attention bridges model the natural "shape → color → material" dependency in 3D asset creation workflows.

**H2 (latent diffusion/flow > direct prediction):** **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT in the 145-paper reading list (tied with 100 TripoSG, 141 Pixie, 142 PhysX-3D, 143 PhysX-Anything, 144 PhysX-Omni)** — the 512×24 latent code + 18-step EDM sampling is the *exact* H2 mechanism, and the *killer* evidence is the 14.6× speedup over Free4D (5 min vs 73 min for the same 4D output) while producing *better* VideoPhy scores. The 2-stage VAE-then-diffusion is *necessary* for joint multi-attribute generation because direct mesh+material generation in pixel space would be intractable.

**H3 (multi-context conditioning improves all individual tasks):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT** — SOPHY is conditioned on DINOv2 image features AND CLIP text features via cross-attention, the *direct* H3 mechanism for multi-modal conditioning. The 5-min runtime for both image and text conditioning (vs STAG4D 66 min text-only and Free4D 73 min text-only) is the *killer* evidence that joint multi-modal conditioning is *more efficient* than separate models per modality. The cross-attention between sub-codes (shape, color, material) is also H3 (multi-context = multi-sub-code).

**H4 (implicit SDF > explicit mesh/voxel):** **MILD CONTRADICTION** — SOPHY uses *explicit* occupancy on 3D grid + Marching Cubes for mesh extraction, *not* implicit SDF. The internal representation is *latent* (which is implicit-like), but the *output* is explicit mesh + per-vertex material, the *direct* contradiction to H4 for joint physics-aware generation. The 142-144 PhysX papers also contradict H4 (all use explicit voxel or mesh), and SOPHY is the *5th* paper in the reading list to make this contradiction, the *clear* consensus that *for physics-aware 3D-gen, explicit mesh + per-vertex material is the right substrate* (not implicit SDF). H4's validity is now *constrained* to non-physics-aware 3D-gen (where 004 Diffusion-SDF and 003 DiGS were the only papers to support it).

**H5 (synthetic + finetune scales 3D generation to data-scarce domains):** **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT in the 145-paper reading list (tied with 142 PhysX-3D, 111 LRM-Zero, 143 PhysX-Anything, 144 PhysX-Omni)** — the 3,004-object SOPHY dataset is *small* (3 months of expert annotation), but combined with the 3DShape2VecSet ShapeNet 55K-pretrain for the *geometry-only* parts, the model generalizes to Objaverse and GSO at inference time, the *direct* H5 mechanism. The VLM-guided + expert-verified annotation pipeline is the *killer* practical recipe for scaling material annotations to *any* 3D dataset (3DTeethSeg22, ToSynFCD, etc.) — for v0, the same pipeline could be applied to clinical dental arches to add 9-dim material annotations to 1,600+ arches in 1-2 months instead of 2-3 years.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **★ The 9-dim per-point material vector (log E, ν, log σ, ϕ, ρ, 4-dim behavior embedding) is the *right* schema for joint shape+material generation** — the log scale for E and σ is *necessary* because Young's modulus varies from 10⁻³ GPa (very soft rubber) to 10³ GPa (diamond, ~6 orders of magnitude). The 4-dim behavior embedding is *learned* (not one-hot) so the model can interpolate between behavior types. For v0, the killer clinical mapping: **zirconia crown = (E~200 GPa, ν~0.3, σ~1 GPa, ϕ~0.3, ρ~6 g/cm³, rigid)**, **enamel = (E~80-90 GPa, ν~0.3, σ~0.5 GPa, ϕ~0.3, ρ~3 g/cm³, rigid)**, **dentin = (E~15-20 GPa, ν~0.3, σ~0.1 GPa, ϕ~0.3, ρ~2 g/cm³, rigid)**, **gum/PDL = (E~few MPa, ν~0.45, σ~few kPa, ϕ~0.5, ρ~1 g/cm³, plastic with softening)**.

2. **★ The cross-attention bridge between sub-codes is the *killer* practical design** — material is conditioned on BOTH shape and color (Eq. 4), the *exact* mechanism that lets the model capture the "thin cantilever = metal" and "thick block = fabric" correlations. The ablation in Table 1 shows that removing the cross-attention (w/o CA) increases Sim-CD by 2× (17.47 vs 8.72), the *direct* evidence that cross-attention bridges are *necessary* for joint shape+material modeling.

3. **★ The VLM-guided + expert-verified annotation pipeline uses MPM simulation as the verification oracle** — 5 mechanical engineering grads don't directly verify the 9-dim material vector; they verify the *resulting 4D simulation video* (drop, throw, tilt, drag, wind scenarios) and provide natural-language feedback ("the headband is too stiff") that the VLM uses to update the material parameters. The killer practical insight: **simulation-based verification is the *right* way to validate material annotations** because it's the *actual* downstream task, not the abstract 9-dim vector.

4. **★ The dataset is *small* (3,004 objects / 15,575 parts / 12 categories) but the *joint* annotation schema is the killer contribution** — most material-aware datasets (3DCoMPaT200 with 200 categories, ABO with 7K+ objects) have *coarse* per-part material labels that are not directly usable in MPM. SOPHY's *9-dim per-point continuous* annotation is the *right* schema, and the *VLM-pipeline* is the *only* practical way to scale this to millions of objects.

5. **★ The "Fused" variant (single latent code, no disentanglement) does slightly better on ν and ϕ but much worse on Sim-CD** — this is a fascinating *negative* result that says *physics-aware* multi-attribute generation *requires* disentangled sub-codes, but *parameter-only* (non-physics) prediction can be done with fused codes. The practical implication: for v0 sub-task 4, *disentangle* the geometry from the material so the model can do *physics-aware* optimization, not just parameter regression.

6. **★ The 18-step EDM sampling is *very* fast** — 5 min for the full image-to-4D pipeline (decode latent → occupancy grid → Marching Cubes → texture + material decoding → MPM simulation → 4D video render). For v0, this is *faster* than DITA 058 + Hwang 061 + DMC 033 cascaded pipeline (~30-60 min) while producing *joint* shape+material output.

7. **★ The 3,000-epoch diffusion training on 3K objects is *undertrained* by absolute standards** (typical 3D latent diffusion is 5K-10K epochs), but the *ShapeNet 55K pretrain of the VAE* means the geometry prior is *already converged*. This is a practical recipe for *resource-constrained* v0 fine-tuning on dental data: pretrain VAE on 3DTeethSeg22 + ToSynFCD (1.5K arches), then fine-tune the diffusion on the same data (1-2K epochs, $200-500 Lambda).

8. **★ "Material behavior" is *not* one-hot** — it's a 4-dim learnable embedding that allows the model to interpolate between pure elastic (rubber), plastic with softening (clay), plastic without softening (metal), and granular (sand). For v0, this is *exactly* the right schema for dental materials: enamel, dentin, zirconia, gum/PDL, pulp can each be assigned a *learned* behavior embedding that's trained from MPM simulation data.
