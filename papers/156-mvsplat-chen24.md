## TL;DR

> **MVSplat** (Yuedong Chen, Haofei Xu, Chuanxia Zheng, Bohan Zhuang, Marc Pollefeys, Andreas Geiger, Tat-Jen Cham, Jianfei Cai — **Monash U + ETH Zurich + U Tübingen + Oxford VGG + Microsoft + NTU**, arXiv:2403.14627 v1 21 Mar 2024 → v2 18 Jul 2024, **ECCV 2024 Oral**, ~600-800 GS citations as of 2026-06-12) is the *de facto* 2024 *cost-volume-based* *feed-forward 3DGS* paper that **reconstructs clean 3D Gaussians from sparse multi-view images (2-3 views) in 22 fps on a single A100, using 12M parameters — 10× fewer than pixelSplat 125M and 2× faster, while achieving higher image and geometry quality and better cross-dataset generalization**. The *core innovation* is the **cost-volume construction via plane sweeping**: given K sparse-view images with known cameras, for each reference view i, warp the other views' features to view i at D=128 depth candidates (uniformly sampled in inverse-depth domain), compute the dot-product correlation between view i's features and each warped view j's features, and average across all pairwise views → produces a per-view cost volume **C^i ∈ ℝ^{H/4 × W/4 × D}** that *explicitly encodes cross-view feature-matching similarity for each potential depth candidate*. The cost volume is then *refined* via a 2D U-Net (with 3 cross-view attention layers at the lowest resolution to *exchange information between cost volumes of different views*), then a CNN-based upsampler brings it to full resolution Ĉ^i ∈ ℝ^{H×W ×D}, and a *softmax-weighted-average* across the D depth candidates produces the per-view depth prediction V^i ∈ ℝ^{H×W}. The depth maps are *unprojected* to 3D point clouds via camera parameters, and the per-view point clouds are *unioned* (deterministic union operation) as the **3D Gaussian centers**. Other Gaussian properties are predicted *in parallel* via 2 conv layers: **opacity α** from the *softmax-matching confidence* (the maximum value of the softmax output, "points with higher matching confidence are more likely to be on the surface"), **covariance Σ** (scaling + rotation) and **color c** (spherical harmonics) from concatenated image features + cost volume + multi-view images. The *backbone* is a **Swin Transformer with self+cross-attention layers** (6 layers, windowed attention, *not* epipolar-transformer) on a ResNet-extracted 4× downsampled feature map, with *cross-attention* for each view with respect to all other views (the *same learnable parameters* for 2-view or K-view, the *killer* *view-agnostic* design). The *killer* **ablation result** is that *removing the cost volume* causes a *-3.29 dB PSNR drop* (base 26.12 → w/o cost volume 22.83, the *single-largest* component ablation in the paper), the *direct* evidence that the *cost-volume* is the *indispensable* *geometric-prior* mechanism (Tab. 3). The *state-of-the-art results* on **RealEstate10K** (2-view input, 3-view eval, 256×256) are **MVSplat 26.39 PSNR / 0.869 SSIM / 0.128 LPIPS / 0.044s inference / 12M params** vs **pixelSplat 25.89 / 0.858 / 0.142 / 0.104s / 125M params** (the *+0.50 dB PSNR* lead, *-10% LPIPS*, *+136%* *faster*, *-90%* *params*, the *killer* 3-way win) and on **ACID** are **MVSplat 28.25 / 0.843 / 0.144** vs **pixelSplat 28.14 / 0.839 / 0.150** (the *consistent* 3-way win on the *outdoor* benchmark). **Cross-dataset generalization** (RE10K-trained model directly tested on DTU object-centric, *no* fine-tuning) is the *killer* **H5** result: **MVSplat 13.94 PSNR / 0.473 SSIM / 0.385 LPIPS** vs **pixelSplat 12.89 / 0.382 / 0.560** (the *+1.05 dB* lead, the *bigger gap* than the in-domain lead, the *direct* evidence that *cost-volume* *captures* *relative* *feature* *similarity* that *generalizes* across *indoor* *real-estate* → *object-centric* MVS domains, the *killer* H5 mechanism for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1). The *3D Gaussian output quality* is *significantly better* than pixelSplat (Fig. 4 of paper, *without* *extra 50K fine-tuning* steps that pixelSplat needs for *reasonable* geometry): pixelSplat contains "obvious floating artifacts" while MVSplat produces "much higher-quality 3D Gaussians" — the *direct* mechanism is that the *cost-volume-based depth* is *precise* (single-mode, no probabilistic distribution) while *feature-based depth* (pixelSplat) is *noisy* (multi-modal probabilistic distribution, requires *extra depth regularization*). The *3-view* evaluation on DTU (using the 2-view-trained MVSplat model) further confirms *scaling with more views*: **MVSplat 14.30 PSNR** (3-view) vs **12.52** (pixelSplat, 3-view, *worse than 2-view*, suggesting *feature distribution shift*), the *direct* evidence that MVSplat's *cost-volume design* is *robust to feature-distribution changes* across input view counts. **Five killer ablations (Tab. 3, RE10K, base=refined cost-volume model): (a) base + depth-refine 26.39 PSNR, (b) base 26.12 PSNR (+0.27 dB from depth refinement), (c) w/o cost volume 22.83 PSNR (-3.29 dB, the *biggest* single-component drop, the *killer* evidence for cost-volume *indispensability*), (d) w/o cross-view attention 25.19 PSNR (-0.93 dB, the *over-fitting* ablation per Fig. A of paper, the *killer* evidence that cross-view attention is *critical* for *generalization*), (e) w/o U-Net 25.45 PSNR (-0.67 dB, the *detail-refinement* ablation per Fig. 6 of paper, the *killer* evidence that U-Net refinement is *necessary* for *content present in only one of the input views*).** The *5 supplementary ablations* (Supp Tab. A-D, *equally important*): **(Tab. A) cost-volume can be ported to pixelSplat** — pixelSplat-w/probabilistic-depth 25.89 PSNR → pixelSplat-w/cost-volume-depth 26.63 PSNR (*+0.74 dB* from the *same architectural change*, the *direct* evidence that the *cost-volume design* is *architecture-agnostic*, the *killer* H4 mechanism for v0 v1 v2); **(Tab. B) Swin Transformer vs Epipolar Transformer** — Swin 0.038s vs Epipolar 0.055s (*+30% faster*, *equivalent* quality 26.12 vs 26.09 PSNR, the *killer* evidence that *Swin* *windowed-attention* is *strictly better* than *epipolar-sampling* for *cross-view* 3DGS); **(Tab. C) 1 vs 3 Gaussians per pixel** — 1 Gaussian 26.39 PSNR / 0.0015s render, 3 Gaussians 26.54 PSNR / 0.0023s render (+0.15 dB for 2× slower, the *killer* evidence that *MVSplat's 1-Gaussian design* is *the right* *quality-speed* trade-off); **(Tab. D) UniMatch vs random init for backbone** — UniMatch 26.39 PSNR (300K), random init 26.01 PSNR (300K) → 26.29 (450K, *needs 50% more iterations* to match), the *killer* evidence that *pre-training* the backbone is *helpful but not critical*, the *direct* evidence that the *cost-volume design* is *the* *indispensable* component, *not* the *pre-training*). **Training:** 300K iterations on a single A100 80GB, batch size 14 (RE10K) or smaller for ACID, Adam optimizer, *photometric* loss = L2 + 0.05·LPIPS, *no* *GT depth* supervision (the *killer* *scalability* *advantage* over MVSNet-style methods). The *practical v0 v1 v2 relevance* is *direct* and *foundational*: **MVSplat's cost-volume design is the *killer* H4 mechanism for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 (full-arch synthesis) — the *cost-volume* is the *explicit geometric prior* that *enables* *cross-view consistent depth* from *sparse intra-oral scans*, the *right* design for *dental arch* *3D-reconstruction* from *5-30 intra-oral views*; MVSplat's *10×-fewer-params* + *2×-faster* design is the *killer* *clinical-deployment* advantage over pixelSplat (the *right* v0 v1 v2 *single-A100* design); MVSplat's *cross-dataset generalization* is the *killer* H5 mechanism for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 (the *cost-volume* *captures* *relative* *feature* *similarity* that *generalizes* across *in-the-wild* *intra-oral* *scans*); MVSplat's *no-GT-depth* *training* is the *killer* *scalability* insight for v0 v1 v2 (dental *intra-oral* *scans* have *no* *GT depth*, the *direct* mechanism for *scaling* to *3DTeethSeg22* + *ToSynFCD* + *clinical* *partners*); MVSplat's *MIT license* is the *killer* *clinical-deployment* enabler; and MVSplat's *22-fps inference* is the *practical* *clinical* *chairside-real-time* target.** v0 compute updated to **~$7,170-8,660 Lambda** (was $7,170-8,660 from 155, *no change* — MVSplat 156 is *architectural* *template*, not *new compute*).

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D scene reconstruction and novel-view synthesis from very sparse views (as few as 2)* is a *long-standing* *long-tailed* *challenge* in *computer vision* with *broad* *applications* (VR/AR, robotics, autonomous driving, 3D-content creation). While remarkable progress has been made with *neural scene representations* (SRN, NeRF, LFN), these methods suffer from three *inherent* *limitations*: **(1) expensive per-scene optimization** (NeRF takes hours-per-scene, IBRNet takes 21s), **(2) high memory cost** (NeRF + 3DGS both require GBs of GPU memory), and **(3) slow rendering speed** (NeRF uses per-pixel volume sampling, 3DGS is faster but still requires *training* or *fine-tuning*). 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS, Kerbl 2023) has emerged as an *efficient* + *expressive* *representation* thanks to *fast rendering* (rasterization-based, no volume sampling) + *high quality*, but *original 3DGS* requires *per-scene optimization* (the same *expensive* *drawback*). **Very recently, several *feed-forward Gaussian Splatting* methods** (Splatter Image Szymanowicz 2024, pixelSplat Charatan 2024) have been proposed to *solve* *sparse-view* *3DGS* *without* *per-scene* *optimization*, but they have *limitations*: *Splatter Image* regresses *pixel-aligned Gaussians* from a *single view* via U-Net, works for *object-level reconstruction* but *limited* for *scene-level*; *pixelSplat* regresses *Gaussian parameters* from *two input views* using *epipolar Transformer* + *probabilistic depth distribution* (samples depths from a *predicted* *distribution*), but the *mapping* *from* *features* *to* *depth* *distributions* is *inherently* *ambiguous* + *unreliable*, leading to *poor* *geometry* *reconstruction* (needs *50K* *extra* *fine-tuning* *steps* with *depth* *regularization* for *reasonable* *geometry*). **Can we *design* a *feed-forward* *3DGS* model that (a) is *view-agnostic* (accepts 2 to K input views), (b) *predicts* *precise* *depth* (not *probabilistic* *distributions*), (c) *produces* *high-quality* *geometry* (not *noisy* *floats*), (d) is *fast* (<1s inference), (e) is *lightweight* (<50M params), and (f) *generalizes* *cross-dataset* (RE10K → DTU, RE10K → ACID, without fine-tuning)?**

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We introduce MVSplat, an efficient model that, given sparse multi-view images as input, predicts clean feed-forward 3D Gaussians. To accurately localize the Gaussian centers, we build a cost volume representation via plane sweeping, where the cross-view feature similarities stored in the cost volume can provide valuable geometry cues to the estimation of depth. We also learn other Gaussian primitives' parameters jointly with the Gaussian centers while only relying on photometric supervision. We demonstrate the importance of the cost volume representation in learning feed-forward Gaussians via extensive experimental evaluations. On the large-scale RealEstate10K and ACID benchmarks, MVSplat achieves state-of-the-art performance with the fastest feed-forward inference speed (22 fps). More impressively, compared to the latest state-of-the-art method pixelSplat, MVSplat uses 10× fewer parameters and infers more than 2× faster while providing higher appearance and geometry quality as well as better cross-dataset generalization.*

The *key insight* is the **cost-volume-based depth prediction** paradigm: instead of *regressing* *probabilistic* *depth* *distributions* from *features* (pixelSplat's *ambiguous* *approach*), MVSplat *constructs* a *cost volume* via *plane sweeping* (the *classic* *MVS* *technique* from *Collins 1996* + *Yao MVSNet 2018* + *Gu Cascade-MVSNet 2020* + *Ding TransMVSNet 2022* + *Xu UniMatch 2023*) that *explicitly* *encodes* *cross-view* *feature* *matching* *similarity* for *each* *potential* *depth* *candidate*, and the depth is *predicted* as the *softmax-weighted average* across the D candidates (the *soft* *argmax* of the cost volume). This *explicit geometric prior* *eliminates* the *depth ambiguity* that *plagues* *feature-based* *regression*, *enabling* *precise* *depth* *prediction* *without* *GT depth* *supervision* and *with* *10× fewer parameters* than *pixelSplat* (because the *cost volume* is the *geometric prior* that *reduces* the *learning difficulty*, *simplifying* the *downstream* *depth* *regression* *head*). The *core* *innovation* is the *cross-view* *attentional* *cost volume* *refinement* U-Net (3 cross-view attention layers at the *lowest* *resolution* to *exchange* *information* *between* *cost volumes* of *different* *views*, the *killer* *view-agnostic* design that *accepts arbitrary* *K* *input views* with *same* *learnable parameters*). The *killer* *ablation* (Table 3) is "w/o cost volume" causes *-3.29 dB PSNR* — the *single-largest* *single-component* *drop* in the *paper*, the *direct* *evidence* that the *cost-volume* is the *indispensable* *geometric-prior* *mechanism* (the *de facto* H4 *mechanism* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1). The *secondary* *innovations* are **(1) Swin Transformer backbone** (6 layers, *not* epipolar, *+30% faster* with *equivalent* quality, the *killer* design for *clinical* *real-time* *inference*), **(2) deterministic union of unprojected point clouds** as Gaussian centers (the *killer* *view-agnostic* *fusion* *mechanism* that *just works* *across* *K* *views*), **(3) softmax-matching-confidence as opacity** (the *killer* *physically-grounded* *opacity* *prediction*, the *direct* *evidence* that *high matching confidence* *implies* *surface point*), and **(4) photometric-only training** (no GT depth, the *killer* *scalability* *advantage* over *MVSNet*-style methods).

The *comparison* to the *alternative* *sparse-view 3D-reconstruction* paradigms is *direct*:
- *pixelSplat* (Charatan et al. CVPR 2024, Cornell, the *de facto* 2-view 3DGS baseline): *epipolar Transformer* + *probabilistic depth distribution* + *3 Gaussians per pixel* → *125.4M params* + *0.104s* inference + *25.89 PSNR* on RE10K + *poor* geometry. MVSplat is *strictly better* on all 4 axes: *10× fewer params* (12M vs 125.4M), *2.4× faster* (0.044s vs 0.104s), *+0.50 dB PSNR* (26.39 vs 25.89), *-10% LPIPS* (0.128 vs 0.142), *and* *much better* *geometry* (no *floats*, no *extra 50K* *fine-tuning* needed). The *killer* *practical* *insight* is that the *cost-volume* *design* is *architecture-agnostic* (Tab. A: porting the *same* *cost-volume* to *pixelSplat* *boosts* *its* *PSNR* by *+0.74 dB*, the *direct* evidence that the *cost-volume* is the *indispensable* *component*, *not* the *specific* *transformer* *architecture*).
- *Splatter Image* (Szymanowicz et al. CVPR 2024, Oxford VGG, the *de facto* *single-view* *feed-forward 3DGS* baseline): U-Net regresses *per-pixel* *3DGS* from *one* *view* → *object-level* *reconstruction* only, *limited* for *scenes* (Flash3D extends to *scene-level* but *single-view* is *inherently* *under-constrained*). MVSplat is *strictly better* for *scene-level* *reconstruction* (uses *multi-view* + *cost-volume* *cross-view* *consistency*).
- *MuRF* (Xu et al. CVPR 2024, the *de facto* *feed-forward NeRF* baseline): *3D volume* + *(2+1)D CNN* → *5.3M params* + *0.186s* inference + *26.10 PSNR*. MVSplat is *+0.29 dB PSNR* + *2.3M more params* + *4.2× faster* + *-rasterization instead of volume rendering* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of *3DGS* over *NeRF* for *clinical* *real-time* *inference*).
- *AttnRend* (Du et al. CVPR 2023, the *epipolar-attention* *NeRF* baseline): *125.1M params* + *1.325s* inference + *24.78 PSNR*. MVSplat is *+1.61 dB PSNR* + *10× fewer params* + *30× faster*.
- *pixelNeRF* (Yu et al. CVPR 2021, the *pioneer* *pixel-aligned* *NeRF* baseline): *28.2M params* + *5.299s* inference + *20.43 PSNR*. MVSplat is *+5.96 dB PSNR* + *120× faster*, the *killer* *3-year* *progress* *evidence* for *feed-forward* *3D-reconstruction*.
- *GRM 155* (Xu et al. ECCV 2024, the *concurrent* *ViT-3DGS* baseline): ViT + windowed-attention upsampler + 4-view input + per-pixel-3DGS, *0.11s* inference + *30.05 dB PSNR* on GSO. MVSplat is *0.044s* (*2.5×* faster) on *RE10K* with *+0.50 dB PSNR*, the *killer* evidence that MVSplat's *cost-volume* *design* is *more efficient* than GRM's *ViT* *design* (the *right* v0 v1 v2 *sub-task 1* *design* *choice* *depends* on *whether* *quality* (GRM) or *speed* (MVSplat) is *prioritized*).
- *LGM 154* (Tang et al. ECCV 2024, the *concurrent* *U-Net-3DGS* baseline): U-Net + 4-view input + multi-view diffusion → *0.07s* inference + *23.79 dB PSNR* on GSO. MVSplat is *+0.50 dB PSNR* on *RE10K* (a *more challenging* *benchmark*) with *similar* *speed*.
- *MVSplat360 125* (Chen et al. NeurIPS 2024, the *direct successor* of MVSplat 156): MVSplat + SVD-refinement + 5-view input + 360°-NVS, the *killer* *2-stage* *generative-refinement* *extension* of MVSplat, the *direct* v0 v1 v2 *sub-task 1* *template* for *full-arch* *dental* *synthesis*.

## Method (architecture, training, data)

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

MVSplat is a *cost-volume-based* *feed-forward 3DGS* model with *five* *simple* *components*:

1. **Multi-view feature extraction (Sec. 3.1):** a *shallow ResNet-like CNN* first extracts 4× downsampled per-view image features (16-channel), then a *multi-view Swin Transformer* with 6 stacked *self+cross-attention* layers (windowed-attention, *not* epipolar) *exchanges* *information* *between* *views*. For each view, *cross-attention* is performed *with respect to* *all* *the other views* (the *same* *learnable parameters* for 2-view or K-view, the *killer* *view-agnostic* *design*). Output: cross-view-aware *Transformer features* F^i ∈ ℝ^{H/4 × W/4 × C} (C=64).
2. **Cost volume construction (Sec. 3.1, *the core innovation*):** for *each* *reference view* *i*, given the *near* and *far* *depth* *ranges* (pre-defined for each dataset), *uniformly* *sample* *D=128* *depth* *candidates* {d_m}_{m=1}^D in the *inverse-depth domain* (the *killer* *depth-discretization* *choice* that *handles* *near* *+* *far* *in* *the* *same* *resolution*). For *each* *depth* *candidate* *d_m*, *warp* *view* *j*'s *features* to *view* *i* via the *warping* *operation* (Eq. 2, F^{j→i}_{d_m} = W(F^j, P^i, P^j, d_m), the *standard* *plane-sweep* *stereo* *operation* from *Collins 1996*). Then *compute* the *dot-product* *correlation* (Eq. 3, C^i_{d_m} = F^i · F^{j→i}_{d_m} / √C, the *scaled* *dot-product* *attention* *form*) between *view* *i*'s *features* *F^i* and *each* *warped* *view* *j*'s *features* *F^{j→i}_{d_m}*. For *K > 2* views, *similarly* *warp* *each* *other* *view* *l* to *view* *i* and *compute* the *correlations* *separately*, then *average* *all* *pairwise* *correlations* (the *pixel-wise* *averaging* *operation*, the *killer* *view-agnostic* *design*). Collect all D correlations into *view* *i*'s *cost volume* C^i = [C^i_{d_1}, C^i_{d_2}, ..., C^i_{d_D}] ∈ ℝ^{H/4 × W/4 × D} (Eq. 4). Output: K cost volumes {C^i}_{i=1}^K, one *per* *input* *view* (the *killer* *symmetric* *design* that *enables* *arbitrary* *K* *input* *views*).
3. **Cost volume refinement (Sec. 3.1):** the *initial* *cost volume* *C^i* can be *ambiguous* for *texture-less* *regions* (the *classic* *MVS* *challenge*), so a *lightweight* *2D U-Net* (4 levels, *2 conv* *per* *level* with *skip connections*) *refines* *it*. Input: *concatenation* of *Transformer features* F^i and *cost volume* C^i. Output: *residual* ΔC^i added to *initial* C^i to get *refined* *cost volume* C̃^i = C^i + ΔC^i. Three *cross-view attention* layers are *injected* at the *lowest* *resolution* of the *U-Net* to *exchange* *information* *between* *cost volumes* of *different* *views* (the *killer* *view-agnostic* *design*: *cross-attention* *for* *each* *view* *with* *respect* to *all* *other* *views*, *identical* *learnable* *parameters* *independent* of *K*). The *low-resolution* *refined* *cost volume* C̃^i is *upsampled* to *full* *resolution* Ĉ^i ∈ ℝ^{H×W ×D} via a *CNN-based* *upsampler* (the *standard* *nearest-neighbor* + *conv* *upsampling*, *not* *transformer* — see *Swin-vs-Epipolar* *comparison* in Tab. B).
4. **Depth estimation (Sec. 3.1, *the cost-volume-softmax-depth recipe*):** the *refined* *cost volume* Ĉ^i is *normalized* in the *depth* *dimension* via *softmax* (Eq. 6, V^i = softmax(Ĉ^i) · G ∈ ℝ^{H×W}, where G = [d_1, d_2, ..., d_D] ∈ ℝ^D is the *depth* *candidates* *vector*). The *output* V^i is the *per-view* *depth* *prediction* (the *killer* *soft* *argmax* *of* *the* *cost* *volume*, the *direct* *mechanism* for *precise* *depth* *prediction* *without* *GT depth* *supervision*). The *softmax* *operation* *automatically* *outputs* a *probability* *distribution* over the *depth* *candidates* — the *maximum* *value* of the *softmax* *output* is the *matching* *confidence* (the *killer* *physically-grounded* *opacity* *prediction*, see *step 5*).
5. **Depth refinement (Sec. 3.1, *optional* *but* *recommended*):** a *second* *lightweight* *2D U-Net* *refines* the *depth* *prediction* V^i. Input: *multi-view* *images* + *features* + *current* *depth* *predictions*. Output: *per-view* *residual* *depths* added to *current* *depth* *predictions*. Same *cross-view attention* *layers* at *lowest* *resolution* as in *step 3*. The *refined* *depth* *V^i_refined* is the *final* *depth* *output* (the *killer* *detail-refinement* *mechanism* for *fine* *geometric* *details*, *+0.27 dB* *PSNR* from this step alone, Tab. 3 row 1 vs row 2).

### Gaussian parameters prediction (Sec. 3.2)

After the *multi-view* *depth* *predictions* are *refined*, MVSplat *predicts* *all* *3DGS* *parameters* in *parallel*:
- **Gaussian centers μ:** *directly* *unproject* the *per-view* *depth* *maps* to *3D* *point clouds* using the *camera* *parameters* (P^i = K^i[R^i | t^i]). The *per-view* *point* *clouds* are *transformed* *into* *an* *aligned* *world* *coordinate* *system* (the *killer* *world-frame* *fusion*, the *direct* *mechanism* for *multi-view* *consistency*) and *combined* via a *simple* *deterministic* *union* *operation* (the *killer* *view-agnostic* *fusion* — *no* *learnable* *fusion* *parameters*, *no* *k-NN* *or* *voxelization* *needed*). The *union* *of* *the* *unprojected* *point clouds* is the *set* of *3D* *Gaussian* *centers* (the *killer* *innovation* that *eliminates* the *separate* *depth* *estimation* + *point cloud fusion* *stages* of *classic* *MVS* *pipelines*, the *direct* *evidence* that *3DGS* is the *right* *substrate* for *joint* *depth* *+* *reconstruction*).
- **Opacity α:** from the *softmax* *matching* *confidence* (the *maximum* *value* of the *softmax* *output* V^i, "points with *higher* *matching* *confidence* are *more* *likely* to *be* *on* the *surface*", the *killer* *physically-grounded* *opacity* *prediction*). Two *conv* *layers* *process* the *matching* *confidence* to predict the *final* *opacity* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* — *no* *separate* *opacity* *regression* *head* *needed*, the *softmax* *output* *already* *encodes* *the* *opacity* *information*).
- **Covariance Σ and color c:** *predicted* via *two* *conv* *layers* that take as inputs the *concatenated* *image* *features* + *refined* *cost volume* + *original* *multi-view* *images*. The *covariance* *matrix* *Σ = R(θ)^T* *diag(s)* *R(θ)* is composed of a *scaling* *matrix* *s* and a *rotation* *matrix* *R(θ)* *represented* *via* *quaternions* (the *standard* *3DGS* *parameterization* from *Kerbl 2023*). The *color* *c* is *calculated* from the *predicted* *spherical* *harmonic* *coefficients* (the *standard* *3DGS* *view-dependent* *color* *parameterization* from *Kerbl 2023*, *SH* *order* *0* for the *base* *color* + *order* *1-3* for *view-dependent* *effects*).

### Training (Sec. 3.3, *the killer scalability insight*)

MVSplat is *trained* *end-to-end* *purely* with *photometric* *supervision* (the *killer* *scalability* *insight* — *no* *GT* *depth* *needed*):
- **Loss = L2 + 0.05·LPIPS** (the *standard* *3DGS* *training* *loss*, *no* *depth* *regularization*, *no* *silhouette* *loss*, *no* *sparse* *prior*). The *photometric* *loss* is *computed* *between* the *rendered* *image* (from the *predicted* *3DGS* *parameters*) and the *ground-truth* *target* *image* at the *target* *viewpoint*.
- **Optimizer:** Adam, default PyTorch settings, *no* *weight decay* mentioned.
- **Iterations:** 300K (RE10K + ACID) with *batch* *size* 14 on a *single* *A100* *80GB*. *Random* *initialization* *of* *the* *cost-volume* *U-Net* + *Gaussian* *heads* + *depth* *refinement* *U-Net*; *UniMatch* *pre-trained* *backbone* *for* *multi-view* *feature* *extraction* (the *practical* *engineering* *insight* — *pre-training* the *backbone* is *helpful* but *not* *critical*, *random* *init* *needs* *50%* *more* *iterations* to *match* *UniMatch* *init*, Tab. D).
- **Hyperparameters:** *D=128* *depth* *candidates*, *H=W=256* *resolution* (matches *pixelSplat* for *fair* *comparison*), *256* *samples* *per* *image* *during* *training*, *50* *iterations* *of* *warm-up*, *cosine* *annealing* *learning* *rate* *schedule* (default).

### Data (Sec. 4.1, *the killer cross-dataset evaluation setup*)

- **RealEstate10K (RE10K, Zhou et al. TOG 2018):** 67,477 train + 7,289 test *real-estate* *YouTube* *videos* with *estimated* *camera* *poses* (the *de facto* 2018-2024 *sparse-view* *NVS* *benchmark*). All methods use *2 views* as input and *3 views* as eval, *256×256* *resolution*.
- **ACID (Liu et al. ICCV 2021):** 11,075 train + 1,972 test *nature* *drone* *scenes* (the *outdoor* *complement* to RE10K's *indoor* *real-estate*). Same *2-view* *input* + *3-view* *eval* *protocol*.
- **DTU (Jensen et al. CVPR 2014):** 16 *validation* *scenes* with *4 views* *per* *scene* (the *de facto* 2014-2024 *object-centric* *MVS* *benchmark*, *used* *only* *for* *cross-dataset* *zero-shot* *evaluation* — *train* *on* *RE10K*, *test* *on* *DTU* *without* *fine-tuning*, the *killer* *H5* *mechanism* *evaluation*).

### Three key architectural choices (Sec. 4.3, *the ablation story*)

- **Cost volume (Tab. 3 row "w/o cost volume"):** *-3.29 dB PSNR* (22.83 vs 26.12), the *single-largest* *single-component* *ablation* in the paper, the *direct* *evidence* that the *cost-volume* *is* *the* *indispensable* *geometric-prior* *mechanism* (the *de facto* *H4* *mechanism* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1). *Visualization* (Fig. 6): w/o cost volume → the *rendered* *image* is a *direct* *overlay* of the *two* *input* *views* (the *killer* *visual* *evidence* that *without* *cost volume* the *3DGS* *parameters* *fail* to *align* *within* *the* *same* *3D* *space*).
- **Cross-view attention (Tab. 3 row "w/o cross-view attention"):** *-0.93 dB PSNR* (25.19 vs 26.12), the *over-fitting* *ablation* (Fig. A of paper: w/o cross-view attention *over-fits* *after* *certain* *training* *iterations*, the *killer* *evidence* that *cross-view* *attention* is *critical* for *model* *robustness* — *without* it, the *model* *memorizes* *RE10K* *train* *scenes* *and* *fails* *to* *generalize*). We *report* *the* *best* *performance* of this variant (not the *final* *over-fitted* *one*), so the *true* *ablation* *is* *larger* than *-0.93 dB* in *practice*.
- **U-Net cost volume refinement (Tab. 3 row "w/o U-Net"):** *-0.67 dB PSNR* (25.45 vs 26.12), the *detail-refinement* *ablation* (Fig. 6: w/o U-Net, the *middle* *regions* (where *content* is *present* in *both* *input* *views*) render *as* *well* as the *base* *model*, but the *left* and *right* *parts* (where *content* is *only* *present* in *one* *of* *the* *inputs*) show *obvious* *degraded* *quality* — the *U-Net* *refinement* is *important* for *mapping* *high-frequency* *details* from *input* *views* to the *Gaussian* *representation*).

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (PARTIAL support — 1-stage with cost-volume priors):** MVSplat is *1-stage* *feed-forward* (predict *3DGS* *parameters* *directly* in a *single* *forward* *pass*, no *per-scene* *optimization*), and *beats* *every* *2-stage* *baseline* (MuRF is *2-stage* *feed-forward* *NeRF*, LGM 154 is *2-stage* *multi-view-diffusion* *+* *3DGS*, GRM 155 is *2-stage* *multi-view-diffusion* *+* *3DGS*). The *direct* *evidence* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 is that *1-stage* *feed-forward* *with* *cost-volume* *priors* is *strictly better* than *2-stage* *diffusion* *+* *3DGS* for *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction* with *fast-inference* *requirement*. The *caveat* is that MVSplat uses an *explicit* *geometric* *prior* (cost-volume, *plane-sweep* *stereo*, *the* *classic* *MVS* *technique*), not *pure* *direct* *regression* — so *H1* *is* *PARTIAL* *support* (1-stage with *geometric* *priors*, not *pure* *direct*). The *practical* *lesson* for v0 v1 v2 is that *cost-volume* *priors* + *1-stage* *feed-forward* is *the* *right* *design* *for* *clinical* *intra-oral* *scan* *3D-reconstruction*.

**H2 (STRONG PARTIAL support — cost-volume as the H2 mechanism for sparse-view):** MVSplat uses *cost-volume* *feature* *matching* as the *core* *geometric* *prior* (a *different* *H2* *mechanism* from *latent-diffusion* or *3D-VAE*, the *direct* *evidence* that *explicit* *geometric* *priors* (cost-volume) *can* *serve* as the *H2* *mechanism* for *3D-reconstruction*). The *practical* *lesson* for v0 v1 v2 is that *cost-volume* is *the* *right* *H2* *mechanism* for *sparse-view* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* (the *cost-volume* *captures* *relative* *feature* *similarity* that *generalizes* *across* *cross-dataset* *domains*, the *killer* H5 *mechanism* for v0 v1 v2). The *practical* *recipe* is to *adopt* the *cost-volume* *design* *as* *the* *core* *H2* *mechanism* in v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 (the *direct* *template* for *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*).

**H3 (STRONG support — multi-view cross-attention is the H3 mechanism):** MVSplat's *multi-view* *Swin* *Transformer* with *self+cross-attention* *exchanges* *information* *between* *input* *views* (the *killer* *H3* *mechanism* for *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*). ## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **The cost-volume design is architecture-agnostic** (Tab. A: porting the *same* *cost-volume* to *pixelSplat* *boosts* *its* *PSNR* by *+0.74 dB*, the *direct* *evidence* that the *cost-volume* is *the* *killer* *indispensable* *component*, *not* the *specific* *transformer* *architecture*). The *practical* *insight* is that the *cost-volume* *design* *can* *be* *dropped* *into* *any* *feed-forward* *3DGS* *architecture* (pixelSplat, MVSplat, GRM 155, LGM 154) for *immediate* *PSNR* *gains*, the *killer* *practical* *recipe* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 *cross-baseline* *adoption*.

2. **Random init needs only 50% more iterations to match UniMatch init** (Tab. D: random init 26.01 PSNR at 300K vs UniMatch 26.39 PSNR at 300K → random 26.29 PSNR at 450K = UniMatch, the *direct* *evidence* that the *cost-volume* *design* is *the* *indispensable* *component*, *not* the *pre-training*). The *practical* *insight* is that *MVSplat* *can* *be* *trained* *from* *scratch* *without* *pre-training* *if* *the* *compute* *budget* *allows* (the *killer* *practical* *recipe* for v0 v1 v2 *clinical* *fine-tuning* *without* *pre-trained* *backbones*).

3. **The cross-view attention is a regularizer, not just an accuracy booster** (Tab. 3: w/o cross-view attention *over-fits* *after* *certain* *training* *iterations*, the *killer* *evidence* that *cross-view* *attention* is *critical* for *model* *robustness*, the *direct* *H3* *mechanism* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1). The *practical* *insight* is that *cross-view* *attention* *should* *be* *the* *default* *H3* *mechanism* in *any* *multi-view* *3D-reconstruction* *architecture*, *not* *just* *MVSplat* (the *killer* *transferable* *design* *pattern*).

4. **1 Gaussian per pixel is the right quality-speed trade-off** (Tab. C: 1 Gaussian 26.39 PSNR / 0.0015s vs 3 Gaussians 26.54 PSNR / 0.0023s, *+0.15 dB* for *2×* *slower*). The *practical* *insight* is that *MVSplat's* *1-Gaussian* *design* *is* *the* *right* *default* for *clinical* *real-time* *inference* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 *single-A100* *deployment*).

5. **MVSplat predicts 3D Gaussians with much higher quality than pixelSplat, *without* extra fine-tuning** (Sec. 4.2 "Assessing geometry reconstruction", Fig. 4: pixelSplat *contains* *obvious* *floating* *artifacts* and *needs* *50K* *extra* *fine-tuning* *steps* with *depth* *regularization* for *reasonable* *geometry*; MVSplat *generates* *high-quality* *geometries* *by* *training* *solely* *with* *photometric* *supervision*). The *practical* *insight* is that the *cost-volume* *design* *is* *the* *killer* *mechanism* for *clean* *3DGS* *geometry* *without* *post-processing* (the *right* v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 *design* for *direct* *3D-print* *use* *case*).

6. **Swin Transformer beats Epipolar Transformer with 30% speedup** (Tab. B: Swin 0.038s vs Epipolar 0.055s, *equivalent* *quality*). The *practical* *insight* is that *Swin* *windowed-attention* *beats* *epipolar-sampling* for *cross-view* *attention* (avoids the *expensive* *epipolar-line* *sampling* *operation*, the *killer* *engineering* *choice* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1).

7. **The 2D U-Net refinement is essential for "content present in only one of the input views"** (Sec. 4.3 "Ablations on the cost volume refinement U-Net", Fig. 6: w/o U-Net, the *middle* *regions* (content *present* in *both* *input* *views*) render *as* *well* as the *base* *model*, but the *left* and *right* *parts* (content *present* *only* in *one* *of* *the* *inputs*) show *obvious* *degraded* *quality*). The *practical* *insight* is that the *U-Net* *refinement* is *important* for *mapping* *high-frequency* *details* from *input* *views* to the *Gaussian* *representation*, the *direct* *H3* *mechanism* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 *dental* *arch* *reconstruction* (where *content* is *often* *only* *in* *one* *of* *the* *intra-oral* *views*).

8. **The "Unifying Flow, Stereo and Depth Estimation" (UniMatch, Xu 2023) is the killer pre-trained backbone** (Sec. 4.1 "Implementation details": "we initialize our backbone... with the publicly available UniMatch pretrained weight"). The *practical* *insight* is that *UniMatch* is a *general* *optical-flow* + *stereo* + *depth* *estimation* *framework* (the *killer* *pre-trained* *backbone* *for* *cross-view* *3D-reconstruction*). The *practical* *insight* for v0 v1 v2 is to *use* *UniMatch* as the *pre-trained* *backbone* for *dental* *intra-oral* *cross-view* *feature* *extraction* (the *killer* *practical* *recipe* for v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1).
