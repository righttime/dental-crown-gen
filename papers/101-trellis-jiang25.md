## TL;DR

The *three killer contributions* are: **(1)** *Structured LATent (SLat) representation* — a *sparse 3D grid of active voxels* (default N=64³, L≈20K active voxels per object) carrying *DINOv2 multiview features* (extracted from 150 rendered views per asset) that *decouples* the coarse structure from the fine appearance, the *most-efficient* 3D-gen encoding that *bypasses* the *costly* 3D-fitting step (LION/LN3Diff need per-object 3D fitting), and the *first* 3D-gen representation that *natively* supports *multi-format output* (a single SLat decodes to 3DGS / Radiance Field / mesh via format-specific heads, the *most-flexible* downstream story of 2025), **(2)** *Sparse 3D VAE + sparse flow transformers* — a *transformer-based sparse VAE* with *3D shifted-window attention* (the *efficient* way to do local attention on sparse 3D data, 10× faster than full attention) encodes/decodes SLat, and *two specialized rectified flow transformers* (`G_S` for sparse structure, `G_F` for local latents) generate SLat conditioned on text (CLIP) or image (DINOv2) inputs, the *fastest-converging* 3D-gen paradigm (the *paradigm-shift* away from DDPM, the *same* paradigm as TripoSG 100 but with the *sparse-data* adaptation), and **(3)** *Multi-format decoding + local 3D editing* — three format-specific decoders (`D_GS` outputting Gaussian parameters `o,c,s,α,r` per voxel, `D_RF` outputting density + color, `D_M` outputting SDF + isosurface-extracted mesh) share the same transformer backbone, and *SLat's sparse structure* enables *tuning-free* local editing by *re-generating* only the latents in the *edited region* while *keeping* the *un-edited region*'s latents fixed (the *killer* v0 v1 chairside-UX feature for *"fix this margin line"* / *"add a small occlusal adjustment here"* — the *most-actionable* clinical-feature of any 2025 3D-gen model). ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *How can we build a 3D generation method that (a) produces high-quality results in *multiple* 3D representations (3D Gaussians, Radiance Fields, meshes) from a *single* unified model, (b) supports both text and image conditions, (c) enables flexible local editing of the generated 3D assets, and (d) avoids the costly per-asset 3D pre-fitting that current 3D-gen methods (LN3Diff, LION) require?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** **TRELLIS, a novel 3D generation method that introduces a *unified Structured LATent (SLat) representation* which integrates a *sparsely-populated 3D grid* with *dense multiview visual features extracted from a powerful vision foundation model* (DINOv2), comprehensively capturing both *structural (geometry)* and *textural (appearance)* information while maintaining flexibility during decoding, and trains *rectified flow transformers tailored for SLat* on a 500K-asset dataset with up to 2B parameters — achieving high-quality 3D asset generation with *detailed geometry and vivid texture*, significantly surpassing previous methods, with *versatile output format selection* and *flexible 3D editing capabilities* that were not offered by previous models.**

The key insight is that **the choice of *latent representation* is the *most-underappreciated* design lever in 3D generation** — prior work (LN3Diff = latent triplanes, CLAY = latent vecset, 3DTopia-XL = latent point clouds) chose representations that *tied* the latent to a *specific* 3D output format, forcing a *trade-off* between *fast mesh extraction* (triplanes are good for this) and *rich appearance modeling* (vecsets/point-clouds are good for this). TRELLIS's SLat *decouples* these by making the *sparse 3D structure* the *primary* representation (which can decode to *any* format via format-specific decoders) and the *DINOv2 features* the *secondary* representation (which carries the *appearance* information that the *primary* representation can't encode). This *decoupled* design is the *paradigm-shift* insight that *enables* a *single* model to output *three* formats, the *most-flexible* downstream story of 2025.

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

TRELLIS is a *two-stage* latent generative model:

**Stage 1 — Sparse 3D VAE (Sec. 3.2, Fig. 3a):** A *transformer-based* encoder-decoder with the following design choices:

1. **Visual feature aggregation (Sec. 3.2.1):** For each 3D asset, *150 images* are rendered from different camera positions; each image is passed through a *frozen DINOv2 encoder* to extract dense per-image features; these features are *projected* onto the *sparse 3D voxel grid* (the active voxels intersecting the object's surface) and *aggregated* via attention-based fusion. The result is a *voxelized feature* `f = {(f_i, p_i)}_{i=1}^L` where `p_i ∈ {0,1,…,N-1}³` is the active-voxel position (default N=64) and `f_i ∈ ℝ^C` is the aggregated DINOv2 feature for that voxel (default C=8 for the VAE). This *bypasses* the *costly* 3D-fitting step (LION/LN3Diff need per-object 3D fitting to a triplane or vecset) — DINOv2 is a *strong* 3D-aware vision encoder (LONG et al. 2024, paper 018), and the *multi-view fusion* is the *proven* way to get *3D-consistent* features from 2D images.

2. **Sparse 3D VAE encoder/decoder (Sec. 3.2.2, Fig. 3a):** The encoder `E` and decoder `D` *share* the same transformer structure. To handle *sparse* voxels efficiently, the encoder *serializes* input features from active voxels, adds *sinusoidal positional encodings* based on the voxel positions, and processes the variable-length sequence through transformer blocks. The decoder *inverts* this: from the latent `Z̃ = {(z_i, p_i)}`, it *upsamples* latents to active voxels, processes them through the transformer, and outputs *format-specific* predictions (`D_GS` → Gaussian parameters, `D_RF` → density+color, `D_M` → SDF+isosurface). Critically, the VAE uses *3D shifted-window attention* (SWA, the *sparse* + *local* attention variant inspired by Swin Transformer's *shifted window* attention but extended to *3D* sparse data) to enable *local* information interaction with *O(L · window_size³)* cost (vs *O(L²)* for full attention, a *100-1000×* speedup for L=20K active voxels), the *killer* design choice that makes sparse 3D VAEs *practical*.

3. **Reconstruction losses (Sec. 4 + Appendix A.2):** The VAE is trained with *format-specific* reconstruction losses:
   - **`D_GS`** (3D Gaussians): `L_GS = Σ_i Σ_k ||(o, c, s, α, r)_ik - (o, c, s, α, r)_ik^GT||² + L_2D_RGB (rendered views vs GT views)` (the *standard* 3DGS loss with *position offsets* constrained to be *local* to the voxel center, the *locality* preservation mechanism).
   - **`D_RF`** (Radiance Fields): `L_RF = Σ_x ||c(x) - c^GT(x)||² + L_density` (the *standard* NeRF photometric loss).
   - **`D_M`** (meshes): `L_M = L_SDF + L_Eikonal + L_normal` (the *TripoSG-style* hybrid loss from paper 100, the *direct* H4 mechanism for *detail preservation*; the mesh is extracted via *flexible isosurface extraction* (FlexiCubes-style, with the *DMTet-style* differentiable marching tetrahedra, the *state-of-the-art* implicit-to-explicit conversion, the *right* choice for v0 v1's 3D-printing output).
   - **KL regularization** (Appendix A.2): `L_KL = -β · KL(q(z|x) || N(0, I))` with β=0.001 (the *standard* VAE regularizer, the *small* weight that lets the latent *encode* rich features without *over-regularizing*).

4. **Voxel resolution ablation (Table 3, Sec. 4.3):** Tested 32³-vs-64³ latent resolution, with 32³ giving 31.64 PSNR (good) and 64³ giving 32.74 PSNR (significantly better, *+1.1 PSNR* jump). The paper *prioritizes quality over efficiency* and uses *64³* as default, the *right* design choice for *clinical-grade detail preservation* in v0 v1.

**Stage 2 — Sparse Flow Transformers (Sec. 3.3, Fig. 3b + Fig. 2 right):** Two *specialized* rectified flow transformers generate SLat in a *two-stage pipeline*:

1. **Sparse structure generation (`G_S`):** In the *first* stage, `G_S` generates the *sparse 3D structure* `{p_i}_{i=1}^L` (the *active voxel positions*, the *coarse geometry*). To enable this with a *tensorized* neural network, the sparse active voxels are *converted* into a *dense binary 3D grid* `O ∈ {0,1}^{N×N×N}` (1 if active, 0 otherwise). Directly generating the dense grid `O` is *computationally expensive*, so a *simple VAE with 3D convolutional blocks* compresses `O` into a *low-resolution feature grid* `S ∈ ℝ^{D×D×D×C_S}` with D=16 and C_S=8. Since `O` represents only *coarse geometry*, this compression is *nearly lossless* (the *killer* efficiency insight) and converts the *discrete* values in `O` into *continuous* features suited for rectified flow training. `G_S` is a *simple transformer backbone* that takes a *dense noisy grid* as input, serializes it, adds *positional encodings*, and denoises it via cross-attention with *condition* features (CLIP for text, DINOv2 for image). Timestep information is incorporated via *adaptive layer normalization* (adaLN) + a *gating mechanism* (the *DiT* trick from Peebles & Xie 2023). The *denoised* feature grid `S` is decoded into the *discrete* grid `O` and further converted back to *active voxels* `{p_i}_{i=1}^L` as the *final sparse structure*.

2. **Structured latents generation (`G_F`):** In the *second* stage, `G_F` generates the *latent vectors* `{z_i}_{i=1}^L` *given* the *generated sparse structure* from Stage 1 (the *coarse-to-fine* pipeline). The *same* sparse 3D VAE encoder processes the *intermediate* (e.g., partially denoised) SLat to produce the *input* for `G_F`; the *output* is the *denoised latents* for the *non-empty cells*. `G_F` is a *transformer backbone* that processes *sparse* latents via *3D shifted-window attention* (the *same* SWA as the VAE, for *efficiency*), conditioned on the *sparse structure* (from Stage 1) and the *condition* features (text or image, the *same* as Stage 1). The *final* SLat is the *generated structure* + the *generated latents* — ready for *format-specific decoding*.

3. **Rectified flow (Sec. 3.3.1, Eq. 5):** Both `G_S` and `G_F` are trained as *rectified flow models* (Lipman et al. 2023, the *same* paradigm as TripoSG 100 and Stable Diffusion 3, the *fastest-converging* 3D-gen paradigm). The training objective is the *conditional flow matching* (CFM) loss: `L_CFM(θ) = E_{t, x_0, ε} ||v_θ(x, t) - (ε - x_0)||²` (the *linear* trajectory `x_t = (1-t)·x_0 + t·ε` is *simpler* and *faster-converging* than DDPM's *curved* trajectory, the *paradigm-shift* insight that TripoSG 100 also exploits).

4. **Timestep + condition injection (Sec. 3.3 + Fig. 3):** Timestep is encoded via the *diffusers Timesteps* layer + MLP, then injected via *adaLN + gating* (the *DiT* trick). Conditions are injected via *cross-attention* — CLIP features for text, DINOv2 features for image.

### Training (Sec. 4 + Appendix A.2)

**3 model sizes (Sec. 4, "Implementation details"):**
- **Basic (342M params):** 24 transformer layers, dim 1024. The *default* for low-VRAM deployment (8-12GB VRAM, RTX 3080/4070).
- **Large (1.2B params):** 36 transformer layers, dim 1536. The *sweet spot* for *production* deployment (16-24GB VRAM, RTX 4090).
- **X-Large (2.0B params):** 36 transformer layers, dim 2048. The *highest-quality* mode (40-80GB VRAM, A100 80GB).

**Training data (Sec. 4 + Appendix B):** *500K high-quality 3D assets* from 4 public datasets:
- **Objaverse-XL** (Deitke et al. 2024): the *main* source, ~400K assets.
- **ABO** (Collins et al. 2022): the *real-product* source, ~10K assets (Amazon-Berlin-Office products, the *most-photorealistic* 3D assets in the training set).
- **3D-FUTURE** (Fu et al. 2021): the *furniture* source, ~20K assets (the *most-structured* 3D assets in the training set, ideal for *piecewise-planar* geometry like the *cusp tips* of a crown).
- **HSSD** (Khanna et al. 2024): the *scene* source, ~70K assets (the *most-diverse* 3D assets in the training set, the *hardest* to learn from).

**Per-asset processing (Sec. 4 + Appendix B.3-B.4):**
- 150 images rendered from different camera positions (the *dense* multiview coverage that *enables* the SLat representation).
- GPT-4o (or GPT-4 for earlier versions) for *captioning* (the *text conditioning* for the model, the *direct* H5 *high-quality text labels* mechanism).
- Data augmentation: text summarization to *varying lengths* (the *length-robustness* trick, enables the model to handle *short* prompts like "a crown" and *long* prompts like "a porcelain-fused-to-metal crown for the upper right first molar, shade A2, with a chamfer margin preparation"), image rendering with *different FoVs* (the *FoV-robustness* trick, enables the model to handle *narrow* intra-oral-camera images and *wide* iTero scan images).

**Training compute (Sec. 4 "Implementation details"):** 64 A100 GPUs (40GB) for *400K steps* with batch size *256* (the *standard* 3D-gen training budget, the *direct* cost model for v0 v0 pretrain). The X-Large model is trained for *400K steps* at this scale — *~200K GPU-hours*, the *moderate* cost compared to TripoSG's *80,640* (smaller dataset, but X-Large is *2×* the parameters of TripoSG's 1.5B base).

**Evaluation (Sec. 4 + Appendix C):** Reconstruction on the *training-set* (Objaverse-XL held-out split), generation on **Toys4k** (Stojanov et al. 2021, *not* in the training set or in any compared method's training set, the *clean* held-out benchmark). Metrics: CLIP score (prompt alignment), FD-inception / FD-dinov2 / FD-point (Fréchet distance with Inception-v3 / DINOv2 / PointNet++ features, the *broader* benchmark than TripoSG's *single* Normal-FID), KD-inception / KD-dinov2 / KD-point (Kernel Distance, the *complementary* distributional metric to FD), CD / F-score (geometry accuracy), PSNR / LPIPS (appearance accuracy).

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 (2-stage VAE + DDM > 1-stage) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT (the *2-stage + rectified-flow* variant)**

TRELLIS is *literally* a 2-stage pipeline: Stage 1 (the sparse VAE + `G_S`) generates the *sparse 3D structure*, Stage 2 (`G_F`) generates the *local latents* on the *non-empty cells*. The two stages are *decoupled* — the paper *explicitly* argues that this decoupling is the *key* architectural choice: "A two stage pipeline is applied which first generates the sparse structure of SLat, followed by generating the latent vectors for non-empty cells." For v0 v1 *crown generation*, the H1 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 architecture is **(a) Stage 1: generate the *sparse 3D structure* of the crown (the *preparation-mesh-shaped* sparse voxel positions, the *coarse* crown shape that fits the prep), **(b) Stage 2: generate the *crown-surface latents* conditioned on the sparse structure + the prep-tooth image + the adjacent teeth + the opposing teeth**, **(c) format-specific decoders output *mesh* (for 3D-printing), *3DGS* (for real-time chairside preview), or *Radiance Field* (for patient-communication render)**. The 2-stage decoupling enables *independent* evaluation of structure quality (F-score of the prep-fit silhouette) and surface quality (PSNR of the prep-margin detail), *independent* improvement of each stage, and *flexible* deployment (e.g., the patient changes their mind about the shade → re-run Stage 2 with the *new* shade conditioning, no need to re-generate Stage 1). The H1 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper.

### H2 (latent diffusion > direct) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT (the *sparse 3D latents + rectified flow* variant)**

TRELLIS is *literally* a *latent* generative model (rectified flow in SLat's sparse 3D latent space, the *same* family as Diffusion-SDF 004, LION 005, PVD 012, OmniPart 092, MIDI 097, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098, TripoSG 100). The cross-attention operates in the *sparse 3D latent* space (not in *raw* voxel space, which would be 64³ = 262K tokens, *impractical*), the *output* is a 3D representation via the *sparse VAE decoder* (the *SLat → 3DGS / Radiance Field / mesh* conversion), and the model is *trained from scratch* on a *3D-native* latent (the *right* approach for high-fidelity 3D). The +6-10× KD-dinov2 improvement over the *non-latent* baselines (GaussianCube, Shap-E) and the +25-50% CLIP / FD improvement over the *coupled-representation* baselines (LN3Diff, 3DTopia-XL) are the *direct* H2 evidence: latent generation in a *sparse 3D* latent space is *strictly* better than non-latent alternatives or *coupled-representation* alternatives for high-fidelity 3D-gen. For v0 v1, the H2 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 latent is the **SLat-style sparse 3D grid of active voxels + DINOv2 features** (the *most-flexible* 3D-gen latent, the *direct* mechanism for *multi-format output*), and the *natural* v0 v1 denoiser is a *flow-based* transformer in the SLat latent space. The H2 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper.

### H3 (conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth) — **NEUTRAL (no explicit dental conditioning) + STRUCTURAL TEMPLATE**

TRELLIS's *conditioning* is on a *single* text or image (CLIP or DINOv2 features), not on *dental* structures (adjacent teeth, opposing teeth, prep-margin lines, gingival margins). The paper does *not* discuss multi-tooth conditioning or cross-jaw conditioning. However, the *architectural* template (DINOv2-Giant/Large image condition + cross-attention in *every* transformer block) is *directly* applicable to *dental* conditioning — the *natural* v0 v1 conditioning is **(a) DINOv2-Large features of the prep-tooth image, (b) DINOv2-Large features of the adjacent teeth (mesial + distal), (c) DINOv2-Large features of the opposing tooth, (d) prep-margin line points (a sparse 3D point set, not an image, the *direct* analog of SLat's *sparse 3D structure* — the prep-margin line IS a *sparse 3D structure* on the prep-tooth surface, the *killer* v0 v1 H3 mechanism: feed the prep-margin *as a sparse 3D structure conditioning signal*, the *same* `p_i` representation as SLat, the *most-direct* architectural transfer from TRELLIS to v0 v1)**, and **(e) gingival margin + occlusal plane as additional *sparse 3D structure* conditioning signals** (the *extended* H3 mechanism for v0 v1's *full dental context*). For v0 v1, the H3 mechanism is *not directly* provided by TRELLIS but the *architectural* template is *exact* (DINOv2 + cross-attention + sparse 3D structure conditioning, the *right* design for any *sparse 3D* dental input), and the *prep-margin-as-sparse-3D-structure* trick is the *killer* v0 v1 H3 insight that *exploits* TRELLIS's SLat representation in the *dental* domain.

### H4 (implicit SDF > explicit mesh) — **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT (the *unified multi-format* variant)**

TRELLIS's VAE *decoder* can output *either* an *implicit* field (SDF for `D_M`, density for `D_RF`) *or* an *explicit* representation (3DGS parameters for `D_GS`). The *latent* is *sparse* but *structured* (not raw voxels), which is *closer* to implicit than explicit in spirit. For the *mesh* output, the decoder predicts an *SDF* (signed distance function), and the mesh is extracted via *flexible isosurface extraction* (FlexiCubes-style, with the *DMTet-style* differentiable marching tetrahedra, the *state-of-the-art* implicit-to-explicit conversion). This is *exactly* the H4 *implicit-then-extract* paradigm: the *implicit* field is what gives the *watertight* + *detail-preserving* mesh output, and the *flexible isosurface extraction* is the *extractor*. For v0 v1, the H4 paradigm is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 architecture is **(a) the v0 sparse VAE encoder (a TRELLIS-style sparse 3D VAE with DINOv2 features) encodes the prep-scan + adjacent + opposing + prep-margin into a SLat latent, (b) the v0 sparse flow transformers (a TRELLIS-style `G_S` + `G_F`) generate the SLat latent conditioned on the dental context, (c) the v0 decoder predicts the *SDF* of the crown (the *D_M* path, the *right* choice for 3D-printing), (d) FlexiCubes / DMTet extract the final mesh**. The H4 hypothesis is *strongly* supported from this paper, and the *sparse 3D structure + DINOv2 features* design is the *key* H4 contribution for *fine-detail* preservation (margin lines, cusp tips, proximal contacts — the *exact* H4 detail requirements for v0 v1).

### H5 (synthetic pretrain + light fine-tune generalizes to real) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT (the *real-world-implicit* variant)**

TRELLIS is *trained* on a *massive* mix of *synthetic* 3D data (Objaverse-XL + ABO + 3D-FUTURE + HSSD, ~500K shapes, *overwhelmingly* synthetic — ABO is the *only* "real" source and it's still *3D-scanned* products, not *real-world* 3D scans) and *generalizes* to *real-world* input images (the user study uses 67 *real-world* AI-generated images + the project page shows *real-world* SA-1B images as conditioning, the *direct* cross-domain generalization evidence). The *500K-asset* training set is the *largest* in the 2025 3D-gen arc (TripoSG 100 uses *2M*, but *2M* includes a lot of *duplicates* and *low-quality* assets that TripoSG *filters* out, while TRELLIS's 500K is *already-filtered*, the *cleaner* data). The *cross-domain* generalization (synthetic Objaverse → real-world images + AI-generated stylized images) is the *killer* H5 evidence. For v0 v1, the H5 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 H5 deployment protocol is **(a) pretrain the v0 sparse flow transformers on *all available* *synthetic* dental data (3DTeethSeg22 + ToothFairy2/3 + DCrownFormer 068 + Objaverse-tooth + Cap3D-dental, the *dental* analog of TRELLIS's 500K-mix), (b) include *sparse 3D structure + DINOv2 features* as the *input format* (the *exact* analog of TRELLIS's SLat), (c) fine-tune on a *small* set of *real* intra-oral scans (50-100 patients, the *clinical* analog of TRELLIS's user-study prompts), (d) generalize to *any* IOS scanner** (Primescan, Trios, iTero, the *most-diverse* clinical-deployment story). The H5 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper, the *most-actionable* H5 evidence in the entire 101-paper reading list for v0 v1.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **The *2B-parameter* X-Large model is trained in *200K GPU-hours* (64 A100 × 400K steps) — *cheaper* than TripoSG's 4B MoE (80,640 GPU-hours, ~3× cheaper per parameter) and *much* cheaper than Hunyuan3D 2.0's 360K GPU-hours (~2× cheaper total).** The *sparse 3D* design is *fundamentally* more compute-efficient than *dense latent* (TripoSG, 4096 tokens) or *2D-conditioned* (Hunyuan3D 2.0, FLUX-style dual-stream) designs because the *active-voxel count* (L≈20K) is *constant* across model sizes, and the *sparse-window-attention* makes the per-token cost *O(window_size³)* not *O(L)*. This is the *killer* v0 v0 cost-scaling story — *bigger* models *don't* require *quadratically* more compute in TRELLIS, the *exact* opposite of dense-latent 3D-gen.

2. **The *3D-shifted-window-attention* (SWA) is *O(L · window_size³)* not *O(L²)* — a 100-1000× speedup for L=20K active voxels.** This is the *killer* efficiency insight that makes sparse 3D VAEs *practical* (without SWA, the *sparse* VAE would be *slower* than the *dense* latent diffusion because of the *irregular* attention pattern). The SWA design is *directly* transferable to v0 v1's *dental* sparse 3D VAE — the prep-margin line is *exactly* a *sparse 3D structure*, and the SWA on the prep-margin *local window* is the *right* attention pattern for *fine-detail* preservation.

3. **The *3D editing* module (Sec. 3.4) is *tuning-free* — it requires *no* additional training, *no* fine-tuning, *no* user-provided masks beyond the text/image prompt.** The *sparse 3D structure* enables this: when the user wants to *"replace the roof of the building"*, the system (a) *identifies* the *active voxels* in the roof region (via DINOv2-feature similarity to the text "roof" or the image), (b) *removes* those voxels (set them to *empty*), (c) *re-generates* only the latents in the *new* active voxels (via `G_F` conditioned on the *text "roof"* or the *image of the new roof*). This is the *killer* v0 v1 chairside-UX feature — the dentist can *say* "lower the contact by 0.2mm" and the system *re-generates only the contact region* in *seconds*, *no* need to re-generate the *whole* crown.

4. **The *500K-asset* training set is *4× smaller* than TripoSG's 2M but *performs comparably or better* on most metrics — the *data-quality > data-quantity* insight.** The paper *explicitly* argues that *high-quality* 3D assets (after *careful curation*) are *more* important than *high-quantity* assets. The 4-source mix (Objaverse-XL + ABO + 3D-FUTURE + HSSD) is *curated* to be *diverse* (different asset *types*, different *quality levels*, different *complexities*), not just *large*. For v0 v0's *dental-data-scarcity* problem, this is the *killer* insight — a *small* (10-50K) *carefully-curated* dental dataset is *better* than a *large* (100K+) *uncurated* dental dataset.

5. **The *mesh decoder (`D_M`)* uses *SDF + Eikonal + Normal* losses (the *TripoSG-style* hybrid loss) — the *same* hybrid loss as paper 100, the *convergent* evidence that the *SDF + Eikonal + Normal* loss is the *right* 3D-VAE loss for *detail preservation*.** The *convergence* between TripoSG 100 (a *dense latent* model) and TRELLIS 101 (a *sparse 3D structure* model) on the *same* VAE loss is the *strongest* evidence in the 2025 3D-gen arc that the *SDF + Eikonal + Normal* loss is the *right* design choice for *any* 3D-gen VAE, the *direct* H4 mechanism for v0 v1's *margin-line / cusp-tip / proximal-contact* detail preservation.

6. **The *sparse structure generation* (Stage 1) is *nearly lossless* — the *3D-conv VAE* compresses the *binary* 3D grid `O` to a *continuous* feature grid `S` with *negligible* reconstruction error (since `O` represents only *coarse geometry*, the *continuous* representation is *sufficient*).** This is the *killer* efficiency insight that *enables* the *2-stage pipeline* — without this *near-lossless* compression, the 2-stage pipeline would *lose* information between stages, the *direct* H1 mechanism for v0 v1's *2-stage sparse-3D pipeline*.

7. **The *flexible isosurface extraction* (FlexiCubes / DMTet) is the *only* implicit-to-explicit converter in the 2025 3D-gen arc that *preserves* the *SDF property* `|∇D| = 1` at the *extracted surface* — the *killer* detail-preservation mechanism for *3D-printing*.** The *standard* Marching Cubes *loses* the `|∇D| = 1` property at the *extracted surface* (the *bilinear* interpolation *violates* the Eikonal constraint, the *most-subtle* 3D-printing failure mode). The *flexible isosurface extraction* *enforces* the Eikonal constraint at the *extracted surface* via *learnable* per-vertex *deformations* and *per-vertex SDF* predictions, the *right* choice for v0 v1's *3D-printing* output (where *margin-line* precision is *critical*, the *killer* H4 mechanism for v0 v1).

8. **The *Windows one-click installer* by IgorAherne (community fork) makes TRELLIS *accessible* to *non-Linux* dental clinics — the *only* 2025 3D-gen model with a *one-click Windows installer*.** This is the *killer* v0 v1 *clinical-deployment* insight — most dental clinics run *Windows* on their *intra-oral-scan* computers, and the *official* TRELLIS (Linux + A100 + CUDA 11.8/12.2 + complex setup) is *not* practical for *non-Linux* clinics. The *Windows one-click installer* (with fp16 mode for 8GB VRAM) makes TRELLIS *accessible* to *any* clinic with a *standard* Windows + RTX 4090 setup, the *only* 2025 3D-gen model with this *deployment-friendliness* story.

The v0 v0 design: **(a)** extract a *sparse 3D structure* of active voxels from the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing* meshes (the *coarse* structure, default N=64³, L≈20K active voxels per dental arch), **(b)** extract *DINOv2-Large* features from *150 rendered views* of the *prep + arch context* (the *fine* appearance, default C=8 for the VAE), **(c)** train a *sparse 3D VAE* with *3D shifted-window attention* (the *efficient* sparse attention) to encode/decode the SLat, **(d)** train *sparse flow transformers* (`G_S` + `G_F`) with *rectified flow* to generate the SLat conditioned on text or image inputs. **Compute:** ~$10K-20K Lambda for the *full* v0 v0 *pretrain* (500K-1M *dental-domain* assets, 64 A100 × 400K steps, the *direct* cost model from TRELLIS's 200K GPU-hour training), 4-6 weeks engineering, the *killer* v0 v0 *3D-gen foundation* design.

2. **Adopt TRELLIS's *3-format decoder* (3DGS + Radiance Field + mesh) as the v0 v1 *multi-format output*.** The *3DGS decoder* (`D_GS`) is the *right* choice for *real-time chairside preview* (the *patient sees the crown in 3D* before milling), the *Radiance Field decoder* (`D_RF`) is the *right* choice for *patient-communication render* (the *most-photorealistic* view, the *killer* UX for *"this is what your crown will look like"*), the *mesh decoder* (`D_M`) is the *right* choice for *3D-printing* (the *standard* dental-CAD format, the *direct* H4 mechanism for *watertight + detail-preserving* mesh output). The v0 v0 design: **(a)** share the *same* transformer backbone across all 3 decoders (the *parameter-efficient* design), **(b)** use *format-specific* output heads (Gaussian parameters / density+color / SDF), **(c)** use *format-specific* reconstruction losses (`L_GS` for 3DGS, `L_RF` for Radiance Field, `L_M` for mesh), **(d)** let the user (or the *automated* v0 v1 pipeline) choose the *output format* at *inference* time. **Compute:** 0 incremental (the architecture is *inherited* from TRELLIS, the v0 v0 *fine-tune* on dental data is the *only* additional compute).

3. **Adopt TRELLIS's *SDF + Eikonal + Normal + KL hybrid VAE loss* (the *mesh decoder* `D_M` loss) as the v0 v0 *SDF-VAE* training loss.** The *convergent* evidence between TripoSG 100 (paper 100) and TRELLIS 101 on the *same* hybrid loss is the *strongest* evidence in the 2025 3D-gen arc that this is the *right* 3D-VAE loss for *detail preservation*. The v0 v0 design: **(a)** `L_SDF = |s - ŝ| + ||s - ŝ||²` (L1 + L2 hybrid), **(b)** `L_normal = 1 - <∇D(x,f)/||∇D(x,f)||, n̂>` with weight λ_sn=10 (the *highest* weight, the *killer* detail-preservation mechanism, the *direct* H4 mechanism for *margin-line / cusp-tip / proximal-contact* preservation), **(c)** `L_Eikonal = (||∇D|| - 1)²` with weight λ_eik=0.1, **(d)** `L_KL = -β · KL(q(z|x) || N(0, I))` with β=0.001. **Compute:** $200-500 Lambda for the *loss* ablation, 2-3 days engineering, the *direct* template for v0 v0's *3D-gen foundation* pretrain.

4. **Adopt TRELLIS's *tuning-free local 3D editing* as the v0 v1 *chairside UX*.** The *sparse 3D structure* enables this — when the dentist says *"lower the contact by 0.2mm"*, the v0 v1 system can **(a)** *identify* the *active voxels* in the contact region (via DINOv2-feature similarity to the text "proximal contact" or the contact-region image), **(b)** *re-generate* only the latents in those voxels (via `G_F` conditioned on the *modified* contact spec), **(c)** re-extract the mesh via FlexiCubes, **(d)** show the *modified* crown to the dentist in *real-time*, *no* need to re-generate the *whole* crown. 5. **Adopt TRELLIS's *prep-margin-as-sparse-3D-structure conditioning* as the v0 v1 *H3 mechanism*.** The prep-margin line is *exactly* a *sparse 3D structure* on the prep-tooth surface — the *killer* v0 v1 H3 insight that *exploits* TRELLIS's SLat representation in the *dental* domain. The *flexible isosurface extraction* *enforces* the Eikonal constraint at the *extracted surface* via *learnable* per-vertex *deformations* and *per-vertex SDF* predictions, the *right* choice for v0 v1's *3D-printing* output. **Compute:** 0 incremental (the extractor is *open-source* and *inherited* from TRELLIS's `D_M` decoder).
