## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Given sufficient 3D data and a large-scale training framework, is it possible to learn a generic 3D prior for reconstructing an object from a single image, the way that GPT learned a generic language prior from large text corpora? More specifically, can a single feed-forward transformer model (a) *handle arbitrary object categories* (not category-specific like prior 2017-2022 single-image-to-3D methods that train *one model per category*), (b) *train end-to-end* with *minimal 3D-aware regularization* (no Chamfer loss, no SDF loss, no 3D-supervision, just 2D image reconstruction from multiple views, the *simple* recipe that *avoids* the *delicate parameter tuning* of prior diffusion-based methods), (c) *infer fast* on a *single GPU* (the *practical* requirement for *real-world* 3D-gen deployment, vs the *per-shape optimization* of NeRF-based methods that take *minutes to hours* per shape), and (d) *produce high-fidelity 3D outputs* (the *quality* requirement that *matches or beats* diffusion-based 3D-gen at the *single-image-to-3D* task)?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** **We propose the first Large Reconstruction Model (LRM) that predicts the 3D model of an object from a single input image within just 5 seconds. In contrast to many previous methods that are trained on small-scale datasets such as ShapeNet in a category-specific fashion, LRM adopts a highly scalable transformer-based architecture with 500 million learnable parameters to directly predict a neural radiance field (NeRF) from the input image. We train our model in an end-to-end manner on massive multi-view data containing around 1 million objects, including both synthetic renderings from Objaverse and real captures from MVImgNet. This combination of a high-capacity model and large-scale training data empowers our model to be highly generalizable and produce high-quality 3D reconstructions from various testing inputs, including real-world in-the-wild captures and images created by generative models.**

The key insight is that **the *GPT formula* (large transformer + huge data + simple self-supervised loss) *transfers* to 3D-reconstruction** — the same *three factors* that *enabled* GPT's *natural-language* success *enable* LRM's *3D-reconstruction* success: (a) *highly-scalable transformer-based architecture* (the *500M-parameter* encoder-decoder, the *standard* 2023 *foundation-model* design), (b) *enormous 3D training data* (the *1M Objaverse + MVImgNet* corpus, the *1-epoch-Objaverse + 3-epoch-MVImgNet* training schedule for *balanced* synthetic/real exposure, the *massive* 2023 *foundation-model* data scale), and (c) *self-supervised-like simple training objective* (the *2D image reconstruction from multiple views* loss, *no* 3D-aware supervision, *no* SDF/Chamfer/mesh loss, the *simple* end-to-end recipe that *avoids* the *delicate parameter tuning* of prior diffusion-based methods). The *comparison* to the *alternative* 2023 3D-gen paradigms is *direct*:
- *Diffusion-based* (Point-E 2022, Shap-E 106, Magic3D 2023, DreamFusion 2023): *slow* inference (1-13s per shape for the *diffusion sampling* alone, vs LRM's *5s end-to-end* including mesh extraction), *limited by pre-trained 2D generative models* (the *practical* bottleneck that LRM *avoids* by *training end-to-end* from scratch), *delicate parameter tuning + regularization* (the *practical* difficulty that LRM *avoids* with *simple image reconstruction loss*).
- *Per-shape optimization* (NeRF, Instant-NGP, NeuS): *slow* (minutes to hours per shape, vs LRM's 5s), *impractical* for *real-world* deployment (the *practical* limitation that LRM *solves* with *feed-forward inference*).
- *Category-specific* (Pixel2Mesh, AtlasNet, Occupancy Networks): *limited* to *specific categories* (the *practical* limitation that LRM *solves* with *1M cross-category* training).
- *Prior-based* (GINA-3D, MCC, SparseFusion): *smaller* model + *smaller* data (the *practical* limitation that LRM *solves* with *500M params + 1M data*).

The *practical* LRM insight is that **the *GPT formula* works for 3D-reconstruction the way it works for NLP** — *scale* (500M params, 1M data) + *simple training* (image reconstruction from multiple views) + *scalable architecture* (transformer encoder-decoder) is *enough* to *learn a generic 3D prior*, the *killer* insight that *every* 2024-2025 *feed-forward 3D-gen* paper *inherits*.

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 1)

LRM is a *fully-transformer* encoder-decoder pipeline for *single-image-to-triplane-NeRF*:

**1. Image Encoder (Sec. 3.1):** *Pre-trained DINO ViT-B/16* takes a *512×512 RGB image* as input and produces *1025 feature tokens* (1024 patch-wise + 1 [CLS]) of *dim 768* (the *standard* DINO ViT-B/16 output). The *key design choice* is to use *all* 1025 tokens (not *just* the [CLS] token, the *practical* choice that *preserves* the *spatial structure* of the image features for *cross-attention* with the triplane tokens). The *DINO* choice (over CLIP / ImageNet-pretrained ResNet / DINOv2) is *critical*: DINO is *trained with self-distillation* and learns *interpretable attention over structure and texture*, the *structural* features that are *essential* for *3D reconstruction* (vs CLIP's *semantic* features that prioritize *high-level concepts* over *spatial structure*). The *DINO* choice is the *direct precursor* to v0 v0 v1 v2's *DINOv2 ViT-L* dental-IOS encoder, the *proven* 2021-2024 *self-supervised* vision transformer family for *structural* image understanding.

**2. Camera Features (Sec. 3.2, first subsection):** The *camera feature* c ∈ ℝ²⁰ is constructed by *flattening* the 4×4 camera extrinsic matrix E (the *camera-to-world* transformation) and *concatenating* the *focal length* (foc_x, foc_y) and *principal point* (pp_x, pp_y): c = [E_{1×16}, foc_x, foc_y, pp_x, pp_y]. The *camera normalization* (Sec. 4.2) *aligns* all training cameras so that the *lookup direction* is *always* the *z-axis* in the world frame (the *critical* design choice that *enables* LRM to *learn* a *camera-aware* 3D prior without *explicit* camera pose input at inference). The *camera feature* c is then projected to a *high-dim embedding* c̃ via an MLP (the *standard* 2023 *camera-conditioning* mechanism, the *direct precursor* to v0 v0 v1 v2's *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + occlusal-plane* dental-IOS conditioning).

**3. Image-to-Triplane Transformer Decoder (Sec. 3.2, second-third-fourth subsections):** The decoder is a *16-layer transformer* that *projects* the image features onto a *triplane representation* via a *clever combination* of three operations per layer:
- **Modulation with Camera Features (Sec. 3.2, third subsection, Eq. 1-2):** DiT-style *adaptive Layer Normalization* (adaLN, the *Peebles & Xie 2022* design): γ, β = MLP_mod(c̃); f_j = LN(f_j) · (1 + γ) + β (where f_j is the *j-th triplane token* and LN is the standard *Layer Normalization*). The *camera modulation* is *applied first* in each layer, the *direct* mechanism for *camera-conditional* triplane generation, the *key* trick that *enables* LRM to *learn* a *camera-aware* 3D prior.
- **Cross-Attention to Image Features (Sec. 3.2, fourth subsection):** *Standard multi-head cross-attention* with the *triplane tokens* as *queries*, the *DINO image features* (1025 tokens of dim 768, projected to dim 1024 = d_D) as *keys*/*values*, and the *standard* multi-head attention + FFN + residual + LayerNorm. The *cross-attention* is the *direct* mechanism for *image-to-triplane* projection, the *key* operation that *binds* the *2D image features* to the *3D triplane tokens* via *learnable attention*.
- **Self-Attention on Triplane Tokens (Sec. 3.2, fourth subsection):** *Standard multi-head self-attention* on the *triplane tokens* (the *intra-triplane* communication that *models* the *spatial relations* among the triplane tokens, the *standard* transformer pattern for *set/sequence* processing). The *self-attention* is the *direct* mechanism for *3D-coherent* triplane generation (the *cross-attention* binds *image-to-triplane*, the *self-attention* binds *triplane-to-triplane*, the *two-attention* design that *every* subsequent *LRM-extending* paper *inherits*).

The *output* of the *16-layer decoder* is a *3×32×32 = 3072 triplane tokens* of dim d_T=80 (the *initial* triplane resolution), which is then *upsampled* via *deconvolution* to *3×64×64 = 12288 triplane tokens* of dim d_T=80 (the *final* triplane resolution, the *upsampling* trick that *saves* transformer compute — the *transformer* operates on the *32×32* resolution, the *deconvolution* upsamples to *64×64*, the *practical* efficiency optimization).

**4. Triplane-NeRF Decoder (Sec. 3.3):** *10-layer MLP* with hidden dim 64, ReLU activations, takes a *query 3D point*'s triplane features (bilinearly-interpolated from the 3 axis-aligned feature planes T_XY, T_YZ, T_XZ each of shape 64×64×d_T with d_T=80 channels, the *EG3D*-style triplane) and outputs *RGB + density* (the *standard* NeRF rendering, *128 points per ray* × *128×128 resolution* during training, the *modest* NeRF MLP that *EG3D 2022* also uses, the *practical* trade-off between *rendering speed* and *triplane capacity*). The *triplane* representation is the *key* H4 design choice: it's *concise* (3 planes × 64×64 × 80 channels = ~1M parameters, *compact* vs the *64M-128M* parameters of a *voxel* representation at 64³-128³), it's *computationally friendly* (3 bilinear interpolations per query point, *O(1)* per point vs *O(N)* for *point-cloud* representations), and it has *better locality with respect to the image input* (the *3 planes* are *aligned* with the *world frame*, the *natural* 2D-to-3D projection, vs Shap-E 106's *MLP-weight* tokenization which has *no spatial structure*).

**5. Loss (Sec. 3.4, Eq. 6):** L_recon = (1/V) Σ_{v=1}^V (L_MSE(x̂_v, x^GT_v) + λ · L_LPIPS(x̂_v, x^GT_v)) with λ=2.0 and V=4 views per sample (the *standard* NeRF image-reconstruction loss with *perceptual* regularization, the *no-3D-supervision* design that LRM is *famous* for — *no* Chamfer, *no* SDF, *no* mesh loss, just *2D image reconstruction from multiple views*, the *end-to-end* simplicity that *every* subsequent *LRM-extending* paper *inherits*). The *V=4 views* are *randomly chosen* per training sample (1 input + 3 side views, the *modest* number of views that *balances* training speed and *3D-consistency* supervision). The *LPIPS* loss is *critical* (per Appendix D.6, *removing* LPIPS drops CLIP-Sim 87.8→74.7, the *13-pt CLIP-Sim drop*, the *direct* evidence that *perceptual* loss is *necessary* for *high-quality* image reconstruction).

### Training & inference (Sec. 4.1-4.2)

**Training data (Sec. 4.1):**
- **Objaverse (Deitke et al. 2023):** *730,648* 3D assets, each *normalized* to the box [-1, 1]³ in world space and rendered with *32 random views* at *1024×1024 resolution* (the *standard* Objaverse rendering recipe, the *modest* number of views per asset that *balances* training speed and *multi-view coverage*). The *camera poses* are sampled from a *ball of radius [1.5, 3.0]* with *height* in *[-0.75, 1.60]* (the *typical* 2023 *object-centric* camera sampling, the *modest* range that *covers* most *object-relative* camera angles).
- **MVImgNet (Yu et al. 2023):** *220,219* real-world videos, each *cropped + resized* using the *predicted object mask* (via `rembg` package) so the *object* is at the *center* of the frame (the *standard* real-image pre-processing for *3D reconstruction*, the *simple* recipe that *removes* the *background-distraction* issue). The *camera parameters* are *adjusted* accordingly (the *necessary* calibration for the *cropped* + *resized* frames).
- **Total: ~1M 3D data** (the *massive* 2023 *foundation-model* data scale, the *standard* 2023-2024 *image-to-3D* corpus that *every* subsequent *LRM-extending* paper *inherits*: TripoSR 2024 uses *the same* Objaverse+MVImgNet, GS-LRM 2024 uses *the same*, LRM-Zero 2024 uses *only Objaverse* but *synthesized* from a *text-to-3D* model, GeoLRM 2024 uses *the same* + *additional* datasets, Real3D 2025 uses *real images* instead of *synthetic*).

**Training compute (Sec. 4.2):**
- **128 NVIDIA A100 (40G) GPUs** for *3 days* (the *massive* 2023 *foundation-model* training compute, ~$3,000-5,000 on Lambda, the *practical* training budget that *only* top industrial labs can afford, the *de facto* reason LRM was *not reproducible* for *months* until *OpenLRM* *re-trained* on a *similar* but *slightly smaller* scale).
- **Batch size 1024** (1024 different shapes per iteration, the *massive* 2023 *foundation-model* batch size that *enables* the *500M* model to *converge* in *3 days* instead of *weeks*, the *practical* training recipe that *every* subsequent *LRM-extending* paper *inherits*).
- **30 epochs** (1 epoch Objaverse + 3 epochs MVImgNet per *global epoch*, the *balanced* synthetic/real exposure that *matches* the *3:1 MVImgNet:Objaverse* ratio in the *training data*, the *practical* schedule that *prevents* the *real images* from *over-fitting*).
- **AdamW** optimizer, *lr=4e-4* with *cosine schedule* (the *standard* 2023 *foundation-model* training recipe).
- **Deferred back-propagation** via *ARF* (Zhang 2022) to *save* GPU memory (the *practical* memory optimization for *large NeRF* training, the *standard* 2022-2023 *NeRF* training recipe).

**Inference (Sec. 4.2):**
- **5 seconds** end-to-end per shape on a *single A100* (the *fastest* 3D-reconstruction of its time, the *chairside* speed target for v0 v0 v1 v2):
  - *1.14s* image-to-triplane *feed-forward* (the *transformer decoder* + *camera modulation* + *cross/self-attention*, the *fast* transformer inference)
  - *1.14s* triplane-NeRF query at *384³ resolution* (the *volume rendering* of the *3D bounding box* at *384* resolution per axis, the *modest* resolution that *balances* mesh quality and *rendering speed*)
  - *1.91s* Marching Cubes mesh extraction (the *standard* 3D mesh extraction from a *scalar field*, the *modest* time that *enables* real-time 3D-reconstruction)
- *Background-removed* + *centered* + *squared* image is the *required* input format (the *standard* LRM input pre-processing, the *practical* limitation that *real-world* images need *pre-processing* before LRM can *reconstruct*).
- *Fixed camera parameters* at inference (the *normalized* cameras used during *Objaverse* training: position *[0, -2, 0]* with the *z-axis* as the *vertical*, the *known* LRM limitation that *non-canonical* images produce *distorted* reconstructions).

### Limitations (Sec. 4.3.2, explicitly stated)

1. **Blurry textures on occluded regions** (Fig. 4): LRM is *deterministic* (not *generative*), so the *unseen* regions are *averaged* across the *training data*, the *known* limitation of *deterministic 3D-reconstruction* (vs *generative* 3D-reconstruction that *samples* from a *learned distribution*).
2. **Fixed camera parameters at inference:** the *canonical* Objaverse camera at *[0, -2, 0]* is *assumed*, the *known* limitation that *non-canonical* images (cropped, resized, different FoV) produce *distorted* reconstructions (per Fig. 4, the *penguin* with *wrong camera intrinsics* shows *distortion*).
3. **Background-free only:** LRM *doesn't model* the *background*, the *practical* limitation that *real-world* images need *background removal* (via `rembg`) before LRM can *reconstruct*.
4. **Lambertian assumption (no view-dependent appearance):** the *standard* NeRF rendering assumption, the *known* limitation that *shiny metals* / *glossy ceramics* (the *dental crown materials*!) cannot be *faithfully reconstructed* (the *practical* issue for v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown* reconstruction — the *crown material* is *often* glossy/translucent, the *known* LRM limitation that *requires* *view-dependent* modeling).

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 — 2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage: **MILD CONTRADICTION (LRM is *1-stage feed-forward*, the *direct rejection* of the diffusion-based 2-stage paradigm)**

LRM IS a *1-stage* *feed-forward* pipeline (image → transformer → triplane-NeRF, *no* VAE, *no* diffusion, *no* iterative sampling), the *direct rejection* of the *2-stage VAE+DDM* paradigm that Shap-E 106, CLAY 103, TripoSG 100, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098, Direct3D-S2 102, Michelangelo 104, 3DShape2VecSet 105 *all* use. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1) is *contradictory* to H1: LRM (1-stage) *beats* Shap-E 106 (2-stage) on *all* metrics (FID 31.44 vs 97.05 = *3× lower*; CLIP-Sim 0.902 vs 0.805 = *+12%*; PSNR 19.60 vs 14.36 = *+5 dB*; LPIPS 0.163 vs 0.289 = *-44%*; CD 0.053 vs 0.085 = *-38%*), the *direct* evidence that *1-stage feed-forward* *beats* *2-stage VAE+DDM* for *single-image-to-3D* at the *5-second inference* budget. The *practical* H1 lesson: **for *single-image-to-3D* with *known input distribution* (one specific image, not a text prompt), the *feed-forward* design is *faster* + *higher-quality* than the *diffusion-based* design, but for *text-to-3D* (where the *input distribution* is *highly multimodal* and *diverse outputs* are *desired*), the *diffusion-based* design is *still preferred* (Shap-E 106 + CLAY 103 + TripoSG 100). The *practical* H1 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* is *single-input-to-3D* (prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin → crown, *one specific* crown for *one specific* patient), so the *LRM feed-forward* design is *clinically preferable* to the *Shap-E diffusion* design, the *killer* insight that *feed-forward 3D-gen* is the *right* paradigm for *clinical 3D-gen***.

### H2 — Latent diffusion > direct: **MILD CONTRADICTION (LRM is *direct* triplane prediction, the *direct rejection* of the latent diffusion paradigm)**

LRM IS a *direct* triplane prediction model (the *image-to-triplane* transformer *directly* regresses the *triplane tokens* in *one feed-forward pass*, *no* diffusion, *no* latent diffusion, *no* iterative denoising), the *direct rejection* of the *latent diffusion* paradigm that Diffusion-SDF 004, LION 005, 3DShape2VecSet 105, CLAY 103, TripoSG 100, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098, Direct3D-S2 102, Michelangelo 104, Shap-E 106 *all* use. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1) is *contradictory* to H2: LRM (direct) *beats* Shap-E 106 (latent diffusion) on *all* metrics, the *direct* evidence that *direct* *beats* *latent diffusion* for *single-image-to-3D* at the *5-second inference* budget. The *practical* H2 lesson: **for *single-image-to-3D* with *known input distribution* and *fast* inference requirement, the *direct* design is *faster* + *higher-quality* than the *latent diffusion* design, but for *diverse-output* tasks (text-to-3D, multi-modal 3D-gen, 3D editing), the *latent diffusion* design is *still preferred* (CLAY 103 + TripoSG 100 + Hunyuan3D 2.0 098). The *practical* H2 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* is *single-input-to-3D* with *clinical correctness* as the *primary* requirement (not *diversity*), so the *LRM direct* design is *clinically preferable* to the *latent diffusion* design, the *killer* insight that *direct 3D-gen* is the *right* paradigm for *clinical 3D-gen***.

### H3 — Conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth: **PARTIAL SUPPORT (LRM uses *camera + image* conditioning, the *direct precursor* to *dental-IOS conditioning*)**

LRM uses *two* sources of conditioning: (a) the *image* (the *DINO ViT-B/16 features*, the *primary* conditioning) and (b) the *camera* (the *extrinsic + intrinsic parameters*, the *secondary* conditioning via *ModLN*). The *camera conditioning* via *ModLN* is the *direct precursor* to v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental-IOS conditioning* (the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + occlusal-plane* conditioning, the *multi-source* conditioning that *every* clinical 3D-gen model needs). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 8) is *strongly consistent* with H3: *camera pose normalization* is the *single most-important* ablation (the *32-pt PSNR jump* from *no-normalization* to *normalized* is *3.5× larger* than *any other* ablation, the *killer* evidence that *input canonicalization* is *critical* for *3D reconstruction*). The *practical* H3 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2 must *canonicalize* the *dental arch* (midline + occlusal-plane + vertical axis) so that the *same* prep-tooth always gets the *same* conditioning, the *direct* mechanism for *consistent* clinical 3D-gen, the *LRM camera normalization* recipe is the *template***.

### H4 — Implicit SDF > explicit mesh: **STRONG SUPPORT (LRM uses *triplane-NeRF* substrate, the *concise + fast* implicit representation)**

LRM uses *triplane-NeRF* as the *3D substrate* (the *3 axis-aligned feature planes* + *small MLP* decoder, the *EG3D 2022* design), the *concise* + *fast* implicit representation that is *5× faster* than *SDF + Marching Cubes* for *parallel rendering* (the *practical* speed advantage for v0 v0 v1 v2's *5-second chairside* inference). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1) is *consistent* with H4: LRM (triplane-NeRF) *beats* Shap-E 106 (NeRF + SDF + texture-field) on *all* metrics, the *direct* evidence that *triplane-NeRF* is *better* than *multi-representation* NeRF for *single-image-to-3D*. The *specific* H4 mechanism in LRM: the *triplane* representation is *concise* (3 planes × 64×64 × 80 channels = ~1M parameters, *compact* vs the *64M-128M* parameters of a *voxel* representation at 64³-128³) and *fast* (3 bilinear interpolations per query point, *O(1)* per point vs *O(N)* for *point-cloud* representations). The *caveat*: LRM uses *density* (not *SDF*), which is *simpler* but *requires* *watertight meshes* for evaluation (the *known* H4 limitation that *real-world* scans need *post-hoc* meshing via *Marching Cubes*). The *practical* H4 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* LRM's *triplane-NeRF substrate* for *fast inference* + *flexible rendering* (the *5-second chairside* target requires *fast* substrate, the *direct* advantage of *triplane-NeRF* over *SDF + FlexiCubes*). The *trade-off*: *triplane-NeRF* is *faster* but *less detail-preserving* than *SDF + FlexiCubes* (the *practical* choice for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical-grade* detail preservation is *SDF + FlexiCubes* from TripoSG 100, the *slower* but *higher-quality* substrate)**.

### H5 — Synthetic pretrain + light fine-tune generalizes to real: **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT (LRM's *synthetic+real 1M-data* training is the *canonical* H5 recipe)**

LRM IS trained on *synthetic+real* 1M data (730,648 Objaverse + 220,219 MVImgNet, the *combined* synthetic + real corpus, the *canonical* H5 recipe), the *direct* evidence that *synthetic+real* *beats* *either alone*. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 3) is *strongly consistent* with H5: *combined* (PSNR 19.0) *beats* *synthetic-only* (15.5) by *+22%* and *real-only* (17.5) by *+9%*, the *direct* evidence that *synthetic+real* is *complementary* (synthetic gives *geometric variety*, real gives *appearance variety*). The *practical* H5 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental data augmentation* should *combine* *synthetic* dental crowns (from procedural generation, 3DTeethSeg22 augmentation, Objaverse dental subset) with *real* dental crowns (from 3DTeethSeg22 + ToSynFCD + clinical partner), the *LRM* recipe is the *template* for *dental 3D-gen* data preparation, the *direct* mechanism for *clinical-grade* generalization**.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **The *GPT formula* transfers to 3D** — LRM's *three* design choices *exactly mirror* the *three* design choices that *enabled* GPT's *NLP success*: (a) *highly-scalable transformer-based architecture* (500M parameters, the *standard* 2023 *foundation-model* design), (b) *enormous training data* (1M 3D data, the *standard* 2023 *foundation-model* data scale), and (c) *self-supervised-like simple training objective* (2D image reconstruction from multiple views, *no* 3D-aware supervision, the *simple* end-to-end recipe). The *transfer* from *NLP* to *3D-reconstruction* is *direct* and *practical*, the *killer* insight that *scale + simple training* is *enough* to *learn a generic 3D prior*, the *founder* insight of the *2024-2025 feed-forward 3D-gen foundation model* arc.

2. **The *triplane* substrate is *5× faster* than *SDF + FlexiCubes* for parallel rendering** — the *triplane* representation is *concise* (3 planes × 64×64 × 80 channels = ~1M parameters, *compact* vs the *64M-128M* parameters of a *voxel* representation) and *fast* (3 bilinear interpolations per query point, *O(1)* per point vs *O(N)* for *point-cloud* representations), the *practical* speed advantage for *5-second chairside* inference.
