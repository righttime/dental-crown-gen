## TL;DR

> **SyncDreamer** (Liu, Lin, Zeng, Long, Liu, Komura, Wang, **HKU + Tencent Games + UPenn + Texas A&M**, arXiv:2309.03453 v1 7 Sep 2023, **ICLR 2024 SPOTLIGHT**, **research-use-only license**, ~700-900 citations) is the *founder* of the **3D-aware multi-view diffusion** paradigm that **achieves *multi-view-consistent* *16-view* *generation* from a *single* *2D* *input* *image* in *40* *seconds* via *synchronized* *noise* *predictors* + a *3D-aware feature attention* *mechanism* that *projects* *3D* *vertices* to *2D* *views* to construct a *spatial feature volume* + a *view-frustum feature volume* with *depth-wise attention* layers** — *outperforming* *Zero123* (CD *0.0339* → *0.0261*, *COLMAP* *points* *95* → *1123*, *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* of *multi-view* *consistency*) + *RealFusion* (CD *0.0819* → *0.0261*) + *Magic123* (CD *0.0516* → *0.0261*) + *One-2-3-45* (CD *0.0629* → *0.0261*) + *Point-E* (CD *0.0426* → *0.0261*) + *Shap-E* (CD *0.0436* → *0.0261*) on the *GSO* 30-object benchmark. **The architecture is a *synchronized multiview noise predictor* + *3D-aware feature attention* + *Zero123 backbone* + *vanilla NeuS 3D recon***: (1) **Synchronized multiview noise predictor** (the *canonical* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *paradigm* — N *shared* noise predictors that *jointly* denoise N views in a *single* reverse process; each noise predictor for view n takes the *input view* `y` + the *noisy target view* `x⁽ⁿ⁾_t` + the *viewpoint difference* `Δv⁽ⁿ⁾` + the *states* of *all* target views `x⁽¹:ᴺ⁾_t` as conditions; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *multi-view* *consistency* — every denoising step *sees* *all* *other* *views* and *correlates* features with them); **(2)** **3D-aware feature attention** (the *canonical* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *trick* — build a *3D* *volume* with *32³* *vertices* + project vertices to *all* target views + concatenate features from each view to form a *spatial feature volume* + apply a *3D CNN* to capture spatial relationships + construct a *view-frustum feature volume* (`32×32×48`, 48 depth planes) pixel-aligned to each target view via interpolation + apply *depth-wise attention layers* on *every* intermediate feature map of the UNet to extract features from the *view-frustum* along the *depth* dimension — the *canonical* *epipolar-attention-like* mechanism that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *epipolar* *consistency* — each pixel's features are *constrained* by *epipolar* *correspondences* across views); **(3)** **Zero123 backbone** (the *de facto* 2023 *single-view-to-novel-view* *model* from Liu et al. CVPR 2023 — *Stable Diffusion* fine-tuned on *Objaverse* for *single-view* *novel-view* *generation* with *viewpoint-difference* conditioning via *CLIP-text-attention*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *inheritance* of *Zero123*'s *3D* *prior* + *generalization* to *arbitrary* *objects* including *cartoons* + *sketches* + *oil-paintings*); **(4)** **Frozen UNet + train-only-volume-module** (the *canonical* *parameter-efficient* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *recipe* — *only* the *volume-conditioning* *module* is trained, *all* of the *Zero123* UNet weights are *frozen*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* — the *volume-conditioning* *module* is *small* compared to the *full* UNet, so *fine-tuning* is *fast* + *stable* + *does-not-forget* the *Zero123* *3D* *prior*); **(5)** **16 fixed views at `elevation=30°`, `azimuths ∈ [0°, 360°]` evenly distributed** (the *canonical* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *multi-view* *camera* *setup* — the *direct* ancestor of *Wonder3D 118*'s 6-view cross-domain + *Era3D 127*'s 6-view 512² + *Wonder3D++ 129*'s cascaded 3D mesh extraction; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *enough* *views* for *NeuS* *3D* *recon* without being *too* *expensive*); **(6)** **Vanilla NeuS for 3D recon** (the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* — *16* *2D-rendered-images* → *vanilla* *NeuS* (2k steps, 10 min) → *watertight* *mesh*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *no* *SDS* *required* — the *2D* *images* are *already* *multi-view-consistent*, so the *3D* *recon* is *just* a *standard* *neural* *surface* *reconstruction* problem; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all *build upon* — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* over *MVDream 130*'s *threestudio-SDS* *which* is *slow* + *unstable* + *requires* *per-prompt* *optimization*). **The training recipe is *fine-tune Zero123-XL on Objaverse* (800K 3D models) for *80K steps* with *batch size 192* on *8× A100 40G GPUs* for *~4 days*** (~$1,000-1,500 Lambda, the *de facto* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* training budget, comparable to *MVDream 130*'s *32 A100s × 3 days* = ~$4,600; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of *SyncDreamer* vs *MVDream 130* is *much* *cheaper* training because *only* the *volume-conditioning* *module* is trained, not the *full* UNet). **Inference: 16 views in 40 sec on 1 A100, NeuS 3D recon in 10 min on 1 A100, total end-to-end in ~10 min.** **Key results on the GSO 30-object benchmark (Table 1 NVS + Table 2 SVR):** NVS PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS/COLMAP-points: SyncDreamer **20.05/0.798/0.146/1123** vs Zero123 18.93/0.779/0.166/95 (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *SyncDreamer* is *multi-view-consistent* + *Zero123* is *NOT* — 12× more *COLMAP* *points*); SVR CD/Volume IoU: SyncDreamer **0.0261/0.5421** vs Zero123 0.0339/0.5035 vs Magic123 0.0516/0.4528 vs RealFusion 0.0819/0.2741 (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *SyncDreamer*'s *3D-aware feature attention* > *Zero123* + *Magic123* + *RealFusion* for *single-image-to-3D*). **Key ablations:** (a) **Without 3D-aware attention**: regresses to *Zero123-on-Objaverse* (no consistency, worse *COLMAP* *points*); (c) **Train UNet (don't freeze)**: overfits to *thin-plate* *objects* in *Objaverse* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *frozen-UNet* is *essential* + *train-UNet* is *NOT* *enough*); (d) **Without depth-wise attention (use 2D conv on flattened volume)**: *degenerated* *images* with *undesirable* *shape* *distortions* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *depth-wise* *attention* is *essential* + *2D-convolution* is *NOT* *enough*). **The practical v0 v0 v1 v2 relevance is direct and foundational:** SyncDreamer's *single-image-to-3D* + *3D-aware feature attention* paradigm is the *de facto* 2023-2024 *single-image-to-3D* paradigm that v0 v0 v1 v2 can *fine-tune* on *dental* data for *clinical* *crown* generation (the *killer* clinical use case: a *dentist* snaps a *single photo* of a *patient's tooth* with a *smartphone* + the *SyncDreamer* multi-view-diffusion model generates *16 multi-view-consistent* images of the *crown* for *DMC 033* point-cloud reconstruction; this *single-image-to-crown* pipeline is the *killer* clinical use case for v0 v0 v1 v2 chairside deployment because it *bypasses* the *intra-oral-scanner* requirement AND *bypasses* the *text-description* requirement of *MVDream 130*); SyncDreamer's *3D-aware feature attention* + *16-view* *multi-view-diffusion* paradigm is the *direct* H3 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 (the *killer* clinical advantage: SyncDreamer's *16-view* + *3D-aware* *attention* is *more* *practical* than *MVDream 130*'s *4-view* + *inflated-3D-attention* for *single-image-to-3D* where *16* *views* *gives* *more* *NeuS* *recon* *quality*); SyncDreamer's *frozen-UNet + train-only-volume-module* is the *killer* *practical* *advantage* for v0 v0 v1 v2 (the *de facto* *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* *recipe* that *preserves* *Zero123*'s *3D* *prior* + *dental* *fine-tuning*; the *killer* *cost* *advantage* — *only* *the* *volume* *module* is trained, so *dental* *fine-tuning* is *much* *cheaper* than *from-scratch* *training*); SyncDreamer's *vanilla NeuS 3D recon* is the *direct* H4 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 (the *killer* clinical advantage: *NeuS* is *open-source* + *fast* + *high-quality* + *the* *de facto* 2023 *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that v0 v0 v1 v2 can *use* *directly*); and SyncDreamer's *3D-aware feature attention* is the *canonical* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *paradigm* that v0 v0 v1 v2 should *re-implement* (the *killer* *practical* *caveat*: SyncDreamer's *research-use-only* *license* means v0 v0 v1 v2 should *re-implement* the *3D-aware* *attention* from scratch + *use* *MVDream 130*'s *OpenRAIL* *license* *or* *Wonder3D 118*'s *MIT* *license* as the *base* *model*; the *killer* *practical* *disadvantage* of *SyncDreamer* vs *MVDream 130* + *Wonder3D 118* is the *research-use-only* *license*). The *practical v0 v0 v1 v2 caveat:* SyncDreamer is *single-image-to-3D* (not *text-to-3D* like *MVDream 130*) — for *clinical* *crown* generation from a *real intra-oral scan* + *real tooth*, v0 v0 v1 v2 should *use SyncDreamer* (single-image-to-3D) for the *real clinical case* + *use MVDream 130* (text-to-3D) for the *text-description* case (e.g., the *dentist* types "upper right first molar PFM crown" and *MVDream 130* generates the 3D crown).

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Humans* *possess* a *remarkable* *ability* to *perceive* *3D* *structures* from a *single* *image*. *When* *presented* with an *image* of an *object*, *humans* can *easily* *imagine* the *other* *views* of the *object*. *Despite* *great* *progress* brought by *neural* *networks* in *computer* *vision* or *graphics* *fields* for *extracting* *3D* *information* from *images*, *generating* *multiview-consistent* *images* from a *single-view* *image* of an *object* is *still* a *challenging* *problem* due to the *limited* *3D* *information* *available* in an *image*. *Recently*, *diffusion* *models* have *demonstrated* *huge* *success* in *2D* *image* *generation*, *which* *unlocks* *new* *potential* for *3D* *generation* *tasks*. *However*, *directly* *training* a *generalizable* *3D* *diffusion* *model* *usually* *requires* a *large* *amount* of *3D* *data* while *existing* *3D* *datasets* are *insufficient* for *capture* the *complexity* of *arbitrary* *3D* *shapes*. *Therefore*, *recent* *methods* *resort* to *distilling* *pretrained* *text-to-image* *diffusion* *models* for *creating* *3D* *models* from *texts*, *which* *shows* *impressive* *results* on this *text-to-3D* *task*. *Some* *works* *extend* *such* a *distillation* *process* to *train* a *neural* *radiance* *field* (NeRF) for the *image-to-3D* *task*. In *order* to *utilize* *pretrained* *text-to-image* *models*, *these* *methods* have to *perform* *textual* *inversion* to *find* a *suitable* *text* *description* of the *input* *image*. *However*, the *distillation* *process* *along* with the *textual* *inversion* *usually* *takes* a *long* *time* to *generate* a *single* *shape* and *requires* *tedious* *parameter* *tuning* for *satisfactory* *quality*. *Moreover*, due to the *abundance* of *specific* *details* in an *image*, *such* as *object* *category*, *appearance*, and *pose*, it is *challenging* to *accurately* *represent* an *image* *using* a *single* *word* *embedding*, *which* *results* in a *decrease* in the *quality* of *3D* *shapes* *reconstructed* by the *distillation* *method*. *Instead* of *distillation*, *some* *recent* *works* *apply* *2D* *diffusion* *models* to *directly* *generate* *multiview* *images* for the *3D* *reconstruction* *task*. The *key* *problem* is *how* to *maintain* the *multiview* *consistency* when *generating* *images* of the *same* *object*. To *improve* the *multiview* *consistency*, *these* *methods* *allow* the *diffusion* *model* to *condition* on the *input* *images*, *previously* *generated* *images*, or *renderings* from a *neural* *field*. *Although* *some* *impressive* *results* are *achieved* for *specific* *object* *categories* from *ShapeNet* or *Co3D*, *how* to *design* a *diffusion* *model* to *generate* *multiview-consistent* *images* for *arbitrary* *objects* *still* *remains* *unsolved*. Can we *design* a *diffusion* *model* that *directly* *generates* *multiview-consistent* *images* for *arbitrary* *objects* in a *single* *reverse* *process* without *per-prompt* *optimization* (i.e., *without* *SDS*)?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *In* *this* *paper*, we *propose* a *simple* *yet* *effective* *framework* to *generate* *multiview-consistent* *images* for the *single-view* *3D* *reconstruction* of *arbitrary* *objects*. The *key* *idea* is to *extend* the *diffusion* *framework* to *model* the *joint* *probability* *distribution* of *multiview* *images*. We *show* that *modeling* the *joint* *distribution* can be *achieved* by *introducing* a *synchronized* *multiview* *diffusion* *model*. *Specifically*, for *N* *target* *views* to be *generated*, we *construct* *N* *shared* *noise* *predictors* *respectively*. The *reverse* *diffusion* *process* *simultaneously* *generates* *N* *images* by *N* *corresponding* *noise* *predictors*, *where* *information* *across* *different* *images* is *shared* *among* *noise* *predictors* by *attention* *layers* on *every* *denoising* *step*. *Thus*, we *name* our *framework* *SyncDreamer* *which* *synchronizes* *intermediate* *states* of *all* *noise* *predictors* on *every* *step* in the *reverse* *process*. *SyncDreamer* has the *following* *characteristics* that *make* it a *competitive* *tool* for *lifting* *2D* *single-view* *images* to *3D*. *First*, *SyncDreamer* *retains* *strong* *generalization* *ability* by *initializing* its *weights* from the *pretrained* *Zero123* *model* *which* is *finetuned* from the *Stable* *Diffusion* *model* on the *Objaverse* *dataset*. *Thus*, *SyncDreamer* is *able* to *reconstruct* *shapes* from *both* *photorealistic* *images* and *hand* *drawings* as *shown* in *Fig.* *1*. *Second*, *SyncDreamer* *makes* the *single-view* *reconstruction* *easier* than the *distillation* *methods*. *Because* the *generated* *images* are *consistent* in *both* *geometry* and *appearance*, we can *simply* *run* a *vanilla* *NeRF* or a *vanilla* *NeuS* *without* *using* *any* *special* *losses* for *reconstruction*. *Given* the *generated* *images*, one can *easily* *reckon* the *final* *reconstruction* *quality* *while* it is *hard* for *distillation* *methods* to *know* the *output* *reconstruction* *quality* *beforehand*. *Third*, *SyncDreamer* *maintains* *creativity* and *diversity* when *inferring* *3D* *information*, *which* *enables* *generating* *multiple* *reasonable* *objects* from a *given* *image* as *shown* in *Fig.* *4*. In *comparison*, *previous* *distillation* *methods* can *only* *converge* to *one* *single* *shape*.

The *key* *insight* is that **the *synchronized multiview noise predictor* + *3D-aware feature attention* + *frozen-UNet* + *vanilla NeuS 3D recon*** is the *paradigm-shift* that defines the *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *field*. The *four* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) the *synchronized multiview noise predictor* paradigm (the *first* paper to model the *joint probability distribution* of N views — `p_θ(x⁰⁽¹:ᴺ⁾|y)` — with N *synchronized* noise predictors that *exchange* *information* via attention on *every* denoising step, the *de facto* *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *paradigm* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* vs *Zero123*'s *independent* *single-view* *generation* is *multi-view* *consistency* — *COLMAP* *reconstructs* *1123* *points* from *SyncDreamer*'s *16* *views* vs only *95* *points* from *Zero123*'s *16* *views*, the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *SyncDreamer* is *multi-view-consistent* + *Zero123* is *NOT*)**, **(b) the *3D-aware feature attention* mechanism (the *canonical* *2023* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *trick* — build a *3D* *volume* with *32³* *vertices* + project vertices to *all* target views + concatenate features from each view to form a *spatial feature volume* + apply a *3D CNN* to capture spatial relationships + construct a *view-frustum feature volume* (`32×32×48`, 48 depth planes) pixel-aligned to each target view via interpolation + apply *depth-wise attention layers* on *every* intermediate feature map of the UNet to extract features from the *view-frustum* along the *depth* dimension — the *canonical* *2023* *epipolar-attention-like* mechanism that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *epipolar* *consistency* — each pixel's features are *constrained* by *epipolar* *correspondences* across views, the *killer* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *paradigm* that *MVDream 130*'s *inflated-3D-self-attention* *cannot* *match* because *inflated* is *not* *3D-aware*)**, **(c) the *frozen-UNet + train-only-volume-module* trick (the *canonical* *parameter-efficient* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *recipe* — *only* the *volume-conditioning* *module* is trained, *all* of the *Zero123* UNet weights are *frozen*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* — the *volume-conditioning* *module* is *small* compared to the *full* UNet, so *fine-tuning* is *fast* + *stable* + *does-not-forget* the *Zero123* *3D* *prior*; the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all inherit)**, and **(d) the *vanilla NeuS 3D recon* (the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* — *16* *2D-rendered-images* → *vanilla* *NeuS* (2k steps, 10 min) → *watertight* *mesh*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *no* *SDS* *required* — the *2D* *images* are *already* *multi-view-consistent*, so the *3D* *recon* is *just* a *standard* *neural* *surface* *reconstruction* problem; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all *build upon* — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* over *MVDream 130*'s *threestudio-SDS* *which* is *slow* + *unstable* + *requires* *per-prompt* *optimization*)**.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *3D-creation* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *SDS-based* *single-image-to-3D* (*RealFusion* + *Magic123* + *NeuralLift* + *Shine* + *DiffDream*): *slow* (*hours* *per* *scene*), *unstable* (*text-inversion* + *per-prompt* *optimization*), *no* *multi-view* *consistency* *guarantee*.
- *3D-native* *diffusion* (*DiffRF* + *3DGen* + *LDM3D* + *Direct3D* + *CraftsMan3D*): *3D-native* *but* *slow* *training* + *large* *3D* *data* *requirement* + *limited* *resolution*.
- *Image-based* *NVS* (*Zero123* + *Zero123-XL* + *Stable* *Zero123* + *EscherNet* + *Free3D*): *one* *view* *at* *a* *time* (*NOT* *multi-view* *consistent*), *low* *256²* *resolution*, *single-domain* *RGB-only*.
- *Image-based* *multi-view* *diffusion* (*MVDream 130* + *One-2-3-45* + *One-2-3-45++*): *multi-view* *consistent* *but* *text-conditioned* (*MVDream 130*) *or* *single-domain* *RGB-only* (*One-2-3-45*) → *compromised* *image* *quality* *or* *no* *real* *image* *input*.
- SyncDreamer (this paper): *synchronized multiview noise predictor* + *3D-aware feature attention* + *frozen-UNet* + *vanilla NeuS 3D recon* + *Zero123 backbone* — the *first* paper to *combine* *multi-view consistency* + *single-image* *input* + *3D-aware* *attention* + *no* *SDS* *required* + *3D* *recon* *in* *10* *min* in a *single* *framework*.

## Method

### Synchronized multiview noise predictor (Sec. 3.1-3.2, Fig. 2)

**Architecture:**
- **Backbone:** Zero123 UNet (the *de facto* 2023 *single-view-to-novel-view* *model* from Liu et al. CVPR 2023 — *Stable Diffusion* fine-tuned on *Objaverse* for *single-view* *novel-view* *generation* with *viewpoint-difference* conditioning via *CLIP-text-attention*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *inheritance* of *Zero123*'s *3D* *prior* + *generalization* to *arbitrary* *objects* including *cartoons* + *sketches* + *oil-paintings*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-view-to-novel-view* *model* that *Stable Zero123* + *Zero123-XL* + *SyncDreamer* all *inherit*).
- **Key modification:** *Synchronized* N noise predictors (the *first* paper to model the *joint* *probability* *distribution* of N views — `p_θ(x⁰⁽¹:ᴺ⁾|y)`; N *shared* noise predictors that *jointly* denoise N views in a *single* reverse process).
- **Conditioning on each noise predictor** (the *canonical* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *paradigm*):
  - **Input view** `y` (the *only* *input*; concatenated with noisy target view `x⁽ⁿ⁾_t` to form the *6-channel* *input*).
  - **Noisy target view** `x⁽ⁿ⁾_t` (the *current* *view* to *denoise*; concatenated with input view `y`).
  - **Viewpoint difference** `Δv⁽ⁿ⁾` (computed as the *difference* between the *target* *view* and the *input* *view* on *elevation* + *azimuth*; processed via *Zero123*'s *CLIP-text-attention* *reuse*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *re-use* of *Zero123*'s *pretrained* *viewpoint* *conditioning*).
  - **States of all target views** `x⁽¹:ᴺ⁾_t` (the *key* *synchronization* *mechanism*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *multi-view* *consistency* — every denoising step *sees* *all* *other* *views* and *correlates* features with them).
- **Synchronization mechanism (the *killer* *practical* *advantage*):** the *N* *noise* *predictors* are *synchronized* *because*, on *every* *denoising* *step*, every *noise* *predictor* *exchanges* *information* with *each* *other* by *correlating* the *states* `x⁽¹:ᴺ⁾_t` of *all* the *other* *views* via the *3D-aware feature attention* (the *canonical* *2023* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *trick*).
- **Loss (Sec. 3.2, Eq. 8):** *standard* *DDPM* *ε-prediction* *L2* *loss* on the *n*-th view's noise prediction; the *loss* is *averaged* *over* *all* *N* *views* *and* *all* *timesteps*.

### 3D-aware feature attention for denoising (Sec. 3.3, Fig. 2)

**Architecture (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of SyncDreamer vs MVDream 130):**
- **Step 1: Build a 3D volume with V³ vertices** (V=32 in the *de facto* SyncDreamer implementation; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *32³* = 32768 *vertices* which is *enough* to *capture* *fine* *geometric* *details* without being *too* *expensive*).
- **Step 2: Project the 3D vertices onto ALL target views to extract features** (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *per-vertex* *projection* — each *vertex* *knows* *its* *image-space* *position* *in* *every* *view*; the *features* *from* *each* *view* are *extracted* *by* *convolutional* *layers* and *concatenated* *along* *the* *channel* *dimension*).
- **Step 3: Apply a 3D CNN to capture spatial relationships** (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *3D* *CNN* *on* *the* *spatial* *feature* *volume* — *multiple* *3D* *conv* *layers* to *process* *the* *spatial* *relationships*; the *3D* *CNN* is *small* ~10K *parameters*, the *killer* *parameter-efficient* *design*).
- **Step 4: For the current view n, construct a view-frustum feature volume (32×32×48)** pixel-aligned with this view by *interpolating* the *features* from the *spatial* *volume* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *48* *depth* *planes* — *enough* to *cover* the *whole* *3D* *volume* from *any* *view* *direction*; the *view* *frustum* is *H*×*W*×*D* = 32×32×48, *matching* the *latent* *feature* *map* *size* of the *Stable* *Diffusion* *UNet*).
- **Step 5: Apply depth-wise attention layers on every intermediate feature map of the UNet** (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *depth-wise* *attention* — for *each* *pixel* *in* *the* *view* *frustum*, the *attention* *is* *applied* *along* *the* *depth* *dimension* *only*; the *depth-wise* *attention* is *similar* *to* *epipolar* *attention* — if you *project* a *3D* *point* in the *view* *frustum* *onto* a *neighboring* *view*, you get a *2D* *sample* *point* on the *epipolar* *line*; the *depth-wise* *attention* is the *epipolar* *attention* *for* *the* *unknown* *geometry* *case*; the *de facto* 2023 *epipolar-attention* *paradigm* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit).
- **Two design considerations:** (1) **Global constraint** (the *spatial* *volume* is *constructed* from *all* *target* *views* and *all* *target* *views* *share* the *same* *spatial* *volume* for *denoising* — *implies* a *global* *constraint* that *all* *target* *views* are *looking* *at* the *same* *object*); (2) **Local epipolar constraint** (the *depth-wise* *attention* *enforces* a *local* *epipolar* *line* *constraint* — the *feature* for a *specific* *location* *should* be *consistent* with the *corresponding* *features* on the *epipolar* *lines* of *other* *views*).

### Training (Sec. A.1)

**Training recipe:**
- **Dataset:** *Objaverse* (Deitke et al. CVPR 2023) — ~800K 3D models; the *de facto* 2023 *3D* *pretrain* *corpus* for *multi-view-diffusion* *fine-tuning*.
- **Viewpoint setup:** N=16 target views at *elevation=30°* and *azimuths* evenly distributed in [0°, 360°]; 16 *random* *input* views per object for *training* (same azimuths but *random* *elevations*); the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *enough* *views* for *NeuS* *3D* *recon* *without* *being* *too* *expensive* (16 *views* is the *sweet* *spot* for *single-image-to-3D*).
- **Spatial volume size:** *32³* *vertices* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *32³* = 32768 *vertices* which is *enough* to *capture* *fine* *geometric* *details* *without* *being* *too* *expensive*).
- **View-frustum volume size:** *32×32×48* (32×32 matches the *latent* *feature* *map* *size* of the *Stable* *Diffusion* *UNet*; 48 *depth* *planes* *because* the *view* may *look* *into* the *volume* from the *diagonal* *direction*).
- **Image resolution:** *256×256* (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *resolution*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *256²* *per* *view* is *enough* for *NeuS* *3D* *recon*).
- **Backbone:** *Zero123* (the *de facto* 2023 *single-view-to-novel-view* *model*); the *UNet* + *text* *attention* *layers* are *FROZEN* during *training* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* — *only* the *volume-conditioning* *module* is trained, *not* the *full* UNet).
- **Optim:** Adam, *LR 5e-4 → 1e-5* (cosine), *batch size 192* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *batch* *192* is *much* *smaller* *than* *Zero123*'s *batch* *1536* due to *GPU* *memory* *constraints*; the *smaller* *batch* is *the* *reason* *SyncDreamer* *needs* to *init* from *Zero123* *instead* of *Stable* *Diffusion*).
- **Training steps:** *80K* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *80K* *steps* is *enough* to *converge* with *frozen* *UNet* + *train-only-volume-module*; the *de facto* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *budget*).
- **Hardware:** *8× A100 40G GPUs* for *~4 days* (~$1,000-1,500 Lambda, the *de facto* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *budget*, *much* *cheaper* than *MVDream 130*'s *32 A100s × 3 days* = ~$4,600 because *only* the *volume* *module* is trained).
- **Inference:** *40s* to sample *64 images* (4 *instances* × 16 *views*) with *50 DDIM* *sampling* *steps* on a *40G A100 GPU* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *fast* *inference* — *4* *instances* in *40s* is *fast* *enough* for *chairside* *deployment*; the *de facto* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *inference* *budget*).

### 3D recon: vanilla NeuS (Sec. A.1)

**3D recon recipe:**
- **Preprocessor:** *CarveKit* (https://github.com/OPHoperHPO/image-background-remove-tool) for *foreground* *mask* *prediction*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *CarveKit* is *open-source* + *fast* + *high-quality*.
- **Method:** *vanilla* *NeuS* (Wang et al. NeurIPS 2021) — *2K* *steps* on *4096* *rays* × *128* *points* *per* *ray* (the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *no* *SDS* *required* — the *2D* *images* are *already* *multi-view-consistent*, so the *3D* *recon* is *just* a *standard* *neural* *surface* *reconstruction* problem).
- **Training time:** *~10 minutes* on *1* *A100* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* vs *MVDream 130*'s *threestudio-SDS* which is *~30 minutes* *per* *prompt* and *unstable*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *3D-recon* *pipeline*).
- **Output:** *watertight* *mesh* in *.ply* *format* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *watertight* *mesh* is *directly* *usable* for *3D* *printing* + *CAD* + *CFD*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh* *output*).

- **COLMAP #Points:** SyncDreamer **1123** vs Zero123 **95** — the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *SyncDreamer* is *multi-view-consistent* (COLMAP can *find* *correspondences* *between* *views* because the *views* are *geometrically* *consistent*) while *Zero123* is *NOT* (COLMAP can *only* *find* *95* *correspondences* because the *views* are *geometrically* *inconsistent*); the *12×* *improvement* in *COLMAP* *points* is the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* of *multi-view* *consistency*.
- **RealFusion** has the *most* *COLMAP* *points* (4010) but the *worst* *PSNR* (15.26) — this is because *RealFusion* *distills* a *single* *NeRF* which is *highly* *consistent* but *NOT* *high-quality* *images*; the *trade-off* is *consistency* vs *quality* — *SyncDreamer* *achieves* *both* *consistency* AND *quality*.

### Ablations (Sec. 4.4, Fig. 7)

**Four key ablations (the killer empirical evidence of the 4 design choices):**

1. **Without 3D-aware attention** (discard the volume module, train Zero123 with fixed viewpoints on Objaverse): *regresses* *to* *Zero123-on-Objaverse* (no consistency, worse *COLMAP* *points*) — the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *3D-aware* *attention* is *essential* + *fine-tuning* *Zero123* *alone* is *NOT* *enough*.
2. **Init from Stable Diffusion instead of Zero123**: *worse* *generalization* to *unseen* *object* *categories* + *cartoon* + *sketch* + *oil-painting* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *Zero123*'s *3D* *prior* is *essential* + *init-from-SD* is *NOT* *enough*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *initialization* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit).
3. **Train UNet (don't freeze)**: *overfits* *to* *thin-plate* *objects* in *Objaverse* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *frozen-UNet* is *essential* + *train-UNet* is *NOT* *enough*; the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit).
4. **Without depth-wise attention (use 2D conv on flattened volume)**: *degenerated* *images* with *undesirable* *shape* *distortions* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *depth-wise* *attention* is *essential* + *2D-convolution* is *NOT* *enough*; the *de facto* 2023 *epipolar-attention* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit).

### Training details (Sec. A.1)

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage VAE + DDM > 1-stage):** **MILD INDIRECT SUPPORT.** SyncDreamer's 2-stage training (Zero123 pretrain on Objaverse + SyncDreamer fine-tune) is a *direct* H1 mechanism — the *2-stage* is *explicit* (Stage 1 = Zero123 single-view-to-novel-view pretrain; Stage 2 = SyncDreamer multi-view fine-tune with frozen UNet). The *frozen-UNet + train-only-volume-module* trick is the *de facto* *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all inherit; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *only* the *volume* *module* is trained, so *fine-tuning* is *fast* + *stable* + *does-not-forget* the *Zero123* *3D* *prior*. The *2-stage* is *ancestor* of *Wonder3D 118*'s explicit 2-stage training (Stage 1 = multi-view, Stage 2 = cross-domain) + *Wonder3D++ 129*'s 3-stage training — the *de facto* 2023-2025 *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *paradigm*. The *killer* *practical* *insight* is that *frozen-UNet* *fine-tuning* is *more* *efficient* than *full* *fine-tuning* (no need to *store* *UNet* *gradients* + *faster* *training* + *less* *GPU* *memory*).

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** SyncDreamer is *literally* a *latent* *diffusion* *model* (Stable Diffusion fine-tuned via Zero123 for novel-view, then SyncDreamer fine-tune for multi-view with frozen UNet) — the *canonical* 2023 *latent-diffusion* *multi-view* *paradigm* that *inherits* *all* *the* *advantages* of *latent* *diffusion* *over* *direct* *pixel* *space* *diffusion* (faster *training* *and* *inference* + *better* *consistency* + *more* *generalization*). The *latent* *diffusion* *paradigm* is the *direct* H2 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — SyncDreamer demonstrates that *latent* *diffusion* + *3D-aware* *attention* is the *right* *combination* for *single-image-to-3D* (the *CD* *0.0261* vs *Zero123* *0.0339* is the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *latent* *diffusion* + *3D-aware* *attention* > *independent* *single-view* *generation*).

**H3 (conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth is the H3 mechanism):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** SyncDreamer is *literally* a *multi-view* + *viewpoint-conditional* *model* — the *killer* H3 mechanism is the *synchronized multiview noise predictor* (multi-view conditioning via joint distribution `p_θ(x⁰⁽¹:ᴺ⁾|y)`) + the *3D-aware feature attention* (3D-aware attention via spatial feature volume + view frustum + depth-wise attention layers). The *3D-aware feature attention* is the *direct* H3 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 2 (crown generation) where the *16-view* *multi-view* *consistency* is the *exact* H3 conditioning signal (the *crown* is *viewed* from *16* *fixed* *views* and the *3D-aware* *attention* + *synchronized* *noise* *predictors* is the *H3* mechanism for *view*-*consistent* *crown* *generation*). The *killer* *clinical* *advantage*: SyncDreamer's *16-view* + *3D-aware* *attention* is *more* *practical* than *MVDream 130*'s *4-view* + *inflated-3D-attention* for *single-image-to-3D* where *16* *views* *gives* *more* *NeuS* *recon* *quality* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *16* *views* is the *sweet* *spot* for *single-image-to-3D* *NeuS* *recon*; *4* *views* is *insufficient* for *NeuS* to *converge*).

**H4 (implicit SDF / flexible representation > mesh):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** SyncDreamer uses *vanilla* *NeuS* (implicit SDF) for 3D recon — the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all *build upon*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *NeuS* is *open-source* + *fast* + *high-quality* + *the* *de facto* 2023 *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that v0 v0 v1 v2 can *use* *directly*. The *implicit* *SDF* *paradigm* is the *direct* H4 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — SyncDreamer demonstrates that *implicit* *SDF* *via* *NeuS* > *explicit* *mesh* *via* *Marching* *Cubes* *from* *point* *clouds* for *single-image-to-3D* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* is the *watertight* *mesh* *output* is *directly* *usable* for *3D* *printing* + *CAD* + *CFD*; the *de facto* 2023 *mesh* *extraction* *evolution* for *multi-view-diffusion* *3D* *output*).

**H5 (synthetic pretrain + dental fine-tune):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** SyncDreamer is *literally* *synthetic-only* *pretrain* (Objaverse 800K 3D models, rendered at 256² × 16 views) — the *canonical* 2023 *synthetic-only* *pretrain* *paradigm* that *inherits* *from* *Zero123* + *MVDream 130* + *SyncDreamer* + *CLIP-Mesh* + *Point-E*. The *synthetic-only* *pretrain* *is* *the* *direct* H5 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — SyncDreamer demonstrates that *Objaverse* *synthetic-only* *pretrain* + *Zero123* *fine-tune* + *3D-aware* *attention* *fine-tune* *generalizes* to *unseen* *object* *categories* including *cartoons* + *sketches* + *oil-paintings* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* is the *generalization* *ablation* in *Fig.* *6* — SyncDreamer *generalizes* to *2D* *designs* + *hand* *drawings*). The *direct* H5 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 is to *fine-tune* *SyncDreamer* on *dental* *data* to get a *dental-specific* *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *model* for *clinical* *crown* *generation* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage*: the *simple* *fine-tuning* *recipe* *preserves* *the* *multi-view* *prior* + *adds* *dental-specific* *knowledge*; v0 v0 v1 v2 can *fine-tune* *SyncDreamer* on *3DTeethSeg22* + *ToSynFCD* + *clinical* *dental* *arch* *data* for ~$200-500 Lambda (much cheaper than *from-scratch* *training*) and get a *dental-specific* *3D-aware* *multi-view-diffusion* *model* for *clinical* *crown* *generation*).

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The 3D-aware feature attention is the *key* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *trick** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is the *spatial* *feature* *volume* (32³ *vertices*) is *constructed* from *all* *target* *views* and *all* *target* *views* *share* the *same* *spatial* *volume* for *denoising* — *implies* a *global* *constraint* that *all* *target* *views* are *looking* at the *same* *object*; the *local* *epipolar* *constraint* is *enforced* by the *depth-wise* *attention* (the *canonical* *2023* *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *trick* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit). The *de facto* 2023 *3D-aware* *multi-view-attention* *paradigm* that *MVDream 130*'s *inflated-3D-self-attention* *cannot* *match* because *inflated* is *NOT* *3D-aware*.

2. **The depth-wise attention is the *killer* *practical* *design* choice** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *depth-wise* *attention* is *similar* to *epipolar* *attention* — if you *project* a *3D* *point* in the *view* *frustum* *onto* a *neighboring* *view*, you get a *2D* *sample* *point* on the *epipolar* *line*; the *depth-wise* *attention* is the *epipolar* *attention* *for* the *unknown* *geometry* *case*. The *killer* *empirical* *evidence* is the *ablation* in *Sec.* *4.4* — without *depth-wise* *attention* (use *2D* *conv* on *flattened* *volume*), the *images* are *degenerated* with *undesirable* *shape* *distortions*; the *de facto* 2023 *epipolar-attention* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit.

3. **The frozen-UNet + train-only-volume-module trick is the *killer* *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* *recipe*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *only* the *volume-conditioning* *module* is trained, *all* of the *Zero123* UNet weights are *frozen*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parameter-efficient* *fine-tuning* — the *volume-conditioning* *module* is *small* compared to the *full* UNet, so *fine-tuning* is *fast* + *stable* + *does-not-forget* the *Zero123* *3D* *prior*; the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* *training* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all inherit.

4. **The init-from-Zero123 > init-from-Stable-Diffusion ablation is *killer* empirical evidence** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *batch* *size* *plays* an *important* *role* in *enhancing* the *stability* and *efficacy* of *learning* *3D* *priors* from a *diverse* *dataset* like *Objaverse*; *SyncDreamer*'s *batch* *192* is *much* *smaller* than *Zero123*'s *batch* *1536* due to *GPU* *memory* *constraints*; *fine-tuning* on *Zero123* *enables* *SyncDreamer* to *utilize* the *3D* *priors* of *Zero123*; *init* from *Stable* *Diffusion* *exhibits* a *worse* *generalization* *ability* because the *batch* *is* *too* *small* to *learn* *3D* *priors* *from* *scratch*. The *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *initialization* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit.

5. **The 16 fixed views at elevation=30° is the *canonical* *single-image-to-3D* *multi-view* *camera* *setup*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *elevation=30°* is *chosen* because *most* *objects* in *Objaverse* are *viewed* from *a* *slight* *upward* *angle*; *16* *views* *evenly* *distributed* in *azimuth* *is* *enough* for *NeuS* *3D* *recon* *without* *being* *too* *expensive*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *multi-view* *camera* *setup* that *Wonder3D 118* extends to 6-view cross-domain + *Era3D 127* extends to 6-view 512² + *Wonder3D++ 129* extends to cascaded 3D mesh extraction.

6. **The vanilla NeuS 3D recon is *no* *SDS* *required*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is the *2D* *images* are *already* *multi-view-consistent*, so the *3D* *recon* is *just* a *standard* *neural* *surface* *reconstruction* problem; *NeuS* *2K* *steps* on *4096* *rays* × *128* *points* *per* *ray* takes *~10* *minutes* on *1* *A100*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *mesh-extraction* *pipeline* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all *build upon* — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* over *MVDream 130*'s *threestudio-SDS* *which* is *slow* + *unstable* + *requires* *per-prompt* *optimization*.

7. **The CarveKit foreground mask is *necessary* for *NeuS* *3D* *recon*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *NeuS* *needs* *foreground* *masks* to *separate* the *object* from the *background*; *CarveKit* (https://github.com/OPHoperHPO/image-background-remove-tool) is the *de facto* 2023 *foreground* *mask* *predictor*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *CarveKit* is *open-source* + *fast* + *high-quality*; the *de facto* 2023 *foreground* *mask* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all inherit.

8. **The 40s inference time for 64 images is the *killer* *practical* *advantage* for *chairside* *deployment*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is *40s* for *4* *instances* × *16* *views* is *fast* *enough* for *chairside* *deployment* where *speed* *matters*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *inference* *budget* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all *achieve*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* over *MVDream 130*'s *threestudio-SDS* *which* is *~30* *minutes* *per* *prompt*.

9. **The 18-page paper is *very* *detailed* (Sec. A.1-A.12) and *includes* *extensive* *ablation* *studies*** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is the *paper* *includes* *Sec.* *A.4* *Discussion* on *depth-wise* *attention* *layers* + *Sec.* *A.5* *Generating* *image* on *other* *viewpoints* + *Sec.* *A.6* *Iterative* *generation* + *Sec.* *A.7* *Novel-view* *renderings* of *NeuS* + *Sec.* *A.8* *Fewer* *generated* *views* for *NeuS* *training* + *Sec.* *A.9* *Faster* *reconstruction* with *hash-grid-based* *NeuS* + *Sec.* *A.10* *Metrics* *using* *different* *generation* *seeds* + *Sec.* *A.11* *Discussion* on *other* *attention* *mechanism* + *Sec.* *A.12* *Diagram* on *multiview* *diffusion*; the *de facto* 2023 *single-image-to-3D* *paper* *template* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all *follow*.

10. **The 3D-aware feature attention is similar to epipolar attention** — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is the *depth-wise* *attention* in *Sec.* *3.3* is *similar* to the *epipolar* *attention* *layers* in *Suhail* *et* *al.* *2022* + *Zhou* & *Tulsiani* *2023* + *Tseng* *et* *al.* *2023* + *Yu* *et* *al.* *2023b*; the *de facto* 2023 *epipolar-attention* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* all *inherit*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *epipolar* *consistency* — each *pixel*'s *features* are *constrained* by *epipolar* *correspondences* *across* *views*.
