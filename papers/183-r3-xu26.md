## TL;DR

A **3D-reconstruction paradigm-shift paper that drops the global-pose head and replaces it with a pairwise relative-pose head with **decoupled rotation/translation confidences** that serve as a *unified primitive* for (1) loss weighting, (2) pose aggregation, (3) keyframe-bank management, AND (4) outlier gating** — all from a single learned signal. The architecture is **DA3 backbone (frozen) + lightweight pairwise MLP pose head** (110M trainable of 372M total), trained on **6×48GB GPUs** for **40k iters total** (15k stage 1 + 25k stage 2). **Result on a single 48 GB GPU budget:** R³ beats every streaming baseline (Spann3R 177, CUT3R 175, Point3R 179, StreamVGGT 178, STream3R 181, TTT3R 182, ZipMap) on 7-Scenes (Acc 0.092 / Comp 0.093 / NC 0.755), 1000-frame 7-Scenes (Acc 0.022 / Comp 0.017 / NC 0.538 — 5.7× better than TTT3R 0.126/0.050/0.525), and DL3DV-Benchmark outdoor (ATE-norm 1.16% vs TTT3R 4.91% / InfiniteVGGT 2.85%, **wins 24/25 scenes**). The killer trick is the **same checkpoint serves both streaming (causal mask on, keyframe bank) AND full-context (causal mask off, optional PGO)** — a "test-time attention switch" with no retraining. **License: Apache-2.0 ✅** — *commercial-deployable* baseline for v0 sub-task 1's full-arch synthesis.

## Research Question + Their Answer

**Q:** Modern feed-forward 3D-reconstruction foundation models (DUSt3R, VGGT, π³, DA3) achieve impressive single-forward-pass depth + pose prediction, but they all share a **global coordinate frame assumption** — every camera is regressed in *one* canonical frame (first camera, learned frame, etc.). This is fine for short offline clips but **breaks for streaming reconstruction**: the model has to maintain an arbitrary temporal origin as new views arrive, and translation magnitudes grow unboundedly. The "global anchor" is also a bottleneck for long sequences, where downstream tasks need to express all cameras in one shared frame but the model has to *learn* the coordinate choice. π³ (Wang 2026) takes a step forward by replacing fixed-origin absolute-pose loss with all-pair relative-pose loss, but the *output* is still a set of global poses in one frame. **How can we get feed-forward 3D reconstruction that works in *both* streaming (bounded memory, real-time) and offline (full-context) settings, without committing to a global frame?**

**A:** Reformulate feed-forward 3D reconstruction as **pairwise relative-pose regression with learned decoupled confidences**:

1. **Drop the global-pose head entirely.** A single lightweight MLP `MLP_rel([z_i; z_j])` takes *any* pair of camera tokens `(z_i, z_j)` from the frozen DA3 backbone and outputs the relative pose `(q̂_{i→j}, t̂_{i→j})` from frame i to frame j, plus *two* scalar confidences `(c^R_{i→j}, c^T_{i→j})` — one for rotation, one for translation. This produces a **fully-connected directed pose graph** (Fig. 2c) where each edge (i, j) carries its own local pose and reliability estimate.
2. **Decoupled confidences for asymmetric failure modes.** Rotation and translation have *different* failure modes — rotation is often well-constrained even when translation is ambiguous (textureless region, baseline degeneracy). Predicting *two* separate confidences (instead of one shared confidence) lets the model express this asymmetry and lets each downstream consumer (loss weight, aggregation weight, keyframe utility) read the right component's reliability.
3. **Confidence-weighted L1 + −log c loss.** The camera loss is `L_rot(i,j) = c^R_{i→j} · ℓ^L1_rot(i,j) − α·log c^R_{i→j}` with α=0.2. The two parts work in tension: a large residual makes `c·ℓ` costly (optimizer shrinks c on inaccurate pairs), but the `−log c` regularizer prevents c from collapsing to zero. **Empirical validation (Fig. 8):** learned confidences are monotone in error — higher predicted confidence ↔ lower mean error, the property needed for all downstream uses.
4. **Confidence-weighted pose aggregation in the active context.** For each new frame j, every reference frame i in the active context `C_t` proposes a candidate pose `T_j^(i) = T_i · (q̂_{i→j}, t̂_{i→j})`. The final pose is **softmax-weighted fusion** of the top-K candidates ranked by `c̄_{i→j} = ½(c^R + c^T)`, with *separate* softmax for rotation and translation. (The all-reference fusion is the upper bound; top-K is the efficiency cap.)
5. **Active context = {frame 1} ∪ keyframe bank B_t.** A bounded keyframe bank with **token-novelty admission** (`max_{i∈B_t} cos(tok_i, tok_j) < τ`, with τ=0.98) and **utility-based eviction** (`u_j = d_j · c_j`, where d_j = min cosine distance to nearest other bank entry, c_j = max pair confidence). When the bank fills (M_max=100), the entry with lowest utility is evicted (frame 1 excluded). This mirrors classical keyframe selection (PTAM, ORB-SLAM, DSO) but replaces hand-tuned motion/covisibility with *learned*-feature similarity and *learned* pose-head confidence.
6. **Train/inference length gap bridged by novelty gate.** Train on 4-32 frames, deploy on 1000+ frames. The novelty gate (τ=0.98) keeps the effective context length close to the training regime even as wall-clock length grows. A force-admit every Δ_max=20 frames prevents staleness. A confidence-based outlier gate (rejection if `c̄_j < τ_out · c̄_ref` with τ_out=0.15) prevents transient failures (motion blur, occlusions, scene cuts) from polluting the bank; N_rej=3 consecutive rejections triggers a **segment reset** (clear KV cache + bank, re-anchor via 3-10 frame bridge, scale via median-aligned DA3 depth).
7. **Single checkpoint, two inference modes.** The same checkpoint runs in (a) **streaming mode** (frame-causal attention, active context) and (b) **full-context mode** (remove causal mask at test time, run lightweight **confidence-weighted pose-graph optimization** over all N² edges via L-BFGS with Huber loss, **no** bundle adjustment, **no** point reprojection, **no** depth optimization). The full-context switch improves the same checkpoint's pose + depth numbers further (Tab. 2, Tab. 3, Tab. 8).

**The critical insight is: the *learned per-edge confidence* is the **unified primitive** that ties together loss weighting, aggregation, keyframe management, and outlier rejection. R³ is "architecturally orthogonal" (their word, Sec. 1) to all prior streaming 3R — no recurrent state, no TTT modules, no extra transformer — yet it matches or surpasses every 1B-class baseline on the standard benchmarks.**

## Method (Architecture, Training, Data)

### Architecture: DA3 backbone + lightweight pairwise MLP head

```
frozen DA3-Large backbone (DINOv2-pretrained, 385M params, with 110M trainable)
    ↓ per-frame camera token z_i ∈ ℝ^d
pairwise pose head MLP_rel([z_i; z_j])  →  (q̂_{i→j}, t̂_{i→j}, c^R_{i→j}, c^T_{i→j})   [shared MLP, all pairs]
focal-length head MLP_focal(z_i)         →  f̂_i   [per-frame, plain L1 supervised]
depth head (from DA3 teacher)            →  D_i, Σ_p  [per-pixel, median-normalized, confidence-weighted]
```

- **Backbone:** DA3-Large (Lin et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.10647) — the 2025 DINOv2-pretrained depth foundation model. Most of it is *frozen*; only the global attention layers + the new pairwise pose head are updated (~110M trainable of 372M total inference params). The DA3-Large teacher is also used for real-world depth supervision (Sec. 3.4, Eq. 12-13).
- **Pairwise pose head (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 1):** Single shared MLP on top of frozen camera tokens. Output: relative rotation as unit quaternion, relative translation, *decoupled* rotation and translation confidences (softplus for positivity). Focal-length head: per-frame MLP → scalar f̂_i (plain L1 supervised).
- **Memory:** 372M params, 20+ FPS on a single NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000, 6×48GB training GPUs.

### Three pose paradigms (Fig. 2, Tab. 1) — the conceptual contribution

R³ explicitly contrasts three pose-regression paradigms:

| Paradigm | World frame | Supervised edges | Edge weights |
|----------|-------------|------------------|--------------|
| **(a) VGGT** | First camera (fixed) | (1, j) for j=2..N | uniform |
| **(b) π³** | Model-chosen (no fixed anchor) | All unordered (i, j) | uniform |
| **(c) R³** | None (downstream assembly) | All *directed* (i, j), i≠j | **learned per-edge R+T confidences** |

The killer move: (c) drops the global-pose head entirely, so the network *never* has to commit to a coordinate choice. Absolute poses are an *inference-time aggregation* problem (Sec. 3.2), not a *learning-time regression* problem. This is the same "local learning, global assembly" idea as classical SfM pose-graph optimization (PTAM, ORB-SLAM) but with *learned* edge weights and *end-to-end* training.

### Streaming inference (Sec. 3.3.1)

- **Active context C_t = {1} ∪ B_t** (frame 1 = trajectory origin, B_t = keyframe bank).
- **Pairwise head runs only on edges between incoming frame j and frames in C_t** (not full history).
- **Aggregation:** For each i ∈ C_t, candidate pose `T_j^(i) = T_i · (q̂_{i→j}, t̂_{i→j})`. Fuse top-K via softmax-weighted (separate R/T weights from `c^R, c^T`) average; quaternion sign-align before averaging.
- **Keyframe admission (Eq. 8):** Frame j admitted to B_t only if `max_{i∈B_t} cos(tok_i, tok_j) < τ` with τ=0.98 (where `tok_j` is the average of j's pre-attention backbone tokens). This uses the *pretrained* DA3 encoder as a feature similarity signal — a learned geometric/appearance redundancy test.
- **Culling (Eq. 9):** When B_t hits M_max=100, evict lowest-utility `u_j = d_j · c_j` (d_j = min cosine distance to nearest other entry, c_j = max pair confidence to rest of bank). Frame 1 is excluded from eviction.
- **Train/inference length gap bridge:** train on 4-32 frames, deploy on 1000+ frames. Strict novelty threshold (τ=0.98) keeps the effective context length close to training regime. Force-admit every Δ_max=20 frames prevents staleness.
- **Outlier rejection (App. C):** Compute `c̄_j = mean_{i∈C_t} c̄_{i→j}`. Calibrate baseline `c̄_ref` from first N_cal=3 frames. Reject frame j if `c̄_j < τ_out · c̄_ref` with τ_out=0.15. On rejection: invalidate KV cache, skip bank admission, no pose estimated. After N_rej=3 consecutive rejections, trigger segment reset.
- **Segment reset (App. C):** Clear DA3 cache + keyframe bank. Rerun backbone on 3-10 frame bridge between segments. Global pose continuity via composition (no Sim(3) / SE(3) registration across segments). Scale drift anchored by aligning first-frame DA3 depth to a frozen pretrained metric-depth model via single median-based scalar.

### Full-context inference (Sec. 3.3.2)

- **Same checkpoint, causal mask off at test time.** Backbone sees all frames at once, pairwise head evaluated on every (i, j) with i≠j.
- **Initialize trajectory with a causal streaming pass, then run one confidence-weighted pose-graph optimization (PGO) over the full edge set.** L-BFGS, Huber loss, no bundle adjustment, no point reprojection, no depth optimization. Edges carry only relative-pose residuals + scalar confidences.
- **Cost:** negligible relative to backbone forward pass.

### Training objective (Sec. 3.4)

- **Camera loss (Eq. 9-10):** `L_cam = (1/|P|) Σ_{(i,j)∈P} [c^R · ℓ^L1_rot − α log c^R + c^T · ℓ^L1_trans − α log c^T]` with α=0.2. For causal, P = past-to-current lower-triangular; for full-context, P = all pairs (i, j) with i≠j. The two parts work in tension: large residual → c·ℓ costly → optimizer shrinks c on inaccurate pairs; −log c regularizer prevents c→0.
- **Depth loss (Eq. 11):** Median-normalized depth `D̃ = D / median(D)`, supervised by GT (synthetic) or frozen DA3 teacher (real). L_depth = (1/|M|) Σ_{p∈M} [Σ_p · |D̃(p) − D̃*(p)| − α log Σ_p] with per-pixel confidence Σ_p. Mask M uses GT validity (synthetic) or DA3-teacher confidence (real).
- **Focal length:** Plain L1 on per-frame scalar f̂_i.

### Training schedule (App. F)

- **Stage 1 (15k iters):** Adapt DA3-Large backbone into frame-causal absolute-pose checkpoint. Constant LR=1e-4, dynamic batch 4-16 views.
- **Stage 2 (25k iters):** Train relative-pose head on top of Stage 1 checkpoint. Cosine LR 1e-4 → 1e-5, gradient accumulation 2, dynamic batch 4-16 (4-32 in second half).
- **Mixed precision:** bfloat16, gradient checkpointing, FlexAttention for frame-causal attention.
- **Per-GPU batch size:** 200 views (no DA3 teacher) or 96 views (with DA3 teacher) on a 48GB GPU.

### Training data (App. F, Tab. 10)

**15 public datasets** (70K+ sequences total):
- **Synthetic (5):** AriaSyntheticENV 2K, HyperSim 344, MVS-Synth 121, OmniWorld-Game 484, Spring 47, TartanAir 355, Virtual KITTI 2 50, Dynamic Replica 524
- **LiDAR/RGB-D (4):** ARKitScenes 4.4K, ScanNet 1.2K, ScanNet++ 230, WildRGBD 23K
- **COLMAP (3):** CO3Dv2 30.6K, DL3DV 6.4K, MapFree 921

The "Largest 2" are **WildRGBD 23K** and **CO3Dv2 30.6K** (in # of sequences). This is the **most-comprehensive 3D-reconstruction training mix in the 175-183 streaming-3R arc** — Spann3R 177 + CUT3R 175 + Point3R 179 + Ray-Aware 180 + STream3R 181 + TTT3R 182 + R³ 183 each use a smaller subset.

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (partial support — 2-stage indirect, NOT direct):** R³'s confidence-weighted aggregation + optional PGO refinement is a **2-stage pipeline** (relative prediction + assembly), but *not* the VAE+DDM 2-stage that H1 originally framed. The H1 2-stage here is **inference-time** (pose aggregation, keyframe bank, PGO refinement) rather than **learning-time** (VAE encoder + DDM decoder). R³ is *deterministic feed-forward* — no latent bottleneck, no diffusion, no variational sampling. The PGO refinement at end-of-sequence is the "step 2" of H1's 2-stage paradigm, but the step 1 is *feed-forward MLP*, not VAE. **H1 is refuted at the architecture level** but **supported at the inference level**.

**H2 (mild contradiction — deterministic feed-forward with learned confidences):** R³ is a *pure* deterministic feed-forward model — **no diffusion, no flow-matching, no VAE, no probabilistic bottleneck**. Yet it *exceeds* every 1B-class deterministic feed-forward baseline (VGGT, DA3, π³) and every diffusion-based 3R model (none in the 3R arc). The learned confidences are the *killer* mechanism — they are *learned reliability estimates* (Fig. 8: monotone in error, both for R and T separately) that *substitute* for probabilistic modeling. The conclusion: **a learned confidence is often *better* than a learned distribution for 3D-reconstruction tasks** — the same insight as LightGlue / SuperGlue matching (learned confidence > learned match probability) and the *direct* contradiction of H2's "latent diffusion > deterministic" claim. For v0, **adopt R³'s learned confidence as a *substitute* for H2's probabilistic modeling**.

**H3 (strongest direct support — H3 paradigm-establishing paper):** R³ is the **founding paper of the "confidence-weighted pairwise conditioning" H3 paradigm**. The three H3 design lessons:
- **Lesson 1:** Conditioning should be at the *pair level* (i, j) for relative regression, not at the *frame level* (single absolute pose). This is the *direct* alternative to Hwang 061's pixel-level opposing-jaw + gap-distance conditioning, DMC 033's 6-tooth context conditioning, and MADCrowner's margin segmentation. For v0, **replace absolute (depth, pose) regression with pairwise (relative) regression at the *tooth* level** for sub-task 2 (crown generation).
- **Lesson 2:** **Decouple rotation and translation confidences** — R and T have different failure modes. For v0 sub-task 2, the "shape" of the crown (rotation-equivariant, i.e., should be invariant to tooth rotation) and the "position" of the crown (translation-equivariant, i.e., should be position-aware) should have *separate* confidence estimates. **Same lesson applies to v0 sub-task 1 (full-arch synthesis): separate "arch-shape" confidence from "tooth-position" confidence**.
- **Lesson 3:** **Confidence as a unified primitive** — the *same* learned confidence that weights the loss also aggregates the poses, manages the keyframe bank, AND gates the outliers. For v0, **use a single learned confidence signal to (a) weight the clinical-fit loss, (b) reject implausible crown shapes, (c) select high-quality training pairs, (d) anchor patient-specific fine-tuning**. The killer "do-everything" mechanism.

**H4 (mild contradiction — pointmap not SDF):** R³ outputs (depth, pose) from the DA3 backbone — *pointmaps* in the global frame, **not SDF**. The DA3 "depth-ray target" is the *unified* depth + ray output (pointmap = depth × ray direction), not an implicit field. The depth head is supervised with median-normalized L1 + confidence, and the rendering for downstream tasks uses pointmap back-projection, **not** sphere-tracing of an SDF. R³ is the *latest* in a long line of 3R models (DUSt3R, MASt3R, VGGT, π³, DA3) that all use *pointmap* (or depth + pose) representations, *not* SDF. The H4 lesson: **SDF is the *right* substrate for *closed* 3D (object-level, watertight) but *not* for *open* 3D (scene-level, multi-view reconstruction)**. For v0 sub-task 2 (crown generation), *SDF* is still the right substrate (closed, watertight mesh output, FlexiCubes per DMC 033). For v0 sub-task 1 (full-arch synthesis), *pointmap* (DMC's indicator-grid + Marching Cubes) is the *right* substrate (open, multi-view, no closed surface). **H4 is supported for sub-task 2 and refuted for sub-task 1.**

**H5 (mild support — train/inference length gap bridge, NOT synthetic+finetune):** R³ is trained on 70K+ sequences from 15 public datasets (largest 3R training mix in the 175-183 arc) but **does NOT use synthetic + finetune** in the H5 sense (Hwang 061: "train on synthetic crowns, finetune on patient data"). Instead, R³ uses a **train/inference length gap bridge** mechanism: train on 4-32 frames, deploy on 1000+ frames, with the **novelty gate** (τ=0.98) keeping the effective context close to training regime. The "synthetic pretraining + clinical finetune" pattern of H5 is the *alternative* design — neither approach is *better*, they are *different*:
- **R³ approach:** Large real-data pretraining + test-time attention switch + keyframe bank. **Generalizes across scenes and tasks** (offline + streaming, indoor + outdoor).
- **H5 approach:** Small synthetic pretraining + per-patient finetuning. **Specializes to clinical cases**.

For v0, **R³'s approach is better for sub-task 1 (full-arch synthesis, cross-patient generalization)** and **H5's approach is better for sub-task 2 (crown generation, patient-specific)**. The two are *complementary*, not contradictory.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The "unified primitive" insight is the killer design lesson.** A *single* learned confidence (decoupled into R and T) serves 4 distinct roles: loss weight, aggregation weight, keyframe utility, outlier gate. This is the *opposite* of the 2024-2025 3R arc, where each role gets its *own* mechanism (StreamVGGT's KV-cache, TTT3R's per-token β, Ray-Aware 180's ray-direction + retain-or-replace, InfiniteVGGT's window attention). R³ proves that **one well-designed primitive is better than four specialized ones** for 3R.

2. **Decoupled R/T confidences are empirically validated as pair-reliability estimators (Fig. 8).** Across all pairs from 20 ScanNet test scenes, the learned confidence is *monotone in error* — higher confidence → lower mean error, tighter spread. The R and T confidences are in *different numerical ranges* (rotation confidence concentrates higher, translation lower), *exactly* the asymmetry the model needs to express. The "asymmetry" is the *justification* for predicting 2 scalars instead of 1.

3. **Train/inference length gap is bridged by token-novelty gate, not architectural changes.** Train 4-32 frames, deploy 1000+ frames, with *no* architectural change, *no* TTT, *no* recurrent state, *no* extra transformer. The strict novelty threshold (τ=0.98) keeps the effective context length close to the training regime. The force-admit (Δ_max=20) prevents staleness. This is the *opposite* of TTT3R 182's "reinterpret state update as TTT" and InfiniteVGGT's "window attention + reset" — R³ does it with a *simple threshold* on *pretrained* features.

4. **Segment reset uses depth-median anchoring, not Sim(3).** Each segment's first frame's depth is aligned to a frozen pretrained metric-depth model via a single median-based scalar. This avoids the "joint rotation, translation, and scale drift" of Sim(3)-style window fusion. The killer simplicity: **one scalar per segment, no online registration, no per-frame scale estimation**.

5. **Optional PGO refinement is L-BFGS, not bundle adjustment.** The full-context switch runs **one** confidence-weighted pose-graph optimization over the full N² edge set via L-BFGS with Huber loss. No bundle adjustment, no point reprojection, no depth optimization. The cost is *negligible* relative to the backbone forward pass. The 2024-2025 3R arc has converged on this "cheap PGO" pattern as the end-of-sequence cleanup (Spann3R 177's global BA, TTT3R 182's optional PGO, R³ 183's PGO). The lesson: **PGO is the new bundle adjustment for feed-forward 3R**.

6. **Two-stage training is *not* the H1 2-stage.** Stage 1 adapts the DA3-Large backbone to a *frame-causal absolute-pose* checkpoint (the *standard* feed-forward setup). Stage 2 *trains the relative-pose head* on top of the Stage 1 backbone. The "2-stage" is *training-stage*, not *model-stage*. The Stage 1 backbone is a *teacher* for Stage 2; Stage 2's relative head is *frozen-friendly* (the frozen Stage 1 backbone provides the camera tokens z_i). The killer design: **Stage 1 is a "warm-up" for the causal attention mask, Stage 2 is the actual training of the relative-pose paradigm**. The Stage 1 backbone is *not used at inference* — the *full DA3-Large* is used at inference.

7. **Robustness validation is on the *Robust-VGGT* protocol, not the standard streaming protocol.** R³ achieves SR=1.000 on ETH3D and SR=0.986 on RobustNeRF, vs RobustVGGT-F's 0.985 / 0.586. The balanced filtering score (0.84-0.93 ETH3D, 0.99+ RobustNeRF) shows the high distractor rejection is *not* a degenerate "reject everything" strategy. The key design: the *same* confidence used for pose aggregation also gates outliers — *no separate rejection head*.

8. **Apache-2.0 license + 372M params + 6×48GB training = the *practical* 3R baseline for v0 sub-task 1.** R³ is *significantly* smaller (372M vs 1.26B for VGGT/DA3) and trained on *substantially* fewer resources (6 GPUs vs 8-32 for VGGT/DA3) yet matches or surpasses them. The Apache-2.0 license + 190 ⭐ community reception makes R³ the *v0-deployable* 3R baseline.
