## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D content generation* from *text* or *a single image* is a *long-standing challenge* with *wide* *applications* (game design, digital arts, human avatars, virtual reality) — *highly ill-posed* because it requires *reasoning the unseen parts* of *any* *object* in *3D* *space* from *single-view* or *textual* *descriptions*. *Prior* 3D-generation approaches fall into 3 categories, *all* *with* *limitations*: **(1) Native 3D methods** (Nichol 2022 Point-E, Jun 2023 Shap-E, Cao 2024 CLAY/Tripo, He 2024 GVGEN, Zhang 2024 GaussianCube — train conditional 3D generative networks *directly* on datasets of *various 3D representations* (voxel, point cloud, implicit function, triplane, 3DGS)) — *limited* *by* *3D* *data* *scale* (max 265K 3D objects, vs 5B+ *images*), *can't* *leverage* *2D* *priors* from *abundant* *pre-trained* *models*, *slow* *to* *train* (DMV3D needs 128 A100 × 7 days); **(2) Rendering-based methods** (RenderDiffusion 2023, HoloDiffusion 2023b, HoloFusion 2023a, Viewset Diffusion 2023, GIBR 2024, DMV3D 2024 — train with *only 2D supervision* via *differentiable rendering*, denoise images rendered from a *corrupted* *implicit* *3D* *representation*) — *unstable* *training* + *same* *drawback* (no 2D *generative* *prior*); **(3) Reconstruction-based methods** (LRM 2024b, Instant3D 2024a, LGM 2024, GRM 2024c, CRM 2024, InstantMesh 2024b, SF3D 2024, MeshFormer, Meta 3D AssetGen — *reconstruct* *implicit* *3D* *representations* from *generated* *multi-view* *images* using *frozen* *pre-trained* *2D* *diffusion* *models* + *generalizable* *3D* *reconstruction* *models*) — *regard* the *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* as a *plug-and-play* *module* (2-stage, *may* *collapse* *due* to *3D* *inconsistency* in *generated* *images*). **Can we *design* a *unified* 3D generation framework that *natively* *generates* 3DGS by *taming* *pre-trained* *2D* *image* *diffusion* *models*, *effectively* *utilizing* *web-scale* 2D priors while *maintaining* 3D consistency, with *seamless* *adaptation* of *all* *image* *techniques* (ControlNet, IP-Adapter, LoRA, …) to 3D?**

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *introduce* *DiffSplat*, *a* *novel* 3D *generative* *framework* *that* *natively* *generates* 3D *Gaussian* *splats* *by* *taming* *large-scale* *text-to-image* *diffusion* *models*. *It* *differs* *from* *previous* 3D *generative* *models* *by* *effectively* *utilizing* *web-scale* 2D *priors* *while* *maintaining* 3D *consistency* *in* *a* *unified* *model*. *To* *bootstrap* *the* *training*, *a* *lightweight* *reconstruction* *model* *is* *proposed* *to* *instantly* *produce* *multi-view* *Gaussian* *splat* *grids* *for* *scalable* *dataset* *curation*. *In* *conjunction* *with* *the* *regular* *diffusion* *loss* *on* *these* *grids*, *a* 3D *rendering* *loss* *is* *introduced* *to* *facilitate* 3D *coherence* *across* *arbitrary* *views*. *The* *compatibility* *with* *image* *diffusion* *models* *enables* *seamless* *adaptions* *of* *numerous* *techniques* *for* *image* *generation* *to* *the* 3D *realm*. *Extensive* *experiments* *reveal* *the* *superiority* *of* *DiffSplat* *in* *text-* *and* *image-conditioned* *generation* *tasks* *and* *downstream* *applications*. *Thorough* *ablation* *studies* *validate* *the* *efficacy* *of* *each* *critical* *design* *choice* *and* *provide* *insights* *into* *the* *underlying* *mechanism*.

The *key* *insight* is the ***"splat latent" framing***: a *structured 2D grid representation* of *3DGS properties* (RGB + opacity + scale + rotation + coordinate + normal) that is *rendered* into a *"special-style image"* amenable to *pre-trained 2D diffusion priors* (the *killer* *practical* *insight*: image diffusion models *trained* on *web-scale* *images* *already* *implicitly* *understand* *3D* *geometry* via *depth* *estimation* + *coordinate* *regression* + *normal* *estimation* (Stan 2023, Ke 2024, Fu 2024, Long 2024, Li 2024b, Xu 2024a) — *treating* *splat* *latents* as *images* *of* *a* *special* *style* *enables* *fine-tuning* *for* *3D* *generation*). The *core* *innovation* is the ***3D rendering loss*** — the *only* *new* *training* *signal* *added* to the *standard* *diffusion* *loss* is L_render(D_φ_d(F_ψ(z̃, t))) (Eq. 6), where *denoised* *splat* *latents* are *decoded* *back* to *3DGS* *properties* and *rendered* from *random* *V* *viewpoints* with *supervision* from *ground-truth* *multi-view* *images* (the *killer* *H2* *mechanism*: the *2D* *diffusion* *model* *learns* 3D consistency *implicitly* via the *rendering* *loss* *gradient*). The *training* *progression* is *3-stage* *decoupled* (the *direct* *H1* *mechanism*): **(1) GSRecon** *learns* to *regress* *per-pixel* *3DGS* from *4-view* (RGB + normal + coordinate) *context* in <0.1s; **(2) GSVAE** *fine-tunes* the *pre-trained* *image* *VAE* to *encode* *structured* *3DGS* *grids* into *splat* *latents* that *share* the *SD* *latent* *shape*; **(3) DiffSplat** *fine-tunes* the *pre-trained* *text-to-image* *diffusion* *UNet* on the *splat* *latents* with *dual* *diffusion* + *rendering* *loss*. The *comparison* to the *alternative* *3D-generation* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *Native 3D* (Point-E, Shap-E, CLAY, GVGEN, GaussianCube): *3D-consistent* but *limited* *by* *3D* *data* *scale* + *can't* *leverage* *2D* *priors* + *slow* *to* *train*.
- *Rendering-based* (HoloDiffusion, Viewset Diffusion, DMV3D): *2D-supervised* but *unstable* + *no* *2D* *generative* *prior*.
- *Reconstruction-based* (LRM, Instant3D, LGM, GRM, CRM, InstantMesh, SF3D, MeshFormer): *2D-prior-utilized* but *2-stage* *pluggable* + *collapses* *on* *multi-view* *inconsistency*.
- *DiffSplat*: the *first* to *unify* *all* *three* (1) *2D* *web-scale* *priors* (via *tame* *pre-trained* *SD* *VAE* + *UNet*), (2) *3D* *consistency* (via *3D* *rendering* *loss* *alongside* *diffusion* *loss*), and (3) *seamless* *adaptation* of *all* *image* *techniques* (ControlNet, IP-Adapter, LoRA, CFG, …) to *3D*.

## Method (architecture, training, data)

**Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 2):** 3 stages, *all* *decoupled* and *trained* *sequentially*:
- **Stage 1 — GSRecon (Structured Splat Reconstruction, Sec. 3.1, 42M params):** a *small* *lightweight* *encoder-decoder* F_θ: given *V_in* posed multi-view images in ℝ^{V_in×3×H×W} (4 views by default: RGB + normal + coordinate + camera), outputs *structured* *multi-view* *3DGS* *grids* (one Gaussian per pixel per view, with *per-pixel* *3DGS* *properties*: mean μ ∈ ℝ³ from back-projection, opacity α ∈ [0,1] from confidence, covariance Σ ∈ ℝ³ˣ³ from local PCA of nearby depths, color c ∈ ℝ³⁽ˣ⁺¹⁾² with spherical harmonics of order S, *plus* the *killer* *innovation*: a *per-Gaussian* *3-channel* *RGB* *grid* that is *rendered* into the *SD* *latent* *space* for *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow*). *Inspired* by *pixelSplat* (Charatan et al. CVPR 2024) and *Splatter Image* (Szymanowicz et al. CVPR 2024), the *generalizable* *3DGS* *reconstruction* *paradigm*. Trained with *rendering* *loss* (Eq. 1): L_render(G) ≔ (1/V) Σ_{v=1}^V [L_MSE(I_v, I_v^GT) + λ_p · L_LPIPS(I_v, I_v^GT) + λ_α · L_MSE(M_v, M_v^GT)] (L2 + LPIPS on RGB + L2 on mask, the *standard* *3DGS* *training* *recipe*). <0.1s per object for *scalable* *3D* *dataset* *curation* (the *killer* *practical* *contribution* of GSRecon: *2-3* *orders* *of* *magnitude* *faster* than *per-instance* *3DGS* *optimization* which takes 10-30 min/object).
- **Stage 2 — GSVAE (Splat Latents, Sec. 3.2, 84M params):** *fine-tunes* the *pre-trained* *image* *VAE* (SD1.5 / SDXL fp16-fix / SD3) to *encode* the *structured* *3DGS* *grids* (concatenated per view into a *multi-channel* *image-like* *tensor* of shape C×H×W, where C = #GS-properties × V_in = 30 channels for 5 channels × 6 views) into *splat* *latents* z ∈ ℝ^{h×w×d} (e.g., SD1.5: 4×64×64 = 16384-dim, SD3: 16×64×64 = 65536-dim) that *share* the *same* *shape* as the *SD* *latent* *space*. The *killer* *insight*: the *fine-tuned* *VAE* *decoder* *maps* *latents* *back* *to* *structured* *3DGS* *grids* (the *inverse* of the *encoder*), enabling *direct* *fine-tuning* of the *pre-trained* *diffusion* *UNet* on the *splat* *latents* with *standard* *image* *diffusion* *recipes*. Trained with *dual* *objective*: L_VAE = L_recon (L2 + LPIPS + SSIM between *decoded* *3DGS* *rendering* and *ground-truth* *image*) + *KL* *divergence* *regularization* (standard VAE regularization) + *the* *critical* *rendering* *loss* L_render (Eq. 1, on the *decoded* *3DGS* *rendering* vs *ground-truth* *image*). The *killer* *ablation* (Table 4): freezing the VAE / encoder gives *catastrophic* 8.64/0.566 PSNR/LPIPS (the *pre-trained* *image* *VAE* *cannot* *represent* *3DGS* *properties*), w/o L_render gives 26.07/0.080 (loses *real-dataset* *supervision*), *full* *fine-tuning* with L_render gives 30.33/0.033 (the *killer* *practical* *insight*: L_render during auto-encoding *enables* *real-dataset* *supervision* not just *reconstruction-model* *supervision*).
- **Stage 3 — DiffSplat (Generative Model, Sec. 3.3, 0.61B-2.24B params):** *fine-tunes* the *pre-trained* *text-to-image* *diffusion* *UNet* (SD1.5 0.86B / SDXL 2.6B / PixArt-α 0.61B / PixArt-Σ 0.61B / SD3-medium 2.0B / SD3.5-medium 2.0B) on the *splat* *latents*. *Architecture* *modifications* are *minimal*: only *new* *parameters* are *zero-initialized* *new* *columns* in the *input* *convolution* *weights* for *Plücker* *embeddings* (6D camera pose encoding, the *standard* *camera* *conditioning* *trick* for *multi-view* *diffusion*). The *diffusion* *process* is *standard*: add Gaussian noise ε ~ N(0, 1) to *splat* *latents* z at *random* *timestep* t, *denoise* with F_ψ(z̃, t), optimize *standard* *diffusion* *loss* L_diff = ω(t) · ‖F_ψ(z̃, t) - z‖²₂ (Eq. 5, the *v-prediction* *or* *ε-prediction* *objective* from the *pre-trained* *SD* *UNet*). The *killer* *innovation* is the *additional* *3D* *rendering* *loss* (Eq. 6): L_DiffSplat = λ_diff · L_diff + λ_render · ω_r(t) · L_render(D_φ_d(F_ψ(z̃, t))), where *denoised* *splat* *latents* are *decoded* *back* into *3DGS* *properties* via the *fine-tuned* *VAE* *decoder* D_φ_d, *rendered* from *random* *V* *viewpoints* via *3DGS* *splatting*, and *compared* *against* *ground-truth* *multi-view* *images* via L_render. The *hybrid* *2D-diffusion* + *3D-rendering* *loss* *enables* *3D-consistent* *generation* in a *purely* *2D* *diffusion* *model* (the *killer* *H2* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1*). *ω_r(t)* *anneals* the *rendering* *loss* *weight* at *different* *noise* *levels* (similar to Min-SNR weighting from Hang et al. 2023).
- **Conditioning (Sec. 3.3.1):** *Text* (CLIP for SD1.5, CLIP+T5 for SD3) + *optional* *single-view* *image* (encoded by *fine-tuned* *VAE*, concatenated to *splat* *latents* along the *view* *dimension* via *view-concat* or *spatial-concat*; 2 ablation choices; *view-concat* is preferred for *flexibility* in *accommodating* *varying* *#viewpoints* and *conditioning*; *spatial-concat* is preferred for *dense* *attention* but *requires* *additional* *padding*). The *killer* *practical* *contribution*: *inherits* *all* *SD* *UNet* *tricks* (CFG, DDIM/DPMSolver/SDE-DPMSolver, ControlNet, IP-Adapter, LoRA, …) *for* *free* — *seamless* *adaptation* of *all* *image* *techniques* to 3D (the *killer* *H3* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1*).

**Training (Appendix A):**
- *Data*: G-Objaverse (Qiu et al. 2024, *265K* *3D* *objects* × *38* *views* = *10.07M* *multi-view* *images*, the *high-quality* *Objaverse* *subset*; *83K* *filtered* *subset* for DiffSplat training, the *LGM* *filtering* *recipe*). *Captions* from *Cap3D* (Luo et al. 2023+2024, refined by *DiffuRank*).
- *Compute*: *8* *A100* *GPUs* × *2-5* *days* = *16-40* *A100-hours* = ~$200-500 Lambda per training run.
- *Hyperparameters*: AdamW, lr 1e-5 (UNet fine-tuning), batch 32-128, training 50-100K steps.
- *Loss* *weights*: λ_diff = 1.0, λ_render = 1.0 (default, the *killer* *insight* from Table 5: *both* *losses* *are* *equally* *important*).
- *Inference*: 1-2s per object on A100, 50 DDIM steps, CFG 4.0 (default for text), CFG 2.0 (image-conditioned).

**Data (Sec. 4.1):**
- *Training*: G-Objaverse 265K (for GSRecon, GSVAE) + 83K filtered (for DiffSplat) + Cap3D captions.
- *Evaluation*: T3Bench (He et al. 2023, *300* *text* *prompts* across *3* *complexity* *levels*: *Single* *Object*, *Single* *Object* *w/* *Surroundings*, *Multiple* *Objects*) for *text-conditioned* + *GSO* (Downs et al. ICRA 2022, *300* *unseen* *objects*) for *image-conditioned*.
- *Metrics*: *CLIP* *Similarity* (ViT-B/32, *prompt*–*image* *alignment*), *CLIP* *R-Precision* (ViT-B/32, *retrieval* *accuracy* via *cosine* *similarity* ranking), *ImageReward* (Xu et al. 2023, *human* *aesthetic* *preference*) for *text*; *PSNR* + *SSIM* + *LPIPS* (3D-aware, *averaged* *across* *viewpoints*) for *image*.

## Connections to H1-H5 (specific)

**H1 (2-stage generative-refinement wins ONLY with end-to-end gradient flow):**
- **H1 STRONG PARTIAL CONFIRMATION** (the *inverse* framing of MVSplat360's H1): DiffSplat's 3-stage pipeline (GSRecon + GSVAE + DiffSplat) is *structurally* 1-stage (the *end-to-end* *trained* *diffusion* *UNet* + *3D* *rendering* *loss* *backprop*), but *practically* 3-stage (the *decoupled* *training* of *GSRecon* → *GSVAE* → *DiffSplat*). The *killer* *insight*: H1 *requires* *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* (MVSplat360's *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* is the *3DGS* *equivalent* of DiffSplat's *rendering* *loss* — *both* *bridging* *losses* enable *end-to-end* *trainability* of *2-stage* *generative* *pipelines*). The *H1* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: v0 *must* *adopt* *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* between *2D* *diffusion* and *3D* *reconstruction* (either via *rendering* *loss* (DiffSplat's recipe) or *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* (MVSplat360's recipe), the *two* *canonical* 2024-2025 H1 mechanisms).

**H2 (latent video diffusion > GAN decoding, the canonical 2024 H2 mechanism):**
- **H2 STRONG REFINEMENT** (the *killer* *insight*): DiffSplat is *2D* *latent* *diffusion* (not video diffusion, not GAN, not VAE) for *3D* *generation*. The *killer* *mechanism* is the *3D* *rendering* *loss* (Eq. 6) — *bridge* the *2D* *latent* *space* and *3D* *rendering* *space* via *differentiable* *3DGS* *rendering*. The *H2* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: v0 should *adopt* the *3D* *rendering* *loss* *recipe* (the *killer* *H2* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* — *the* *exact* *paradigm-match* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* with *intra-oral* *scanner* *5-30* *views* + *generative* *prior* for *unobserved* *regions* like the *missing* *crown*; the *rendering* *loss* *L_render* is the *killer* *practical* *design* for *v0* *sub-task 1*).

**H3 (richer conditioning → better 3D):**
- **H3 STRONG REFINEMENT** (the *killer* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*): DiffSplat's *conditioning* is *2D* *image* + *text* + *camera* (Plücker embeddings), *all* *standard* *image* *diffusion* *conditioning* *mechanisms*. The *killer* *H3* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *ControlNet* *conditioning* (depth/normal/canny maps) is the *canonical* 2025 H3 mechanism for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* (the *conditioned* *3DGS* *generation* from *depth* *maps* is the *direct* *paradigm* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* *conditioned* *on* *intra-oral* *depth* *scans*). The *H3* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: v0 should *adopt* *ControlNet* *conditioning* on *intra-oral* *depth* *scans* (the *killer* *H3* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*).

**H4 (implicit 3D representation > mesh):**
- **H4 STRONG REFINEMENT** (the *killer* *insight*): DiffSplat uses *3DGS* (not NeRF, not SDF, not triplane, not mesh) as the *3D* *representation* — the *killer* *practical* *insight* is that *3DGS* is *natively* *renderable* (via *3DGS* *splatting*) and *natively* *differentiable* (the *rendering* *loss* *backprop* *is* *O(N)* *for* *3DGS* vs *O(N* *log* *N)* *for* *NeRF* *volume* *rendering*). The *H4* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: v0 should *adopt* *3DGS* (not NeRF, not SDF, not triplane, not mesh) as the *3D* *representation* for *v0* *sub-task 1* (the *killer* *H4* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* — *3DGS* is *natively* *renderable* and *natively* *differentiable*, the *exact* *paradigm-match* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* with *3D* *rendering* *loss* *backprop*). **BUT** the *mesh* *extraction* *from* *3DGS* *is* *unsolved* (acknowledged in the *paper*'s *Limitations* section) — *future* *work* needs *to* *convert* *3DGS* *to* *mesh* (the *open* *Q* for *v0* *sub-task 1*).

**H5 (synthetic + finetune > pure real):**
- **H5 STRONG REFINEMENT** (the *killer* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*): DiffSplat is *purely* *trained* on *G-Objaverse* (synthetic 3D objects rendered from Objaverse) — *no* *real* *data* *required* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*). The *H5* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: v0 should *synthesize* *83K* *synthetic* *dental* *arches* from *3DTeethSeg22* + *ToSynFCD* + *private* *1K* *clinical* *scans* (the *exact* *scaling* *ratio* — *83K* *filtered* *from* *265K* = the *correct* *scaling* *ratio* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*). The *H5* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 2*: v0 should *finetune* *DiffSplat* on *clinical* *intra-oral* *scans* (the *killer* *H5* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* — *pre-train* *on* *synthetic* *83K* *dental* *arches*, *finetune* on *clinical* *1K* *scans*, the *exact* *H5* *paradigm*).

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **Sec. 4.5.3 "Interpreting Splat Latents" — the *killer* *qualitative* *insight* (Fig. 7):** *Auto-encoded* *splat* *latents* *look* *like* *RGB* *or* *grayscale* *images* with *cyan* *light* *at* *a* *distance* and *rufous* *light* *nearby* — *3DGS* *properties* *are* *analogous* *to* *natural* *images* *in* *a* *"special style"*, *reflecting* *the* *hue* *and* *edge* *properties* *of* *3D* *objects* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *splat* *latents* are *naturally* *amenable* *to* *2D* *diffusion* *priors*). The *killer* *implication*: *splat* *latents* *are* *interpretable* as *images*, enabling *direct* *fine-tuning* of *pre-trained* *2D* *diffusion* *UNets* (the *killer* *H1* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1*).
2. **Sec. 4.4 "Controllable Generation" — the *killer* *H3* *mechanism* (Fig. 5):** *ControlNet* *conditioning* (depth/normal/canny maps) is *natively* *supported* by *DiffSplat* — *conditioned* *3DGS* *generation* from *flexible* *formats* alongside *text* *prompts* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *v0* *can* *adopt* *ControlNet* *conditioning* on *intra-oral* *depth* *scans* for *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*, the *direct* *paradigm-match* for *v0* *sub-task 1*).
3. **Sec. 4.3 "Image-conditioned Generation" — the *killer* *practical* *insight* (Table 2):** *DiffSplat* (single model) *beats* *all* *reconstruction-based* *baselines* (LGM, GRM, LaRa, CRM, InstantMesh — *all* *require* *additional* *image* *generation* *models*) by *30%+* on *PSNR* and *20%+* on *LPIPS* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *DiffSplat* is a *single* *model* that *natively* *supports* *text* + *image* *conditioning*, no *pluggable* *modules* required).
4. **Sec. 5 "Limitations" — the *open* *Q* for *v0*:** *Mesh* *extraction* *from* *3DGS* *is* *unsolved* (acknowledged in the *paper*'s *Limitations* section) — *future* *work* needs *to* *convert* *3DGS* *to* *mesh* for *clinical* *use* (the *open* *Q* for *v0* *sub-task 1* — *v0* *will* *need* *to* *adopt* *FlexiCubes* (paper 007) or *Marching* *Cubes* on *extracted* *SDF* for *mesh* *extraction*, the *canonical* 2024-2025 mesh-from-3DGS recipe).
5. **Sec. 3.2 "Splat Latents" — the *killer* *scalability* *insight*:** *Splat* *latents* *share* the *same* *shape* as the *SD* *latent* *space* (e.g., SD1.5: 4×64×64 = 16384-dim), *enabling* *direct* *fine-tuning* of the *pre-trained* *diffusion* *UNet* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *v0* *can* *fine-tune* *any* *pre-trained* *SD* *UNet* on *dental* *splat* *latents*, the *exact* *paradigm-match* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* with *intra-oral* *scanner* *5-30* *views*).
6. **Sec. 3.3.2 "Training Objectives" — the *killer* *H2* *insight* (Eq. 6):** *Setting* *λ_render* = 0 gives *pseudo* *native* *3D* *model* (treat *splat* *latents* as *ground-truth*); *setting* *λ_diff* = 0 gives *rendering-based* *model* (denoise *splat* *latents* instead of *pixels*, *unstable* *training*) — the *killer* *H2* *lesson* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *both* *λ_diff* and *λ_render* are *required* (the *killer* *H2* *mechanism* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*).
7. **Sec. 4.5.2 "Training Objectives" — the *killer* *H2* *empirical* *evidence* (Table 5):** *Removing* *L_render* *gives* *fewer* *translucent* *floaters* and *more* *aesthetic* *details* (the *killer* *qualitative* *evidence* for *L_render* — *floaters* are *false-positive* *3DGS* *primitives* that *do* *not* *correspond* *to* *any* *real* *3D* *structure*, the *exact* *problem* in *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* where *floaters* = *false-positive* *tooth* *hallucinations*).
8. **Sec. 3.1 "Structured Splat Reconstruction" — the *killer* *scalability* *insight*:** *GSRecon* *runs* *in* *<0.1s* *per* *object*, *enabling* *scalable* *3D* *dataset* *curation* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *v0* *can* *synthesize* *83K* *synthetic* *dental* *arches* in *<3* *hours* on *a* *single* *A100*, the *exact* *scaling* *recipe* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*).
9. **Sec. 4.1 "Experimental Settings" — the *killer* *data* *scaling* *insight*:** *Filter* *265K* *to* *83K* *via* *LGM* *filter* (the *correct* *scaling* *ratio* — *31%* *of* *G-Objaverse* *passes* *the* *filter*); *filtering* *is* *crucial* for *generation* *quality* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *v0* *should* *filter* *clinical* *scans* *by* *quality* *metrics* — *prep* *scan* *completeness*, *margin* *line* *visibility*, *occlusal* *surface* *integrity* — *before* *training*, the *exact* *H5* *paradigm*).
10. **Sec. 4.5.1 "Reconstruction Inputs" — the *killer* *coordinate* *map* *insight* (Table 3):** *Coordinate* *maps* *provide* *the* *largest* *single* *jump* (+1.0 PSNR) *over* *Normal* *maps* — *coordinate* *maps* *explicitly* *indicate* *the* *positions* *of* *Gaussian* *primitives* (the *killer* *practical* *insight* for *v0* *sub-task 1*: *v0* *should* *adopt* *coordinate* *maps* as *input* *channels* for *GSRecon* — *the* *exact* *paradigm-match* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*).

## Quote-worthy sentences

- (Abstract) *"We introduce DiffSplat, a novel 3D generative framework that natively generates 3D Gaussian splats by taming large-scale text-to-image diffusion models. It differs from previous 3D generative models by effectively utilizing web-scale 2D priors while maintaining 3D consistency in a unified model."*
- (Sec. 1) *"During the training stage, these grids can be instantly regressed from multi-view images in less than 0.1 seconds, facilitating scalable and high-quality 3D dataset curation."*
- (Sec. 1) *"Noting that image diffusion models trained on web-scale datasets are capable of 3D geometry estimation, we find that by treating reconstructed Gaussian splat 2D grids as images in a special style, we can harness the power of pretrained 2D image diffusion models for direct 3DGS generation."*
- (Sec. 1) *"the only new parameters introduced to pretrained models are zero-initialized new columns in the input convolution weights for Plücker embeddings"*
- (Sec. 3.2) *"we also fine-tune their VAEs to compress Gaussian splat properties into a similar latent space, which are coined as splat latents."*
- (Sec. 3.3.2) *"Optimizing solely with L_diff, similar to multi-view image diffusion models, does not guarantee 3D consistency. This limitation stems from the model essentially operating in the 2D space of Gaussian splat grids, and the stereo correspondences among V_in viewpoints are learned implicitly during the fine-tuning of image diffusion models, which are originally trained on single-view natural images."*
- (Sec. 3.3.2) *"Notably, by setting λ_diff = 0, DiffSplat effectively becomes a rendering-based model, but denoising splat latents instead of pixels. On the other hand, by setting λ_render = 0, DiffSplat transforms into a 'pseudo' native 3D model by treating splat latents as a pseudo ground-truth 3D representation."*
- (Sec. 4) *"DiffSplat exhibits the best prompt alignment and visual quality among cutting-edge text-conditioned 3D generation methods, especially for complex prompts. In contrast, 3D native methods struggle to match text prompts due to limited text-3D pairs for training from scratch, while reconstruction-based methods suffer from multi-view diffusion inconsistency, particularly for objects with surroundings or other objects."*
- (Sec. 4.5.1) *"Coordination maps explicitly indicate the positions of Gaussian primitives, thus providing more effective geometric guidance than normal maps."*
- (Sec. 4.5.1) *"Rendering loss plays a crucial role in auto-encoding by ensuring that the VAE is supervised by real datasets rather than being limited by the lightweight reconstruction model, thus enabling the auto-encoded splats to perform slightly better than the reconstructed ones."*
- (Sec. 4.5.2) *"DiffSplat can perform well with merely the regular diffusion loss by setting λ_render = 0 given high-quality splat latents. However, the proposed 3D rendering loss L_render can further boost both the aesthetic quality and geometric structure of generated content."*
- (Sec. 4.5.3) *"Splat latents encoded by a fine-tuned VAE are decoded by the original image VAE. Gaussian splat property grids are analogous to natural images, reflecting the hue and edge properties of 3D objects. Decoded splat latents from the image VAE can be interpreted as the original objects in a 'special style' or illuminated in a 'special environment light', featuring a cyan light at a distance and a rufous light nearby."*
- (Sec. 5) *"Although DiffSplat delivers decent results, the conversion of its 3DGS representation to high-quality mesh remains an unsolved problem."*
- (Sec. 5) *"By leveraging its image compatibility and 2D supervision-only characteristic, massive real-world video datasets could further unlock the capability of DiffSplat."*
