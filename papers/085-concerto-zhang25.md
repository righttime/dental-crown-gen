## Research question + their answer

**Q:** The *single biggest open question* in 3D self-supervised learning after Sonata (paper 084) is *how to integrate 2D image information into a 3D point cloud SSL framework* in a way that *exceeds* the *sum of its parts*. Sonata (paper 084) demonstrated that *pure-3D* SSL can *exceed* DINOv2-lifted-to-3D by +9.4pp (72.5% vs 63.1% ScanNet linear probing) and that *naive* feature-concatenation (Sonata + DINOv2.5 = 76.44% linear probing) provides a *+3.9pp* gain over Sonata-alone, the *first* evidence that 2D and 3D SSL are *complementary*. But the *naive* concatenation has a *fundamental* limitation: it *integrates* the two modalities at *inference* time, *after* each model has been *trained independently* on its *own* data, so the *synergy* is *bounded* by the *information* in each individual model. The *central question* of the Concerto paper is: **can a *joint* 2D-3D self-supervised learning framework, where the two modalities are *trained together* (the *LeCun JEPA* paradigm, not the *concatenation* paradigm), produce a *unified* representation that *exceeds* even the *oracle* feature-concatenation upper bound?** And, *if yes*, what is the *minimalist* design that achieves this *synergy* without the *complexity* of prior 2D-3D SSL methods (Pixel-Pretrain 2024, Bridge3D 2023, OpenScene 2023, CLIP2Scene 2023, which all use *intricate* 2D-to-3D projection / rendering / attention / distillation mechanisms)?

**A:** **Yes — and the *minimalist* design is the *LeCun JEPA* paradigm (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture, LeCun 2022): train a *point encoder* to *predict* the *image-patch features* of a *frozen* DINOv2 image encoder, using a *cosine-similarity* loss for the cross-modal branch and the *DINOv2-style restricted online-clustering* loss for the *intra-modal* (point-only) branch, with the *cross-modal* loss computed at the *upcast-level 3* of the point encoder (the *level* where the *spatial granularity* of the point cloud *matches* the *spatial granularity* of the image patches) and the *intra-modal* loss computed at the *upcast-level 2* (the *level* where the *3D-specific* geometric information is *preserved*).** The *key* insight is that *one* objective per branch is the *right* design: the *looser* cross-modal cosine-sim *enables* the point encoder to *predict* image features without *collapsing* to a *trivial* solution (cross-modal cross-entropy would be *too strict* and would *force* the point encoder to *mimic* the image features, *losing* the 3D-specific information), while the *stricter* intra-modal restricted online-clustering *prevents* the point encoder from *forgetting* the 3D-specific geometric information (the *same* Sonata geometric-shortcut fix, but *without* the cross-modal gradient interfering). The *ablation evidence* is *direct* (Table 6a): **(cross=cosine-sim, intra=clustering) = 75.6% lin/78.6% dec**, (cross=clustering, intra=clustering) = 60.7%/72.0%, (cross=cosine-sim, intra=cosine-sim) = 31.6%/66.0%, (cross=clustering, intra=cosine-sim) = 74.7%/78.3% — the *optimal* is *cross=cosine-sim, intra=clustering*, with the *gap* to the *suboptimal* configurations being +1-44pp. The *minimalist* design is *crucial* because it *avoids* the *complexity* of prior 2D-3D SSL methods: no 2D-to-3D projection, no differentiable rendering, no attention-based feature fusion, no text-aligned image encoders for open-vocabulary, no separate *imitation* objective — just *one* frozen image encoder (DINOv2) and *one* trainable point encoder (PTv3, same as Sonata) and *two* loss functions. The *result* is the *first* paper to *explicitly* demonstrate that *joint* multi-modal SSL *exceeds* even the *oracle* feature-concatenation upper bound: **77.3% ScanNet linear probing** with *joint* 2D-3D SSL *exceeds* **75.9% with *naive* Sonata+DINOv2 feature concatenation** by **+1.4pp**, the *direct* evidence that *integration during training* beats *integration at inference*. The *ablation* on *image-usage ratio* (Table 6b) is *the* practical enabler: **even 20% of training data with paired images → 73.5% lin (vs 70.9% with 0% images, +2.6pp), 50% → 75.2% (+4.3pp), 100% → 75.6% (+4.7pp)**, the *direct* evidence that *joint* 2D-3D SSL is *robust* to the *availability* of paired 2D-3D data (the *practical* implication for v0 v2: *even if only 20% of v0's clinical scans have paired intra-oral photos*, the *joint* 2D-3D SSL paradigm *still provides* a *significant* gain). The *data scaling* evidence is *also* direct (Table 6g): **40k datasets → 76.6% lin (vs 23k → 75.6%, +1.0pp),** the *moderate* but *non-zero* gain from *scaling* the pre-training data, and the *video-lifted* extension (50k point clouds + 200k images from RE10K using VGGT) provides *additional* scaling that the 207M model can *absorb* (the 207M model reaches 77.5% ScanNet linear probing, *higher* than the 108M model trained *without* video data). The *SOTA results* are *uniform* on *all* major indoor benchmarks: **80.7% ScanNet val** (+1.3pp over Sonata-full's 79.4%, +3.1pp over PTv3-from-scratch's 77.6%), **39.2% ScanNet200 val** (+2.4pp over Sonata-full's 36.8%, +3.9pp over PTv3's 35.3%), **50.7% ScanNet++ val** (+1.4pp over Sonata-full's 49.3%, +2.5pp over PTv3's 48.2%), **77.4% S3DIS Area 5** (+1.4pp over Sonata-full's 76.0%, +4.0pp over PTv3's 73.4%), *uniform* SOTA on 4 indoor semantic-segmentation benchmarks, plus *uniform* SOTA on 4 indoor instance-segmentation benchmarks (79.5 mAP ScanNet, 45.8 mAP ScanNet200, 44.3 mAP ScanNet++, 67.5 mAP S3DIS), plus the *language-probing* result (44.56% mIoU on ScanNet zero-shot segmentation by linearly projecting Concerto features into CLIP's text space, the *direct* evidence that *joint* 2D-3D SSL learns *language-aligned* representations *without* any language supervision, the *killer* v0 v3 feature for "describe the crown in natural language"). **The cross-modal generalization to *out-of-distribution* scanners** is the *practical* v0 v2 deployment insight: the *joint* 2D-3D SSL learns *scanner-invariant* features (the *2D* branch *anchors* the *3D* branch to the *visual* appearance, which is *more* scanner-invariant than the *raw* 3D point coordinates), the *direct* mechanism for v0 v2's *cross-IOS-scanner* deployment (3Shape Trios, iTero, Medit, Cerec, etc. all have *different* 3D point-cloud noise / sampling / completeness patterns, but the *2D* intra-oral photo is *more* scanner-invariant).

## Method

### The two questions the paper *frames* (Section 2)

**Question 1: "Is there a superior representation space beyond single-modality learning?"** The paper's *pilot study* (Table 1) is *direct* evidence: simply *concatenating* DINOv2 image features (lifted to 3D via depth + camera) and Sonata point features *outperforms* either modality alone (75.91% vs DINOv2-alone 63.09% or Sonata-alone 72.52%, +3.39pp and +12.82pp respectively on ScanNet val linear probing), the *first* explicit evidence that 2D and 3D *independently-trained* SSL features are *complementary*, not *redundant*. But the *conjecture* is that *integration during training* (the *JEPA* paradigm) *should* beat *integration at inference* (the *concatenation* paradigm), and the *method* is the *minimalist* design that *tests* this *conjecture*.

**Question 2: "Can multi-modal self-supervised representations speak the language of concepts?"** The paper's *language-probing* result (Table 5) is *direct* evidence: a *linear* projection of Concerto features into CLIP's text space achieves 44.56% mIoU on ScanNet zero-shot segmentation (vs MSC's 12.42% and Sonata's 41.71%), the *first* paper to *demonstrate* that *joint* 2D-3D SSL learns *language-aligned* representations *without* any language supervision, the *direct* mechanism for v0 v3's "describe the crown in natural language" capability. The *practical* implication: the *future* of 3D foundation models is *multi-modal* (3D + image + text), the *direct* template for v0 v3's text-prompted crown generation.

### The Concerto framework (Section 3)

**Architecture: PTv3 (same as Sonata) + frozen DINOv2 (image encoder) + cross-modal predictor.**

**(a) Intra-modal self-distillation (Section 3.1, *inherited* from Sonata):** the *intra-modal* branch is the *same* as Sonata (paper 084): a PTv3 (paper 083) encoder-only model trained with DINOv2-style self-distillation (student + EMA teacher, restricted online clustering + KoLeo + Sinkhorn-Knopp centering, with the *same* Sonata micro-designs: decoder removal, feature up-casting, masked-points Gaussian jitter, progressive parameter scheduler). The *intra-modal* loss is computed at *upcast-level 2* (the *level* where the 3D-specific geometric information is *preserved* and the Sonata geometric-shortcut fix is *effective*). The *student* takes *local* (crop ratio 5-40%) and *masked* (mask ratio 30-70%, mask size 10-40cm) views, the *teacher* takes *global* views (crop ratio 40-100%), and the EMA teacher *stabilizes* training.

**(b) Cross-modal joint embedding prediction (Section 3.2, the *novel* contribution):** the *cross-modal* branch takes (1) the *student*'s *point features* `s_x` from the *first masked view* of a point cloud + (2) the *frozen* DINOv2 image encoder's *image-patch features* `s_y` from the *corresponding* 2D images of the *same* scene (using *known camera parameters* + *depth maps* to establish *point-pixel correspondences*, with a *visibility check* `|d_proj - d_camera| < 0.01` to *filter* occluded points, the *exact* depth-based visibility check used in classical multi-view stereo). The *predictor* takes the *student*'s point features `s_x` and the *camera parameters* `z` as input, and *outputs* the *predicted image-patch features* `ŝ_y` for *each* image patch (by computing the *mean* of the point features of all points that *fall within* that image patch, the *aggregation* operation). The *cross-modal* loss is the *cosine similarity* between `s_y` (the *true* image-patch features from DINOv2) and `ŝ_y` (the *predicted* image-patch features from the point encoder + predictor). The *cosine similarity* is *crucial* (the *ablation* in Table 6a is *direct* evidence): the *looser* constraint (cosine-sim ∈ [-1, 1]) *enables* the point encoder to *predict* image features *without* *collapsing* to a *trivial* solution, while the *stricter* cross-entropy (used in DINOv2-style methods) would *force* the point encoder to *mimic* the image features, *losing* the 3D-specific information.

**(c) Data pieces (Section 3.2):** the paper *divides* scenes with a *large* number of images (e.g., ScanNet 5,000+ images per scene) into *several* "data pieces", *each* consisting of *one* point cloud + *4* images (the *practical* memory constraint: 4 images per point cloud is the *sweet spot* for a 16× H20 GPU at batch size 1). For scenes with *few* images (e.g., 5 images per scene), the *original* dataset divisions are *retained*. The *total* pre-training data is 522,270 image-level samples and 85,138 point-cloud-level samples (Table 7-8), the *single largest* multi-modal pre-training dataset in the 3D-deep-learning literature.

**(d) Synergy emerges from joint learning (Section 3.3):** the *combination* of the *intra-modal* self-distillation and the *cross-modal* joint-embedding prediction *emerges* a *superior* spatial representation that *surpasses* the *naive* combination of self-supervised features from images and point clouds (the *conjecture* of the paper, *validated* by the +1.4pp gain on ScanNet linear probing over the Sonata+DINOv2 concatenation baseline). The *key* insight is that the *complementary* cognitive signals from image SSL *stimulate* the point intra-modal self-distillation to *extend* its *capacity* beyond a *single* modality, and the *emergent* representation *cannot* be *obtained* by *merely* concatenating features from *separate* 2D and 3D models. The *architecture* also *supports* training on point clouds *without* paired images (the *intra-modal* branch can be trained *alone*, enabling *hybrid* self-supervised learning *without* compromising *scalability* on large-scale 3D datasets), the *practical* v0 v2 mechanism: *pre-train on 50k arches + 300k clinical intra-oral photos, even if some arches don't have paired photos*.

### The video-lifted extension (Section 4.1, the *third* contribution)

**The 50k point clouds + 200k images from RE10K (Zhou et al. SIGGRAPH 2018) are lifted to 3D using the *feed-forward* reconstruction model VGGT (Wang et al. CVPR 2025)**, the *first* paper to *demonstrate* that *video-lifted* point clouds can be *added* to the pre-training mix for *free*. The *process* is *straightforward*: take a *video clip* from RE10K, *run* VGGT to *reconstruct* the *point cloud* + *camera poses* for *each* frame, *select* clips with *large* camera-pose transforms (the *heuristic* to *ensure* the *lifted* point cloud has *enough* coverage), and *use* the *lifted* point cloud + *reconstructed* images as *additional* pre-training data. The *result* is the *large* 207M model trained with the *additional* video data reaches *higher* linear-probing mIoU (77.5% ScanNet, 38.6% ScanNet200) than the 108M model trained *without* video data (77.3% / 37.4%), the *direct* evidence that *video-lifted* data continues to *help* at the *largest* scale.

### Implementation and training cost (Appendix A.4)

**Training cost:** **85 hours for base (108M) model on 16× NVIDIA H20 GPUs** (the *equivalent* of 8× A100 80GB, the *single largest* pre-training cost in the reading list, *justified* by the SOTA results on 5 indoor + 3 outdoor + 4 instance-seg + language-probing benchmarks), 360 CPU cores for pre-training, 3.6 TB RAM (the *single largest* memory footprint in the reading list, *justified* by the *large* 522,270 image-level + 85,138 point-cloud-level dataset). **Software:** CUDA 12.4, PyTorch 2.4.1, Python 3.10.15, FlashAttention, AdamW, cosine annealing, max learning rate 0.004 (adjusted with encoder depth), 100 pre-training epochs. **Data preparation:** the *visibility check* (`|d_proj - d_camera| < 0.01`) is *computed* on-the-fly during pre-training (no need to *pre-compute* the *point-pixel correspondences*), the *RGBD datasets* (Structured3D) use the *depth channel* directly, the *mesh-based datasets* (ScanNet, ScanNet++, S3DIS, ArkitScenes) *render* depth maps from the *ground-truth* mesh using the *camera parameters*, the *HM3D* dataset *simulates* the scenes using *Habitat-Sim* with *4 random images per room* (90° angular difference between consecutive images, the *heuristic* to *ensure* sufficient coverage), and the *video-lifted* RE10K dataset uses *VGGT* to *reconstruct* point clouds + camera poses from video frames.

## Surprises and interesting things buried in the paper

(1) **"Joint multi-modal SSL exceeds the *oracle* feature-concatenation upper bound"** is the *single most counter-intuitive* conceptual claim in the 3D-SSL literature since Sonata's *geometric shortcut* claim (paper 084). The *naive* assumption is that *integration at inference* (feature concatenation) should *outperform* *integration during training* (joint SSL) because the *two* modalities are *independently* trained on *their own* data and the *integration* is *oracle*. But the *empirical* result is the *opposite*: Concerto-lin 77.3% > Sonata+DINOv2 concat 75.9% (+1.4pp), the *direct* evidence that *integration during training* beats *integration at inference*. The *philosophical* implication: *synergy* requires *joint* learning, not just *joint* inference.

(2) **The *minimalist* design is *the* right design**: the Concerto paper has *zero* of the *complex* 2D-to-3D mechanisms used in prior 2D-3D SSL methods (no Pixel-Pretrain-style multi-view projection, no Bridge3D-style attention-based feature fusion, no OpenScene-style 3D feature lifting, no CLIP2Scene-style 2D-3D contrastive learning, no text-aligned image encoders for open-vocabulary). Just *one* frozen image encoder (DINOv2) and *one* trainable point encoder (PTv3) and *two* loss functions (cosine-sim cross-modal + restricted online-clustering intra-modal). The *simplicity* is *the* insight: *complex* 2D-3D methods *overfit* to *specific* 2D-3D tasks, *minimalist* 2D-3D methods *generalize* across *all* 2D-3D tasks.

(3) **The criteria ablation is *direct* evidence that *one* objective per branch is the *right* design** (Table 6a): (cross=cosine, intra=clustering) = 75.6%/78.6% (optimal), (cross=clustering, intra=clustering) = 60.7%/72.0% (-14.9pp/-6.6pp), (cross=cosine, intra=cosine) = 31.6%/66.0% (-44.0pp/-12.6pp, the *collapse* mode), (cross=clustering, intra=cosine) = 74.7%/78.3% (-0.9pp/-0.3pp, the *close second*). The *key* insight is that *the strictness of the two objectives should be asymmetric*: the *cross-modal* objective should be *looser* (cosine-sim) to *allow* the point encoder to *predict* image features *without* *collapsing* to a *trivial* solution, while the *intra-modal* objective should be *stricter* (restricted online-clustering) to *prevent* the point encoder from *forgetting* the 3D-specific geometric information.

(4) **The image-usage ablation is *the* practical v0 v2 insight** (Table 6b): *even 20% of training data with paired images → +2.6pp lin/0pp dec*, the *direct* evidence that *joint* 2D-3D SSL is *robust* to the *availability* of paired 2D-3D data. The *practical* v0 v2 implication: *even if only 20% of v0's clinical scans have paired intra-oral photos*, the *joint* 2D-3D SSL paradigm *still provides* a *significant* gain, the *practical* v0 v2 mechanism for *partially-paired* clinical data.

(5) **The data-scaling + video-lifted extension is *the* video-augmentation v0 v2 mechanism** (Table 3, Fig. 4): the *large* 207M model trained with the *additional* 50k video-lifted point clouds + 200k images from RE10K reaches *higher* linear-probing mIoU (77.5% ScanNet, 38.6% ScanNet200) than the 108M model trained *without* video data, the *direct* evidence that *video-lifted* data continues to *help* at the *largest* scale. The *practical* v0 v2 mechanism: *lift dental-arch videos to point clouds using VGGT-style feed-forward reconstruction, pre-train on the *combined* 3DTeethSeg22 + clinical-internal + dental-video-lifted mix*.

(6) **The 1% data result is the *killer* v0 v2 H5 evidence** (Table 2c): **Concerto-lin at 1% scenes = 48.2% mIoU**, the *single largest* data-efficiency result in the 85-paper reading list, **+22.4pp over PTv3-from-scratch's 25.8%**, **+1.7pp over Concerto-full's 46.5%** (the *counter-intuitive* finding that *linear probing beats full fine-tuning* at *1%* data, the *exact* DINOv2+LoRA paradigm that makes image-foundation-models *practical*).
