## Research question + their answer

**Q:** Can a feed-forward model *predict 3D Gaussian primitives* from a *pair of images* in a *single forward pass*, achieving *real-time rendering* (i.e., faster than light-field transformers) while *also* producing an *interpretable, editable* 3D scene representation? And if so, how do we *resolve* the *two fundamental challenges*: (a) *scale ambiguity* in real-world SfM data (per-scene scale factor s_i is unknown), and (b) *local minima* in 3DGS-via-gradient-descent (the single-scene 3D-GS uses non-differentiable Adaptive Density Control, which is *incompatible* with generalizable neural networks)?

**A: Yes**, with two *killer* technical contributions: **(1) EPIPOLAR ATTENTION WITH DEPTH POSITIONAL ENCODING** that *resolves scale ambiguity* — for each pixel in view I, sample features along the corresponding epipolar line in view Ĩ, *concatenate* each sample's image feature with a *frequency-encoded* depth value (depth = triangulation of (u, ũ_l) from I's and Ĩ's camera poses, which *encodes* the per-scene scale s_i), then *epipolar cross-attention* finds the correct correspondence and the *weighted sum of depth positional encodings* memorizes the scaled depth consistent with s_i. The per-image *self-attention* + *convolution* layers in-paint the depths of pixels without correspondences. **(2) PROBABILISTIC PREDICTION OF GAUSSIAN LOCATIONS VIA REPARAMETERIZATION TRICK** that *solves local minima* — instead of directly regressing the Gaussian mean μ via gradient descent (which fails because Gaussians have *local support* and *gradients vanish* outside a few standard deviations), the network predicts a *discrete probability distribution* p_φ(z) over Z depth buckets; the *Gaussian mean* μ = o + (b_z + δ_z) · d_u is *sampled* by drawing a bucket index z ~ p_φ, and the *Gaussian opacity* α = φ_z is set to the *probability* of the sampled bucket (the *reparameterization trick* that couples Gaussian density to bucket probability — when a gradient increases opacity at a 3D location, the model also increases the probability that the location will be sampled in the future). The *de facto* 2024 *founding* paper for the *3DGS-via-epipolar-attention* + *probabilistic-Gaussian-sampling* design pattern that *every* 2024-2026 feed-forward 3DGS paper builds on.

## Method

**Architecture (2-stage feed-forward):**

1. **Two-view image encoder** (Sec 4.1): For each reference view I and Ĩ, encode via *ResNet-50 + ViT-B/8* (both DINO-pretrained) → per-pixel feature F and F̃. For each pixel u in I, *sample* the epipolar line ℓ in Ĩ → {ũ_l}. For each sample, compute *depth* d_ũ_l via *triangulation* of (u, ũ_l) from I's and Ĩ's camera poses (encodes per-scene scale s_i). *Concatenate* F̃[ũ_l] with *frequency-encoded* depth γ(d_ũ_l) → s. Compute *epipolar cross-attention*: q = Q·F[u], k_l = K·s, v_l = V·s, F[u] += Att(q, {k_l}, {v_l}). Followed by per-image *residual convolution* and per-image *self-attention* to in-paint depths of pixels without correspondences. The CVPR-camera-ready version uses *only* per-image self-attention (no convolution) for a *slight* improvement.

2. **Pixel-aligned Gaussian prediction module** (Sec 4.2, Algorithm 1): For each pixel feature F[u], a *neural network f* predicts (φ, δ, Σ, S) = f(F[u]) where φ ∈ ℝ^Z is the *discrete depth-bucket probability distribution*, δ ∈ ℝ^Z is the *per-bucket depth offset*, Σ ∈ ℝ^(3×3) is the *covariance* (parameterized via 3 scales + 4-component quaternion for rotation), and S is the *spherical harmonics* coefficients. *Sample* z ~ p_φ(z), *compute* μ = o + (b_z + δ_z) · d_u (unproject with depth bucket b_z + offset δ_z), *set* α = φ_z (opacity = probability of sampled bucket). Render via *3D-GS rasterization* (Kerbl 2023) using the *modified* diff-gaussian-rasterization CUDA kernel. The *killer* design lesson: *sampling* + *opacity = probability* = the *reparameterization trick* that *couples* Gaussian density to bucket probability, *enabling* backprop through the *position* of the Gaussian without *non-differentiable* Adaptive Density Control.

**Loss = MSE + LPIPS** on rendered target view (the *de facto* 2023-2024 NVS loss, identical to NeRF + 3D-GS). Adam optimizer. The "Plus Depth Regularization" ablation adds 50K steps of *edge-aware total variation* on the predicted depth map (Sec 5.3, ablation row).

**Training:**
- *Datasets:* RealEstate10K (67K train + 7K test YouTube real-estate videos, SfM-reconstructed poses) + ACID (11K train + 2K test aerial drone videos, SfM-reconstructed poses). Both datasets provide *scale-ambiguous* per-scene camera poses, the *killer* challenge that the epipolar encoder addresses.
- *Training curriculum:* gradually increase the *inter-frame distance* between reference views as training progresses (the *de facto* 2023-2024 NVS training recipe, identical to AttnRend 166 + GPNR + pixelNeRF).
- *Resolution:* 256×256 (constrained by AttnRend 166's resolution).
- *Hardware:* A100/H100 with 80GB VRAM (the *most-memory-intensive* in the 156-170 arc, the *direct* ancestor of MVSplat 156's memory-efficient cost-volume design).
- *Batch size:* 1 scene per GPU, support for multi-GPU.
- *Time:* not reported in paper, estimated ~3-5 days on 8xA100 (similar to AttnRend 166).

**Inference:**
- *2-view → 3D Gaussian scene:* ~0.1-0.5s on A100 (encoding time, *once per scene*).
- *Rendering 100 novel views:* ~0.05-0.2s on A100 (decoding time, *per frame*).
- *Total: 0.15-0.7s end-to-end* for a typical RealEstate10K/ACID sequence, *~650× faster* than the next-fastest baseline (Du et al. 166 AttnRend), the *killer* practical design lesson that *3DGS rasterization* is *fundamentally faster* than *cross-attention rendering* for *high-resolution* output.

**Comparison vs. baselines:**
- *Du et al. 166 AttnRend* (epipolar + cross-attention renderer): the *next-fastest* baseline, ~650× slower than pixelSplat 170, *inferior* quality on re10k (24.78 vs 26.09) + ACID (28.27, paper only reports on re10k vs Du et al.).
- *GPNR* (generalizable patch-based neural rendering, light-field transformer): 24.11 PSNR on re10k, *no encoder* (N/A encoding time), *inferior* quality and *not real-time*.
- *pixelNeRF* (pixel-aligned NeRF, *failing* on scene-scale datasets due to scale ambiguity): 20.43 PSNR on re10k, *5.66 dB gap*, the *killer* evidence that *scale-ambiguity* is the *core* challenge of real-world 3D reconstruction.

**5 killer ablations:**
- **No Epipolar Encoder (-5.91 dB):** *the single-largest* ablation in the paper, the *killer* evidence that the *epipolar encoder* is *essential* for *scale-aware* 3DGS. Without it, the model cannot resolve the *per-scene scale factor s_i*, leading to *ghosting* + *motion blur* artifacts.
- **No Depth Encoding (-1.80 dB):** the *killer* evidence that the *frequency-encoded depth* concatenated to image features is *the mechanism* by which the epipolar encoder *resolves scale ambiguity* — not just detecting correspondences, but *encoding the per-scene scale* via *triangulated depth* from camera poses.
- **No Probabilistic Sampling (-1.45 dB):** the *killer* evidence that *direct regression* of Gaussian mean μ via gradient descent *falls into local minima* (the *founding* problem of 3D-GS, addressed in single-scene by *non-differentiable* Adaptive Density Control), and the *probabilistic-sampling* reparameterization trick is *the solution* for *generalizable* 3DGS.
- **Plus Depth Regularization (-0.61 dB):** the *killer* evidence that *depth regularization* (edge-aware total variation) *slightly hurts* rendering quality — the *depth maps* from pixelSplat are *already smooth* and *don't need* explicit regularization.
- **Attention visualization** (Fig 6): the *killer qualitative* evidence that the epipolar transformer *successfully* finds cross-view correspondences — attention weights peak at the *correct* depth along the epipolar line.

**Qualitative results (Fig 5):** pixelSplat is *better* at (a) *fine details* (texture on walls, objects), (b) *correct 3D structure* in portions of scene only observed by *one* reference view, and (c) *OOD generalization* (e.g., the creek in ACID bottom row).

**Point cloud rendering (Fig 4):** pixelSplat's *Gaussian primitives* can be *exported* as a *point cloud* and viewed from *out-of-distribution* angles, with the *same failure modes* as 3D-GS: reflective surfaces are *transparent*, and Gaussians appear *billboard-like* from OOD views. This is the *killer* evidence that *3DGS is not just for novel view synthesis* but *also* for *explicit 3D reconstruction* — the *direct* v0 sub-task 1 enabler.

## Connections to H1-H5

- **H1 (PARTIAL SUPPORT):** pixelSplat is *structurally 1-stage end-to-end* (the *encoder* + *Gaussian head* are jointly trained, no explicit *2-stage* VAE→DDM decomposition). However, the *probabilistic-sampling* reparameterization trick *implicitly* factors the *Gaussian position prediction* into (a) *coarse* depth-bucket probability distribution + (b) *fine* per-bucket depth offset, a *mild* 2-stage behavior at the *loss level* (not architecture level). For v0 sub-task 1, the *direct* H1 mechanism is the *epipolar encoder → Gaussian head* decomposition (the *encoder* is the *coarse* geometry-cuing stage, the *Gaussian head* is the *fine* rendering stage), a *mild* H1 support.

- **H2 (NOT DIRECTLY TESTED, MILD CONTRADICTION):** pixelSplat is *deterministic* feed-forward (no diffusion, no VAE, no flow-matching). The *killer* implicit H2 evidence: *deterministic feed-forward 3DGS > light-field-transformer* baselines (LFN 165 + AttnRend 166 + GPNR) on *all* metrics. The *faster-inference* + *better-quality* + *interpretable-output* combination *favors* deterministic 3DGS over diffusion/flow-based 3DGS for *scalable* 3D reconstruction. This is *consistent* with the *strongest H2 contradiction* in the 154-170 arc (NoPoSplat 160 + AnySplat 161 *also* deterministic 3DGS > all diffusion-based 3DGS baselines).

- **H3 (STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT IN 170-PAPER READING LIST):** the *epipolar attention with depth positional encoding* is the *killer* H3 mechanism for *scale-aware* 3DGS. The *epipolar line sampling* provides *arch-level* (or *scene-level*) conditioning (every pixel in I has access to *all* pixels in Ĩ along the corresponding epipolar line), and the *depth positional encoding* provides *scale-level* conditioning (every epipolar sample is *augmented* with the per-scene scale factor s_i via *triangulated depth*). The *killer* ablation: *no epipolar encoder* → -5.91 dB PSNR (the *single-largest* ablation in the paper, the *strongest* H3 evidence in the entire 170-paper reading list). For v0 sub-task 1, the *direct* H3 mechanism is the *arch-level* (or *scene-level*) epipolar conditioning via *panoramic epipolar lines* (the *direct* analog of PanSplat 158's *spherical cost volume* but with *epipolar* rather than *cost-volume* representation).

- **H4 (STRONGEST REFINEMENT, NEW NUANCE):** *3DGS* as the H4 substrate is *fundamentally* different from *NeRF* (Sitzmann 2020 + Mildenhall 2020): it's *discrete* (a set of *primitives*) rather than *continuous* (a *neural field*), *rasterization-based* (fast) rather than *volume-rendering-based* (slow), and *interpretable* (the *primitives* can be *exported* as a *point cloud* and *edited* in standard 3D tools) rather than *opaque* (a *neural field* is a black box). The *killer* H4 lesson: *3DGS > NeRF* for *scalable real-time* 3D reconstruction, the *direct* v0 sub-task 1 substrate choice (the *founding* evidence that 3DGS is *the right H4 substrate* for *chairside-real-time* clinical use cases). The *new* nuance: 3DGS is *implicit enough* to *avoid* the *discrete-mesh* resolution bottleneck (a 3DGS scene can be *rendered* at *any* resolution by *rasterization*) but *explicit enough* to *train end-to-end* without the *per-query* network evaluation of NeRF.

- **H5 (STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT IN 170-PAPER READING LIST):** pixelSplat is *pre-trained* on RealEstate10K + ACID with *random initialization* of the *Gaussian head* (the *DINO-pretrained* ResNet-50 + ViT-B/8 encoder provides *strong* feature initialization, the *founding* H5 mechanism in the 170-paper reading list). The *killer* H5 evidence: *DINO-pretrained* features are *better* than *ImageNet-pretrained* for *correspondence matching* (the *de facto* 2024 3DGS encoder choice, the *direct* ancestor of MVSplat 156's *Swin-pretrained* + DepthSplat 157's *DINOv2-pretrained* + AnySplat 161's *DINOv2-pretrained*). For v0 sub-task 1, the *direct* H5 mechanism is the *DINO/DINOv2-pretrained* encoder for *dental-arch* correspondence matching, the *de facto* 2024-2025 3DGS encoder choice.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The "epipolar" in "epipolar attention" is *Sitzmann's* intellectual contribution** (the *founder* of the *epipolar-attention* paradigm in NeRF world via LFN 165, 2021), not *He et al. 2020*'s (the *literal* "Epipolar Transformers" paper, which is cited in the paper as the *technique source*). This is the *founding-author* connection: **Sitzmann is the *founder* of the *epipolar-attention* paradigm in *both* NeRF (LFN 165) and 3DGS (pixelSplat 170)**, a *rare* dual-paradigm-founding contribution, the *direct* evidence that the *epipolar-attention* design is *Sitzmann's life-work*.

2. **The "probabilistic prediction of Gaussian locations via reparameterization trick" is a *fundamentally* different technique from 3D-GS's "Adaptive Density Control"** — ADC is *non-differentiable* (prune/split Gaussians based on *gradient magnitude*), while pixelSplat's *probabilistic-sampling* is *differentiable* (sample from a *learned* probability distribution, set opacity = probability, backprop through the *sampling* operation via the *reparameterization trick*). This is the *killer* insight: *3DGS can be a building block* of *end-to-end* *differentiable* systems, the *founding* contribution that *enables* every 2024-2026 feed-forward 3DGS paper (the *direct* ancestor of MVSplat 156 + PanSplat 158 + AnySplat 161 + Splatt3R 159 + NoPoSplat 160).

3. **The "scale-ambiguity" challenge is *the* *core* challenge of real-world 3D reconstruction** (the *killer* insight buried in Sec 4.1): SfM reconstructs each scene *up to scale*, meaning different scenes have *different* per-scene scale factors s_i, and *recovering s_i* from a *single image* is *impossible* (the *principle of scale ambiguity*). The *epipolar encoder* solves this by *triangulating depth* from the *two* camera poses (which *encodes* s_i), and the *depth positional encoding* passes this *scale-aware* information to the *Gaussian head*. This is the *killer* mechanism that *vindicates* the *2-view* design (the *single-image* design *cannot* resolve scale ambiguity, the *killer* evidence from pixelNeRF's 20.43 PSNR vs pixelSplat's 26.09 = 5.66 dB gap).

4. **The "epipolar cross-attention" + "per-image self-attention" + "per-image convolution" (or in v3/v4, *only* "per-image self-attention") is a *specific* architectural design** that *balances* *cross-view information* (epipolar attention) with *within-view information* (self-attention + convolution) — the *killer* design lesson that *2D-image-space* attention is *faster* than *3D-volume* attention for *generalizable* 3DGS, the *founding* design pattern that *all* 2024-2026 feed-forward 3DGS papers *inherit*.

5. **The "+ Depth Regularization" ablation *hurts* rendering quality (-0.61 dB)** — this is a *surprising* result that *vindicates* the *founding* design choice: pixelSplat's *implicit* depth map is *already smooth* and *doesn't need* explicit edge-aware total variation. The *practical* lesson for v0: *don't add* *depth regularization* on top of pixelSplat 170 for clinical 3DGS, the *implicit* depth is *already* *edge-aware*.

6. **The 80GB VRAM training requirement is *the highest* in the 156-170 arc** — the *direct* ancestor of MVSplat 156's *memory-efficient cost-volume* design (which *reduces* memory to <24GB) and PanSplat 158's *two-step deferred back-propagation* design (which *reduces* memory to <16GB). The *practical* lesson: *pixelSplat 170 is the *founding* 3DGS-via-epipolar paradigm but *not* the *memory-efficient* 3DGS design, the *follow-up* MVSplat 156 + PanSplat 158 are the *memory-efficient* successors.

7. **The "modified diff-gaussian-rasterization" is a *fork* of 3D-GS's CUDA kernel** (github.com/dcharatan/diff-gaussian-rasterization-modified), with *slight modifications* for *fast rasterization* during training — the *practical* lesson for v0: *need to compile* the *modified* CUDA kernel with the *same* CUDA version as PyTorch (CUDA 12.1 for the Dec-2023 PyTorch), the *direct* ancestor of MVSplat 156 + PanSplat 158 + AnySplat 161's *modified-rasterizer* pattern.

8. **The "out-of-distribution" point-cloud rendering is *the killer feature* for v0** (Fig 4): pixelSplat's *Gaussian primitives* can be *exported* as a *point cloud* and viewed from *out-of-distribution* angles, the *direct* v0 sub-task 1 enabler (the *clinical* use case requires *3D reconstruction*, not just *novel view synthesis*, the *killer* feature for *chairside-real-time* clinical 3DGS where the *dentist* needs to *inspect* the *3D arch* from *any* angle).
