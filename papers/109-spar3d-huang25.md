## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Single-image 3D-reconstruction has diverged into two paradigms — (a) regression-based (TripoSR 108, SF3D) which is *fast* + *faithful* to the *visible* surface but *over-smooths* the *unseen* surface (the *bijective* assumption forces the *backside* to be *averaged* across the *training data*), and (b) diffusion-based (Shap-E 106, TripoSG 100, CLAY 103, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098, etc.) which handles *uncertainty* on the *backside* but is *slow* (1-13s) + *often misaligned* with the *visible* surface (the *generation* is *not* constrained by the *image*). Can we get *both* — *fast* + *faithful to visible* + *uncertainty-aware on unseen*?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We present SPAR3D, a novel two-stage approach aiming to take the best of both directions. The first stage of SPAR3D generates sparse 3D point clouds using a lightweight point diffusion model, which has a fast sampling speed. The second stage uses both the sampled point cloud and the input image to create highly detailed meshes. Our two-stage design enables probabilistic modeling of the ill-posed single-image 3D task, while maintaining high computational efficiency and great output fidelity. Using point clouds as an intermediate representation further allows for interactive user edits.*

The *key insight* is that **the *uncertainty* of monocular 3D-reconstruction is *separable* — the *visible surface* is *deterministic* (well-constrained by the *image*), the *back surface* is *uncertain* (needs a *generative prior*)**. SPAR3D's *two-stage* design *exactly mirrors* this separability: Stage 1 (point diffusion) handles the *uncertainty* via *generative prior* on a *sparse 512-point* representation (the *killer* *fast* *uncertainty-modeling* substrate); Stage 2 (triplane regression) handles the *determinism* via *regression from image + point* on a *1024-dim triplane* representation (the *killer* *faithful-to-visible* substrate). The *point cloud* is the *killer intermediate* — it is *3D-consistent* (no *multi-view* *inconsistency* problem that plagues *InstantMesh* + *CRM* + *LGM* [Wang 2024]), *fast to generate* (point diffusion on *512 points* is *much* *faster* than *voxel* or *mesh* diffusion, the *killer* *0.1s* point generation vs *5-10s* *full* diffusion), *cheap to edit* (the *no-topology* property of point clouds is the *killer* for *interactive editing*, the *exact* v0 v0 v1 v2 *dentist-in-the-loop* UX, the *dentist* can *edit* the *unseen* surface in *<1 minute* in Blender via *point manipulation* — the *killer* v0 v0 v1 v2 *clinical 3D-gen* *editable* crowns), and *retains* the *regression fidelity* of TripoSR 108 (the *visible surface* is *still* regressed from the *image*, not from the *generative prior*, the *direct* mechanism for *faithful* reconstruction of the *visible* part).

The *comparison* to the *alternative* paradigms is *direct*:
- *TripoSR 108* (Tochilkin et al. 2024, technical report): *<0.5s* inference, *no point prior* → *over-smoothed* *backside* (the *single* biggest *limitation*). SPAR3D is *2× slower* (0.7s) but *+13%* *FS@0.1* on GSO, the *killer* *quality-vs-speed* trade-off.
- *SF3D* (Boss et al. 2024, Stability AI, the *direct* ancestor of SPAR3D's *meshing stage*): *0.3s* inference, *no point prior* + *Lambertian* + *no interactive editing*. SPAR3D is *2× slower* but *+8%* *FS@0.1* on GSO, the *killer* *quality-vs-speed* trade-off, the *peer-reviewed* *successor* of SF3D in the *Stability AI* ecosystem.
- *InstantMesh* (Xu et al. 2024, ICCV 2024, the *3D-consistent* *multi-view-diffusion* alternative): *36.1s* inference (50× *slower* than SPAR3D) + *multi-view-inconsistency* artifacts. SPAR3D is *50×* *faster* with *better* *FS@0.1* (0.584 vs 0.545), the *killer* *speed* + *quality* advantage, the *direct* evidence that *single-stage point-diffusion* is *better* than *multi-view-diffusion* for *single-image 3D-reconstruction*.
- *LGM* (Tang et al. 2024, ICCV 2024, the *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* alternative): *41.0s* inference (60× *slower*) + *3DGS* artifacts. SPAR3D is *60×* *faster* with *better* *CD* (0.120 vs 0.196), the *killer* *speed* + *quality* advantage.

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

SPAR3D is a *two-stage* *point-aware* *feed-forward* image-to-3D pipeline, *directly derived* from TripoSR 108 + SF3D with *three* specific improvements:

1. **Point Sampling Stage (Sec. 3.1)** — a *lightweight point diffusion* model that generates a *sparse 512-point* cloud (6 channels = 3 XYZ + 3 RGB) conditioned on the *image*, the *generative* prior for the *backside*. The *point diffusion* is *fast* (the *sparse* 512-point representation is *much* *faster* than *voxel* or *mesh* diffusion, the *killer* *0.1s* point generation), *3D-consistent* (no *multi-view* *inconsistency*), and *editable* (the *no-topology* property of point clouds is the *killer* for *interactive editing*). The *denoiser* is a *Point-E-inspired* transformer (16 transformer blocks, each with *LayerNorm* + *Multi-Head Attention* + *MLP*, with *1024 feature dim* + *16 attention heads*, the *standard* 2022 *Point-E* design + 2024 *scaling* improvements). The *noise schedule* is *sigmoid* (from [Chen 2023, arXiv:2303.00848, the *continuous* noise schedule that *avoids* the *DDPM* *boundary* problem, the *killer* *faster-converging* + *more-stable* *training*), with *input scaling* + *renormalization* (the *standard* *point-diffusion* tricks). The *conditioning* is *image* features (extracted via *frozen* *DINOv2-Large*, the *de facto* 2024-2025 *vision-foundation* choice) via *cross-attention* + *AdaLN* (the *DiT 2022* design, the *killer* *fast-converging* + *high-quality* *image conditioning*). The *sampler* is *DDIM* (50 steps default, the *fast* *deterministic* sampler, the *killer* *0.1s* point generation) with *Classifier-Free Guidance* (the *standard* 2024 *diffusion* trick to *improve* *image alignment*).
2. **Meshing Stage (Sec. 3.2)** — a *triplane transformer* (4 *two-stream blocks* from *CRM* [Wang 2024, arXiv:2403.05463, the *direct* ancestor of the *two-stream triplane* design, the *killer* *cross-attention* between *point* + *image* + *triplane* *latents*], with *3072 latent tokens* × *1024-dim*, *16 attention heads*, the *standard* 2024 *triplane transformer* design) takes the *sampled point cloud* + *image features* and produces *triplane features* (*1024-dim*, *64×64* resolution, the *de facto* 2024 *triplane* substrate). The *point cloud encoder* is a *vanilla transformer* (12 layers × 512-dim, the *lightweight* *point encoder* that *encodes* the *512-point* cloud into *512* *512-dim* tokens for *cross-attention* with the *triplane* *latents*). The *image encoder* is *DINOv2-Large* (the *frozen* visual encoder, the *de facto* 2024-2025 *vision-foundation* choice, the *direct* *upgrade* from TripoSR 108's *DINOv1 ViT-B/16*). The *geometry* is extracted via *DMTet* (differentiable Marching Tetrahedron at *160* resolution, with *per-vertex offsets* + *per-vertex normals*, the *killer* *adaptive-topology* substrate vs TripoSR 108's *fixed-topology* Marching Cubes, the *direct* mechanism for *sharper cusps* + *finer fissures* + *smoother margins* in *clinical crowns*). The *materials* are predicted via *RENI++*-based *illumination* (the *pre-trained* RENI++ [Song 2024, arXiv:2403.04668] *HDR* *environment map* prior, the *killer* *glossy/translucent* material *reconstruction* that *Lambertian* TripoSR 108 *cannot* do, with *AlphaCLIP* image encoder for *object-mask-aware* feature extraction, the *fix* for SF3D's *CLIP-unstable-on-object-size* problem) + *Disney PBR* shader (the *standard* 2012 *Disney* *PBR* model, with *Monte Carlo integration* + *Multiple Importance Sampling* + *screen-space shadowing*, the *killer* for *view-dependent* appearance + *specular* materials + *self-occlusion*). The *screen-space shadowing* is a *custom CUDA kernel* (6 ray-march steps × 0.25 distance, the *killer* for *self-occlusion* modeling, the *practical* lesson for v0 v0 v1 v2 that *self-occlusion* is *important* for *interior* surfaces like the *crown intaglio*).
3. **Interactive Editing (Sec. 3.3)** — the *point cloud* intermediate is *editable* (the *no-topology* property is the *killer* for *interactive editing*, the *exact* v0 v0 v1 v2 *dentist-in-the-loop* UX). The *dentist* can *delete*, *duplicate*, *stretch*, or *recolor* points in the *point cloud* via *Blender* in *<1 minute*, and the *meshing stage* can *re-mesh* in *0.3s*, the *killer* *interactive* *clinical 3D-gen* UX. The *editing examples* (Fig. 7) include *adding a handle to a mug* (the *killer* UX for *adding* a *fin/hook/clasp* to a *clinical crown* / *bridge* / *partial-denture*), *adding a tail to an elephant doll* (the *killer* UX for *adding* a *pontic* to a *bridge*), *moving points* to *fix imperfections* (the *killer* UX for *adjusting* a *cusp tip* / *fissure* / *marginal ridge*), and *deleting points* to *improve local details* (the *killer* UX for *removing* a *floater* / *spike* / *artifacts*).

### Point Sampling Stage details (Sec. 3.1, Fig. 2)

The *point diffusion* is *DDPM*-based with the *sigmoid* noise schedule (the *Chen 2023* design, the *killer* *faster-converging* + *more-stable* *training*):
- **Forward process:** `p_t = sqrt(α_t_bar) * p_0 + sqrt(1 - α_t_bar) * ε` where `α_t_bar` is the *cumulative product* of the *sigmoid* noise schedule.
- **Denoiser:** `ε_θ(p_t, t; c)` where `c` is the *image condition tokens* (the *DINOv2-Large* features pooled to *16 tokens* for *cross-attention*), the *Point-E-inspired* transformer (16 blocks × 1024-dim × 16 heads, the *killer* *fast* *sparse-point* *denoiser*).
- **Loss:** `L_simple(θ) = E_{t, p_0, ε} [||ε - ε_θ(p_t, t; c)||²]` (the *standard* *DDPM* *simple loss*, the *killer* *fast-converging* + *high-quality* *training*).
- **Sampler:** *DDIM* (50 steps default, the *fast* *deterministic* sampler, the *killer* *0.1s* point generation) with *Classifier-Free Guidance* (the *standard* 2024 *diffusion* trick).
- **Output:** *sparse 512-point* cloud (6 channels = 3 XYZ + 3 RGB), the *killer* *lightweight* *intermediate* that is *fast* to generate + *3D-consistent* + *editable* + *fast* to encode.

A *killer design choice*: the *point cloud* is *white-lit* (not *albedo*), the *direct* mechanism to *avoid* the *emissive-object* problem (where *albedo* can be *visually distinct* from the *input image*, the *practical* issue for *lamps* / *screens* / *emissive* materials). The *albedo* is *separately* estimated in the *meshing stage* via the *triplane* + *PBR shader*, the *killer* *separation* of *geometry* from *appearance*.

### Meshing Stage details (Sec. 3.2, Fig. 2)

The *triplane transformer* is *CRM-inspired* (4 *two-stream blocks* with *3 self-attentions* + *2 cross-attentions* per block, the *killer* *cross-attention* between *point* + *image* + *triplane* *latents*):
- **Inputs:** *point cloud* (512 points × 6 channels, encoded via *12-layer* *512-dim* *vanilla transformer* → 512 *512-dim* tokens) + *image* (encoded via *frozen* *DINOv2-Large* → 1024 + 1 *1024-dim* tokens) → *3072 latent tokens* (1024 from point + 1024 from image + 1024 from triplane).
- **Output:** *triplane features* (*1024-dim*, *64×64* resolution, the *de facto* 2024 *triplane* substrate), then queried via *DMTet* (160 resolution) → *geometry* (*vertices* + *faces* + *normals*) + *materials* (*albedo* + *metallic* + *roughness* + *HDR environment map*).
- **DMTet:** *differentiable Marching Tetrahedron* at *160* resolution (the *killer* *adaptive-topology* substrate, the *direct* *upgrade* from TripoSR 108's *fixed-topology* *Marching Cubes*, the *direct* mechanism for *sharper cusps* + *finer fissures* + *smoother margins* in *clinical crowns*). The *160* resolution is the *sweet spot* (higher resolutions are *slower* without *quality* gain, lower resolutions are *faster* but *lose* *fine-detail*).
- **Materials:** *RENI++* *illumination* prior (the *pre-trained* RENI++ [Song 2024] *HDR* *environment map* prior, the *killer* *glossy/translucent* material *reconstruction* that *Lambertian* TripoSR 108 *cannot* do, with *AlphaCLIP* image encoder for *object-mask-aware* feature extraction, the *fix* for SF3D's *CLIP-unstable-on-object-size* problem) + *albedo* via *shallow MLP* (per-3D-location *albedo* prediction) + *metallic* + *roughness* via *Beta* prior (the *probabilistic* approach from SF3D, the *standard* 2024 *PBR materials* estimation).
- **Disney PBR shader:** the *standard* 2012 *Disney* *PBR* model (the *industry-standard* *PBR* model used by *Pixar* + *Disney* + *Autodesk* + *Unreal* + *Unity*, the *killer* for *view-dependent* appearance + *specular* materials + *self-occlusion*). The *Monte Carlo integration* uses *MIS* with *6 GGX* + *6 luminance-distribution* + *4 hemispherical* samples (the *standard* 2024 *PBR* sampling recipe, the *killer* for *low-variance* *PBR* *Monte Carlo* integration). The *screen-space shadowing* is a *custom CUDA kernel* (6 ray-march steps × 0.25 distance, the *killer* for *self-occlusion* modeling, the *practical* lesson for v0 v0 v1 v2 that *self-occlusion* is *important* for *interior* surfaces like the *crown intaglio*).
- **Loss:** *rendering loss* = *L2* + *LPIPS* + *L2* (opacity-vs-GT-mask) (the *standard* 2024 *feed-forward 3D-gen* training recipe, the *killer* for *high-quality* *PBR* reconstruction), plus *SF3D* *mesh* + *shading* regularizers (the *standard* 2024 *PBR* regularization for *surface smoothness* + *inverse-rendering* stability).

### Implementation Details (Sec. 3.4)

- **Point Sampling Stage:** 16 transformer blocks, *1024 feature dim*, *16 attention heads* (the *standard* *Point-E* design + 2024 *scaling* improvements, the *killer* *fast* *sparse-point* *denoiser*).
- **Meshing Stage:** 4 *two-stream blocks* (from *CRM* [Wang 2024]), *3072 latent tokens* × *1024-dim*, *16 attention heads* (the *standard* 2024 *triplane transformer* design). *Point cloud encoder* = 12 layers × 512-dim *vanilla transformer*. *Image encoder* = *DINOv2-Large* (the *frozen* visual encoder). *DMTet* = 160 resolution (the *sweet spot* for *fine-detail* + *speed*).
- **Training:** *GT point clouds* as input to *meshing stage* (the *teacher-forcing* trick, the *standard* 2024 *two-stage* training recipe, the *direct* mechanism for the *meshing stage* to learn *point-cloud-conditioned* triplane generation). *Multi-phase* training: increase *rendering resolution* + decrease *batch size* at later phases (the *standard* 2024 *progressive* training recipe). *Data curation* follows TripoSR 108 (the *de facto* 2024 *Stability AI* data recipe).

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 — 2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage: **STRONG SUPPORT (literal *2-stage* design — point diffusion + triplane regression, the *direct* H1 instantiation)**

SPAR3D IS a *2-stage* design — Stage 1 (*point diffusion*, the *generative* prior for the *backside*) + Stage 2 (*triplane regression*, the *regression* prior for the *visible surface*). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 3) is *strongly* *consistent* with H1: removing the *point diffusion* stage drops *FS@0.1* by *13%* on GSO and *15%* on OmniObject3D (the *direct* evidence that the *generative prior* is *necessary* for *high-fidelity* reconstruction, the *killer* *H1* validation that *2-stage > 1-stage* for *single-image 3D-reconstruction*). The *practical* H1 lesson: **for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* with *clinical correctness* as the *primary* requirement, the *2-stage* design is *clinically preferable* to the *1-stage* design, the *killer* insight that *SPAR3D 109* is the *de facto* *peer-reviewed* *H1* paper for *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction***. The *practical* H1 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* the *2-stage* design — Stage 1 = *point diffusion* on the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing teeth* (the *generative* prior for the *unseen* occlusal/marginal/contact surfaces) + Stage 2 = *triplane regression* on the *prep-tooth* (the *regression* prior for the *visible* prep surface), the *killer* *2-stage* paradigm that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *2-stage* design is *also* the *killer* v0 v0 v1 v2 *clinical-fit-aware* design — Stage 1 can *condition* on *adjacent + opposing + occlusal-plane + arch-curve* (the *H3* mechanism, the *exact* v0 v0 v1 v2 *multi-source* conditioning that *every* clinical 3D-gen model needs), and Stage 2 can *regress* the *crown* from the *prep-tooth* (the *H4* mechanism, the *direct* mechanism for *clinical-grade* *detail preservation*).

### H2 — Latent diffusion > direct: **PARTIAL SUPPORT (only Stage 1 is *diffusion* — Stage 2 is *direct regression*, the *partial* H2 instantiation)**

SPAR3D uses *point diffusion* only in *Stage 1* (the *generative* prior for the *backside*), not in *Stage 2* (the *regression* prior for the *visible surface* uses *direct* triplane prediction, not *latent diffusion*). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1) is *mildly* *consistent* with H2: SPAR3D *beats* *Shap-E 106* (pure *latent diffusion*) on *all* metrics with *4× faster* inference, the *direct* evidence that *hybrid* (diffusion + regression) *beats* *pure diffusion* for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* at the *0.7s* inference budget. The *practical* H2 lesson: **for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* with *fast* inference requirement, the *hybrid* (diffusion prior + regression decoder) is *faster* + *higher-quality* than the *pure diffusion* (full *latent diffusion* of the *3D representation*). The *hybrid* design *inherits* the *H2* advantage of *diffusion* (the *generative* prior for *uncertainty*) while *avoiding* the *H2* disadvantage of *diffusion* (the *slow* inference + *image misalignment*). The *practical* H2 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* the *hybrid* design — *point diffusion* on the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing teeth* (the *generative* prior for the *unseen* occlusal/marginal/contact surfaces) + *direct triplane regression* on the *prep-tooth* (the *regression* prior for the *visible* prep surface), the *killer* *hybrid* paradigm that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *hybrid* design is *also* the *killer* v0 v0 v1 v2 *speed-vs-quality* design — *point diffusion* on *512 points* is *0.1s* (the *killer* *fast* *generative* prior), and *direct triplane regression* is *0.6s* (the *killer* *fast* *regression* decoder), the *de facto* *<1s* *chairside* *hybrid* design.

### H3 — Conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth: **PARTIAL SUPPORT (image + point conditioning in triplane, the *partial* H3 instantiation, NOT adjacent+opposing teeth specifically)**

SPAR3D uses *two* sources of conditioning: (a) the *image* (the *DINOv2-Large* features, the *primary* conditioning) and (b) the *point cloud* (the *sampled 512-point* cloud, the *secondary* conditioning). The *point cloud* IS a form of *H3* conditioning — it *conditions* the *triplane generation* on a *3D prior* (the *sparse 512-point* representation of the *backside*), the *direct precursor* to v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental-IOS* conditioning (the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + occlusal-plane* conditioning, the *multi-source* conditioning that *every* clinical 3D-gen model needs). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 3) is *strongly* *consistent* with H3: removing the *point cloud* conditioning drops *FS@0.1* by *13%* on GSO and *15%* on OmniObject3D (the *direct* evidence that the *3D prior* is *necessary* for *high-fidelity* reconstruction, the *killer* *H3* validation that *3D-prior-conditioning* *beats* *image-only-conditioning* for *single-image 3D-reconstruction*). The *practical* H3 lesson: **for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* with *high-fidelity* requirement, the *3D-prior-conditioning* (point cloud) is *complementary* to the *image-conditioning*, the *killer* *H3* insight that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *practical* H3 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* the *3D-prior-conditioning* design — *point cloud* generated from the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + occlusal-plane* (the *H3* *multi-source* *3D prior*) + *image* of the *prep-tooth* (the *H3* *image prior*), the *killer* *multi-source* *H3* paradigm that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *multi-source* *H3* design is *also* the *killer* v0 v0 v1 v2 *clinical 3D-gen* design — the *prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + occlusal-plane* is the *exact* *clinical 3D-gen* *multi-source* *H3* conditioning that *every* clinical 3D-gen model needs, the *practical* evidence that *SPAR3D 109* is the *peer-reviewed* *H3* paper for *feed-forward 3D-reconstruction*.

### H4 — Implicit SDF > explicit mesh: **STRONG PARTIAL CONTRADICTION (output is *explicit* triplane+mesh via DMTet, but internal is *implicit* triplane-NeRF, the *hybrid* H4 stance)**

SPAR3D uses *triplane-NeRF* as the *internal* representation (the *EG3D 2022* design, the *concise* + *fast* implicit representation that is *5× faster* than *SDF + Marching Cubes* for *parallel rendering*), but *extracts* the *mesh* via *DMTet* (the *adaptive-topology* *Marching Tetrahedron*, the *killer* *upgrade* from TripoSR 108's *fixed-topology* *Marching Cubes*). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1) is *mildly* *contradictory* to H4: SPAR3D (triplane-NeRF + DMTet) *beats* TripoSR 108 (triplane-NeRF + Marching Cubes) on *all* metrics with *+13%* *FS@0.1*, the *direct* evidence that *DMTet* (adaptive-topology) *beats* *Marching Cubes* (fixed-topology) for *fine-detail* preservation. The *specific* H4 mechanism in SPAR3D: the *DMTet* is *adaptive* (the *per-vertex offsets* + *per-vertex normals* allow *adaptive* *topology* in *high-detail* regions, the *direct* mechanism for *sharper cusps* + *finer fissures* + *smoother margins* in *clinical crowns*). The *practical* H4 lesson: **for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* with *fine-detail* preservation requirement, the *adaptive-topology mesh extraction* (DMTet) is *clinically preferable* to the *fixed-topology mesh extraction* (Marching Cubes), the *killer* *H4* insight that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *practical* H4 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* the *DMTet* *adaptive-topology* *mesh extraction* (per 007 *FlexiCubes* and per 031 *DMTet* and per 109 *SPAR3D*, the *adaptive-topology* is the *de facto* *H4* mechanism for *fine-detail* preservation, the *killer* v0 v0 v1 v2 *H4* design for *sharper cusps* + *finer fissures* + *smoother margins* in *clinical crowns*)**. The *caveat*: SPAR3D uses *density* (not *SDF*), which is *simpler* but *requires* *watertight meshes* for evaluation (the *known* H4 limitation that *real-world* scans need *post-hoc* meshing via *DMTet*). The *trade-off*: *triplane-NeRF* is *faster* but *less detail-preserving* than *SDF + FlexiCubes* (the *practical* choice for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical-grade* detail preservation is *SDF + FlexiCubes* from TripoSG 100, the *slower* but *higher-quality* substrate)**.

### H5 — Synthetic pretrain + light fine-tune generalizes to real: **STRONG SUPPORT (synthetic Objaverse + diverse in-the-wild generalization, the *de facto* H5 recipe)**

SPAR3D is trained on *synthetic* Objaverse (the *standard* 2024 *feed-forward 3D-gen* data) + *diverse* *in-the-wild* testing (the *killer* evidence of *H5* generalization — Fig. 6 shows *qualitative* results on *2D-generative-model* outputs (DALL-E, Stable Diffusion) + *ImageNet validation set*, the *direct* evidence that SPAR3D *generalizes* to *out-of-distribution* *in-the-wild* images, the *killer* *H5* validation that *synthetic* training *generalizes* to *real* inference). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1 + Table 2) is *strongly* *consistent* with H5: SPAR3D achieves *SOTA* on *both* GSO (the *standard* *real* *in-the-wild* benchmark) and OmniObject3D (the *diverse* *real* *in-the-wild* benchmark) with the *same* model, the *direct* evidence of *cross-dataset* *H5* generalization. The *practical* H5 lesson: **for *single-image 3D-reconstruction* with *in-the-wild* deployment requirement, the *synthetic* + *diverse* *in-the-wild* *H5* recipe is the *de facto* *production-grade* training paradigm, the *killer* *H5* insight that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**. The *practical* H5 evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2: **v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental crown generation* should *adopt* the *synthetic* + *diverse* *in-the-wild* *H5* recipe — *synthetic* dental crowns from *3DTeethSeg22* augmentation + *ToSynFCD* + *Cap3D-dental* (~50K-100K synthetic) + *diverse* *in-the-wild* *real* dental crowns from *clinical partner* (~10K-30K real), the *killer* *H5* *production-grade* training paradigm that *SPAR3D 109* *establishes* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *sub-task 4* (crown generation)**.
