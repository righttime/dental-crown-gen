## TL;DR

> **Michelangelo** (Zhao, Liu, Chen et al., **ShanghaiTech U + Tencent PCG + Fudan U**, arXiv:2306.17115 v2 3 Jul 2023, **NeurIPS 2023**) introduces the *first* **3D shape generation model that *aligns* 3D shape, 2D image, and text in a *unified latent space* via contrastive learning** (the *SITA-VAE* = *Shape-Image-Text-Aligned Variational Auto-Encoder*), then *generates* 3D shapes from the aligned latent space via a *UNet-like transformer latent diffusion model* (the *ASLDM* = *Aligned Shape Latent Diffusion Model*). The pipeline: (1) **SITA-VAE** — a *perceiver-based transformer* encoder (1 cross-attention + 8 self-attention blocks, 513 learnable queries = 1 head + 512 local, projects to R^{512×64} via linear layer) compresses 3D point clouds into *shape embeddings*, while a *transformer-based implicit field decoder* (16 self-attention + 1 cross-attention blocks) reconstructs the *occupancy field* from these embeddings; the *alignment* with CLIP-ViT-L-14 (frozen) image+text embeddings is enforced via *symmetric contrastive loss* (ℒ_{(shape,image)} + ℒ_{(shape,text)}, both InfoNCE, λ_c=0.1 in paper, the *standard* CLIP-style contrastive loss adapted to *3 modalities*); the *occupancy reconstruction* is BCE + KL (λ_KL=0.001) with the *neural implicit field* decoder; (2) **ASLDM** — a *UNet-like transformer* with 13 self-attention blocks + *skip-connections*, conditioned on *CLIP image or text* via cross-attention (the *standard* 2023 latent-diffusion architecture), with *DDIM 50-step* sampling, generating 3D shapes in *~10 seconds*. Trained on **ShapeNet** (50K, 55 categories, 3DILG protocol) + **Cartoon Monster** (811, 615/71/125 split, custom dataset) for *5 days* on *8 Tesla V100 GPUs*, with image data from *4 views* + *ControlNet-depth* augmentation and text data from *shape tag + 65 ULIP-style predefined phrases*. Achieving **IoU 0.966 overall** (vs OccNet 0.825, ConvOccNet 0.888, IF-Net 0.934, 3DILG 0.950, 3DS2V 0.955 — the *best* in 55 categories) and **P-FID 1.586 image-cond / 2.075 text-cond** (vs 3DS2V 2.921/5.704, 3DILG 4.592/10.283 — the *best* in both image and text conditions), with **~10s** end-to-end inference per shape, **SITA-VAE + ASLDM** is the *founder* of the *shape-image-text aligned latent* paradigm for 3D generation, the *technical foundation* that **CLAY 103 + TripoSG 100 + Hunyuan3D 2.0 098 + Direct3D-S2 102** all *inherit*. The *three killer contributions* are: **(1) Alignment-before-generation** — the *first* 3D-gen paper to *align* 3D shape, 2D image, and text in a *unified latent space* via *symmetric InfoNCE contrastive loss* (the *novel* training objective that *closes* the 3D↔2D↔text domain gap *before* any diffusion happens, the *founder* insight of the *aligned-shape* 3D-gen paradigm), **(2) Perceiver-based transformer VAE** — the *first* 3D VAE to use a *perceiver-based transformer* encoder (1 cross-attention + 8 self-attention blocks) with 513 learnable query tokens (1 head + 512 local) that compresses a point cloud into a *set of latent embeddings* (the *vecset* pattern that *all* subsequent 2024-2026 3D-gen foundation models inherit), and **(3) Transformer-based implicit field decoder** — the *first* 3D-gen decoder to use *16 self-attention + 1 cross-attention blocks* with *cross-attention* from query 3D points to shape embeddings for *occupancy prediction* (the *standard* 3DGen implicit-field decoder that CLAY scales to 24 self-attention). The *practical v0 relevance* is *direct* and *foundational*: **Michelangelo's *perceiver-based transformer VAE* is the *direct technical predecessor* of CLAY 103's vecset VAE, the *direct technical predecessor* of TripoSG 100's vecset VAE, the *direct technical predecessor* of Hunyuan3D 2.0 098's vecset VAE, and the *direct technical predecessor* of Direct3D-S2 102's vecset VAE — understanding Michelangelo's VAE design is *necessary* to understand the *vecset* paradigm that *all* of v0 v1+ v2 dental 3D-gen models can inherit**, but the **GPL-3.0 license is a *major* roadblock for v0 v1 commercial deployment** (the *only* 2023-2026 3D-gen paper with GPL-3.0 in the reading list, the *most-restrictive* license in the 3D-gen arc, would require either a *commercial license* from Tencent PCG or a *reimplementation* of the architecture from scratch, the *practical* consequence is that Michelangelo's *code* cannot be *vendored* into v0 v1's commercial product without *legal review* and likely *reimplementation*).

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *How can we build a 3D generation method that (a) handles the *significant distribution gap* between 3D shape space, 2D image space, and text space (the *fundamental* 3D-gen challenge of 2023), (b) generates *high-quality* 3D shapes with *fine details* (vs the *over-smooth* or *noisy* results of prior 2022-2023 3D-gen methods), (c) supports *both* text and image conditions, (d) generates *diverse* 3D shapes (vs the *low-diversity* results of 3DILG), and (e) avoids the *per-asset 3D fitting* cost that LN3Diff / LION / 3DILG require?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** **A novel framework for cross-modal 3D shape generation that involves *aligning 3D shapes with 2D images and text* — introducing a new 3D shape representation that can reconstruct high-quality 3D shapes from latent embeddings and incorporate *semantic information* by aligning 3D shapes, 2D images, and text in a *compatible space*. This aligned space effectively closes the domain gap between the shape latent space and the image/text space, making it easier to learn a better probabilistic mapping from the image or text to the aligned shape latent space. As a result, our proposed method generates higher-quality and more diverse 3D shapes with greater semantic consistency that conform to the conditional image or text inputs.**

The key insight is that **the *alignment-before-generation* paradigm is the *fundamental* solution to the 3D↔2D↔text domain gap problem** — prior 2022-2023 3D-gen methods (SDFusion, 3DShape2VecSet, 3DILG) tried to *directly* learn a conditional diffusion model from text/image to 3D shape latent, but the *domain gap* (3D is *ordered* while 2D image is *grid* and text is *sequence*, the *geometric* vs *photometric* vs *linguistic* representations are *fundamentally different*) causes *inconsistent* results. Michelangelo's *alignment* approach *solves* this by *first* learning a *unified latent space* where 3D shape, 2D image, and text are *mutually aligned* (via *symmetric InfoNCE contrastive loss*), and *then* learning a diffusion model in the *aligned* space (which is *easier* because the *domain gap* is *already closed*). This *alignment-before-generation* paradigm is the *founder* insight of the *aligned-shape* 3D-gen paradigm that *every* subsequent 2024-2026 3D-gen foundation model *inherits* (the *closest* inheritor is UniMesh 088 which uses BAGEL's 2D+text alignment as a *cross-model* conditioning mechanism; the *most-similar* 2023 paper is CLIP-Forge which uses *invertible normalizing flow* for CLIP image/text → shape alignment but *without* the *3D-2D-text* joint alignment).

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 3, Fig. 1)

Michelangelo is a *two-stage* alignment-before-generation pipeline:

**Stage 1 — SITA-VAE (Shape-Image-Text-Aligned Variational Auto-Encoder, Sec. 3.1):** A *perceiver-based transformer* encoder-decoder with the following design choices:

1. **CLIP image+text encoders (frozen, ℰ_i + ℰ_t):** The pre-trained CLIP-ViT-L-14 image and text encoders are *frozen* during training and inference. They take 2D image `I ∈ ℝ^{H×W×3}` and tokenized text `T ∈ ℝ^{L_t × d_t}` and produce image tokens `E_i ∈ ℝ^{(1+L_i)×d}` and text tokens `E_t ∈ ℝ^{L_t × d}`. The CLIP projection heads (ℱ_i, ℱ_t) are *also* frozen and used to project the CLIP tokens to a *common* shape dimension for the contrastive loss.

2. **3D shape encoder (trainable, ℰ_s, Sec. 3.1):** A *perceiver-based transformer* with **1 cross-attention block + 8 self-attention blocks** (12 heads, 64 dim per head, 3072-dim FFN, GELU, Pre-LN, the *standard* perceiver/transformer configuration). The encoder first samples N points `P ∈ ℝ^{N×(3+C)}` from the surface of 3D shapes (N=8192 in paper, C=0 for xyz-only), then projects them via a linear layer (Fourier positional encoded) to `X ∈ ℝ^{N×d}` (d=768). The encoder uses a *cross-attention layer* to inject the 3D shape information from `X` into a series of *learnable query tokens* `Q ∈ ℝ^{(1+L_s)×d}` where `1+L_s = 513` (1 head-class token + 512 local tokens), then *8 self-attention blocks* iteratively improve the feature representation and output the final *shape embeddings* `E_s ∈ ℝ^{513×768}`. A *learnable shape projection layer* projects `E_s` to `E_s' ∈ ℝ^{512×64}` (the *compact* VAE space) — note the *head-class token* is *dropped* from the VAE space and *kept* for the *multi-modal contrastive loss* (the *clean* separation between the *contrastive* role and the *VAE* role).

3. **3D shape decoder (trainable, 𝒟_s, Sec. 3.1):** A *transformer-based implicit field decoder* with **16 self-attention blocks + 1 final cross-attention block** (same hyperparams as encoder, the *deliberate asymmetric* design that *concentrates* capacity on the *decoding* side where the *high-quality geometry* is reconstructed, the *Michelangelo* paradigm that CLAY 103 inherits as *24 self-attention + 1 cross-attention* and TripoSG 100 inherits as *16 self-attention*). The decoder takes the *shape embeddings* `E_s ∈ ℝ^{513×768}` and a *query 3D point* `x ∈ ℝ^3` (Fourier positional encoded to `X_q ∈ ℝ^{d}`), then *cross-attends* `X_q` to `E_s` to predict the *occupancy* `𝒪(x) = 𝒟(x | E_s)`. The decoder is *trained* with *binary cross-entropy (BCE) loss* against the *ground truth* occupancy field (the *standard* occupancy loss for neural fields, simpler than SDF but *requires watertight meshes* — the *practical* constraint of Michelangelo).

4. **Alignment loss (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 1-3):** The *symmetric InfoNCE contrastive loss* between (shape ↔ image) and (shape ↔ text) embeddings:
   ```
   ℒ_SITA = λ_c (ℒ_{(shape,image)} + ℒ_{(shape,text)}) + ℒ_r + λ_KL ℒ_KL
   ```
   where `ℒ_{(shape,image)} = ℒ_{(shape,text)} = -1/2 Σ_{(j,k)} (log exp(e_s^j · e_i^k) / Σ_l exp(e_s^j · e_i^l) + log exp(e_s^j · e_i^k) / Σ_l exp(e_s^l · e_i^k))` is the *standard* symmetric InfoNCE (a.k.a. CLIP loss), `ℒ_r` is the BCE reconstruction loss, and `λ_c = 0.1` (contrastive weight), `λ_KL = 0.001` (KL weight) are the *trade-off* hyperparameters. The *negative pairs* are the other shapes in the same training batch (the *standard* CLIP-style negative sampling).

**Stage 2 — ASLDM (Aligned Shape Latent Diffusion Model, Sec. 3.2):** A *UNet-like transformer* latent diffusion model with the following design choices:

1. **UNet-like transformer (Sec. 3.2):** A *standard* 2023 latent diffusion architecture with **13 self-attention blocks** (no encoder-decoder split, just 13 *equal* blocks with *long skip-connections* between block `i` and block `12-i` for the first 6 pairs, the *standard* U-ViT 2023 design that *every* 2023 3D-gen paper inherits). 12 heads, 64 dim per head, 3076-dim FFN (the *same* configuration as SITA-VAE, the *deliberate symmetric* design that *simplifies* the *joint* training). The ASLDM operates in the *aligned shape latent space* `E_s ∈ ℝ^{512×64}` produced by SITA-VAE, with *DDPM noise* added to `E_s` (the *standard* 2023 latent diffusion training, *1000* timesteps, *linear* β-schedule).

2. **Conditioning (Sec. 3.2):** The condition `C` is the *CLIP image or text embedding* (the *frozen* CLIP-ViT-L-14 embedding, *not* the full token sequence but the *global* pooled embedding), which is *broadcasted* to *every* ASLDM block via *cross-attention* (the *standard* 2023 conditioning mechanism, the *same* paradigm as 3DILG and 3DShape2VecSet). *Classifier-free guidance* (CFG) is used at inference time with *10%* of training conditions *randomly dropped* (the *standard* 2023 CFG recipe), and the guidance scale `λ` is *tuned* on the validation set (the *practical* detail that *enables* the *high-fidelity* conditional generation).

3. **Sampling (Sec. 3.2):** *DDIM 50-step* sampling (the *fast* 2023 sampling recipe, *20×* faster than DDPM 1000-step, the *practical* inference speed that makes Michelangelo *production-viable*), generating a 3D shape embedding in *~5 seconds* and the *full* mesh (via SITA-VAE decoder + Marching Cubes) in *~10 seconds* (the *fastest* 3D-gen inference of the 2023 arc, the *direct* reason Michelangelo was *immediately* used in the 2023 3D-gen *product* arc).

### Training & inference (Sec. 4.1, Appendix A)

**Training data (Sec. 4.2):**
- **ShapeNet** (Chang et al. 2015): 50K manufactured meshes in 55 categories, with the *3DILG* train/val/test protocol (the *standard* 2023 3D-gen split, ~70%/15%/15% by default).
- **Cartoon Monster**: 811 custom-collected 3D shapes with *detailed structures*, 615/71/125 split (the *small* but *complex* dataset, the *test bed* for *geometric-detail* generation).
- **Text augmentation**: For each 3D shape, generate texts by *concatenating* the shape tag and a description in the format "a 3D model of (shape tag), in the style of (description)" or "a 3D model of (shape tag), (description)", plus 65 *ULIP-style* predefined phrases for *diversity* (the *standard* 2023 3D-gen text-augmentation recipe).
- **Image augmentation**: 4 camera poses per mesh, with *ControlNet-depth* (Zhang et al. 2023) for *rendering diversity* (the *first* 3D-gen paper to use *ControlNet* for *multi-view* image augmentation, the *direct* precursor to the *2D-projection-consistency* mechanism in 060 Diff-TRGN).

**Training compute (Sec. 4.1):**
- **8 Tesla V100 GPUs** for *5 days* (the *modest* training cost, ~$200-400 on Lambda, the *practical* training budget for *any* v0 v1 replication).
- **AdamW** optimizer, *learning rate 1e-4*, *weight decay 0.01* (the *standard* 2023 training recipe).
- **DDIM** 50-step sampling at inference (the *fast* 2023 sampling recipe).

**Inference (Sec. 4.1):**
- **~10 seconds** end-to-end per shape on a *single* V100 (the *fastest* 2023 3D-gen inference, the *direct* reason Michelangelo was *immediately* productizable).
- **Watertight meshes** via *Marching Cubes* at *isovalue 0.5* (the *standard* 2023 mesh extraction, the *necessary* post-processing for occupancy-based fields).

### Differences from 3DShape2VecSet (the *direct* predecessor)

The 103 CLAY note *claimed* Michelangelo is the "3DShape2VecSet+DiT" paper. This is *partially correct* but *understates* the *novelty*:

| Component | 3DShape2VecSet (ICCV 2023) | Michelangelo (NeurIPS 2023) | CLAY (SIGGRAPH 2024) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VAE encoder** | 6 self-attention blocks (no perceiver) | 1 cross-attention + 8 self-attention (perceiver) | 24 self-attention (multi-resolution) |
| **VAE latent dim** | 64 | 64 | 64 |
| **VAE decoder** | 6 self-attention + 1 cross-attention | 16 self-attention + 1 cross-attention | 24 self-attention + 1 cross-attention |
| **Multi-modal alignment** | ❌ (no alignment, just KL) | ✅ SITA-VAE (3-modality contrastive) | ❌ (just CLIP+DINOv2 frozen encoders) |
| **Diffusion model** | 12-block DiT (no skip-connections) | 13-block UNet-like transformer (with skip-connections) | 24-block DiT (with adaptive latent length) |
| **Conditioning** | Image only (CLIP) | Image OR text (CLIP, CFG) | Image + text (CLIP + DINOv2, multi-view) |
| **Sampling** | DDPM 1000-step | DDIM 50-step | DDIM 100-step |
| **Reconstruction loss** | BCE (occupancy) | BCE (occupancy) | BCE (occupancy) |

So Michelangelo is *not* "3DShape2VecSet+DiT" — it is a *substantive* *extension* with: (a) *perceiver-based* encoder (vs vanilla self-attention), (b) *3-modality alignment* (vs no alignment), (c) *UNet-like skip-connections* (vs no skip-connections), (d) *text+image* conditioning (vs image-only), and (e) *DDIM 50-step* (vs DDPM 1000-step). The *practical* lesson for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's *specific* contributions (perceiver encoder + 3-modality alignment + UNet-skip + DDIM + text+image conditioning) are the *exact* mechanisms that v0 v1+ v2 should *adopt* over 3DShape2VecSet's *baseline* design — the *vecset pattern* is *necessary but not sufficient*, the *3-modality alignment* is the *killer* differentiator**.

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 — 2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage: **STRONG PARTIAL SUPPORT**

Michelangelo IS a *2-stage* pipeline (SITA-VAE then ASLDM), and the *quantitative* evidence (Table 1, 2) is *consistent* with the *H1 hypothesis* in the reading list. *However*, the *specific* H1 mechanism is *novel* in Michelangelo: the *first stage* is *not just a VAE* but a *3-modality-aligned VAE*, the *second stage* is *not just a DDM* but an *aligned latent diffusion model*. So Michelangelo *supports* H1 in the *general* sense (2-stage > 1-stage) but *adds* the *specific* insight that the *alignment* in the *first stage* is *the* mechanism for the *second stage*'s *quality*. The *practical* H1 evidence for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's 2-stage + alignment is the *complete* H1 + H3 + H5 evidence (2-stage for quality, 3-modality alignment for semantic consistency, large-scale pre-training for generalizability)**, the *direct* template for v0 v1's *2-stage dental VAE + dental latent diffusion* design.

### H2 — Latent diffusion > direct: **STRONG SUPPORT**

Michelangelo is *literally* a *latent diffusion* model (ASLDM operates in the SITA-VAE latent space `E_s ∈ ℝ^{512×64}`, not on voxels or points directly), the *standard* 2023 H2 paradigm. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1 reconstruction IoU 0.966, *better* than 3DS2V 0.955 and 3DILG 0.950) *supports* H2 in the *general* sense. The *specific* H2 mechanism in Michelangelo: the *3-modality alignment* in SITA-VAE *enables* the *ASLDM* to *learn* the *aligned shape latent distribution* *faster* and *more accurately* than a *direct* 3D diffusion (e.g., Diffusion-SDF 004 or Point-Voxel Diffusion 012) because the *latent space* is *already* aligned with the *conditioning space* (image+text), the *first* 3D-gen paper to *explicitly* show this *alignment-acceleration* effect. The *practical* H2 evidence for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's *latent diffusion + alignment* is the *complete* H2 + H3 evidence, the *direct* template for v0 v1's *latent dental diffusion* design where the *latent* is *aligned* with the *clinical conditioning* (prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + clinical text)**.

### H3 — Conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth is the H3 mechanism: **STRONG SUPPORT (via 3-modality alignment)**

Michelangelo is the *only* 2023 3D-gen paper to *explicitly* solve the *3D-2D-text domain gap* via *3-modality alignment*, the *general* H3 mechanism that *every* subsequent 2024-2026 3D-gen foundation model *inherits*. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 2, *all 4 text-cond metrics* are *significantly better* than 3DS2V) *supports* H3 in the *text* and *image* conditioning direction. The *specific* H3 mechanism in Michelangelo: the *3-modality contrastive loss* `ℒ_{(shape,image)} + ℒ_{(shape,text)}` *closes* the *domain gap* between 3D shape, 2D image, and text, *enabling* the *latent diffusion* to *learn* a *better* conditional distribution. The *practical* H3 evidence for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's *3-modality alignment* is the *direct* template for v0 v1's *N-modality alignment* (shape + prep-tooth + adjacent + opposing + margin + clinical text), the *killer* mechanism for v0 v1's *multi-modal* dental conditioning**.

### H4 — Implicit SDF > explicit mesh: **STRONG SUPPORT (with caveat)**

Michelangelo IS an *implicit field* model (occupancy, the *binary* version of SDF), the *standard* 2023 H4 paradigm. The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1, IoU 0.966 *beats* every 2022-2023 baseline including *mesh-based* methods like AtlasNet, Occupancy Networks, ConvOccNet) *supports* H4 in the *general* sense. The *specific* H4 mechanism in Michelangelo: the *transformer-based implicit field decoder* (16 self-attention + 1 cross-attention) *predicts occupancy* at *any* query 3D point, the *continuous* representation that *enables* arbitrary-resolution mesh extraction via *Marching Cubes* (the *standard* 2023 H4 substrate). The *caveat*: Michelangelo uses *occupancy* (not *SDF*), which is *simpler* but *requires* *watertight meshes* for training (the *known* H4 limitation that TripoSG 100 *fixes* with *SDF* and *eikonal* regularization). The *practical* H4 evidence for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's *transformer-based implicit field decoder* is the *direct* template for v0 v1's *transformer-based SDF decoder* (the *Michelangelo 16+1 architecture* + *SDF instead of occupancy* + *eikonal regularization* from TripoSG 100, the *killer* combination for *clinical-grade* detail preservation)**.

### H5 — Synthetic pretrain + light fine-tune generalizes to real: **WEAK INDIRECT SUPPORT**

Michelangelo is *trained* on *ShapeNet + Cartoon Monster* (~51K assets), the *small* but *general* 3D-gen corpus, and *achieves* excellent *quantitative* results on the *ShapeNet* test set (the *general* H5 mechanism of *large-scale pre-training* + *zero-shot* generalization). The *quantitative* evidence (Table 1, 2) *supports* H5 in the *general* sense. *However*, Michelangelo does *not* test *cross-domain* transfer (ShapeNet → real-world scans), so the H5 evidence is *weak* compared to papers that *explicitly* test *cross-domain* (e.g., Sonata 084, Concerto 085, Utonia 086, AnchorFormer 011, UniMesh 088, TripoSG 100). The *practical* H5 evidence for v0 v1: **Michelangelo's *ShapeNet pre-training* + *fine-tuning* on a *dental* corpus is the *direct* H5 template for v0 v1's *dental 3D-gen foundation model*, but v0 v1 would *inheriting* TripoSG 100's *larger pre-training* (2M Objaverse(-XL) vs Michelangelo's 50K ShapeNet) + *dental fine-tuning* (30-50K dental meshes) is the *more practical* path than *inheriting* Michelangelo's *smaller pre-training***.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **513 learnable query tokens (1 head + 512 local)** — the *head-class token* is *kept* for the *multi-modal contrastive loss* (the *classification* role) and *dropped* from the *VAE space* (the *generation* role). This *clean separation* between *classification* and *generation* is *novel* and *cleaner* than 3DShape2VecSet's *single* set of 64 learnable queries (which *mixes* both roles). For v0 v1, the *parallel* design would be: *1 head token* for *per-tooth FDI classification* + *512 local tokens* for *per-tooth crown generation*, the *direct* mechanism for *multi-task* learning.

2. **Cartoon Monster dataset** — a *custom-collected* 811-shape dataset with *detailed structures*, the *test bed* for *geometric-detail* generation. The *small* but *complex* dataset is the *first* 3D-gen paper to *explicitly* include *out-of-distribution* shapes (the Cartoon Monster shapes are *non-ShapeNet*, *more complex* than ShapeNet chairs/tables, the *direct* test of *generalization*). For v0 v1, the *parallel* would be: a *small* but *complex* *clinical crown dataset* (50-100 *complex* clinical cases, the *out-of-distribution* test of *generalization* from ShapeNet/Michelangelo to *real clinical* crowns).

3. **ControlNet-depth image augmentation** — the *first* 3D-gen paper to use *ControlNet* (Zhang et al. 2023) for *multi-view* image augmentation. The *ControlNet-depth* augmentation *enriches* the *rendering diversity* (the *standard* 2023 *data augmentation* recipe for *text-to-image* applied to *3D-gen*), the *direct* precursor to the *2D-projection-consistency* mechanism in 060 Diff-TRGN. For v0 v1, the *parallel* would be: *ControlNet-margin* image augmentation for *dental crown* generation, the *killer* mechanism for *margin-line diversity* training.

4. **Shape tag + 65 ULIP-style predefined phrases** — the *text augmentation* recipe. The *standard* 2023 3D-gen text-augmentation is *concatenate* (shape tag + description), the *first* 3D-gen paper to *explicitly* use *ULIP's* 65 predefined phrases. The *practical* lesson: *text augmentation* is *necessary* for *text-cond 3D-gen* (the *small* ShapeNet text descriptions are *insufficient* for *generalization*, the *augmentation* *enables* the *text-cond P-FID* to *improve* by 63.6% over 3DS2V). For v0 v1, the *parallel* would be: *clinical description augmentation* (prep-tooth morphology + adjacent teeth + opposing teeth + clinical text prompt) with *50-100* predefined *clinical* phrases, the *killer* mechanism for *clinical text-cond* dental crown generation.

5. **5 days on 8 V100 GPUs** — the *modest* training cost. The *5 days × 8 V100* = *40 V100-days* = *~$200-400 on Lambda* is *practical* for *any* v0 v1 replication. The *practical* lesson: *Michelangelo is the most practical 2023 3D-gen model to *fine-tune* on dental data*, the *fastest* training iteration loop of the 2023 arc.

6. **GPL-3.0 license** — the *most-restrictive* license of any 2023-2026 3D-gen paper in the reading list. The *practical* consequence for v0 v1 *commercial* deployment: Michelangelo's *code* cannot be *vendored* into v0 v1's commercial product without *legal review* and likely *reimplementation* of the architecture from scratch (the *only* 2023 3D-gen paper with this *license restriction* in the reading list, the *key* reason v0 v1 should *adopt TripoSG 100 (MIT)* or *Hunyuan3D 2.0 098 (Tencent, more permissive)* instead of *Michelangelo* for *commercial* deployment).

7. **Cross-attention from query 3D points to shape embeddings (the *decoder* design)** — the *transformer-based implicit field decoder* uses *cross-attention* from *query 3D points* to *shape embeddings* for *occupancy prediction*, the *novel* 2023 3D-gen decoder design that CLAY 103 scales to 24 self-attention. The *practical* lesson: *cross-attention* is *necessary* for *high-quality* occupancy prediction (the *better* than *concatenation* + *MLP* approach of DeepSDF and DiGS), the *direct* mechanism for *clinical-grade detail preservation*.

8. **Asymmetric 8+16 design (encoder 1+8, decoder 16+1)** — the *deliberate asymmetric* design that *concentrates* capacity on the *decoding* side where the *high-quality geometry* is reconstructed. CLAY 103 *inverts* this to *24 self-attention* encoder and *24 self-attention + 1 cross-attention* decoder (the *more-symmetric* design for *multi-resolution VAE*), TripoSG 100 uses *8+16* (the *Michelangelo* design, the *most-validated* 2023 3D-gen encoder-decoder architecture). For v0 v1, the *8+16 asymmetric* design is the *most-validated* choice, the *direct* template for v0 v1's *dental VAE*.

9. **The "alignment-before-generation" paradigm name** — the *specific* paradigm name used in the paper's title and abstract, the *first* 3D-gen paper to *explicitly* name and *formalize* the *alignment-before-generation* paradigm. The *practical* lesson: the *paradigm name* is *useful* for v0 v1's *paper organization* (the *related work* section can be organized as *alignment-before-generation* vs *direct conditional* vs *optimization-based*, the *clean* taxonomy for v0 v1's *3D-gen literature review*).

10. **Limitations explicitly stated in Sec. 5 (Discussion and Conclusion):** (a) *needs ground truth 3D shapes* (the *3D data is an order of magnitude smaller than 2D*, the *fundamental* 3D-gen data-scarcity problem), (b) *occupancy field requires watertight meshes* (the *known* H4 limitation that TripoSG 100 *fixes* with *SDF*). The *honest* discussion of *limitations* is *rare* in 2023 3D-gen papers, the *direct* signal of the *paper's maturity*.
