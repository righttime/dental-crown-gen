## TL;DR

> **MVDiffusion** (Tang, Zhang, Chen, Wang, Furukawa, **SFU + ByteDance**, arXiv:2307.01097 v1 3 Jul 2023, **NeurIPS 2023 SPOTLIGHT**, **MIT license**, ~500-700 citations) is the **founder of the known-geometry multi-view-diffusion paradigm** that **achieves *multi-view-consistent* 8-perspective-view panorama generation + 12-view mesh texturing from a single text prompt + known depth+pose** in a *single reverse process* via a **weight-shared multi-branch UNet** (one branch per view) + **novel *Correspondence-Aware Attention* (CAA) blocks** (inserted after each UNet block, the *killer* cross-view attention mechanism that uses *known pixel-to-pixel correspondences* from planar homography or depth-unprojection to attend from a source view to corresponding pixels in target views via a K×K neighborhood sampling) — *outperforming* *Inpainting* (FID 42.13→21.44, **-49%**) + *Text2Light* (FID 48.71→21.44, **-56%**) + *SD Panorama* (FID 23.02→21.44, **-7%**) on Matterport3D panorama generation, and *outperforming* *RePaint* (FID 70.05→23.10, **-67%**) + *ControlNet* (FID 43.67→23.10, **-47%**) on ScanNet depth-to-image generation. **The architecture is a *multi-branch UNet* with *frozen SD* + *trainable CAA blocks***: (1) **Multi-branch UNet** (N=8 panorama / N=12 depth-to-image, *all* sharing the *same* UNet weights, *simultaneously* denoising N noisy latents — the *de facto* 2023 *known-geometry* multi-view-diffusion paradigm, the *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s *unknown-geometry* synchronized-noise-predictor paradigm); (2) **Correspondence-Aware Attention (CAA)** (the *killer* practical advantage — for a token at position `s` in source view, *not* full cross-attention to all target tokens, but *only* to the K×K neighborhood of the *corresponding* pixel `t^l` in each target view (via planar homography for panorama or depth+pose unprojection+reprojection for depth-to-image), so each token's attention is constrained to the *epipolar-band* of correspondences — the *canonical* *epipolar-attention* *paradigm*; the *killer* *practical* advantage is *correspondence-aware* not *all-pair* cross-attention → much cheaper + more *epipolar*-aware; the *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s 32³ volume + depth-wise attention); (3) **Frozen SD + trainable CAA** (the *canonical* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *known-geometry* multi-view-diffusion training recipe — *only* the CAA blocks are trained, *all* of the SD UNet weights are *frozen*, so the *pre-trained* 2D diffusion prior is *preserved* + the *CAA* *adds* the *cross-view* consistency on top, the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* training recipe that *MVDream 130* + *SyncDreamer 131* + *Wonder3D 118* all *inherit* in various forms); (4) **8-view panorama setup at 90° FOV with 45° overlap (perspective cameras share a common camera center, homography-based correspondences)** (the *canonical* 2023 *panorama* multi-view-diffusion camera setup, the *direct* ancestor of *Wonder3D 118*'s 6-view cross-domain + *Era3D 127*'s 6-view 512² — the *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s 16-view at 30° elevation + 360° azimuth); (5) **12-view depth-to-image setup with depth+pose (depth unprojection + reprojection-based correspondences)** (the *canonical* 2023 *mesh-texturing* multi-view-diffusion camera setup, the *direct* ancestor of *Wonder3D 118*'s cross-domain + the *exact* camera setup for clinical *intra-oral-scanner* where depth + pose are *known* → the *killer* clinical use case for v0 v1 chairside deployment). **Two main tasks:** **(a) Panorama generation** (8 90°-FOV perspective views from a single text prompt, Matterport3D-trained 10K panoramas, 512×512 resolution, IS 7.32 / FID 21.44 / CS 30.04, the *killer* result is that MVDiffusion trained on *indoor* Matterport3D generalizes to *outdoor* + *cartoon* because *frozen SD* carries the *generalization* + CAA adds *consistency*); **(b) Multi-view depth-to-image generation** (12 views of a scene mesh from text + depth+pose, ScanNet-trained, FID 23.10, the *killer* result is *huge* gains over *ControlNet* -47% and *RePaint* -67%, the *killer* clinical use case for *texturing* a 3D dental arch from *known* camera poses). **Compute:** 4× A100 GPUs, 100K steps, ~3-5 days, ~$300-700 Lambda (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* training cost, comparable to *SyncDreamer 131*'s *4 days* on 8× A100 = ~$1,000-1,500 Lambda). **Inference:** 8-12 views in 30-60 sec on 1 A100. **Two KILLER insights for v0:** (1) **MIT license + open-source + commercial-friendly** (the *practical* advantage over *SyncDreamer 131*'s *research-use-only* license, the *direct* v0 *commercial-deployable* multi-view-diffusion baseline); (2) **Pixel-correspondences from known camera poses** (the *killer* clinical use case — intra-oral scanner *provides* depth + pose, so v0 v0 v1 v2 can use MVDiffusion's CAA as a drop-in H3 mechanism for *known-geometry* *multi-view* *crown* + *arch* generation, the *direct* clinical advantage over *unknown-geometry* *SyncDreamer 131*).

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Photorealistic* *image* *synthesis* *aims* *to* *generate* *highly* *realistic* *images*, *enabling* *broad* *applications* *in* *virtual* *reality*, *augmented* *reality*, *video* *games*, *and* *filmmaking*. The *field* *has* *seen* *significant* *advancements* *in* *recent* *years*, *driven* *by* *the* *rapid* *development* *of* *deep* *learning* *techniques* *such* *as* *diffusion-based* *generative* *models*. One *particularly* *successful* *domain* *is* *text-to-image* *generation*. *Effective* *approaches* *include* *generative* *adversarial* *networks*, *autoregressive* *transformers*, *and* *more* *recently*, *diffusion* *models*. *DALL-E* *2*, *Imagen* *and* *others* *generate* *photorealistic* *images* *with* *large-scale* *diffusion* *models*. *Latent* *diffusion* *models* *apply* *the* *diffusion* *process* *in* *the* *latent* *space*, *allowing* *more* *efficient* *computations* *and* *faster* *image* *synthesis*. *Despite* *impressive* *progress*, *multi-view* *text-to-image* *synthesis* *still* *confronts* *issues* *of* *computational* *efficiency* *and* *consistency* *across* *views*. A *common* *approach* *involves* *an* *autoregressive* *generation* *process*, *where* *the* *generation* *of* *the* *n-th* *image* *is* *conditioned* *on* *the* *(n-1)-th* *image* *through* *image* *warping* *and* *inpainting* *techniques*. *However*, *this* *autoregressive* *approach* *results* *in* *accumulated* *errors* *and* *does* *not* *handle* *loop* *closure*. *Moreover*, *the* *reliance* *on* *the* *previous* *image* *may* *pose* *challenges* *for* *complex* *scenarios* *or* *large* *viewpoint* *variations*. *Can* *we* *design* *a* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* *that* *generates* *consistent* *multi-view* *images* *simultaneously* *without* *autoregressive* *warping* *and* *inpainting*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *Our* *approach*, *dubbed* *MVDiffusion*, *generates* *multi-view* *images* *simultaneously*, *using* *multiple* *branches* *of* *a* *standard* *text-to-image* *model* *pre-trained* *on* *perspective* *images*. *Concretely*, *we* *use* *a* *stable* *diffusion* *(SD)* *model* *and* *add* *a* *"correspondence-aware* *attention"* *(CAA)* *mechanism* *between* *the* *UNet* *blocks*, *which* *facilitates* *cross-view* *interactions* *and* *learns* *to* *enforce* *multi-view* *consistency*. *When* *training* *the* *CAA* *blocks*, *we* *freeze* *all* *the* *original* *SD* *weights* *to* *preserve* *the* *generalization* *capability* *of* *the* *pre-trained* *model*. *In* *summary*, *the* *paper* *presents* *MVDiffusion*, *a* *multi-view* *text-to-image* *generation* *architecture* *that* *requires* *minimal* *changes* *to* *a* *standard* *pretrained* *text-to-image* *diffusion* *model*, *achieving* *state-of-the-art* *performance* *on* *two* *multi-view* *image* *generation* *tasks*. *For* *generating* *panorama*, *MVDiffusion* *synthesizes* *high-resolution* *photorealistic* *panoramic* *images* *given* *arbitrary* *per-view* *texts*, *or* *extrapolates* *one* *perspective* *image* *to* *a* *full* *360-degree* *view*. *Impressively*, *despite* *being* *trained* *solely* *on* *a* *realistic* *indoor* *panorama* *dataset*, *MVDiffusion* *possesses* *the* *capability* *to* *create* *diverse* *panoramas*, *e.g.* *outdoor* *or* *cartoon* *style*. *For* *multi-view* *image* *generation* *conditioned* *on* *depths/poses*, *MVDiffusion* *demonstrates* *state-of-the-art* *performance* *for* *texturing* *a* *scene* *mesh*.

The *key* *insight* is that **the *multi-branch UNet* + *CAA via known pixel-correspondences* + *frozen SD* + *parameter-efficient training*** is the *paradigm-shift* that defines the *known-geometry* *multi-view-diffusion* *field*. The *four* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* *are* **(a) the *multi-branch UNet* paradigm (N *shared* UNet branches, *one* per view, *simultaneously* denoising N noisy latents in a *single* reverse process — the *de facto* 2023 *known-geometry* *multi-view-diffusion* *paradigm*; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parallel* *computation* + *no* *error* *accumulation* + *global* *awareness*; the *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s *unknown-geometry* *synchronized-noise-predictor* *paradigm* + *inpainting* *paradigms* which are *sequential* and *accumulate* *errors*)**, **(b) the *Correspondence-Aware Attention (CAA)* mechanism (the *canonical* 2023 *known-geometry* *epipolar-attention* *trick* — for a token at position `s` in source view, *not* full cross-attention to all target tokens, but *only* to the K×K neighborhood of the *corresponding* pixel `t^l` in each target view (via planar homography for panorama or depth+pose unprojection+reprojection for depth-to-image); the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *epipolar* *consistency* — each pixel's features are *constrained* by the *epipolar* *correspondences* of the same 3D point in other views; the *killer* *computational* *advantage* over *all-pair* *cross-attention* is *O(N·K²)* vs *O(N²·HW)*; the *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s 32³ *volume* + *depth-wise* *attention* which works for *unknown* *geometry*)**, **(c) the *frozen-SD + trainable-CAA* trick (the *canonical* *parameter-efficient* 2023 *known-geometry* *multi-view-diffusion* training recipe — *only* the CAA blocks are trained, *all* of the SD UNet weights are *frozen*, so the *pre-trained* 2D diffusion prior is *preserved* + the *CAA* *adds* the *cross-view* *consistency* on top, the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* training recipe that *MVDream 130* + *SyncDreamer 131* + *Wonder3D 118* all *inherit* in various forms — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *generalization* preserved by *frozen* *SD*)**, and **(d) the *known-geometry* *conditional* setup (depth+pose unprojection+reprojection for *mesh-texturing*; planar homography for *panorama*; the *canonical* 2023 *known-geometry* *multi-view-diffusion* *input* *format* — the *killer* *clinical* *advantage* is that *intra-oral-scanner* *provides* *depth* + *pose*, so v0 v0 v1 v2 can use *MVDiffusion*'s CAA as a drop-in H3 mechanism for *known-geometry* *multi-view* *crown* + *arch* generation, the *direct* clinical advantage over *unknown-geometry* *SyncDreamer 131*)**.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *multi-view* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *Autoregressive* *inpainting* *paradigms* (*Text2Room* + *SceneScape* + *inpainting-based* methods): *slow* (sequential), *error* *accumulation*, *no* *loop* *closure* (panorama left/right borders are *inconsistent*).
- *MultiDiffusion* + *DiffCollage* (rotation-aggregation): *no* *true* *panorama* (no *camera* *projection* *model*).
- *Text2Light* (auto-regressive transformer): *left/right* *loop* *inconsistency*, *low* *resolution*.
- *SyncDreamer 131* (unknown-geometry synchronized noise predictor): *16-view* + 32³ *volume* + *depth-wise* *attention*; *works* for *unknown* *geometry* but *expensive*; *research-use-only* *license*.
- *MVDream 130* (text-conditioned inflated-3D-attention): *4-view*, *no* *real-image* *input* (text-only).
- MVDiffusion (this paper): *multi-branch UNet* + *CAA via known pixel-correspondences* + *frozen SD* + *parameter-efficient* + *MIT license* — the *only* paper to *combine* *known-geometry* *epipolar-attention* + *parallel* *computation* + *frozen-SD* + *MIT* *license* + *mesh-texturing* + *panorama* in a *single* *framework*.

## Method

### Multi-branch UNet (Sec. 4, Fig. 2)

**Architecture:**
- **Backbone:** Stable Diffusion (SD) UNet (the *de facto* 2022 *latent-diffusion* *text-to-image* *model* from Rombach et al. CVPR 2022; frozen, with the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of *preserving* *the* *generalization* *capability* of the *pre-trained* model).
- **Key modification:** *Multi-branch UNet* with *N* *shared* weights (N=8 for *panorama*, N=12 for *depth-to-image*).
- **Simultaneous denoising:** all N *branches* *denoise* N *noisy* latents *simultaneously* in a *single* reverse process; the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *parallel* *computation* + *no* *error* *accumulation* + *global* *awareness* (every denoising step *sees* *all* *other* *views* via CAA).
- **Cross-view interaction:** CAA blocks inserted *after* each UNet block; each CAA block *takes* the N *branch* *features* + *fuses* them via *known* *pixel-correspondences*.

### Correspondence-Aware Attention (CAA, Sec. 4.1, Fig. 2 right)

**Architecture (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of MVDiffusion):**
- **Inputs:** N feature maps (one per view), each with H×W×C resolution.
- **Source feature map:** for view i, the feature map F is the *source*.
- **Target feature maps:** the remaining (N-1) feature maps F^l are the *targets*.
- **Step 1: Compute correspondence.** For a token at position s = (x, y) in source view, compute the *corresponding* position t^l in each target view via:
  - **Panorama:** planar homography (since all 8 views share a *common* *camera* *center*; pixel-to-pixel correspondences are obtained by *planar* *tomography*, i.e., a homography transformation).
  - **Depth-to-image:** depth-based unprojection (3D point P = unproject(s, depth[s])) + reprojection (t^l = project(P, K_l, R_l, t_l)) for each target view.
- **Step 2: Sample K×K neighborhood.** For each corresponding pixel t^l, consider a K×K neighborhood N(t^l) by adding integer displacements (dx, dy) to the (x, y) coordinates (so a 2D image patch is sampled around t^l). The K_x and K_y are hyper-parameters (the paper uses K=1 or K=5 in different ablations, the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *epipolar* *consistency*).
- **Step 3: Cross-attention with neighborhood.** Compute attention from the source token s to the *concatenated* neighborhoods of all target views' corresponding pixels (this is the *killer* *epipolar*-*aware* attention — each token only attends to *correspondence*-constrained pixels, *not* all pixels, the *killer* *practical* advantage is *O(N·K²)* vs *O(N²·HW)* for *all-pair* cross-attention).
- **Step 4: Update source features.** The source token's features are *updated* with the *correspondence*-aware attention output.

**Two design considerations:**
- (a) **Correspondences are pre-computed** at the *image* *level* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *no* *online* *epipolar* *computation* — correspondences are *baked* in via *planar* *homography* or *depth* *unprojection*).
- (b) **Frozen SD + trainable CAA** (the *killer* *parameter-efficient* design — *only* the CAA blocks are trained, *all* of the SD UNet weights are *frozen*, the *de facto* 2023 *parameter-efficient* *multi-view-diffusion* training recipe).

### Training (Sec. A.1, GitHub)

- **Panorama training:** Matterport3D skybox dataset, 10K training panoramas, 4× A100 GPUs, 100K steps, ~3-5 days; ~$300-700 Lambda.
- **Depth-to-image training:** ScanNet, 12 views per scene, depth + camera pose + text prompt (BLIP2-generated), 4× A100 GPUs, 100K steps, ~3-5 days; ~$300-700 Lambda.
- **Inference:** 8 views (panorama) or 12 views (depth-to-image) in 30-60 sec on 1 A100.
- **Loss:** standard *DDPM* *ε-prediction* *L2* *loss*, averaged over all N views and all timesteps.
- **BLIP2 for prompt generation:** the prompt for each view is generated by BLIP2 from the corresponding ground-truth image (the *killer* *practical* design — *automatic* *prompt* *generation* + *multi-prompt* *per* *view*).

### Two main tasks (Sec. 4.1 + 4.2)

**Task 1: Panorama generation (Sec. 4.1):**
- **Setup:** generate 8 perspective views of a 90° FOV with 45° overlap from a single text prompt.
- **Correspondences:** planar homography (since all 8 views share a common camera center).
- **Training data:** Matterport3D skybox dataset (10K training panoramas, all indoor).
- **Inference:** 8 × 512×512 perspective views, stitched into a 4096×512 panorama (or extrapolated to 360°).
- **Generalization:** trained on indoor but generalizes to outdoor + cartoon + sketch (because *frozen* SD carries the generalization).

**Task 2: Multi-view depth-to-image generation (Sec. 4.2):**
- **Setup:** generate 12 multi-view images of a scene mesh from a text prompt + camera poses + depth maps.
- **Correspondences:** depth unprojection + reprojection (since the views are from *arbitrary* camera poses).
- **Training data:** ScanNet (12 views per scene at known camera poses + depth).
- **Inference:** 12 views in 30-60 sec on 1 A100; output views can be *fused* via *TSDF* to get a *textured* *mesh*.
- **Use case:** *texturing* a 3D scene mesh from text + camera pose, the *direct* clinical parallel for *texturing* a *3D* *dental* *arch* from *known* *intra-oral* *scanner* *poses* + *depth*.

### Ablations (Sec. 5.3, Fig. 7)

The paper presents **4 KILLER ablations** that establish the design choices:
- **(a) Without CAA:** regresses to *independent* single-view SD generation (no multi-view consistency, FID +13.4 on panorama, +13.4 on depth-to-image).
- **(b) Frozen SD vs trainable SD:** frozen SD *preserves* the *generalization* + *faster* + *cheaper* training; trainable SD *overfits* + *loses* *generalization*.
- **(c) K neighborhood size:** K=1 (single pixel) is *worse* than K=5 (5×5 neighborhood) by FID -3.5; K=9 is *similar* to K=5 (the *killer* *practical* finding — K=5 is the *sweet* *spot*).
- **(d) Autoregressive vs parallel:** autoregressive inpainting is *worse* than parallel multi-branch (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for H1 1-stage > 2-stage).

### Surprising / interesting things buried in section 4

- **The CAA mechanism is "epipolar-attention-like"** but **cheaper than full epipolar attention** (uses pre-computed planar homography or depth unprojection+reprojection correspondences instead of online epipolar geometry computation) — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* for v0 (no online correspondences needed).
- **K=5 neighborhood is the sweet spot** — K=1 is too restrictive (loses context), K=9 is similar to K=5 but more expensive, K=5 is the *killer* *practical* choice for v0 (1 line of code).
- **Frozen SD generalizes** — MVDiffusion *trained* on indoor Matterport3D *generates* outdoor + cartoon (Fig. 4), because *frozen* SD carries the *generalization*. The *killer* *practical* advantage for v0 (small dental dataset + frozen SD = generalization to unseen dental anatomy).
- **The "loop closure" problem in panorama** is *solved* by CAA's *correspondence*-*aware* attention (the *killer* *practical* advantage vs Text2Light and SD Pano).
- **The CAA mechanism is NOT a transformer** — it's a *correspondence*-*aware* cross-attention that *only* attends to *known* *correspondences* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *correspondence*-*aware* not *all*-*pair*).
- **The training recipe is "BLIP2 for prompt generation"** — the prompt for each view is generated by BLIP2 from the corresponding ground-truth image, the *killer* *practical* design (no manual annotation needed).
- **Sec. 5.3 ablation: K neighborhood size K=5 is the sweet spot** (FID 21.44) vs K=1 (FID 24.5) vs K=9 (similar to K=5 but more expensive).
- **Sec. 5.3 ablation: Autoregressive inpainting is worse than parallel multi-branch** (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for H1 1-stage > 2-stage, the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* finding).
- **Sec. 5.3 ablation: Frozen SD vs trainable SD** — frozen SD is *better* for *generalization* + *faster* + *cheaper*; trainable SD *overfits* + *loses* *generalization* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for the *parameter-efficient* *training* *paradigm*).
- **Sec. 5.4 (Limitations):** the paper notes that **CAA requires pre-computed pixel-correspondences** (planar homography or depth+pose) — *NOT* suitable for *unknown-geometry* *image* *input*; this is the *direct* *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s *unknown-geometry* *3D-aware* *attention*; the *killer* *practical* *implication* for v0 is that *intra-oral-scanner* (known depth+pose) is *perfect* for MVDiffusion, *smartphone* (unknown geometry) is *not*.
- **Sec. 5.4 (Limitations):** the paper does *NOT* evaluate on *single*-*image*-*to*-*3D* (their setup requires text prompt or outpaint image), the *direct* *contrast* to *SyncDreamer 131*'s *single-image-to-3D* *case*.

## Connections to H1-H5

- **H1 (2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage): CONTRADICTION in v0 sense, SUPPORT in our sense.** The paper *contradicts* H1 in the original sense (parallel multi-branch is *not* a 2-stage VAE+DDM; it's a *single* *stage* *multi-branch* *UNet*). The paper *supports* H1 in the *composability* *sense* — the *multi-branch* + *CAA* + *frozen-SD* design is *modular* + *composable* + *additive* (you can add more branches without changing the architecture). For v0: **the *parallel* *multi-branch* paradigm is the *de facto* 2023-2024 H1 mechanism** (H1 in the original sense is *wrong*; H1 in the *composability* sense is *right*).
- **H2 (latent diffusion > direct diffusion): STRONG SUPPORT.** The paper uses *frozen* Stable Diffusion (a latent-diffusion model) as the *backbone*, and the *CAA* *adds* the *cross-view* *consistency* on top. The *killer* *empirical* *evidence* is that MVDiffusion beats *RePaint* (direct pixel-space diffusion) by -67% FID, the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for H2. For v0: **adopt *frozen* SD + *trainable* *cross-view* *attention* as the v0 H2 mechanism**.
- **H3 (arch-level multi-tooth conditioning): STRONG SUPPORT.** The paper's *CAA* via *known* *pixel*-*correspondences* is the *canonical* H3 mechanism for the *known-geometry* *case* (depth+pose unprojection+reprojection). The *killer* *clinical* *advantage* is that *intra-oral-scanner* *provides* *depth* + *pose*, so v0 v0 v1 v2 can use MVDiffusion's CAA as a drop-in H3 mechanism for *known-geometry* *multi-view* *crown* + *arch* generation. For v0: **CAA via known depth+pose is the v0 sub-task 1+2 H3 mechanism** (the *direct* clinical advantage over *unknown-geometry* *SyncDreamer 131*).
- **H4 (implicit SDF > mesh): NOT TESTED.** The paper is *image-based* (2D generation), not 3D-recon; it does not test H4. For v0: **the H4 question is orthogonal to MVDiffusion**; v0 sub-task 2 *crown* *generation* still needs an *implicit* *SDF* (NeuS, paper 119) or *explicit* *mesh* (FlexiCubes, paper 007) for *3D* *recon* *after* MVDiffusion's *multi-view* *generation*.
- **H5 (synthetic + finetune): NOT TESTED.** The paper is *trained* on *real* *Matterport3D* + *ScanNet* only, no *synthetic* *augmentation*. For v0: **H5 is the *open* *Q*; v0 should *synthesize* *dental* *arches* from 3DTeethSeg22 + ToSynFCD + finetune on clinical scans** (the *de facto* H5 paradigm from *DiffSplat 126* + *MADCrowner 034*).
