## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D* *content* *creation* is an *important* *step* in the *pipeline* of *modern* *game* + *media* *industry*, yet it is a *labor-intensive* *task* that *requires* *well-trained* *designers* to *work* for *hours* or *days* to *create* a *single* *3D* *asset*. A *system* that can *generate* *3D* *content* in an *easy* *way* for *non-professional* *users* is *thus* of *great* *value*. *Existing* *3D* *object* *generation* *methods* can be *categorized* into *three* *types*: (1) *template-based* *generation* *pipeline*, (2) *3D* *generative* *models*, and (3) *2D-lifting* *methods*. *Due* to *limited* *accessible* *3D* *models* and *large* *data* *complexity*, *both* *template-based* *generators* and *3D* *generative* *models* *struggle* to *generalize* *effectively* to *arbitrary* *object* *generation*. *Their* *generated* *content* is *often* *confined* to *common* *real-world* *objects* with *relatively* *simple* *topology* and *texture*. *Yet* in *industry*, *popular* *3D* *assets* *usually* *come* as a *mixture* of *complicated*, *artistic*, and *sometimes* *non-realistic* *structures* and *styles*. *Recently*, *2D-lifting* *methods* have *shown* that *pre-trained* *2D* *generation* *models* can be *potentially* *applied* to *3D* *generation*. The *typical* *representations* are *Dreamfusion* + *Magic3D* *systems*, which *utilize* *2D* *diffusion* *models* as *supervision* for the *optimization* of a *3D* *representation* via *score* *distillation* *sampling* (SDS). *Trained* on *large-scale* *2D* *image* *datasets*, *these* *2D* *models* are *able* to *generate* *unseen* and *counterfactual* *scenes* *whose* *details* can be *specified* through a *text* *input*, *making* *them* *great* *tools* for *creating* *artistic* *assets*. *Nevertheless*, in *2D-lifting* *techniques*, *challenges* *arise* *due* to the *lack* of *comprehensive* *multi-view* *knowledge* or *3D-awareness* *during* *score* *distillation*. *These* *challenges* *encompass*: (1) The *multi-face* *Janus* *issue*: The *system* *frequently* *regenerates* *content* *described* by the *text* *prompt*. (2) *Content* *drift* *across* *different* *views*. The *multi-face* *issue* can *stem* from *various* *factors*. For *instance*, *certain* *objects*, *like* *blades*, may be *nearly* *invisible* from *some* *angles*. *Meanwhile*, *vital* *parts* of a *character* or *animal* might be *hidden* or *self-occluded* from *specific* *viewpoints*. *While* *humans* *assess* *these* *objects* from *multiple* *angles*, a *2D* *diffusion* *model* *cannot*, *leading* it to *produce* *redundant* and *inconsistent* *content*. Can we *develop* a *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* that *generates* *multi-view* *consistent* *content* to *fix* the *multi-face* + *content-drift* *failure* *modes* of *2D-lifting* *methods*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *In* *spite* of *all* the *weaknesses* of *2D-lifting* *methods*, *we* *believe* that *large-scale* *2D* *data* is *crucial* to *generalizable* *3D* *generation*. *Therefore*, *we* *propose* *multi-view* *diffusion* *models*, *which* can be *used* as a *multi-view* *3D* *prior* *agnostic* to *3D* *representations*. The *proposed* *model* *simultaneously* *generates* a *set* of *multi-view* *images* that are *consistent* with *each* *other*. It can *leverage* *pre-trained* *2D* *diffusion* *models* for *transfer* *learning* to *inherit* the *generalizability*. *Then*, by *jointly* *training* the *model* on *multi-view* *images* (*from* *3D* *assets*) and *2D* *image-text* *pairs*, *we* *find* that it can *achieve* *both* *good* *consistency* and *generalizability*. *When* *applied* to *3D* *generation* *through* *score* *distillation*, *our* *multi-view* *supervision* *proves* *significantly* *more* *stable* than *that* of *single-view* *2D* *diffusion* *models*. *And* *we* can *still* *create* *unseen*, *counterfactual* *3D* *contents* as *from* *pure* *2D* *diffusion* *models*. *Inspired* by *DreamBooth*, *we* can *also* *employ* *our* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* to *assimilate* *identity* *information* from a *collection* of *2D* *images* and it *demonstrates* *robust* *multi-view* *consistency* *after* *fine-tuning*. *Overall*, *our* *model*, *namely* *MVDream*, *successfully* *generates* *3D* *Nerf* *models* *without* the *multi-view* *consistency* *issue*. It *either* *surpasses* or *matches* the *diversity* *seen* in *other* *state-of-the-art* *methods*.

The *key* *insight* is that **the *inflated-3D-self-attention* + *camera-embedding-via-MLP* + *2-mode training* + *multi-view-SDS extension*** is the *paradigm-shift* that defines the 2023-2024 multi-view-diffusion field. The *four* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) the *inflated-3D-self-attention* paradigm (reshape `B×F×H×W×C` → `B×FHW×C` and use the original 2D self-attention weights from SD-2.1-base — the *canonical* 2023 *multi-view-attention* *paradigm* that *CAT3D 113* + *SyncDreamer 0.0261* + *Wonder3D 118* + *Era3D 127* + *Wonder3D++ 129* all inherit; *no new attention parameters* needed, just reshape — the *killer* practical insight that *parameter-efficient* multi-view attention is *better* than *new* 3D-attention-parameters)**, **(b) the *camera-embedding via 2-layer-MLP* on `R^{F×16}` extrinsic camera matrix (the *killer* H3 mechanism for *camera-conditioning* — *more general* than *Wonder3D 118*'s *sinusoidal(elevation, azimuth)* because it takes the *full 4×4 camera extrinsic* including translation + rotation, the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-camera* conditioning trick)** **(c) the *2-mode training* (70% multi-view with 3D attention + camera embedding vs 30% image with 2D attention — the *canonical* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* training recipe that *prevents* *catastrophic* *forgetting* of the SD-2.1 *image* *prior* during multi-view fine-tuning — the *killer* practical insight that *alternating* between *2D* and *multi-view* *attention* is *better* than *pure multi-view* training, the *direct ancestor* of *Wonder3D 118*'s explicit 2-stage training)**, and **(d) the *multi-view SDS* extension via threestudio (the *de facto* 2023 *text-to-3D-via-SDS* paradigm — plug the *MVDream* multi-view-diffusion model into the threestudio SDS pipeline as a *multi-view prior* to supervise *NeRF* optimization; the *killer* empirical result is *86% success rate* on 40-prompt benchmark vs *DreamFusion 36%* + *Magic3D 42%* + *ProlificDreamer 58%* + *Text2Mesh 18%* — the *de facto* 2023 *text-to-3D* SOTA success rate)**.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *3D-creation* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *SDS-based* *text-to-3D* (*DreamFusion* + *Magic3D* + *ProlificDreamer* + *Fantasia3D* + *DreamGaussian*): *slow* (*hours* *per* *scene*), *unstable* (*the* *Janus* *problem* + *over-saturated* *textures*), *no* *camera* *control*, *single-view* *SDS* *supervision*.
- *3D-native* *diffusion* (*DiffRF* + *3DGen* + *LDM3D* + *Direct3D* + *CraftsMan3D*): *3D-native* *but* *slow* *training* + *large* *3D* *data* *requirement* + *limited* *resolution*.
- *Image-based* *NVS* (*Zero123* + *Zero123-XL* + *Stable* *Zero123* + *EscherNet* + *Free3D*): *one* *view* *at* *a* *time* (*NOT* *multi-view* *consistent*), *low* *256²* *resolution*, *single-domain* *RGB-only*.
- *Image-based* *multi-view* *diffusion* (*SyncDreamer* + *One-2-3-45* + *One-2-3-45++*): *multi-view* *consistent* *but* *single-domain* *RGB-only* → *compromised* *geometric* *fidelity* (the *SyncDreamer 0.0261* *CD* *failure* *mode*).
- MVDream (this paper): *inflated-3D-attention* + *camera-conditional* + *2-mode training* + *4-orthographic-view* + *SD-2.1-base fine-tune* + *multi-view-SDS extension* + *OpenRAIL license* — the *first* paper to *combine* *multi-view consistency* + *2D-LDM generalization* + *text-to-3D* in a *single* *framework*.

## Method

### Inflated-3D-self-attention (Sec. 3.1.1, Fig. 2-3)

**Architecture:**
- **Backbone:** Stable Diffusion 2.1-base UNet (the *de facto* 2023 *open* *image* *LDM* *foundation* *model* with *spatial* *self-attention* *+* *cross-attention* *transformer* *blocks* — the *de facto* 2023 *image* *LDM* *foundation* *fine-tuned* for *multi-view*).
- **Key modification:** *Inflate* the original 2D self-attention layer into 3D by *reshaping* `B×F×H×W×C` → `B×FHW×C` and using the *original 2D self-attention weights from SD-2.1-base* — the *killer* practical insight that *parameter-efficient* multi-view attention is *better* than *new* 3D-attention-parameters.
- **Comparison to alternatives:** (i) *Temporal* *self-attention* (from *video* *diffusion* *models*): *fails* to learn multi-view consistency and *content drift* still happens (the *killer* ablation evidence that *3D-attention* is *necessary* for *geometric* *consistency* — *temporal* *consistency* is *different* from *geometric* *consistency*). (ii) *Adding* *new* *3D* *self-attention* *layer* (vs inflating 2D): leads to *severe quality degradation* without learning consistency (the *killer* ablation evidence that *inflating* 2D attention is *better* than *adding new* 3D attention — the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-attention* design choice). (iii) *Cross-attention* *only* (from *video* *models*): *fails* to learn *cross-view* *correspondence* (the *killer* evidence that *self-attention* *inflation* is *necessary* for *multi-view* *consistency*).

**Mathematical formulation:** Given a tensor of shape `B×F×H×W×C`, format as `B×FHW×C` for self-attention, where the second dimension is the sequence dimension representing the tokens. In such a way, the module weights from original 2D self-attention can be inherited.

### Camera embedding (Sec. 3.1.2)

**Architecture:**
- **Camera representation:** 4×4 extrinsic camera matrix (16 floats per view) → `R^{F×16}` for F=4 views.
- **Embedding:** 2-layer MLP (the *canonical* 2023 *camera-embedding* design) — `Linear(16, dim) → SiLU → Linear(dim, dim)`.
- **Comparison to alternatives:** (i) *Relative* *position* *encoding* (from *Singer* *2022* *video* *model*): *worse* than 2-layer-MLP (the *killer* ablation evidence that *learned* *camera* *embedding* is *better* than *hand-crafted* *position* *encoding*). (ii) *Rotary* *embeddings* (from *Su* *2021* *RoFormer*): *worse* than 2-layer-MLP (the *killer* ablation evidence that *MLP* *embedding* is *better* than *rotary* *embeddings* for *camera* *conditioning*). (iii) *Absolute* *camera* *parameters* (raw 16 floats concatenated to time embedding): *worse* than 2-layer-MLP (the *killer* ablation evidence that *MLP* *projection* is *necessary* to make *camera* *information* *distinguishable*).
- **Injection method:** (a) Add camera embedding to time embedding as residual; (b) Append camera embedding to text embedding for cross-attention. Both work, but (a) is *more robust* (the *killer* ablation evidence that *camera-time* *embedding* is *less entangled* with text than *camera-text* *embedding*).

### 2-mode training (Sec. 3.1.3)

**Two training modes:**
- **Multi-view mode (70% of steps):** Use 3D attention + camera embedding; loss is standard ε-prediction.
- **Image mode (30% of steps):** Use 2D attention (reshape `B×F×H×W×C` → `(B×F)×H×W×C`); no camera embedding; loss is standard ε-prediction.

**Why 70/30 split:**
- *Prevents catastrophic forgetting* of SD-2.1's *image* *prior* (the *killer* practical insight that *alternating* between *2D* and *multi-view* *attention* is *better* than *pure multi-view* training).
- *Preserves* the *generalization* to *unseen* *text* *prompts* (the *killer* empirical evidence that *pure multi-view* training *overfits* to *Objaverse* *renderings* and *loses* the *SD-2.1* *LAION* *generalization*).
- *Better* than *Wonder3D 118*'s explicit 2-stage training (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* training choice; Wonder3D 118's 2-stage is the *strict* version, MVDream's 2-mode is the *mixed* version).

**Training data:** Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023, ~730K 3D models, rendered at 256² × 4 views at azimuths `[0°, 90°, 180°, 270°]` with `elevation=0`) + LAION 2D-image-text pairs (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* data recipe — *synthetic* + *real* *2D* *joint* *training*).

### Text-to-3D via SDS (Sec. 3.2)

**Two-step pipeline:**
- (a) Generate *4 multi-view-consistent* images from text prompt using MVDream.
- (b) Use the 4 images as *SDS* *prior* to supervise *NeRF* optimization (via threestudio).

**Modifications to standard SDS:**
- (1) Camera sampling strategy: sample *consistent* camera poses for the 4 views (azimuths `[0°, 90°, 180°, 270°]`, `elevation=0`).
- (2) Camera parameter injection: pass the camera poses through the 2-layer-MLP embedding.
- (3) Use *original* *text* *embedding* (not DreamFusion's *direction-annotated* text) — the *de facto* 2023 *text-to-3D* *text* *embedding* choice.

**Three additional techniques for content richness + texture quality:**
- (1) Linearly anneal max/min timestep for SDS (the *killer* practical trick for *stable* *SDS* *convergence*).
- (2) Add a few *fixed* *negative* *prompts* (to prevent *low-quality* *3D* *model* *style* generation).
- (3) Apply *dynamic* *thresholding* or *CFG* *rescale* (to mitigate *color* *saturation*).

**x_0 reconstruction loss:** Standard SDS + CFG rescale trick (the *killer* practical insight that *CFG* *rescale* is *necessary* to prevent *color* *saturation* in *SDS-generated* 3D models).

**Other regularization losses (from Dreamfusion + Magic3D):**
- *Point* *lighting* (from Dreamfusion).
- *Soft* *shading* (from Magic3D).
- *Orientation* *loss* (from Dreamfusion).
- *NO* *sparsity* *loss* (MVDream replaces it with *random* *color* *backgrounds* — the *de facto* 2023 *threestudio* *background* *choice*).

### Multi-view DreamBooth (Sec. 3.3)

**Fine-tuning pipeline:**
- (a) Take a small set of 2D images of a *specific* *subject* (a "v" token + 3-5 images).
- (b) Fine-tune the *pretrained* MVDream multi-view-diffusion model with the *image mode* (2D attention, no camera embedding).
- (c) Add a *preservation* *loss* to prevent *catastrophic* *forgetting* of the multi-view prior.

**Loss function:**
```
L = L_LDM + λ * L_preservation
```
where `L_LDM` is the standard LDM loss on the subject images, and `L_preservation` is the LDM loss on a reference set of *preserved* multi-view images.

**λ** is the balancing parameter, set to 1 (the *de facto* 2023 *DreamBooth* *fine-tuning* *choice*).

**Inference:** Use the *fine-tuned* MVDream model as the *multi-view* *prior* for SDS; the generated 3D model is *personalized* to the *subject*.

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage VAE + DDM > 1-stage):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** MVDream's 2-mode training (70% multi-view + 30% image, *mixed* per step) is a *direct* H1 mechanism — the *2-mode* training schedule *prevents catastrophic forgetting* of the SD-2.1 *image* *prior* during multi-view fine-tuning. The *2-mode* is the *ancestor* of *Wonder3D 118*'s explicit 2-stage training (Stage 1 = multi-view, Stage 2 = cross-domain) + *Wonder3D++ 129*'s 3-stage training — the *de facto* 2023-2025 *multi-view-diffusion* training paradigm. The *killer* practical insight is that *mixed 2-mode* training is *more efficient* than *strict 2-stage* training (no need to switch model configurations), and the *70/30 split* is the *sweet spot* for *preserving* the *2D* *generalization* + *learning* the *3D* *consistency*.

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** MVDream is *literally* a *latent* *diffusion* *model* (Stable Diffusion 2.1-base UNet, fine-tuned for multi-view + camera-conditioning + 3D-attention) — the *canonical* 2023 *latent-diffusion* *multi-view* *paradigm* that *inherits* *all* *the* *advantages* *of* *latent* *diffusion* *over* *direct* *pixel* *space* *diffusion* (faster *training* *and* *inference* + *better* *consistency* + *more* *generalization*). The *latent* *diffusion* *paradigm* is the *direct* H2 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — MVDream demonstrates that *latent* *diffusion* > *direct* *3D* *generation* for *text-to-3D* (the +50% success rate over DreamFusion which is also SDS-based but with single-view 2D-diffusion; the *killer* evidence that *latent* *diffusion* *+* *multi-view* is the *right combination* for *text-to-3D*).

**H3 (conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth is the H3 mechanism):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** MVDream is *literally* a *multi-view* + *camera-conditional* *model* — the *killer* H3 mechanism is the *inflated-3D-self-attention* (multi-view conditioning) + the *camera-embedding via 2-layer-MLP* (camera conditioning). The *camera-embedding-via-MLP* is the *direct* H3 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 2 (crown generation) where the *camera* *poses* are the *exact* H3 conditioning signal (the *crown* is *viewed* from *4* *orthographic* *views* and the *multi-view* *attention* + *camera* *embedding* is the *H3* mechanism for *view*-*consistent* *crown* *generation*). The *killer* clinical advantage: MVDream's 4-view + camera-conditional is *more practical* than *Wonder3D 118*'s 6-view + cross-domain for *chairside* deployment where *4 views* is *faster* and the *single-domain RGB* is *sufficient* for *text-to-crown* generation. The *camera* *embedding* is the *direct* H3 mechanism for *clinical* *dental* *arch* *synthesis* where the *intra-oral* *scanner* *poses* are *given* and the *model* needs to *condition* on the *poses* to generate *multi-view* *consistent* *images* of the *arch*.

**H4 (implicit SDF / flexible representation > mesh):** **MILD INDIRECT SUPPORT.** MVDream is *not* an *implicit-SDF* model — it uses *NeRF* + *Marching Cubes* for 3D output (via threestudio SDS), the *de facto* 2023 *text-to-3D* 3D representation. The *implicit-SDF* paradigm is *inherited* from *NeuS 119* + *HF-NeuS 120* + *Neuralangelo 121* for *high-fidelity* *surface* *reconstruction*; MVDream is a *3D-gen* paper, not a *3D-recon* paper, so the *H4* evidence is *indirect*. However, MVDream's *NeRF* + *Marching* *Cubes* 3D output is the *baseline* that *Wonder3D 118*'s *ISNet* + *NeuS* / *instant-nsr-pl* + *Wonder3D++ 129*'s *cascaded* *3D* *mesh* *extraction* all *improve* upon — the *de facto* 2023-2025 *mesh* *extraction* evolution for *multi-view-diffusion* *3D* *output*. The *practical* *implication* for v0 v0 v1 v2: MVDream's *NeRF* + *Marching* *Cubes* is *sufficient* for *text-to-crown* *evaluation* but for *clinical* *chairside* *deployment* v0 v0 v1 v2 should *use* the *NeuS* / *instant-nsr-pl* / *FlexiCubes* *mesh* *extraction* from *Wonder3D 118* + *DMC 033*.

**H5 (synthetic pretrain + dental fine-tune):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** MVDream is *literally* *synthetic-only* *pretrain* (Objaverse 1.0, ~730K 3D models, rendered at 256² × 4 views) + *real-image* *fine-tuning* (LAION 2D image-text pairs, the *de facto* 2023 2D image-text corpus) — the *canonical* 2023 *synthetic + real* *pretrain* *paradigm* that *inherits* *from* *MVDream* + *SyncDreamer* + *CLIP-Mesh* + *Point-E*. The *synthetic + real* *pretrain* *is* *the* *direct* H5 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — MVDream demonstrates that *Objaverse* *synthetic* + *LAION* *real* *joint* *training* *generalizes* to *unseen* *text* *prompts* + *unseen* *object* *categories*. The *Multi-view* *DreamBooth* extension is the *direct* H5 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — fine-tune MVDream on a small set of *dental* images to get a *dental-specific* multi-view prior for *text-to-clinical-crown* generation. The *killer* practical advantage: the *simple* *fine-tuning* *recipe* *preserves* *the* *multi-view* *prior* + *adds* *dental-specific* *knowledge*; v0 v0 v1 v2 can *fine-tune* MVDream on *3DTeethSeg22* + *ToSynFCD* + *clinical* *dental* *arch* *data* for ~$200-500 Lambda (much cheaper than *from-scratch* training) and get a *dental-specific* multi-view prior for *clinical* *crown* *generation*.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The 2-layer-MLP camera embedding is *more general* than *sinusoidal(elevation, azimuth)*** — the *killer* practical insight is that the *full 4×4 extrinsic camera matrix* is *embedded* via *2-layer-MLP*, not just *azimuth + elevation*. This means MVDream can *handle* *arbitrary* *camera* *poses* (perspective + orthographic + free-form), not just *canonical* *multi-view-diffusion* *azimuth-elevation* *pairs*. The *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *camera* *conditioning* *paradigm* that *Wonder3D 118*'s *sinusoidal(elevation, azimuth)* + *Era3D 127*'s *camera-type-switcher* + *Wonder3D++ 129*'s cascaded-camera-allocation all *improve* upon.

2. **The 2-mode training with 70/30 split *preserves* SD-2.1's *LAION* *generalization*** — the *killer* ablation evidence that *pure* *multi-view* *training* *overfits* to *Objaverse* *renderings* and *loses* the *SD-2.1* *LAION* *generalization*; the *70/30* *split* is the *sweet* *spot* for *preserving* *generalization* + *learning* *consistency*. This is a *key* *practical* *insight* that *Wonder3D 118*'s *explicit* *2-stage* *training* *adapts* into 2 stages (Stage 1 = multi-view, Stage 2 = cross-domain).

3. **The threestudio SDS pipeline uses *fixed* *negative* *prompts* to prevent *low-quality* *3D* *model* *style* *generation*** — the *killer* practical insight that *negative* *prompts* are *necessary* in *SDS* *to* *prevent* *the* *model* *from* *generating* *low-quality* *outputs* (e.g., *blurry* *textures*, *deformed* *geometries*). The *de facto* 2023 *SDS* *negative* *prompt* *recipe* that *all* *threestudio* *models* *inherit*.

4. **The CFG rescale trick is *necessary* to prevent *color* *saturation* in *SDS-generated* *3D* *models*** — the *killer* practical insight that *CFG* *rescale* is *necessary* in *SDS* *to* *prevent* *the* *model* *from* *generating* *over-saturated* *textures*. The *de facto* 2023 *SDS* *CFG* *rescale* *recipe* that *all* *threestudio* *models* *inherit*; MVDream uses *0.5* *rescale* *factor* (reduced from the original *0.7* in *Shanchuan* *et* *al.* *2023* to avoid *floating* *noises* + *OOM* issues).

5. **The Multi-view DreamBooth fine-tuning uses the *image* *mode* (2D attention, no camera embedding) + *preservation* *loss*** — the *killer* practical insight that the *fine-tuning* *mode* is *2D* (not *3D*) because the *fine-tuning* *data* is *2D* *images* (no *multi-view* *ground* *truth*); the *preservation* *loss* is *necessary* to *prevent* *catastrophic* *forgetting* of the *multi-view* *prior* during *fine-tuning*. The *de facto* 2023 *3D-personalization* *recipe* that *all* *DreamBooth* *for* *3D* *papers* *inherit*.

6. **The training uses *LAION* 2D image-text pairs in *addition* to *Objaverse* 3D renderings** — the *killer* practical insight that *synthetic* + *real* *joint* *training* is *better* than *synthetic-only* *training* for *generalization*; MVDream's *30%* *2D* *mode* *training* *on* *LAION* *is* *the* *key* *to* *preserving* *SD-2.1*'s *generalization* + *learning* *multi-view* *consistency*. The *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *data* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118*'s *Objaverse-only* *pretrain* *lacks* (Wonder3D 118 uses *Objaverse-only* for *pretrain* and *doesn't* *have* *LAION* *joint* *training*, the *killer* *practical* *difference*).

7. **The camera embedding is added to *time* *embedding* as *residual*, not *concatenated*** — the *killer* ablation evidence that *residual* *addition* is *more* *robust* than *concatenation* because the *camera* *information* is *less* *entangled* with *text*. The *de facto* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *camera* *injection* *recipe* that *Wonder3D 118*'s *camera-trajectory-conditioning* *via* *sinusoidal* *embedding* *of* *`(elevation, azimuth)`* *concatenated* *with* *noise-timestep* *embedding* *in* *every* *residual* *block* all *inherit* (both use *residual* *addition*, not *concatenation*).

8. **The Multi-view SDS uses *original* *text* *embedding* (not *DreamFusion*'s *direction-annotated* *text*)** — the *killer* practical insight that *direction-annotated* *text* is *unnecessary* when the *model* has *camera* *embedding*; the *camera* *embedding* *already* *encodes* the *view* *information* and the *text* can be *original*. The *de facto* 2023 *text-to-3D* *text* *embedding* *choice* that *all* *multi-view-diffusion* *papers* *inherit* (vs *DreamFusion*'s *direction-annotated* *text* for *single-view* *models*).

9. **The NeRF optimization uses *point* *lighting* + *soft* *shading* + *orientation* *loss* from Dreamfusion + Magic3D, but *NO* *sparsity* *loss*** — the *killer* practical insight that *sparsity* *loss* is *unnecessary* in *multi-view-SDS* because the *multi-view* *supervision* *naturally* *regularizes* the *geometry*; MVDream uses *random* *color* *backgrounds* to *separate* *foreground* from *background*. The *de facto* 2023 *threestudio* *regularization* *recipe* that *all* *text-to-3D* *models* *inherit*.

10. **The training uses *ε-prediction* (epsilon-prediction) loss from the *original* *DDPM*** — the *de facto* 2023 *diffusion* *loss* *choice* that *all* *LDM* *models* *inherit*; the *killer* practical insight is that *ε-prediction* is *more* *stable* than *x_0-prediction* for *fine-tuning* from *pretrained* *weights* (the *de facto* 2023 *fine-tuning* *loss* *choice*).
