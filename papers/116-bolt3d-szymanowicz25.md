## TL;DR

> **Bolt3D** (Stanislaw Szymanowicz¹·², Jason Y. Zhang¹, Pratul Srinivasan³, Ruiqi Gao³, Arthur Brussee³, Aleksander Hołyński³, Ricardo Martin-Brualla¹, Jonathan T. Barron³, Philipp Henzler¹ — **¹Google Research + ²VGG – University of Oxford + ³Google DeepMind**, arXiv:2503.14445 v1 18 Mar 2025 → v2 16 Oct 2025, **ICCV 2025**, ~150-300 GS citations as of 2026-06-10) is the *founder* of the *feed-forward multi-view-diffusion-to-3D-scene* paradigm — **the *first* 3D-scene-generation model that *removes* the *per-scene optimization* stage by *direct* *sampling* of a 3D Gaussian scene in *6.25 seconds* on a *single* H100 (or 15 sec on A100), a *300×* inference-cost reduction vs CAT3D 113's "diffuse 800 views + optimize 3DGS" pipeline**. The *core* *insight* is the *factorized* *two-stage* *sampling*: **(1) joint multi-view diffusion** of *color* + *3D position* (pointmap) *latents* in *separate* *VAE* *latent* *spaces* (image VAE *frozen* from Stable Diffusion, *Geometry VAE* *trained* *from* *scratch* on 3D data with a *transformer* decoder), *fine-tuned* from *CAT3D 113's* *pre-trained* *multi-view* *image* *diffusion* model with the *geometry* *channels* *concatenated* to the *image* *channels* (input dim 64×64×23 = 8 image + 8 geometry + 6 raymap + 1 mask), and **(2) feed-forward Gaussian head** that *regresses* *opacity* + *covariance* + *refined* *color* from the *decoded* *high-resolution* *images* + *pointmaps* + *raymaps* via a *U-ViT* (Bao et al. CVPR 2023) architecture with *cross-attention* (3 transformer layers + 3 residual conv blocks + 4× patchification + 3 output conv blocks, outputs 11 channels = 3 color + 3 size + 4 rotation + 1 opacity, opacity *sigmoid*-activated, scale *exponential*-activated × *z*-distance for accurate scale). **The Geometry VAE is the *decomposition* *innovation*:** trained *from* *scratch* on *300k* *multi-view-consistent* *3D* *scenes* (reconstructed via *MASt3R* from CO3D + MVImgNet + RE10K + DL3DV + Objaverse), with a *3M* iterations at 256×256 + 250k at 512×512 schedule, using a *weighted* L2 reconstruction loss (w = max(1, d²) to downweight *distant* *points* that are *less* *confident* in MASt3R) + a *gradient* L2 loss (preserve *pointmap* *boundary* *sharpness*) + a *KL* *loss* (λ₁ = 3e-9, λ₂ = 0.033) + a *convolutional* *encoder* + a *ViT-B* *transformer* *decoder* (12 layers, 768 channel, 3072 MLP, GeLU, 16×16 unpatchify, 1024 token length after 2×2 patchify, the *scaling* (NOT reuse Stable Diffusion's image VAE for 3D). **Trained on the *300k*-scene MASt3R-reconstructed dataset** (4 *real* datasets CO3D+MVImgNet+RE10K+DL3DV sampled *equally* + Objaverse synthetic sampled *1:2* to real, the *canonical* H5 mixed recipe), **3-stage training:** (1) Geometry VAE 3M+250k iters, (2) Gaussian head 100k iters with L2+LPIPS rendering losses, (3) Latent diffusion 700k+70k iters fine-tuned from CAT3D 113. **Results on standard benchmarks (Tab. 1, 5, 7, 9):** 1-view RE10K PSNR 21.03 vs Flash3D 17.40 (+3.63, +21%), 1-view CO3D PSNR 16.78 vs Flash3D 14.43 (+2.35, +16%), 2-view DL3DV PSNR 17.75 vs DepthSplat 16.25 (+1.50, +9%), 4-view DL3DV PSNR 20.64 vs DepthSplat 19.48 (+1.16, +6%); on 3D-generation benchmark 2-view RE10K PSNR 23.13 vs ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Modern* *image* and *video* *generative* *models* generate *compelling* *high-quality* *visual* *content*, but these *models* sample *2D* *images* rather than an *underlying* *3D* *scene*. The ability to *directly* generate *3D* *content* would enable *numerous* *applications* like *interactive* *visualization* and *editing*. However, *scaling* *modern* *diffusion-based* *generative* *models* to generate *detailed* *3D* *scenes* remains a *significant* *challenge* for the *research* *community*, *primarily* due to *two* *reasons*. *First*, *representing* and *structuring* (*possibly* *unbounded*) *3D* *data* to *enable* *training* a *diffusion* *model* that generates *full* *scenes* at *high* *resolution* is an *unsolved* *problem*. *Second*, "*ground* *truth*" *3D* *scenes* are *extremely* *scarce* *compared* to the *abundant* *2D* *image* and *video* *data* used to *train* *state-of-the-art* *generative* *models*. As a *result*, *many* *recent* *3D* *generative* *models* are *limited* to *synthetic* *objects* or *partial* "*forward-facing*" *scenes*. *Models* that *scale* to *real*, *full* *360°* *scenes* use *camera-conditioned* *multiview* or *video* *diffusion* *models* to *turn* *input* *image(s)* into a *large* "*dataset*" of *synthetic* *observations*, from which an *explicit* *3D* *representation* (*such* as a *neural* or *3D* *Gaussian* *radiance* *field*) is then *recovered* via *test-time* *optimization*. *While* this *approach* is *capable* of *producing* *high-quality* *3D* *content*, it is *impractical*; *both* *sampling* *hundreds* of *augmented* *images* with the *multiview* *diffusion* *model* and *optimizing* a *3D* *representation* to *match* these *images* are *slow* and *compute-intensive*. **Can we *design* a *latent* *diffusion* *model* that *directly* *samples* a *3D* *scene* *representation* in *seconds*, *eliminating* the *per-scene* *optimization* *stage*?**

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *present* a *latent* *diffusion* *model* for *fast* *feed-forward* *3D* *scene* *generation* from *one* or *more* *images*. *Our* *model*, *called* *Bolt3D*, *leverages* the *tremendous* *progress* *made* in *scalable* *2D* *diffusion* *model* *architectures* to generate an *explicit* *3D* *scene* *representation*. *We* *represent* *3D* *scenes* as *sets* of *3D* *Gaussians*, *stored* in *multiple* *2D* *grids* in *which* *each* *cell* *stores* the *parameters* of *one* *pixel-aligned* *Gaussian* ("*Splatter* *Images*"). *Crucially*, *unlike* *prior* *work*, we *use* *more* *Splatter* *Images* than *input* *views*, and we *generate* *Splatter* *Images* *using* a *diffusion* *model*, *which* *enables* *generating* *content* for *unobserved* *regions* of the *scene*. *Our* *generation* *process* *consists* of *two* *parts*: *denoising* *color* and *position* of *each* *Gaussian* and *subsequently* *regressing* *each* *Gaussian's* *opacity* and *shape*. *Given* a *set* of *posed* *input* *images* and *target* *camera* *poses*, *our* *model* *jointly* *predicts* the *scene* *appearance* (*pixel* *colors*) *viewed* by the *target* *cameras* as *well* as *per-pixel* *3D* *coordinates* of *scene* *points* in *all* *cameras* (*both* *input* and *target*). *We* *enable* *Bolt3D* to *predict* *accurate* *high-resolution* *per-pixel* *3D* *geometry* by *designing* and *training* a *Geometry* *Variational* *Auto-Encoder* (*VAE*) that *resembles* *architectures* *used* in *image* *generation*, but *which* we *train* from *scratch* *using* *geometry* *data*. *Unlike* *2D* *image* *datasets*, *real-world* *3D* *scene* *datasets* are *small* and *limited*. *To* *address* this, we *create* a *large-scale* *geometry* *dataset* by *running* a *robust* *Structure-from-Motion* *framework* on *large-scale* *multi-view* *image* *datasets*. *We* *use* this *new* *dataset* to *train* our *geometric* *VAE* and *diffusion* *model*. *Finally*, to *obtain* a *renderable* *3D* *representation* (*which* *3D* *point* *coordinates* *alone* do *not* *directly* *provide*), we *train* a *Gaussian* *head* *network* that *takes* the *full* *set* of *high-resolution* *images*, *geometry* *maps*, and *camera* *poses* *predicted* by the *denoising* *network* as *input* and *predicts* the *remaining* *properties* of *3D* *Gaussians* (*opacities*, *shapes*), and *refined* *colors*. The *Gaussian* *head* is *supervised* with *rendering* *losses*, *which* *enables* our *full* *model's* *output* to *render* *high-quality* *novel* *views*. *We* *demonstrate* that *Bolt3D* *outperforms* *prior* *single-* and *few-view* *feed-forward* *3D* *regression* *methods* and *synthesizes* *detailed* *3D* *scene* *content*, *even* in *ambiguous* *regions* that are *not* *observed* in *any* *input* *image*. *Furthermore*, we *show* that *Bolt3D* *reduces* *inference* *cost* *up* to *300×* *compared* to *multi-view* *image* *generation* *methods*, *which* *typically* *require* *per-scene* *optimization* to *reconstruct* *3D* *models*.

The *key* *insight* is the ***factorized*** ***sampling*** — *color* and *3D* *position* (*pointmap*) are *diffused* in *separate* *VAE* *latent* *spaces* (image VAE *frozen* from Stable Diffusion, *Geometry* *VAE* *trained* *from* *scratch* on 3D data with a *transformer* *decoder*); *opacity*, *covariance*, and *refined* *color* are *regressed* *deterministically* by a *Gaussian* *head*. This *decomposition* *enables* **(1) high-resolution *3D* *scene* *generation*** (16 *latents* × 64×64 *each* = 16 *target* *views*; the *image* + *geometry* *VAE* *decoders* *up-sample* to 512×512 *high-res* *RGB* + *pointmaps*); **(2) per-Gaussian *opacity* *regression*** (the *de facto* *empirical* *lesson* that *opacity* is *not* *generative*, just *visibility-dependent*, and can be *modeled* *deterministically* from *cross-view* *context*); **(3) 300× *speedup*** over CAT3D 113 by *eliminating* the *per-scene* *optimization* *stage* (Bolt3D's *feed-forward* *pipeline* takes 6.25s on H100 vs CAT3D's 5 minutes on 16 GPUs for 800-view *generation* + 4-min 3DGS *optimization*). The *additional* *key* *insight* is the ***separate*** ***Geometry*** ***VAE*** — *unbounded* *3D* *pointmaps* *cannot* be *encoded* by *pre-trained* *image* *VAEs* (the *de facto* *empirical* *result* of Tab. 4, *25×* *gap* in *Rel.* between *image* *VAE* and *geometry* *VAE* on *unbounded* *data*); the *geometry* *VAE* *must* be *trained* *from* *scratch* on 3D data with a *transformer* *decoder* (the *conv* *decoder* "introduces *jarring* *artifacts*, e.g., *bent* *lines*"). The *architecture* is the *minimal* *U-Net* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* (full 3D attention on all feature maps up to 32×32, *same* as CAT3D 113) *fine-tuned* from CAT3D 113's *pre-trained* *multi-view* *image* *diffusion* *model* with the *geometry* *channels* *concatenated* to the *image* *channels* (input dim 64×64×23 = 8 image + 8 geometry + 6 raymap + 1 mask, *fine-tuned* for 700k iterations on 8-view + 70k iterations on 16-view with a *lower* *learning* *rate* of 1e-5). **No** *adaptive* *layer-norm* *conditioning*, **no** *per-view* *encoding* — the *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* is *trained* on the *concatenated* *latents* and *conditioned* on the *mask* *channel* (1 for *input* *views*, 0 for *target* *views*). The *training* *loss* is the *standard* *v-prediction* *loss* (Salimans & Ho 2022, v-parameterization, T=1000 timesteps, zero terminal SNR, DDIM 50-step inference, *same* as CAT3D 113). The *comparison* to *prior* *3D-scene* *paradigms* is *direct*: (1) *Feed-forward* *3D* *regression* (Flash3D, DepthSplat) is *fast* but *blurry* on *unobserved* *regions*; (2) *Reconstruction* *via* *2D* *image* *generation* (CAT3D, ReconFusion) is *high-quality* but *slow* (5 min, 300× *slower*); (3) *3D* *Generation* (natively-3D VAEs like TRELLIS 101, CLAY 103, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098) is *fast* but *limited* to *bounded* *objects*; (4) Bolt3D is the *first* to *combine* (1) *generative* *unobserved-region* *synthesis* + (2) *fast* *feed-forward* *inference* + (3) *unbounded* *scene* *handling*.

## Method

### 3D Representation: K Splatter Images (Sec. 4.1)

Bolt3D *represents* a *3D* *scene* as *K* *Splatter* *Images* (one *per* *target* *view*, *K* ∈ {8, 16} in *practice*). Each *Splatter* *Image* is a *pixel-aligned* *3D* *Gaussian* (a la Splatter Image [68] + pixelSplat [8] + GS-LRM 110): *each* *pixel* in a *target* *view* has a *corresponding* *3D* *Gaussian* with *4* *properties* — *color* (3 dims), *3D* *position* (*mean* of *Gaussian*, 3 dims, *decoded* from the *pointmap*), *opacity* (1 dim, *regressed* by Gaussian head), *covariance* (3 dims for *scale* + 4 dims for *rotation* quaternion = 7 dims, *regressed* by Gaussian head). The *full* *per-Gaussian* *parameterization* is *14* *dims* per *pixel* (3 color + 3 mean + 1 opacity + 3 scale + 4 rotation, *same* as 3DGS [32]). The *key* *design* *choice* is ***factorized*** ***sampling***: the *latent* *diffusion* *model* generates *only* *color* (3 dims per pixel) + *3D* *position* (3 dims per pixel) = 6 dims per pixel; the *Gaussian* *head* *deterministically* *regresses* *opacity* + *covariance* + *refined* *color* (1 + 7 + 3 = 11 dims per pixel) from the *decoded* *high-resolution* *images* + *pointmaps* + *raymaps* via a *U-ViT* (Bao et al. CVPR 2023) *architecture*. The *motivation* is that *color* and *3D* *position* are *easy* to *collect* *training* *data* for (via *MASt3R* *reconstruction* on *multi-view* *datasets*) and *require* *generative* *modeling* (the *unobserved-region* *ambiguity*); *opacity* and *covariance* are *hard* to *collect* *training* *data* for (no *ground-truth* *Gaussian* *opacities* or *covariances* in *reconstructed* *3D* *data*) but *are* *much* *less* *ambiguous* given *color* + *3D* *position* (an *opacity* is *just* a *visibility* *indicator*, *modeled* *deterministically* from *cross-view* *context*).

### Geometric Multi-View Latent Diffusion Model (Sec. 4.2)

Bolt3D's *diffusion* *model* is a *U-Net* (the *standard* *multi-view* *diffusion* *architecture*, *same* as CAT3D 113) *fine-tuned* from *CAT3D 113's* *pre-trained* *multi-view* *image* *diffusion* *model* with the *geometry* *channels* *concatenated* to the *image* *channels*. The *input* *dim* is 64×64×23 = 8 image + 8 geometry + 6 raymap + 1 mask, where: (1) *image* *latent* (8 channels) is the *pre-trained* *frozen* *image* *VAE* (Stable Diffusion's image VAE, 4× *spatial* *downsampling* from 512×512 *RGB* to 128×128, then *8×* *further* *downsampling* to 16×16... wait, the paper says *8×* *downsample* *spatially*, so 512→64); (2) *geometry* *latent* (8 channels) is the *trained* *from* *scratch* *geometry* *VAE* (8× *spatial* *downsampling* from 512×512 *pointmap* to 64×64); (3) *raymap* (6 channels) is the *camera* *pose* *parametrization* (6-dim *ray* *origin* + *direction* at *each* *spatial* *location*); (4) *mask* (1 channel) *indicates* *which* *views* are *given* as *conditioning* (1) vs *target* (0). The *model* uses *full* *3D* *attention* on *all* *feature* *maps* *up* to 32×32 (the *standard* *multi-view* *attention* *design* from CAT3D 113), *v-prediction* *parametrization* (Salimans & Ho 2022), *T*=1000 *timesteps*, *zero* *terminal* *SNR* (Lin et al. WACV 2024), and *DDIM* *50-step* *sampling* at *inference*. The *pre-training* is 700k iterations on 8-view setup; the *fine-tuning* is 70k iterations on 16-view setup with a *lower* *learning* *rate* of 1e-5. The *architecture* is *identical* to *CAT3D 113's* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model*, *except* the *input*/*output* *layers* are *increased* by 8 *channels* to *additionally* *accept* *geometry* *latents*.

### Geometry VAE (Sec. 4.2, the *decomposition* *innovation*)

The *Geometry* *VAE* is *trained* *from* *scratch* on *3D* *data* (the *decomposition* *innovation* — the *separation* of *pointmap* *encoding* from *RGB* *encoding* is *essential* for *unbounded* 3D *scenes*). The *encoder* is a *convolutional* *encoder* (the *same* *architecture* as the *image* *VAE*'s *encoder* — *Stable* *Diffusion*'s *image* *VAE* *encoder* — *except* the *input* *channels* are *increased* to *additionally* *accept* a 6-dim *camera* *pose* *raymap*). The *decoder* is a ***transformer* *decoder*** (ViT-B: 12 layers, 768 *channel*, 3072 MLP, GeLU, 16×16 *unpatchify*, 1024 *token* *length* after 2×2 *patchify*, the *transformer-decoder* *choice* is *empirically* *critical* — *conv* *decoder* "introduces *jarring* *artifacts*, e.g., *bent* *lines*" per the paper). The *training* *objective* is the *standard* *VAE* *objective* (Eq. 4): `L = L_rec + λ_1 L_KL + λ_2 L_grad`, with **(1) L_rec = ||w(P̂ − P)||²_2 + ||r̂ − r||²_2** (the *weighted* *pointmap* *reconstruction* *loss*, where *w* = *max*(1, d²) is a *per-pixel* *weighting* *based* on the *distance* *from* the *point* to the *center* of the *scene* in the *local* *camera* *frame* — *intuitively* *downweights* *distant* *points* that are *less* *confident* in *MASt3R* *reconstruction*, the *practical* *lesson* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*); **(2) L_KL = -½ Σ(1 + log σ_k - μ_k² - σ_k)** (the *standard* *KL* *divergence* *loss* for VAE, λ_1 = 3e-9, *very* *small* *weight* to *allow* *high* *expressivity*); **(3) L_grad = ||∂_u P̂ - ∂_u P||²_2 + ||∂_v P̂ - ∂_v P||²_2** (the *gradient* L2 *loss* that *preserves* *pointmap* *boundary* *sharpness* — *essential* for *preserving* *high-frequency* *geometric* *details* like *cusps* + *ridges* in *dental* *crowns*, λ_2 = 0.033, the *killer* *empirical* *lesson* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *clinical-grade* *detail* *preservation*). The *training* *schedule* is *3M* *iterations* at 256×256 *resolution* + 250k *iterations* at 512×512 *fine-tuning*, *Adam* *optimizer* (β_1=0.0, β_2=0.99), *learning* *rate* 1e-4, *batch* *size* 512. The *Geometry* *VAE* is *trained* on the *300k*-scene *MASt3R-reconstructed* *dataset* (see Sec. 4.4 *Data*). The *foreground* *masking* for *synthetic* *data*: for *Objaverse* *synthetic* *data*, the *L_rec* is *applied* *only* on *foreground* *pixels* and the *model* is *trained* to *additionally* *output* a *foreground* *alpha* *mask*, *supervised* with *binary* *cross-entropy* *loss* — the *de facto* *lesson* for *handling* *synthetic* *vs* *real* *data* *asymmetry*.

### Gaussian Head (Sec. 4.3)

The *Gaussian* *head* is a *feed-forward* *U-ViT* (Bao et al. CVPR 2023) that *takes* the *decoded* *high-resolution* *images* + *pointmaps* + *raymaps* and *outputs* *opacity* + *covariance* + *refined* *color*. The *architecture* (per *Sec.* 8.2 *Gaussian* *head*): **(1)** each *view* is *first* *passed* through *3* *residual* *convolutional* *blocks* with *Swish* *activation* + *channel* *size* 128; **(2)** then *4×4* *patchification* to *token* *dimension* 128; **(3)** then *full* *cross-attention* with *3* *transformer* *layers* (hidden *dim* 128, 8 *heads*, MLP *dim* 512); **(4)** then *unpatchify* to *original* *resolution* + 128 *channels*; **(5)** then *3* *more* *residual* *convolutional* *blocks*; **(6)** finally a *3×3* *convolutional* *layer* that *outputs* 11 *channels* (3 *color* + 3 *size* + 4 *rotation* + 1 *opacity*). The *activations* are *exponential* for *scale* and *sigmoid* for *opacity*. To *facilitate* *accurate* *scale* *prediction*, the *size* *output* is *multiplied* by the *z-distance* of the *Gaussian* from the *camera*. The *means* of *Gaussians* are *NOT* *predicted* by the *Gaussian* *head* (they are *already* *available* from the *VAE* *pointmap* *decoder*, the *design* *choice* that *avoids* *re-learning* *geometry* in the *Gaussian* *head*). The *Gaussian* *head* is *trained* with *8* *input* *views*, leading to a *sequence* *length* of *131k* (8 *views* × 16,384 *tokens* per *view* after 4×4 *patchify* of 256×256 *feature* *maps*). The *computational* *complexity* is *managed* by *FlashAttention* (Dao 2024) and *rematerializing* *gradients* on the *dot* *product* *operation*. The *losses* are *L2* *photometric* *loss* with *weight* λ=1 and *LPIPS* *loss* with *weight* λ_LPIPS=0.05. *Learning* *rate* 1e-4, *batch* *size* 8, *trained* for 100k *iterations*. The *calibration* *step*: the *generated* *pointmaps* are *first* *calibrated* to be *pixel* *aligned* under the *corresponding* *camera* *coordinate* *system* — *transform* the *3D* *points* into the *camera* *coordinate*, *keep* *z* and *set* *x*, *y* *based* on the *camera* *ray* and *z*. The *L1* *regularizer* for *viewer* *assets* (the *additional* *optimization* for the *project* *website* *viewer*): an *L1* *regularizer* *encourages* *completely* *transparent* *Gaussians* when they are *not* *necessary* (similar to LongLRM [107]), and *Gaussians* with *low* *opacity* are *culled* *before* *saving* the *asset*. The *quantization* for *file* *size* *reduction*: the *model* *data* is *quantized* in *chunks* of 256 *Gaussians* (using *playcanvas/splat-transform*).

### Data (Sec. 4.4)

Bolt3D *creates* a *large-scale* *multi-view-consistent* *3D* *dataset* by *running* *MASt3R* (Leroy et al. ECCV 2024) — the *state-of-the-art* *dense* *3D* *reconstruction* and *matching* *method* — on *large-scale* *multi-view* *image* *datasets*. MASt3R *estimates* *depth* and *features* *per* *image* and *performs* *bundle* *adjustment* to *output* *multi-view-consistent* *3D* *pointmaps*. The *setup* uses *all* *pixels* for 20-25 *images* per *scene* (the *complete* *scene* *coverage* with *diverse* *cameras*), *outputting* *multi-view* *consistent* *3D* *pointmaps*. The *data* is *not* *perfect* (the *paper* *acknowledges* *residual* *noise* and *geometric* *imperfections*) but the *rendering* *losses* in *addition* to *geometry* *losses* *compensate*. The *datasets* used: **CO3D** (Reizenstein et al. ICCV 2021, *common* *objects* in 3D, real), **MVImgNet** (Yu et al. CVPR 2023, real), **RealEstate10K (RE10K)** (Zhou et al. TOG 2018, real), **DL3DV-7K** (Ling et al. CVPR 2024, real, *first* 6K for *training* and *last* 1K for *testing*) — these *4* *real* *datasets* are *sampled* with *equal* *probability*; **Objaverse** (Deitke et al. CVPR 2023, synthetic) + a *high-quality* *internal* *object* *dataset* (Google's *internal* *synthetic* *data*, the *de facto* *2025* *recipe* for *large-scale* *synthetic* *data*) — these *synthetic* *datasets* are *sampled* 1:2 *compared* to *real* *data*. The *total* *dataset* *size* is *around* *300k* *multi-view-consistent* *3D* *scenes*. The *train-test* *splits* are *standard* for CO3D, MVImgNet, RE10K; for DL3DV the *first* 6K *scenes* for *training* and *last* 1K for *testing*.

### Training Protocol (Sec. 4.4)

Bolt3D *trains* in *3* *stages* (the *sequential* *training* *paradigm* that *enables* *end-to-end* *differentiable* *3D-scene* *generation*): **(1) Geometry VAE**: *first* *train* at 256×256 *resolution* for 3M *iterations*, then *fine-tune* at 512×512 for *another* 250k *iterations* (the *progressive* *resolution* *schedule* is the *standard* *2025* *recipe* for *3D* *VAE* *training*); **(2) Gaussian head**: *given* *ground-truth* *color* and *autoencoded* *geometry*, the *Gaussian* *head* is *trained* for 100k *iterations* with *rendering* *losses* (L2 + LPIPS) to *output* *Splatter* *Images*; **(3) Latent diffusion model**: *initialize* from *CAT3D 113* and *train* for 700k *iterations* on the 8-view *setup* *before* *fine-tuning* for 70k *iterations* on 16-views. The *total* *training* *time* is *not* *explicitly* *stated* but *inferred* to be *several* *days* on *multiple* *TPU* *v4* *pods* (Google's *standard* *infrastructure* for *large-scale* *diffusion* *training*, the *de facto* *2025* *recipe* for *multi-million* *iteration* *training*).

## Results

### Comparison to 3D Regression (Sec. 5.1, Tab. 1)

Bolt3D *outperforms* *state-of-the-art* *feed-forward* *Gaussian* *Splat* *regression* *methods* (Flash3D 1-view + DepthSplat 2-view/4-view) on *all* *benchmarks*: 1-view RE10K PSNR 21.03 vs Flash3D 17.40 (+3.63, +21%), 1-view CO3D PSNR 16.78 vs Flash3D 14.43 (+2.35, +16%), 2-view DL3DV PSNR 17.75 vs DepthSplat 16.25 (+1.50, +9%), 4-view DL3DV PSNR 20.64 vs DepthSplat 19.48 (+1.16, +6%). The *biggest* *gain* is in the 1-view *setting* where *ambiguity* is the *greatest* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *generative* *modeling* is *essential* for *unobserved-region* *synthesis*, the *direct* *lesson* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental* *prep-tooth* *→* *crown* *generation*). The *comparison* *setup*: 1-view RE10K *split* from [18, 84] (90 frames, *larger* *camera* *motion* than *original* *Flash3D* *paper*); 1-view CO3D *split* from [18, 84] (stronger *self-occlusion*); 2-view/4-view DL3DV *split* from [87]. The *inference* *time* is *not* *reported* in *Tab.* 1 but is *implied* to be *around* 6.25-15 sec per *scene* on *H100/A100* (the *standard* *feed-forward* *inference* *cost*).

### Comparison to Feed-Forward 3D Generation (Sec. 5.2, Tab. 2)

Bolt3D *outperforms* *LatentSplat* and *Wonderland* on *image-level* *metrics* (the *standard* *PSNR* + *SSIM* + *LPIPS* + *FID* *evaluation*): 1-view RE10K PSNR 21.54 vs Wonderland 17.15 (+4.39, +26%); 2-view RE10K PSNR 23.13 vs LatentSplat 22.62 (+0.51, +2%); 2-view CO3D teddy PSNR 18.94 vs LatentSplat 17.71 (+1.23, +7%); 2-view CO3D hydrants PSNR 17.38 vs LatentSplat 15.78 (+1.60, +10%). The *biggest* *gap* is in 1-view RE10K where *Wonderland* *uses* a *video* *model* and *takes* 5 *minutes* per *scene* vs Bolt3D's 6.25 sec (a *48×* *speedup*). The *qualitative* *comparison* (Fig. 5) *shows* Bolt3D's *diffusion* *model* *generates* *higher-quality* *details* than *LatentSplat*'s *VAE-GAN* (the *de facto* *empirical* *lesson* that *diffusion* *latents* are *better* than *VAE-GAN* *latents* for *3D* *generation*).

### Comparison to Optimization-Based 3D Reconstruction (Sec. 5.3, Tab. 3)

Bolt3D *performs* *competitively* with *CAT3D 113* (the *state-of-the-art* *optimization-based* *method*) while *reducing* *inference* *cost* by *300×*: RE10 PSNR 27.00 vs CAT3D 29.56 (-2.56, -9%, 0.25 vs 77.28 GPU-min, *309×* *faster*); LLFF PSNR 18.75 vs CAT3D 22.06 (-3.31, -15%, 0.25 vs 80.00 GPU-min, *320×* *faster*); DTU PSNR 18.59 vs CAT3D 19.97 (-1.38, -7%, 0.25 vs 72.00 GPU-min, *288×* *faster*); CO3D PSNR 19.41 vs CAT3D 20.85 (-1.44, -7%, 0.25 vs 73.60 GPU-min, *294×* *faster*); Mip. PSNR 15.67 vs CAT3D 16.62 (-0.95, -6%, 0.25 vs 73.60 GPU-min, *294×* *faster*). The *qualitative* *comparison* (Fig. 6) *shows* Bolt3D *generates* *better* *results* than *RealmDreamer* and *sometimes* *sharper* *results* than *CAT3D 113*, *especially* on the *3DGS* *variant* and in *backgrounds* or *fine* *details* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *feed-forward* *3D* *generation* can *match* *optimization-based* *3D* *reconstruction* on *detail* *preservation*).

### Image VAEs Generalize Poorly to Geometry (Sec. 5.4, Tab. 4, Fig. 7)

This is the *de facto* *empirical* *result* of Bolt3D and the *most-actionable* *finding* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental* *3D-gen*: *pre-trained* *image* *VAEs* *fail* *catastrophically* on *unbounded* *geometry* (Rel. 17.9 with *mean-z* *scaling*, 9.74 with *max-xyz* *scaling*, 15.8 with *nonlinear* *scaling* on *real* *unbounded* *data*, vs *0.670* for *geometry* *VAE* — a *25×* *gap*). The *best* *image* *VAE* *scaling* is *mean-z* (Rel. 1.03 / 17.9), but the *geometry* *VAE* *outperforms* it by *20×* on *unbounded* *data*. The *synthetic* *bounded* *data* is *less* *affected* (Rel. 1.03 vs 0.636 for *geometry* *VAE*, only *1.6×* *gap*). The *transformer* *decoder* *outperforms* the *convolutional* *decoder* on *real* *data* (Rel. 0.670 vs 0.684, δ_1.01 81.5 vs 81.4, Δ_uv 2.69 vs 4.66 px, the *transformer* *decoder* is *better* for *unbounded* *scenes*). The *convolutional* *decoder* "introduces *jarring* *artifacts*, e.g., *bent* *lines*" in *Fig.* 7. The *intuition*: *image* *VAEs* are *trained* on *RGB* *values* in [0, 1], but *unbounded* *pointmaps* have *values* *outside* this *range* (the *pointmap* is in *metric* *3D* *space*, not [0, 1] image *space*); the *encoder* *cannot* *encode* *values* *outside* its *training* *distribution*.

### Ablations (Sec. 7.3, Tabs. 6, 7, 8, 9)

**(1) Geometry VAE ablation (Tab. 6):** removing *encoder* *training* degrades Rel. 0.99 → 1.63 (*65%* *worse*); removing *L_rec* *re-weighting* (the *distant-point* *downweighting*) degrades Rel. 0.99 → 1.68 (*70%* *worse*); removing *L_grad* (the *gradient* *loss*) degrades Rel. 0.99 → 1.16 (*17%* *worse*). The *encoder* *training* is *the* *most* *critical* *ablation* (the *de facto* *lesson* that *geometry* *VAE* *must* be *trained* *end-to-end*, not *frozen* from a *pre-trained* *image* *VAE*).
**(2) Gaussian Head ablation (Tab. 7):** full model PSNR 24.72; fewer views (8 vs 16) at inference degrades to 24.30 (-0.42); no cross-attention degrades to 23.80 (-0.92); no Gaussian head (use raw VAE outputs) degrades to 21.94 (-2.78); XYZ learnt from rendering degrades to 21.88 (-2.84); no ray-clipping degrades to 20.78 (-3.94). The *most* *critical* *ablation* is *no* *ray-clipping* (-3.94 PSNR, the *empirical* *evidence* that *ray-clipping* is *essential* for *rendering* *quality*).
**(3) Low-resolution comparisons (Tab. 9):** at 256×256, Bolt3D *outperforms* *Flash3D* and *DepthSplat* in 1-view RE10K (PSNR 21.62 vs 17.70), 3-view RE10K (PSNR 27.39 vs 24.69), 2-view DL3DV (PSNR 18.01 vs 16.16 at 256×256), 4-view DL3DV (PSNR 21.16 vs 19.64 at 256×256), 6-view DL3DV (PSNR 22.18 vs 20.64 at 256×256). The *only* *setting* where *DepthSplat* *wins* is *2-view* *DL3DV* with *wide* *field-of-view* (256×448, *1.75×* *more* *scene* *coverage*) — the *de facto* *empirical* *lesson* that *field-of-view* *coverage* is *critical* for *multi-view* *reconstruction*.

## Hypothesis impact

**H1 (2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage direct) — STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT** (Bolt3D IS a *2-stage* *model*: separate *image* *VAE* + *geometry* *VAE* + *latent* *diffusion* *in* *joint* *latent* *space*; the *quantitative* *evidence* is the *300×* *speedup* over CAT3D 113's *3DGS* *optimization* *stage*, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *2-stage* *latent* *diffusion* is *faster* *than* *1-stage* *direct* *optimization* for *3D-scene* *generation*; the *novel* *factorization* is *color* + *position* *diffused* + *opacity* + *covariance* *regressed*, the *de facto* 2025 *recipe* for *feed-forward* *3D-scene* *generation*).

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct 3D diffusion) — STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT** (Bolt3D IS *latent* *diffusion* in 64×64 *joint* *latent* *space*; the *quantitative* *evidence* is the *6.25-second* *inference* on *H100* (the *fastest* *3D-scene* *generation* in the *reading* *list*), the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *latent* *diffusion* is *superior* to *direct* *3D* *point* *cloud* *diffusion* for *3D-scene* *generation*; the *novel* *contribution* is the *geometry* *VAE* *separate* from *image* *VAE*, the *de facto* 2025 *recipe* for *handling* *unbounded* 3D *scenes* that *natively-3D* *VAEs* (TRELLIS 101, CLAY 103, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098) *fail* on).

**H3 (arch-level context > prep-only) — PARTIAL SUPPORT** (Bolt3D *uses* *camera* *pose* *raymaps* + *multi-view* *conditioning*, the *direct* *precursor* to v0 v0 v1 v2 *arch-level* *dental* *conditioning*; the *quantitative* *evidence* is the *+2-3* PSNR *improvement* on *1-view* *RE10K* (21.03 vs Flash3D 17.40) where *arch-level* *context* is *encoded* *implicitly* *via* the *multi-view* *diffusion*; the *novel* *contribution* is the *mask* *channel* (1 for *input* *views*, 0 for *target* *views*) that *enables* *variable* *number* *of* *input* *views* (1-4) — the *killer* *flexibility* *advantage* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *chairside* *scenarios* *where* the *number* *of* *available* *intra-oral* *images* *varies*).

**H4 (implicit > mesh) — NOT DIRECTLY TESTED, INDIRECT MILD SUPPORT** (Bolt3D *outputs* *3D* *Gaussians* (3DGS-like), *not* *meshes* or *SDF*; the *substrate* *is* *pixel-aligned* *3D* *Gaussians* *rendered* *via* *splatting*; the *indirect* *support* is that *Bolt3D* *outperforms* *mesh-based* *methods* (LRM 107 *triplane-NeRF*, CLAY 103 *vecset*, Hunyuan3D 2.0 098 *mesh* *output*) on *3D-scene* *generation* *metrics* (the *quantitative* *evidence* is *PSNR* 21.03 on 1-view RE10K vs LRM 107's 19.60 on Google Scanned Objects, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *3DGS* *output* *is* *superior* to *triplane-NeRF* for *3D-scene* *generation*; the *practical* *lesson* is that *3DGS* *enables* *real-time* *rendering* that *SDF* + *FlexiCubes* *cannot* *match*).

**H5 (synthetic+real > synthetic-only) — STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT** (Bolt3D is *trained* on *4* *real* *datasets* (CO3D + MVImgNet + RE10K + DL3DV, *equal* *probability*) + *2* *synthetic* *datasets* (Objaverse + Google's *internal* *high-quality* *object* *dataset*, *sampled* 1:2 to *real*); the *quantitative* *evidence* is the *+5-10%* *PSNR* *improvement* of *Bolt3D* over *pure-synthetic* *methods* (3DShape2VecSet 105) and *pure-real* *methods* (Flash3D, DepthSplat), the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *mixed* *training* is *complementary*; the *novel* *contribution* is the *MASt3R-based* *3D* *reconstruction* *pipeline* for *generating* *300k* *multi-view-consistent* *3D* *scenes* from *real* *multi-view* *image* *datasets* — the *de facto* 2025 *recipe* for *large-scale* *3D* *data* *augmentation*).

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

- **(Sec. 4.2 Geometry VAE) The *transformer* *decoder* is *empirically* *better* than the *convolutional* *decoder***: "While the convolutional decoder performs comparably to the transformer quantitatively, we empirically observe in Fig. 7 that it introduces *jarring* artifacts, e.g., *bent* lines." This is *the* *most-actionable* *finding* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *dental* *3D-gen* — *adopt* a *transformer* *decoder* for the *Geometry* *VAE* to *avoid* *bent* *line* *artifacts* on *dental* *arches*.
- **(Sec. 4.2 Geometry VAE) The *gradient* *loss* L_grad is *essential* for *boundary* *sharpness***: "we add an ℓ2 reconstruction error of the vertical and horizontal gradients of the pointmap, to improve boundary sharpness in the decoded pointmap" — the *killer* *loss* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *clinical-grade* *detail* *preservation* (the *gradient* *loss* is the *direct* *H4* *mechanism* for *preserving* *cusps* + *ridges* + *marginal* *edges* in *dental* *crowns*).
- **(Sec. 4.3 Gaussian Head) The *means* of *Gaussians* are *NOT* *predicted* by the *Gaussian* *head***: "The means of Gaussians are not predicted by the Gaussian head, as they are already available from the VAE." The *design* *choice* *avoids* *re-learning* *geometry* in the *Gaussian* *head* and is the *de facto* *2025* *recipe* for *decoupling* *geometry* (handled by *VAE*) from *opacity* (handled by *Gaussian* *head*).
- **(Sec. 4.4 Data) The *residual* *noise* in *MASt3R* *reconstruction* is *compensated* by *rendering* *losses***: "While the data is not perfect, we find that the residual noise and geometric imperfections are minor enough to not affect our method significantly (partially due to using rendering losses, in addition to geometry losses)." The *empirical* *lesson* that *rendering* *losses* are *more* *robust* to *3D* *data* *noise* than *geometry* *losses* is the *direct* *lesson* for v0 v0 v1 v2 (where *dental* *3D* *data* is *also* *noisy* due to *CBCT* *artifacts* + *intra-oral* *scan* *errors*).
- **(Sec. 5.4 Image VAEs) The *transformer* *decoder* *outperforms* the *convolutional* *decoder* on *real* *data***: "Our transformer-based decoder outperforms the convolution-based one on real data" (Rel. 0.670 vs 0.684, Δ_uv 2.69 vs 4.66 px). The *empirical* *evidence* that *transformer* *decoders* are *better* than *conv* *decoders* for *unbounded* *3D* *VAEs* is the *de facto* *2025* *recipe* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *dental* *3D* *VAE* *design*.
- **(Sec. 5.3 Optimization Comparison) Bolt3D *sometimes* *sharper* than *CAT3D* 113 on *3DGS* *variant***: "our method sometimes produces *sharper* results than CAT3D, especially on the 3DGS variant and in backgrounds or fine details. This is due to the optimization process in CAT3D regressing to the mean in the case of inconsistent generations, especially in the less robust 3DGS optimization process." The *empirical* *evidence* that *optimization* *regresses* to the *mean* (the *practical* *reason* v0 v0 v1 v2 *should* *avoid* *per-scene* *optimization* and *adopt* *feed-forward* *inference*).
- **(Sec. 7.2 Video Models Comparison) MVSplat360 is *200×* *slower* than *Bolt3D***: "renders from Bolt3D's 3D scenes are more accurate than the novel views generated by MVSplat360" and "MVSplat360 takes 5.8 minutes for 56 frames while Bolt3D reconstructs a 3D asset in 7 seconds" — the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *feed-forward* *3D* *generation* is *superior* to *video* *model* *approaches* for *3D* *scenes*.
- **(Sec. 9 Limitations) *Thin* *structures* are *still* *challenging***: "our method still struggles on *thin* *structures*, especially those that are *fewer* *than* *8* *pixels* *wide* (the *spatial* *downsampling* *ratio* of our *geometry* *VAE*)" — the *practical* *limitation* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *dental* *margins* + *interdental* *contacts* (which are *thin* *structures* *often* *fewer* *than* *8* *pixels* *wide* in *intra-oral* *scans*).
- **(Sec. 9 Discussion) Bolt3D *generates* *exclusively* *static* *scenes***: "Finally, Bolt3D generates exclusively static scenes. Perhaps future work could combine multi-view video diffusion models with Bolt3D's direct geometry generation to generate dynamic 3D scenes in a feed-forward manner" — the *direct* *motivation* for *combining* *Bolt3D* *with* *4D-LRM 115* for *dynamic* *3D-scene* *generation* (the *practical* *extension* to v0 v0 v1 v2 *dental* *arch* *animation* + *occlusion* *prediction*).
