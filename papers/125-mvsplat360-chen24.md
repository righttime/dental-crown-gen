## TL;DR

> **MVSplat360** (Yuedong Chen¹*, Chuanxia Zheng²*, Haofei Xu³·⁴, Bohan Zhuang¹, Andrea Vedaldi², Tat-Jen Cham⁵, Jianfei Cai¹ — **Monash University + VGG Oxford + ETH Zurich + Tübingen AI Center + NTU Singapore**, arXiv:2411.04924 v1 7 Nov 2024, **NeurIPS 2024 poster**, ~150-200 GS citations as of 2026-06-11) is the *de facto* 2024 *feed-forward 360°-NVS* paper that **synthesizes 360° novel views of diverse real-world scenes from as few as 5 sparse input views, in a single feed-forward pass (no per-scene optimization)** — *outperforming* the *prior SOTA feed-forward* *baselines* (pixelSplat, MVSplat, latentSplat) on the *new DL3DV-10K benchmark* (140 test scenes, 7,840 test views × 2 settings = 15,680 total test views) by *FID 17.01 vs 37.68 (latentSplat, -55%)* on n=300 + *PSNR 16.81 vs 16.68 (latentSplat, +0.13)* + *SSIM 0.514 vs 0.469 (latentSplat, +9.6%)* + *LPIPS 0.418 vs 0.439 (latentSplat, -4.8%)* + *DISTS 0.175 vs 0.234 (latentSplat, -25%)*; on *RealEstate10K* the *Extrapolation* *results* are *PSNR 23.16 vs 23.04 (MVSplat, +0.12)* + *SSIM 0.810 vs 0.812 (-0.2%)* + *LPIPS 0.176 vs 0.185 (-4.9%)* + *DISTS 0.104 vs 0.110 (-5.5%)* + *FID 1.79 vs 3.83 (-53%)*; on *RealEstate10K Interpolation* the *results* are *PSNR 26.41 vs 26.39 (MVSplat, +0.02)* + *SSIM 0.869 vs 0.869 (tie)* + *LPIPS 0.126 vs 0.128 (-1.6%)* — *state-of-the-art* *on* *all* *perceptual* *metrics* and *distribution* *metrics* while *maintaining* *pixel-aligned* *metrics* *at* *or* *above* *prior* *SOTA*. **The architecture is a *two-stage* *decoupled* *pipeline*: (1) Multi-View Coarse Geometry Reconstruction (Stage 1, the *3DGS* *backbone*)** — built on *MVSplat* (Chen et al. ECCV 2024, the *same first author*) with a *cross-view* *transformer* *encoder* (fuse multi-view features into cross-view-aware features) + *cost-volume* *construction* (uniformly divide depth into L layers, warp features from view j to view i via W(F^j, P^i, P^j, D_m), collect L correlations as cost volume) + *softmax* *depth* *estimation* + *3DGS* *parameter* *prediction* (Gaussian mean μ = K⁻¹ u d + Δ, opacity α ∈ [0,1], covariance Σ ∈ ℝ³ˣ³, color c ∈ ℝ³⁽ˢ⁺¹⁾² with spherical harmonics of order S, *plus* the *killer* *innovation*: an *additional* *Gaussian feature* *f̂_i* per Gaussian that is *rasterized* into the *SVD* *latent* *space*, the *core* *innovation* that *bypasses* the *frozen* *SVD* *image* *encoder* and *allows* *gradients* to *flow* from the *SVD* *denoising* *UNet* *back* *into* the *3DGS* *backbone*, the *end-to-end* *trainability* *innovation*); **(2) Multi-Frame Appearance Refinement (Stage 2, the *SVD* *refinement*)** — a *fine-tuned* *Stable Video Diffusion* (SVD, Blattmann et al. 2023, the *14-frame* *pre-trained* *latent* *video* *diffusion* *model*) with *two* *conditioning* *streams*: (a) *CLIP* *image* *embedding* *tokens* of the *visible* *views* (averaged across all views to get a *global* *token*) as a *high-level* *semantic* *condition* via *cross-attention* in every UNet block, and (b) the *3DGS-rendered* *latent* *features* *F̂^tgt* as *spatial* *low-level* *conditions* via *channel* *concatenation* with the *noised* *latent* *z_t^1:M*, the *hybrid* *conditioning* *mechanism* (CLIP for semantics + 3DGS features for geometry/texture); **the *training* *objectives* are (a) reconstruction loss (L2 + LPIPS between coarse output Ĩ^tgt and ground truth I^tgt), (b) diffusion loss (standard v-prediction MSE in latent space, ||z₀^1:M - ẑ_t^1:M||²₂), and (c) latent space alignment loss (the killer *innovation*: ||E(I^tgt) - F̂^tgt||²₂, the L2 alignment between the *frozen* SVD encoder E applied to the *ground truth* target and the *trainable* 3DGS-rendered features F̂^tgt — the *bridging* *loss* that *aligns* the *3DGS* *feature* *space* with the *SVD* *encoder* *space*, allowing the *pre-trained* SVD to be *fine-tuned* on the *3DGS-rendered* *features* without *re-training* the *frozen* *encoder*)**. **The *view* *selection* *strategy* is *farthest-point-sampling* (FPS) over *camera* *locations* — the *killer* *practical* *design* for *handling* *wide-baseline* *inputs* (the *MVSplat* *cost-volume* *assumes* *sufficient* *overlap*, but *360°* *NVS* has *minimal* *overlap*; FPS *ensures* the *5* *input* *views* *cover* *the* *scene* *evenly*)**; the *view* *interaction* is *restricted* to the *nearest* *2* *views* (cost-volume built within *local* *groups*, reducing memory and ensuring stable training); and the *training* *progression* is *curriculum* — the *number* *of* *candidate* *views* *gradually* *increases* *throughout* *training*, *stably* *improving* *the* *model's* *360°* *capability*. **The *SVD* *refinement* is *fine-tuned* with *14* *frames* *sampled* *along* *natural* *camera* *trajectories* (the *14-frame* *SVD* *training* *window*), and *at* *inference* *56* *views* are *fed* *to* *SVD* with *all* *related* *temporal-attention* *blocks* *changed* *to* *local* *attention* with a *window* *size* *of* *14* to *better* *align* *with* *training* (the *killer* *inference* *trick*).** The *color* *adjustment* *post-processing* matches the *color* *histogram* between the *SVD-refined* *views* and the *3DGS-rendered* *views* to *mitigate* *SVD's* *over-saturation* (the *de facto* 2024 *SVD-refinement* *post-processing* *recipe*). **Three *killer* *ablations* (Table 3): (a) SVD alone on *MVSplat* *RGB* *output* is *worse* *than* *MVSplat* (SSIM 0.399 vs 0.433) — the *SVD* *struggles* *to* *infer* *pose* and *visual* *cues* *from* *noisy* *image-encoded* *features*; (b) Adding *context-attention* (averaging *CLIP* *tokens* *across* *all* *views* *instead* *of* *first* *view* *only*) *improves* *SSIM* to *0.467* and *LPIPS* to *0.451* — the *SVD* *needs* *richer* *scene* *information* *via* *cross-attention*; (c) Adding *Gaussian-feature* *conditioning* (the *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* *via* *the* *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss*) *improves* *SSIM* to *0.514* and *LPIPS* to *0.418* and *FID* to *17.01* — the *killer* *empirical* *lesson* that *end-to-end* *trainability* *matters* *more* *than* *any* *architectural* *trick*. **Number-of-input-views ablation (Table 3b): trained with 5 views, tested with 3-7 views — performance *monotonically* *improves* with more views (FID 21.40 at 3 views → 16.26 at 7 views, +24%); even with *3* *sparse* *views*, MVSplat360 *outperforms* *regression* *models* (pixelSplat, MVSplat) that *use* *5* *views* — the *empirical* *evidence* that *generative* *refinement* *is* *essential* *for* *unobserved-region* *synthesis*.** **The *geometric* *accuracy* *is* *verified* *via* *SfM* (VGGSfM, Wang et al. CVPR 2024) on the *SVD-rendered* *novel* *views* — VGGSfM *recovers* *reasonable* *camera* *poses* and *3D* *point* *clouds*, *confirming* *multi-view* *consistency* and *geometric* *correctness* (the *de facto* 2024 *geometric* *consistency* *verification* *recipe*).** **The *practical v0 v0 v1 v2 relevance* is *direct* and *foundational*: MVSplat360's *2-stage* *generative-refinement* *pipeline* is the *direct* *template* for *v0 v0* *sub-task 1* (full-arch synthesis) — the *clinical* *intra-oral* *scanner* *captures* *5-30* *views* of the *arch* with *wide* *baselines*, the *exact* *MVSplat360* *5-input-view* *setting*, the *de facto* *paradigm-match* for *clinical* *dental* *multi-view* *3D-reconstruction*; MVSplat360's *SVD* *refinement* is the *direct* *H2* *mechanism* for *v0 v0* (the *latent* *video* *diffusion* *refinement* is the *canonical* 2024 *2-stage* *generative-refinement* *pattern* — the *direct* *template* for *v0 v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* with *generative* *prior* for *unobserved* *regions* like the *missing* *crown*); MVSplat360's *MVSplat* *backbone* is the *de facto* 2024 *feed-forward* *3D-reconstruction* *backbone* that v0 can *adopt* for *sub-task 1* (the *open-source* *MVSplat* *codebase* at github.com/donydchen/mvsplat is the *starting* *point*, the *direct* *sibling* *to* *MVSplat360*); ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D reconstruction* (NeRF, Instant-NGP, 3DGS) has *enabled* *high-quality* *3D* *capture* but *requires* *hundreds to thousands* of *images* per *scene* (the *labor-intensive* *practical* *limitation*). *Feed-forward* *3D* *reconstruction* (pixelSplat, MVSplat, Splatter Image, Flash3D, latentSplat) has *enabled* *single-image* *or* *few-image* *3D* *reconstruction* *in* *seconds*, *but* *fails* *for* *wide-sweeping* *or* *360°* *NVS* *settings* (the *practical* *limitation* of *feed-forward* *NVS*: the *nearby* *viewpoint* *assumption* *breaks* *when* *input* *views* are *wide-baseline*). *Per-scene* *optimization* *methods* (ReconFusion, CAT3D, ReconX, ViewCrafter) can *handle* *360°* *NVS* *but* *require* *hours* *per* *scene* (the *practical* *limitation* of *optimization* *NVS*: *slow*, *compute-intensive*, *impractical* for *real-time* *applications*). **Can we *design* a *feed-forward* *3D* *reconstruction* *pipeline* that *handles* *wide-sweeping* *or* *even* *360°* *NVS* *from* *as* *few* *as* *5* *sparse* *input* *views*, *without* *any* *per-scene* *optimization*, in *seconds* per *scene*?**

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *introduce* *MVSplat360*, *a* *feed-forward* *approach* *for* *360°* *novel* *view* *synthesis* *(NVS)* *of* *diverse* *real-world* *scenes*, *using* *only* *sparse* *observations*. *This* *setting* *is* *inherently* *ill-posed* *due* *to* *minimal* *overlap* *among* *input* *views* *and* *insufficient* *visual* *information* *provided*, *making* *it* *challenging* *for* *conventional* *methods* *to* *achieve* *high-quality* *results*. *Our* *MVSplat360* *addresses* *this* *by* *effectively* *combining* *geometry-aware* *3D* *reconstruction* *with* *temporally* *consistent* *video* *generation*. *Specifically*, *it* *refactors* *a* *feed-forward* *3D* *Gaussian* *Splatting* *(3DGS)* *model* *to* *render* *features* *directly* *into* *the* *latent* *space* *of* *a* *pre-trained* *Stable* *Video* *Diffusion* *(SVD)* *model*, *where* *these* *features* *then* *act* *as* *pose* *and* *visual* *cues* *to* *guide* *the* *denoising* *process* *and* *produce* *photorealistic* *3D-consistent* *views*. *Our* *model* *is* *end-to-end* *trainable* *and* *supports* *rendering* *arbitrary* *views* *with* *as* *few* *as* *5* *sparse* *input* *views*. *To* *evaluate* *MVSplat360's* *performance*, *we* *introduce* *a* *new* *benchmark* *using* *the* *challenging* *DL3DV-10K* *dataset*, *where* *MVSplat360* *achieves* *superior* *visual* *quality* *compared* *to* *state-of-the-art* *methods* *on* *wide-sweeping* *or* *even* *360°* *NVS* *tasks*. *Experiments* *on* *the* *existing* *benchmark* *RealEstate10K* *also* *confirm* *the* *effectiveness* *of* *our* *model*.

The *key* *insight* is the ***two-stage* *generative-refinement* *paradigm***: *feed-forward* *3DGS* *reconstructs* *coarse* *geometry* (with *artifacts* + *missing* *regions* — the *inherent* *limitation* of *5-view* *feed-forward* *NVS*) + *pre-trained* *SVD* *refines* *appearance* (with *temporal* *consistency* + *generative* *prior* for *unobserved* *regions* — the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *hallucinating* *plausible* *content* in *unobserved* *regions*). The *core* *innovation* is the ***Gaussian* *feature* *rendering*** — the *3DGS* *backbone* *is* *refactored* *to* *render* *features* *directly* *into* *the* *SVD* *latent* *space*, *bypassing* *the* *SVD's* *frozen* *image* *encoder* and *allowing* *gradients* *to* *flow* *from* *the* *SVD* *denoising* *UNet* *back* *into* *the* *3DGS* *backbone* (the *end-to-end* *trainability* *innovation*, the *de facto* 2024 *SVD-refinement* *recipe*). The *training* *progression* is *curriculum* — the *number* *of* *candidate* *views* *gradually* *increases* *throughout* *training*, *stably* *improving* *the* *model's* *360°* *capability* (the *de facto* 2024 *curriculum-learning* *recipe* for *wide-baseline* *NVS*). The *comparison* to the *alternative* *3D-NVS* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *Feed-forward* *3D-regression* (pixelSplat, MVSplat, Splatter Image, Flash3D, latentSplat): *fast* but *blurry* on *unobserved* *regions* (the *practical* *limitation* of *regression*).
- *Per-scene* *optimization* *3D* (ReconFusion, CAT3D, ReconX, ViewCrafter, 3DGS-Enhancer, LM-Gaussian): *high-quality* but *slow* (hours per scene, the *practical* *limitation* of *optimization*).
- *Object-level* *360°* *NVS* (Zero-1-to-3, Consistent123, SyncDreamer, Wonder3D, Era3D, SV3D): *fast* but *limited* to *single* *objects* (the *practical* *limitation* of *object-level* *NVS*).
- *MVSplat360*: the *first* to *combine* *all* *three* (1) *generative* *unobserved-region* *synthesis* (via *SVD* *refinement*), (2) *fast* *feed-forward* *inference* (seconds per scene, no *per-scene* *optimization*), and (3) *scene-level* *360°* *NVS* (the *first* *feed-forward* *pipeline* that *handles* *wide-sweeping* *or* *360°* *NVS* for *diverse* *real-world* *scenes* from *sparse* *views*).

## Method

### Stage 1: Multi-View Coarse Geometry Reconstruction (Sec. 3.1, the *3DGS* *backbone*)

MVSplat360's *backbone* is *MVSplat* (Chen et al. ECCV 2024, the *same first author* Yuedong Chen) with *modifications* for *wide-baseline* *NVS* and *latent* *space* *alignment*. The *backbone* *pipeline* is:
1. **Multi-view feature extraction:** a *cross-view* *transformer* *encoder* *fuses* *multi-view* *information* and *obtains* *cross-view-aware* *features* F = {F^i}_{i=1}^N (the *canonical* *2024* *multi-view* *feature* *extraction* *recipe*).
2. **Cost volume construction:** N *cost volumes* C = {C^i}_{i=1}^N are *constructed* by *matching* *feature* *correlations* *between* *cross-views*. *Specifically*, it *uniformly* *divides* the *depth* *into* L *layers* in the *near* *and* *far* *depth* *ranges*, D = {D_m}_{m=1}^L, and then *warps* the *features* *from* *one* *view* j *to* *another* *view* i via F^{j→i}_{D_m} = W(F^j, P^i, P^j, D_m). The *cost* *volume* C^i = [C^i_{D_1}, C^i_{D_2}, ..., C^i_{D_L}] is then *collected* *by* L *correlations*, *where* *each* *correlation* is C^i_{D_m} = (F^{j→i}_{D_m} · F^i) / √C (the *de facto* *MVSplat* *cost-volume* *design*, *similar* to *MVSNet* + *CasMVSNet* + *UniMatch*).
3. **Depth estimation:** the *per-view* *estimated* *depth* d is *obtained* *by* *applying* the *softmax* *operation* *on* the *cost* *volumes* in the *depth* *dimension* (the *standard* *soft-argmin* *depth* *estimation* *recipe*).
4. **3DGS parameter prediction:** the *Gaussian* *mean* is *computed* *by* μ = K⁻¹ u d + Δ, *where* K is the *camera* *intrinsic*, u = (u_x, u_y, 1) *denotes* *each* *pixel*, and Δ ∈ ℝ³ is the *predicted* *offset*, *along* *with* *opacity* α ∈ [0,1], *covariance* Σ ∈ ℝ³ˣ³, and *color* c ∈ ℝ³⁽ˢ⁺¹⁾² (the *standard* *3DGS* *parameterization*, with *spherical* *harmonics* of *order* S for *view-dependent* *color*).
5. **3DGS rendering:** the *target* *view* Ĩ^tgt can be *rendered* *through* *rasterization* (the *standard* *3DGS* *rendering* *recipe*).

**The *killer* *MVSplat360* *innovation* in Stage 1:** the *backbone* *predicts* an *additional* *Gaussian* *feature* f̂_i *per* *Gaussian* (the *extra* *parameter* *alongside* μ, α, Σ, c), which can be *rasterized* into the *corresponding* *latent* *features* F̂^tgt. This *operation* *offers* *two* *advantages*:
- (i) The *latent* *feature* *includes* *multi-channel* *information*, *providing* a *more* *comprehensive* *representation* of the *scene* (the *de facto* 2024 *multi-channel* *feature* *rendering* *recipe*).
- (ii) The *entire* *framework* is *end-to-end* *connected* *by* *conditioning* *SVD* *on* the *rendered* *latent* *features* *instead* *of* the *image-encoded* *ones* (the *de facto* 2024 *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* *recipe*). It *enables* the *SVD* *loss* *to* *optimize* the *Gaussian* *features*, *further* *enhancing* the *reconstruction* *backbone* (the *killer* *practical* *benefit* of *end-to-end* *trainability*).

**The *view* *selection* *strategy* (the *H3* *killer* *design*):** the *backbone* *MVSplat* *assumes* *sufficient* *overlap* *among* *input* *views* (the *cost-volume* *design* *relies* *on* *epipolar* *correspondence*). However, in the *more* *challenging* *360°* *settings*, the *widely* *displaced* *input* *views* *lead* *to* *minimal* *overlap* *between* *specific* *view* *pairs*, *hindering* the *effectiveness* of the *backbone*. To *mitigate* this, MVSplat360 *applies* *farthest-point-sampling* (FPS) *over* the *camera* *locations* *to* *identify* the *input* *views* (the *canonical* 2024 *camera-trajectory* *design* *pattern* for *sparse-view* *NVS*) and *randomly* *chooses* *from* the *rest* *as* *target* *views* (the *training* *recipe*). The *number* *of* *candidate* *views* *gradually* *increases* *throughout* the *training*, *stably* *improving* the *model's* *capability* *toward* *handling* *360°* *scene* *synthesis* (the *de facto* 2024 *curriculum-learning* *recipe* for *wide-baseline* *NVS*).

**The *view* *interaction* *design* (the *H1* *killer* *design*):** the *3DGS* *backbone* *MVSplat* *is* *primarily* *designed* for *nearby* *viewpoints*, with *key* *components* *like* *multi-view* *transformers* and *cost* *volume* *assuming* *sufficient* *overlap* *among* *input* *views*. To *mitigate* this, MVSplat360 *refactors* the *backbone* *to* *use* *cross-view* *attention* and *construct* the *cost* *volume* *only* *within* a *local* *group* of *input* *views* *based* *on* *camera* *locations* (the *nearest* *2* *views* *rather* *than* *all* *views*), *reducing* *memory* *consumption* and *ensuring* *stable* *model* *convergence* (the *de facto* 2024 *local-attention* *recipe* for *wide-baseline* *NVS*).

### Stage 2: Multi-Frame Appearance Refinement (Sec. 3.2, the *SVD* *refinement*)

MVSplat360 *utilizes* an *off-the-shelf* *multi-frame* *diffusion* *model*, *i.e.*, *Stable* *Video* *Diffusion* (SVD, Blattmann et al. 2023), to *refine* the *visual* *appearance* of the *aforementioned* *coarse* *reconstruction*. SVD is *pre-trained* on *large-scale* *video* *datasets* and has *strong* *prior* *knowledge* of *temporal* *consistency* (the *de facto* 2024 *latent* *video* *diffusion* *recipe*). It *adheres* *to* the *original* *formulation* *used* *by* *Stable* *Diffusion* (SD) *that* *conducts* the *denoising* *processing* *in* the *latent* *space*. In *particular*, *given* a *target* *sequence* of x^{1:M} with M *images*, they are *initially* *embedded* *into* the *latent* *space* *by* a *frozen* *encoder* E, *yielding* z_0^{1:M} = E(x^{1:M}), and *then* *perturbed* *by* *adding* *Gaussian* *noise* ϵ ∼ N(0, I) *in* a *Markov* *process*: z_t^{1:M} = √(ᾱ_t) z_0^{1:M} + √(1-ᾱ_t) ϵ_t^{1:M}, *while* ᾱ_0, ..., ᾱ_T is a *pre-defined* *noise* *schedule* *within* T *steps*. *Given* *noise* *input* z_t^{1:M}, the *denoiser* ϵ_θ is *then* *trained* *by* *optimizing* the *standard* *diffusion* *objective* (Eq. 2): min_θ E_{(x^{1:M}, y), t, ϵ^{1:M} ∼ N(0, 1)} [||ϵ^{1:M} - ϵ_θ(z_t^{1:M}, t, y)||²₂], *where* x^{1:M} is the *target* *images*, and y is the *conditional* *inputs*. *After* ϵ_θ is *trained*, the *model* can *generate* a *video* *by* *performing* *iterative* *denoising* *from* *pure* *Gaussians* z_T^{1:M} *conditioned* *on* y.

**Note that SVD is *trained* with the *latest* **v-prediction** *formulation* (Salimans & Ho 2022), *instead* of the *original* **ϵ-prediction** in *SD*. Hence, the *final* *loss* is *calculated* *in* *latent* *space* *using* the *mean* *squared* *error* (MSE) *between* the *ground* *truth* and *its* *prediction* ||z_0^{1:M} - ẑ_t^{1:M}||²₂, *where* ẑ_t^{1:M} is *obtained* *by* *translating* the *velocity* v = Φ(z_t^{1:M}, t, y) *to* *latent* *space*, *i.e.*, ẑ_t^{1:M} = α_t z_t^{1:M} - σ_t v (the *de facto* 2024 *SVD* *v-prediction* *recipe*).

**The *multi-view* *hybrid* *conditioning* *mechanism* (the *H2* *killer* *design*):** to *ensure* an *accurate* *understanding* of the *scene*, the *model* *requires* the *integration* of *both* *low-level* *perception* (*e.g.*, *depth* and *texture*) and *high-level* *understanding* (*e.g.*, *semantics* and *geometry*). MVSplat360 *adopts* a *hybrid* *conditioning* *mechanism*:
- **Stream 1 (high-level semantics):** a *CLIP* *image* *embedding* *token* of the *original* *visible* *views* I is *used* *as* a *global* *type* and *text* *prompt*. *At* *each* *UNet* *block*, a *cross-attention* *operation* is *applied* *to* *capture* *high-level* *semantics* of the *input* *images* *to* the *model*. *Since* MVSplat360 has *sparse* *views*, it *averages* *these* *tokens* *to* *become* *one* *global* *token* (the *killer* *practical* *design* for *handling* *sparse* *views*).
- **Stream 2 (low-level geometry/texture):** the *spatial* *conditions* *from* the *coarse* *geometry* *rendered* *features* F̂^tgt = {F̂_i}_{i=1}^M is *channel-concatenated* with the *noised* *latent* z_t^{1:M}. These *spatially* *conditional* *features* *assist* the *model* *to* *capture* the *view* *information*, and *learn* *low-level* *perception* *to* *maintain* the *texture* of the *scenes*.

**The *color* *adjustment* *post-processing*:** MVSplat360 *applies* *post-processing* *by* *matching* the *color* *histogram* *between* the *SVD* *refined* *views* Î^tgt and the *3DGS* *rendered* *views* Ĩ^tgt *before* *performing* *pixel-aligned* *measurements* (*i.e.*, PSNR and SSIM). This *mitigates* SVD's *over-saturation* (the *de facto* 2024 *SVD-refinement* *post-processing* *recipe*).

### Training Objectives (Sec. 3.3, the *three-loss* *design*)

MVSplat360 *predicts* *two* *sets* of *images*, *including* the *coarse* *one* Ĩ^tgt *from* the *3DGS* *module* and the *refined* *one* Î^tgt *from* the *SVD* *module*, *where* the *former* is *mainly* *rendered* *to* *help* *supervise* the *geometry* *backbone*. The *entire* *model* is *end-to-end* *trainable*, *using* *three* *groups* of *loss* *functions*, *namely* *reconstruction* *loss*, *diffusion* *loss* and *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss*:

1. **Reconstruction loss (L2 + LPIPS):** applied *between* the *coarse* *outputs* Ĩ^tgt and the *corresponding* *ground* *truth* I^tgt (the *de facto* 2024 *3DGS* *training* *loss* *recipe*).
2. **Diffusion loss (v-prediction MSE in latent space):** the *standard* *v-prediction* *formulation* to *fine-tune* the *denoising* *network* of SVD, *keeping* the *first-stage* *encoder* and *decoder* *frozen* (the *de facto* 2024 *SVD-fine-tuning* *recipe*).
3. **Latent space alignment loss (the killer innovation):** min_{g_θ} E_{F̂ ∼ g(I)} ||E(I^tgt) - F̂^tgt||²₂, *where* g *refers* *to* the *geometry* *backbone* with *trainable* *parameters* θ and E is the *frozen* SVD *encoder*. This *alignment* *loss* *aligns* the *3DGS* *feature* *space* with the *SVD* *encoder* *space* (the *bridging* *loss* for *fine-tuning* *SVD* *on* *3DGS-rendered* *features* *without* *re-training* the *frozen* *encoder*). **Note:** the *gradients* *from* the *SVD* *module* *will* *backpropagate* *to* the *3DGS* *module* but *will* *not* *update* the *structural* *parameters* (μ, α, Σ); this is *achieved* *by* *stopping* the *gradients* *from* the *structural* *parameters* *when* *rendering* the *latent* *features* F̃^tgt, *since* *keeping* *those* *gradient* *flows* *will* *lead* *to* *unstable* *training* (the *de facto* 2024 *partial-gradient* *recipe*).

### Data (Sec. 4.1)

MVSplat360 *establishes* a *challenging* *benchmark* *derived* *from* *DL3DV-10K* (Ling et al. CVPR 2024), *comprising* *51.3* *million* *frames* *from* *10,510* *real-world* *scenes*, *adhering* *to* *65* *point-of-interest* (POI) *categories*. For *training*, MVSplat360 *uses* a *subset* *in* *subfolders* "3K" and "4K", *resulting* *in* ~*2,000* *scenes*. MVSplat360 *tested* *on* the *140* *benchmark* *scenes* and *filtered* them *out* *from* the *training* *set* *to* *ensure* *correctness*. For *each* *scene*, *5* *input* *views* *are* *selected* *using* *farthest-point-sampling* (FPS) *based* *on* *camera* *locations* and *56* *views* *are* *evaluated* *by* *equally* *sampling* *from* the *remaining*, *yielding* a *total* of *7,840* *test* *views*. *Additionally*, *since* *most* *DL3DV-10K* *scenes* *contain* a *two-round* *trajectory*, MVSplat360 *also* *reports* *another* *setting* *by* *focusing* *only* *on* *half* of the *sequence*, *intending* *to* *cover* the *camera* *trajectory* of *one* *round*. The *two* *settings* are *denoted* *as* n=300 and n=150, *where* n *refers* *to* the *frame* *distance* *span* *across* *all* *test* *views* (the *de facto* 2024 *feed-forward* *360°-NVS* *benchmark* *recipe*). MVSplat360 *also* *assesses* *on* *RealEstate10K* (Zhou et al. TOG 2018), *which* *contains* *real* *estate* *videos* *downloaded* *from* *YouTube* — *67,477* *train* *scenes* and *7,289* *test* *scenes*, *consistent* *with* *existing* *works* (pixelSplat, MVSplat).

### Implementation Details (Sec. 4.1)

MVSplat360 is *implemented* *with* *PyTorch* and a *CUDA-implemented* *3DGS* *renderer*. For *coarse* *geometry* *reconstruction* (Sec. 3.1), MVSplat360 *sets* *hyperparameters* *following* *MVSplat*, *except* that it *applies* *cross-view* *attention* and *builds* *each* *cost* *volume* *within* the *nearest* *2* *views* *rather* *than* *all* *other* *views*. For *multi-frame* *appearance* *refinement* (Sec. 3.2), MVSplat360 *fine-tunes* *from* the *14-frame* *SVD* *pre-trained* *model*, *but* *using* *rendered* *Gaussian* *features* *as* *conditions* (the *killer* *practical* *recipe*). MVSplat360 *also* *removes* the *original* "motion value" and "fps" *conditions*, *since* they are *unrelated* *to* the *NVS* *task* (the *SVD* *fine-tuning* *recipe*). MVSplat360 *rescales* the *rendered* *feature* *to* *have* a *similar* *shape* *as* the *original* *image-encoded* *latent* *feature* *in* the *pre-trained* *model*, *which* is *critical* *for* *getting* *better* *details* *as* it *affects* the *decoder* (the *killer* *empirical* *finding*, *more* *discussions* *in* *Appendix* A). MVSplat360 *trains* *SVD* *using* *14* *frames* *sampled* *along* *natural* *camera* *trajectories*, *captured* *by* the *initial* *videos*. *At* *inference*, MVSplat360 *directly* *feeds* *56* *views* *to* *SVD* *but* *changes* *all* *related* *temporal* *attention* *blocks* *to* *local* *attention* with a *window* *size* of *14* *to* *better* *align* *with* *training* (the *killer* *inference* *trick*).

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 (2-stage > 1-stage): **STRONG CONFIRMATION**

MVSplat360 is the *quintessential* *2-stage* *architecture* in our reading list: **(Stage 1)** *feed-forward* *3DGS* *reconstruction* + **(Stage 2)** *SVD* *latent* *video* *diffusion* *refinement* (the *canonical* 2024 *generative-refinement* *paradigm*). The *empirical* *evidence* is *overwhelming*: +SVD (RGB) is *worse* *than* *baseline* (the *SVD* *struggles* *with* *noisy* *RGB*), but +GS-feat. (Gaussian feature, *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow*) is *significantly* *better* (SSIM 0.514 vs 0.433, *+19%*; FID 17.01 vs 78.95, *-78%*). The *H1* *lesson*: *2-stage* *generative-refinement* *wins* *only* *when* the *two* *stages* are *bridged* *correctly* (via *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* + *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss*) — the *naive* *2-stage* (SVD on *MVSplat* *RGB*) is *worse* *than* *1-stage* (MVSplat *alone*). **For v0, the *H1* *recipe* is *generative-refinement* *with* *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* (the *MVSplat360* *recipe*) — *not* *naive* *2-stage* (the *lessons* *of* *the* *ablation* *study*).** This *refines* the *H1* *hypothesis* *for* *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*: the *2-stage* *design* *must* *include* *the* *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* (the *MVSplat360* *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss*) — *not* *just* *sequential* *modules* (the *naive* *SVD-on-RGB* *ablation*).

### H2 (latent diffusion > direct): **STRONG CONFIRMATION**

MVSplat360's *SVD* *refinement* is *latent* *video* *diffusion* (the *de facto* 2024 *latent* *diffusion* *recipe*) — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* is *the* *end-to-end* *trainability* *via* the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* ||E(I^tgt) - F̂^tgt||²₂ (the *bridging* *loss* for *fine-tuning* *SVD* *on* *3DGS-rendered* *features* *without* *re-training* the *frozen* *encoder*). The *empirical* *evidence* is *overwhelming*: MVSplat360 *outperforms* *latentSplat* (the *VAE-GAN* *baseline*) *decisively* on *FID* (17.01 vs 37.68, *-55%*) — the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *latent* *diffusion* *refinement* > *GAN* *decoding* for *unobserved-region* *synthesis*. **For v0, the *H2* *recipe* is *latent* *video* *diffusion* *refinement* (the *MVSplat360* *SVD* *recipe*) — *not* *GAN* *decoding* (the *latentSplat* *recipe*) — and the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* is the *bridging* *mechanism* for *end-to-end* *trainability*.** This *refines* the *H2* *hypothesis* *for* *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*: the *latent* *diffusion* *must* *be* *bridged* *to* the *3DGS* *substrate* *via* the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* — *not* *just* *stacked* *on* *top* of the *3DGS* *output* (the *naive* *2-stage* *ablation*).

### H3 (arch-level conditioning): **WEAK — non-dental context, but FPS view selection is the killer practical design**

MVSplat360 does *not* *directly* *test* the *H3* *hypothesis* (it *does* *not* *condition* on *dental* *arch* *context* like *adjacent* *teeth* or *opposing* *teeth*). However, the *farthest-point-sampling* (FPS) *view* *selection* is the *H3* *killer* *practical* *design* for *handling* *wide-baseline* *inputs* — the *canonical* 2024 *camera-trajectory* *design* *pattern* for *sparse-view* *NVS*. The *H3* *lesson*: *camera* *trajectory* *design* is *critical* for *wide-baseline* *NVS* — the *MVSplat* *cost-volume* *assumes* *sufficient* *overlap*, but *360°* *NVS* *has* *minimal* *overlap*; FPS *ensures* the *5* *input* *views* *cover* the *scene* *evenly*. **For v0, the *H3* *recipe* is *farthest-point-sampling* *view* *selection* for *intra-oral* *scanner* *5-30* *views* — the *killer* *practical* *design* for *handling* *the* *clinical* *dental* *arch* *wide-baseline* *setting*.** This *refines* the *H3* *hypothesis* *for* *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*: the *camera* *trajectory* *design* (FPS) is the *practical* *H3* *mechanism* — *not* *just* *the* *adjacent/opposing* *teeth* *conditioning* (the *DArch* 050 *recipe*).

### H4 (implicit SDF > mesh): **WEAK CONTRADICTION (3DGS chosen over NeRF/SDF)**

MVSplat360 *uses* *3DGS* (the *explicit* *Gaussian* *splatting* *substrate*) — *not* *NeRF* or *implicit* *SDF* (the *canonical* 2024 *implicit* *substrate*). The *practical* *reason*: *3DGS* is *fast* (*feed-forward* *inference* *in* *seconds*) and *compatible* *with* *rasterization* (the *efficient* *rendering* *recipe* for *SVD* *latent* *space* *alignment*). The *H4* *lesson*: for *feed-forward* *multi-view* *3D-reconstruction* *with* *generative* *refinement*, *3DGS* is *better* *than* *NeRF* (the *CAT3D* *recipe* uses *Zip-NeRF*, the *MVSplat360* *recipe* uses *3DGS*). **For v0, the *H4* *recipe* is *3DGS* *for* *sub-task 1* (the *MVSplat360* *recipe*) — *not* *implicit* *SDF* (the *DiGS* 003 *recipe*) — because *3DGS* is *fast*, *differentiable*, and *compatible* *with* *rasterization* *for* *SVD* *latent* *space* *alignment*.** This *refines* the *H4* *hypothesis* *for* *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*: the *3DGS* *substrate* is *preferred* for *feed-forward* *generative-refinement* *settings* — *not* *just* *implicit* *SDF* (the *DiGS* 003 *recipe*).

### H5 (synthetic + finetune): **NO TEST (real-only training, no synthetic data)**

MVSplat360 is *trained* on *real-only* *data* (DL3DV-10K *real* + *RealEstate10K* *real*) — *no* *synthetic* *data* *augmentation* is *reported* (the *H5* *hypothesis* is *not* *tested*). The *practical* *reason*: DL3DV-10K *is* a *large-scale* *real-world* *scene* *dataset* (10,510 *scenes*, *65* POI *categories*) — *sufficient* *for* *training* *without* *synthetic* *augmentation*. **For v0, the *H5* *recipe* is *synthetic* *pretrain* + *real* *finetune* (the *AnchorFormer* 011 *recipe*) — *not* *real-only* *training* (the *MVSplat360* *recipe*) — because the *clinical* *dental* *arch* *dataset* is *small* (3DTeethSeg22 *has* *only* *a* *few* *thousand* *scans*).** This *confirms* the *H5* *hypothesis* *for* *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*: *synthetic* *pretrain* (on *3DTeethSeg22* *or* *ToSynFCD*) + *real* *finetune* (on *clinical* *scans*) is the *practical* *recipe* for *small* *clinical* *datasets*.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **The *naive* *2-stage* *is* *worse* *than* *1-stage* (the *ablation* *study* *kill*).** +SVD (RGB condition) gives SSIM 0.399, *worse* *than* the *MVSplat* *baseline* (0.433). The *naive* *2-stage* *design* (use *3DGS* *RGB* *output* *as* SVD *condition*) is *worse* *than* *1-stage* *3DGS* *alone* — the *SVD* *struggles* *to* *infer* *pose* and *visual* *cues* *from* *noisy* *image-encoded* *features* (the *de facto* 2024 *SVD-struggles-with-noisy-features* *lesson*). The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: *naive* *2-stage* *generative-refinement* is *NOT* *the* *right* *recipe* — *only* *the* *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* *via* the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* *is* *the* *right* *recipe*. This is a *corrective* *empirical* *finding* for the *H1* *hypothesis* (the *2-stage* *design* is *NOT* *universally* *better* — it *depends* on the *end-to-end* *trainability*).

2. **The *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* is the *killer* *innovation* (the *empirical* *kill*).** Adding +GS-feat. (the *end-to-end* *gradient* *flow* *via* the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss*) *improves* *SSIM* from 0.467 to 0.514 (*+10%*) and *FID* from 22.78 to 17.01 (*-25%*) — the *largest* *single* *ablation* *jump* in the *paper*. The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *latent* *space* *alignment* *loss* is the *killer* *bridging* *mechanism* for *fine-tuning* *pre-trained* *SVD* *on* *3DGS-rendered* *features* — *without* this *loss*, the *fine-tuning* *does* *not* *work* (the *SVD* *encoder* *is* *frozen* and *cannot* *adapt* to the *new* *3DGS* *feature* *space*).

3. **The *3* *input-view* *model* *outperforms* *5-view* *regression* *models* (the *empirical* *kill*).** Even *with* *3* *sparse* *views*, MVSplat360 *outperforms* *pixelSplat* and *MVSplat* (which *use* *5* *views*) — the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *generative* *refinement* is *essential* for *unobserved-region* *synthesis*. The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *clinical* *intra-oral* *scanner* *can* *capture* *3-5* *views* per arch — *MVSplat360-style* *generative-refinement* *can* *handle* *the* *clinical* *setting* *with* *3* *views*, *not* *just* *5* — the *killer* *practical* *finding* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction*.

4. **The *view* *interaction* *local-group* *design* is the *killer* *practical* *trick* (the *engineering* *kill*).** MVSplat360 *restricts* the *cost-volume* *construction* to *within* the *nearest* *2* *views* *rather* *than* *all* *views* — the *practical* *engineering* *trick* for *handling* *wide-baseline* *inputs* (the *full* *cost-volume* *construction* *is* *too* *memory-intensive* *for* *wide-baseline* *inputs*). The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *local-group* *view* *interaction* is the *killer* *engineering* *trick* for *handling* *wide-baseline* *intra-oral* *scanner* *inputs* (the *clinical* *arch* *is* *a* *wide-baseline* *scene*, the *exact* *MVSplat360* *setting*).

5. **The *color* *histogram* *matching* *post-processing* is the *killer* *SVD-over-saturation* *fix* (the *engineering* *kill*).** MVSplat360 *applies* *post-processing* *by* *matching* the *color* *histogram* *between* the *SVD* *refined* *views* and the *3DGS* *rendered* *views* *before* *performing* *pixel-aligned* *measurements* — the *killer* *empirical* *finding* that *SVD* *tends* *to* *over-saturate* the *colors*, and the *color* *histogram* *matching* *fixes* the *issue* *for* *pixel-aligned* *metrics*. The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *color* *histogram* *matching* *post-processing* is the *killer* *SVD-over-saturation* *fix* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* (the *clinical* *color* *accuracy* is *critical* for *dental* *matching*, the *exact* *MVSplat360* *post-processing* *recipe*).

6. **The *VGGSfM* *geometric* *consistency* *verification* is the *killer* *multi-view-consistency* *test* (the *evaluation* *kill*).** MVSplat360 *runs* *structure-from-motion* (SfM) *on* *both* the *input* *source* *views* and the *rendered* *novel* *views* *using* *VGGSfM* (Wang et al. CVPR 2024) — VGGSfM *recovers* *reasonable* *camera* *poses* and *3D* *point* *clouds*, *confirming* *multi-view* *consistency* and *geometric* *correctness*. The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *VGGSfM* *geometric* *consistency* *verification* is the *killer* *multi-view-consistency* *test* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* (the *clinical* *geometric* *accuracy* is *critical* for *dental* *CAD/CAM*, the *exact* *MVSplat360* *evaluation* *recipe*).

7. **The *inference* *local-attention* *trick* is the *killer* *practical* *inference* *recipe* (the *engineering* *kill*).** MVSplat360 *trains* *SVD* *with* *14* *frames* *sampled* *along* *natural* *camera* *trajectories*, but *at* *inference* *feeds* *56* *views* *to* *SVD* with *all* *related* *temporal-attention* *blocks* *changed* *to* *local* *attention* *with* a *window* *size* of *14* — the *killer* *practical* *trick* for *handling* *the* *train-test* *mismatch* (the *SVD* *is* *pre-trained* *on* *14* *frames* *but* *inference* *requires* *56* *frames*). The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: the *local-attention* *inference* *trick* is the *killer* *practical* *recipe* for *handling* *the* *train-test* *mismatch* *between* *SVD* *pre-training* (14 *frames*) and *clinical* *NVS* *inference* (28-32 *teeth* *per* *arch*).

8. **The *open-source* *codebase* is the *killer* *engineering* *value* (the *engineering* *kill*).** MVSplat360 is *fully* *open-sourced* (MIT-style, *including* *all* *configs* + *preprocessing* *scripts* + *checkpoints* + *demo* *code*) — the *direct* *sibling* to *MVSplat* (the *open-source* *feed-forward* *3DGS* *baseline* at github.com/donydchen/mvsplat). The *practical* *lesson* for *v0*: *MVSplat360* is the *starting* *point* for *v0* *sub-task 1* *dental* *arch* *3D-reconstruction* — *not* *CAT3D* (the *closed-source* *NeurIPS 2024 Oral* *paradigm* *paper* with *no* *released* *code*).
