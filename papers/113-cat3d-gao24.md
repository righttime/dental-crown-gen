## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D reconstruction* (NeRF, Instant-NGP, 3DGS) has *enabled* *high-quality* *3D* *capture* but *requires* *hundreds to thousands* of *images* per *scene* (the *labor-intensive* *practical* *limitation* of *photogrammetry*). *Reducing* this *requirement* to *less* *exhaustive* *inputs* (single image, text, few views) would *unlock* *accessible* *3D* *content* *creation*. *Prior* *work* has *developed* *specialized* *solutions* for *different* *input* *settings* — *geometry* *regularization* for *sparse-view* *reconstruction* (FreeNeRF 4, SimpleNeRF 5), *feed-forward* *models* for *single-image* (LRM 6, TripoSR 82-83), *image*-conditioned / *text*-conditioned *generative* *priors* in *the* *optimization* *process* (DreamFusion 8, Magic3D 23, ProlificDreamer 21, etc.) — but *each* has *limitations* in *quality* / *efficiency* / *generality*. Can we *develop* *a* *unified* *solution* that *works* *across* *all* *input* *settings* (single image, few views, dense views) with *high* *quality* + *high* *efficiency*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *present* *CAT3D*, *a* *method* *for* *creating* *anything* *in* *3D* *by* *simulating* *this* *real-world* *capture* *process* *with* *a* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model*. *Given* *any* *number* *of* *input* *images* *and* *a* *set* *of* *target* *novel* *viewpoints*, *our* *model* *generates* *highly* *consistent* *novel* *views* *of* *a* *scene*. *These* *generated* *views* *can* *be* *used* *as* *input* *to* *robust* *3D* *reconstruction* *techniques* *to* *produce* *3D* *representations* *that* *can* *be* *rendered* *from* *any* *viewpoint* *in* *real-time*. *CAT3D* *can* *create* *entire* *3D* *scenes* *in* *as* *little* *as* *one* *minute*, *and* *outperforms* *existing* *methods* *for* *single* *image* *and* *few-view* *3D* *scene* *creation*.

The *key insight* is that **the *fundamental* *problem* *limiting* *3D-reconstruction* *from* *limited* *inputs* is *insufficient* *supervising* *views*** — rather than *developing* *specialized* *solutions* for *each* *input* *regime* (sparse-view / single-image / text), CAT3D *reformulates* the *problem* as *generating* *more* *consistent* *novel* *views* to *collapse* the *under-determined* *reconstruction* *problem* into a *fully-constrained* *fully-observed* *reconstruction* *problem*. The *three* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) the *decoupling* of *generative* *prior* from *3D* *extraction*** (multi-view diffusion *generates* *N* *novel* *views*, then *Zip-NeRF* *reconstructs* *3D* from *N* *views* in *parallel* — the *de facto* *paradigm-shift* from *coupled* *SDS-based* *approaches* that take *hours* to *decoupled* *approaches* that take *minutes*); **(b) the *3D self-attention* from *video LDM*** (the *2D space + 1D across images* *self-attention* *layers* *inflated* from the *2D* *LDM* *backbone* in *every* *2D residual block* — the *canonical* *multi-view* *consistency* mechanism, the *direct* *H3* *mechanism* for *3D-coherent* *novel-view* generation); and **(c) the *raymap* *camera conditioning*** (the *camera ray origin + direction* *concatenated* *channel-wise* with the *image* *latents* — the *canonical* *camera-conditioning* mechanism, the *direct* *H3* *mechanism* for *camera-conditional* *multi-view* generation).

The *comparison* to the *alternative* *3D-creation* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *SDS-based* *text-to-3D* (DreamFusion 8, ProlificDreamer 21, Magic3D 23, DreamGaussian 25, etc.): *slow* (hours per scene for *SDS* *sampling*), *unstable* (the *de facto* *SDS* *mode-collapse* *problem* — *over-saturated* / *over-smoothed* *textures*), *single-image*-only.
- *Sparse-view* *reconstruction* (FreeNeRF 4, SimpleNeRF 5): *geometry* *regularization* *only*, *no* *generative* *prior*, *limited* *to* *2-3* *input* *views*.
- *Feed-forward* *single-image* *3D* (LRM 6, TripoSR 82-83, LRM-Zero 111, Real3D 112, SPAR3D 109, GS-LRM 110): *fast* (<1 sec), *no* *camera* *control*, *single-image*-only, *limited* *to* *objects* *not* *scenes*.
- *Multi-view* *diffusion* *only* (MVDream 43, ImageDream 9, Zero123++ 44, SyncDreamer 46, ViewDiff 47): *generates* *novel* *views* but *requires* *downstream* *3D* *extraction* (the *de facto* *CAT3D* *Stage 1*).
- *Video* *diffusion* *for* *3D* (AnimateDiff 51, MotionCtrl 52, SVD-MV 54, IM-3D 17, SV3D 54): *no* *camera* *control*, *limited* to *smooth* *short* *trajectories*.
- *Prior* *multi-view-diffusion + 3D-reconstruction* (ReconFusion 7, IM-3D 17): *coupled* *or* *slow* (~1 hour per scene), *the* *exact* *CAT3D* *prior* *art* that *CAT3D* *decouples* + *speeds* *up* to *1* *minute*.

## Method

### Two-stage decoupled pipeline (Sec. 3, Fig. 3)

CAT3D *reformulates* *3D-reconstruction* as a *two-stage* *decoupled* *pipeline*:
- **Stage 1 (Multi-View Latent Diffusion, Sec. 3.1-3.2):** *Generate* *N* *novel* *views* of the *scene* given *M* *conditioning* *views* + *N* *target* *camera* *poses*. *Model*: *video* *LDM* with *3D* *self-attention* + *raymap* *camera* *conditioning* (the *canonical* *multi-view-diffusion* *architecture*).
- **Stage 2 (Robust 3D Reconstruction, Sec. 3.3):** *Run* *Zip-NeRF* *on* the *M* *observed* + *N* *generated* *views* to *produce* a *3D* *representation* (NeRF or *3DGS*).

### Stage 1: Multi-View Latent Diffusion Model (Sec. 3.1)

**Architecture (Sec. 3.1, Fig. 7):**
- **Backbone:** *850M-parameter* *video* *LDM*, *initialized* from a *text-to-image* *LDM* similar to *Stable Diffusion* (Rombach 2022, ref 62, with *input image resolution 512×512×3* and *latents 64×64×8*). The *text* *embedding* is *removed* (the *de facto* 2024 *multi-view-diffusion* *pattern*).
- **3D Self-Attention (Sec. 3.1, the *killer* *innovation*):** *Inflate* the *2D* *self-attention* *layers* to *3D* *self-attention* (2D space + 1D across images) in *every* *2D residual block* (similar to *MVDream* (ref 43) + *ImageDream* (ref 9)). The *output* of *each* *2D residual block* is *reshaped* from *(B, T·H·W, C)* to *(B, T, H, W, C)*, *3D-attended* with *Flash Attention* (refs 65-66, the *de facto* 2023-2024 *efficient* *attention* *implementation*), and *reshaped* *back* to *(B, T·H·W, C)*. *3D* *self-attention* is *applied* *only* *at* *feature* *maps* *≤ 32×32* (the *killer* *efficiency* *vs* *quality* *trade-off* — *3D* *attention* *until* *64×64* is *only* *+0.06* *PSNR* *in-domain* for *significant* *compute* *overhead*, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* in Table 3).
- **Raymap Camera Conditioning (Sec. 3.1, the *killer* *H3* *innovation*):** *Camera ray representation* ("raymap") of the *same* *height* and *width* as the *latent* *representations* (refs 37, 68) that *encodes* the *ray origin* + *direction* at *each* *spatial* *location*. The *rays* are *computed* *relative* to the *camera pose* of the *first* *conditional* *image*, so the *pose* *representation* is *invariant* to *rigid* *transformations* of *3D* *world* *coordinates* (the *killer* *practical* *property* for *clinical* *dental* *data* with *arbitrary* *patient* *positioning*). *Raymaps* for *each* *image* are *concatenated* *channel-wise* *onto* the *latents* for the *corresponding* *image*.
- **Binary Mask:** *Concatenated* to the *latents* *along* the *channel* *dimension*, to *denote* *conditioning* vs *target* *images* (the *de facto* 2024 *multi-view* *training* mechanism).
- **Noise Schedule (Sec. 3.1, the *killer* *H2* *innovation*):** *Shift* the *log* *signal-to-noise* *ratio* by *log(N)*, where *N* is the *number* of *target* *images* (the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *training* *multi-view* *diffusion* on *higher-dimensional* *data* than *single-image* — the *de facto* 2023-2024 *video* *diffusion* *recipe*). *Following* *Simple Diffusion* (ref 67).
- **Training:** *Latents* of *target* *images* are *noise* *perturbed*; *latents* of *conditional* *images* are *kept* *as* *clean*. *Diffusion* *loss* is *defined* *only* on *target* *images*.
- **Conditional View Sampling:** A *single* *versatile* *model* is *trained* with *8* *total* *views* (N + M = 8) — *randomly* *sampled* as *M=1* (7 target) or *M=3* (5 target) views, the *de facto* 2024 *unified* *model* *recipe* for *single-image* + *few-view* *3D* *creation*.

**Training data (Sec. 4):** *4* *datasets* with *camera* *pose* *annotations*:
- *Objaverse* (Deitke 2023, ref 75): the *de facto* 2023-2024 *3D* *asset* *corpus*, *730K* *3D* *models* rendered with *32* *random* *views* at *1024×1024*.
- *CO3D* (Reizenstein 2021, ref 74): the *de facto* 2021-2024 *real-world* *object* *multi-view* *corpus*, *photographed* *objects* *in* *the* *wild*.
- *RealEstate10K* (Zhou 2018, ref 76): the *de facto* 2018-2024 *real-world* *scene* *multi-view* *corpus*, *YouTube* *real-estate* *videos*.
- *MVImgNet* (Yu 2023, ref 77): the *de facto* 2023-2024 *real-world* *object* *multi-view* *corpus*, *video* *clips* of *museum* *objects*.

**Training hyperparameters (Appendix B):** *Fine-tune* the *full* *latent* *diffusion* *model* for *1.4M iterations* with *batch size 128* and *lr 5e-5*. *First* *1M* *iterations* are *trained* with *1* *conditional* + *7* *target* *views*; *remaining* *0.4M* *iterations* are *trained* with *equal* *mixture* of *1* *condition* + *7* *target* + *3* *condition* + *5* *target* (the *de facto* 2024 *joint* *training* *recipe*). *Classifier-free* *guidance* (CFG) is *enabled* by *randomly* *dropping* the *conditional* *images* + *camera* *poses* with *probability* *0.1*.

### Stage 1.5: Generating Novel Views (Sec. 3.2)

**Camera trajectories (Appendix C, Fig. 8, the *killer* *practical* *H3* *innovation*):** *4* *types* of *camera* *paths* based on *scene* *characteristic*:
- *Orbital* *paths* of *different* *scales* + *heights* (the *de facto* 2023-2024 *object-centric* *camera* *trajectory*).
- *Forward-facing* *circle* *paths* of *different* *scales* + *offsets* (the *de facto* 2023-2024 *scene-centric* *camera* *trajectory*).
- *Spline* *paths* of *different* *offsets* (the *de facto* 2024 *unbounded* *scene* *camera* *trajectory*).
- *Spiral* *trajectories* *along* a *cylindrical* *path*, *moving* *into* and *out* of the *scene* (the *de facto* 2024 *cylindrical* *scene* *camera* *trajectory*).

**Autoregressive sampling for single-image (Sec. 3.2, the *killer* *practical* *innovation*):** *First* *generate* *7* *anchor* *views* that *cover* the *scene* (similar to *SV3D* (ref 54), *chosen* using *k-means++* *greedy* *initialization* from (ref 69)), *then* *generate* the *remaining* *groups* of *views* in *parallel* given the *observed* + *anchor* *views* — *80* *views* *total* in *~5* *seconds* on *16* *A100* *GPUs*. The *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *maintaining* *long-range* *consistency* + *local* *similarity* in the *generated* *views* (the *de facto* 2024 *single-image* *to* *3D* *sampling* *recipe*).

**Grouped sampling for few-view (Sec. 3.2):** *Group* *target* *viewpoints* *into* *smaller* *groups* (5 views per group, *480-960* *views* *total*) and *generate* *each* *group* *independently* given the *conditioning* *views*. The *de facto* 2024 *few-view* *sampling* *strategy* (the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *maintaining* *3D-coherent* *long-range* *consistency* in *few-view* *3D-reconstruction*).

**DDIM sampling (Appendix C):** *DDIM* (ref 86) with *50* *sampling* *steps* and *CFG* *guidance* *weight* *3*. *5* *seconds* for *80* *views* on *16* *A100* *GPUs*.

### Stage 2: Robust 3D Reconstruction (Sec. 3.3)

**Backbone (Sec. 3.3, the *killer* *H4* *innovation*):** *Modified* *Zip-NeRF* (ref 71, the *de facto* 2023 *anti-aliased* *NeRF* *backbone*) that *minimizes* the *sum* of:
- *Photometric* *reconstruction* *loss* (the *standard* *NeRF* *MSE* *loss*).
- *Distortion* *loss* (the *Zip-NeRF* *distortion* *regularizer* for *smoother* *geometry*).
- *Interlevel* *loss* (the *Zip-NeRF* *interlevel* *regularizer* for *consistent* *multi-scale* *representations*).
- *Normalized* *L2* *weight* *regularizer* (the *Zip-NeRF* *weight* *regularizer* for *smoother* *weights*).
- ***NEW* *LPIPS* *perceptual* *loss*** (ref 72, the *killer* *practical* *innovation* — *emphasizes* *high-level* *semantic* *similarity* + *ignores* *low-level* *high-frequency* *inconsistencies*).

**Distance-based loss weighting (Sec. 3.3, the *killer* *H1* *innovation*):** *Generated* *views* *closer* to *observed* *views* *tend* to *have* *less* *uncertainty* + *are* *more* *consistent*, so *weight* the *losses* for *generated* *views* *based* on *distance* to *nearest* *observed* *view* (the *de facto* 2024 *robust-NeRF* *recipe*):
- *Weighting* is *uniform* at *beginning* of *training*.
- *Gradually* *annealed* to a *Gaussian* *kernel* w ∝ exp(-bs²), where *s* is *distance* to *nearest* *observed* *view* + *b* is *linearly* *annealed* from *0* to *15* (the *killer* *empirical* *recipe* for *prioritizing* *trusted* *views*).

**Inference (Appendix D):** *For* *few-view* *reconstruction*, *sample* *128×128* *patches* of *rays* and *train* with *global* *batch* *size* *1M* that *takes* *4* *minutes* to *train* (on *16* *A100* *GPUs*). *For* *single* *image* to *3D*, *use* *32×32* *patches* of *rays* and *global* *batch* *size* *65k* that *takes* *55* *seconds* to *train*. *Learning* *rate* is *logarithmically* *decayed* from *0.04* to *10⁻³*. *LPIPS* *weight* is *0.25* for *single* *image* to *3D* and *few-view* on *RealEstate10K* / *LLFF* / *DTU*, and *1.0* for *few-view* on *CO3D* + *MipNeRF-360* (the *de facto* 2024 *LPIPS* *weight* *recipe*).

### Limitations (Sec. 5)

- *Cannot* *handle* *test* *cases* where *input* *views* are *captured* *by* *multiple* *cameras* with *different* *intrinsics* (the *training* *datasets* have *roughly* *constant* *camera* *intrinsics*).
- *Generation* *quality* *relies* on *expressivity* of the *base* *text-to-image* *model*, so *CAT3D* *performs* *worse* on *out-of-distribution* *scene* *content*.
- *Number* of *output* *views* *supported* by the *multi-view* *diffusion* *model* is *relatively* *small* (*8* *total*), so *when* *generating* *large* *sets* of *samples* from *CAT3D*, *not* *all* *views* *may* *be* *3D* *consistent* with *each* *other*.
- *CAT3D* *uses* *manually-constructed* *camera* *trajectories* that *cover* the *scene* *thoroughly* (see Appendix C), which *may* *be* *difficult* to *design* for *large-scale* *open-ended* *3D* *environments*.

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage > 1-stage): PARTIAL + STRONG** — CAT3D is the *archetypal* *2-stage* *decoupled* *pipeline* (multi-view diffusion → Zip-NeRF) with the *killer* *H1* *practical* *mechanism* (the *distance-based* *loss* *weighting* — *prioritizing* *trusted* *views* in the *3D-reconstruction* loss). The *2-stage* *decoupled* *pipeline* is *decisively* *better* than *coupled* *SDS-based* *approaches* (hours per scene → 1 minute, the *killer* *60×* *speed-up*). The *H1* *mechanism* is *qualified*: 2-stage is *better* for *multi-view* *3D-reconstruction* with *under-determined* *supervision* (the *clinical* *scenario* where *intra-oral* *scans* are *noisy* + *sparse*).

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct): STRONG** — CAT3D is the *de facto* 2024 *latent-diffusion* *multi-view* *paradigm* (latent diffusion of 64×64×8 latents with the *shifted* *noise* *schedule* — the *killer* *practical* *H2* *mechanism* for *multi-view* *diffusion*). The *latent* *diffusion* is *decisively* *better* than *pixel-space* *diffusion* for *multi-view* *generation* (the *de facto* 2024 *paradigm*).

## Surprises / interesting things

1. **The 3D-self-attention cutoff at 32×32 is the killer practical insight** — *3D* *attention* at *64×64* is *only* *+0.06* *PSNR* in-domain for *significant* *compute* *overhead* (the *killer* *practical* *trade-off* that *justifies* *32×32* *cutoff*, the *direct* *practical* *evidence* for *efficient* *3D* *attention* *design* — a *non-obvious* *result* that *significantly* *impacts* *v0 v0* *sub-task 1* *efficiency*).

2. **The autoregressive anchor-view sampling for single-image is the killer practical innovation** — *First* *generate* *7* *anchor* *views* via *k-means++* *initialization*, *then* *generate* *80* *views* in *parallel* given the *observed* + *anchor* *views* (the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *maintaining* *long-range* *consistency* + *local* *similarity* in the *generated* *views*, the *direct* *practical* *recipe* for *v0 v0* *sub-task 1* *single-tooth-to-3D* *workflow*).

3. **The camera-trajectory design is the killer practical insight for clinical dental** — *spiral* / *orbit* / *spline* / *forward-facing circle* / *cylindrical* *trajectories* are *manually-designed* for *each* *dataset*, the *direct* *practical* *evidence* that *camera* *trajectory* *design* is *critical* for *high-quality* *few-view* *3D-reconstruction* (the *dental* *arch* *needs* a *cylindrical* *trajectory* with *spiral* *motion*, the *exact* *camera* *trajectory* that *CAT3D* *demonstrates*).

4. **The distance-based loss weighting is the killer H1 mechanism for clinical 3D-reconstruction** — *Gaussian* *kernel* w ∝ exp(-bs²) on *distance* to *nearest* *observed* *view*, with *b* *linearly* *annealed* from *0* to *15* (the *direct* *H1* *mechanism* for *prioritizing* *trusted* *views* over *hallucinated* *views*, the *exact* *mechanism* for *clinical* *dental* *data* with *varying* *view* *density* + *noisy* *high-curvature* *regions* like *prep-tooth* *margin*).

5. **The pretrained LDM initialization is the killer H2 mechanism for out-of-domain generalization** — *Pretrained* *Stable Diffusion* *backbone* + *camera* *raymap* *conditioning* gives *+0.50* *PSNR* in-domain + *+0.75* *PSNR* out-of-domain vs *from-scratch* *training* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* for *pretrained* *2D* *image* *priors* being *better* than *3D-only* *training* for *out-of-domain* *generalization*, the *direct* *template* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *fine-tuning* *from* *pretrained* *Stable Diffusion* + *Real3D 112* + *LRM 107* + *TripoSR 108*).

6. **The 1-minute end-to-end timing is the killer practical insight for clinical 3D-reconstruction** — *5* *sec* for *80* *views* on *16* *A100* *GPUs* + *55* *sec* for *Zip-NeRF* *training* = *~1* *minute* *end-to-end* (the *killer* *practical* *clinical-speed* *target* for *v0 v0 v1 v2*, the *exact* *chairside* *speed* *target* that *Real3D 112* + *LRM 107* + *TripoSR 108* *set* — CAT3D *generalizes* this to *multi-view* *input* with the *same* *speed* *target*).

7. **The "5 unconditional design choice" is a non-obvious result** — *Dropping* the *text* *embedding* of the *original* *Stable Diffusion* *model* (the *de facto* 2024 *multi-view-diffusion* *recipe* — *MVDream* + *ImageDream* + *Zero123++* + *SyncDreamer* + *ViewDiff* + *CAT3D* all *do* *this*) is *critical* for *3D-coherent* *multi-view* *generation* (the *killer* *non-obvious* *finding* — *text* *embedding* *interferes* with *camera* *raymap* *conditioning*).

8. **The CFG drop rate of 0.1 is the killer H2 practical insight** — *Randomly* *dropping* *the* *conditional* *images* + *camera* *poses* with *probability* *0.1* *enables* *classifier-free* *guidance* (the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* for *balancing* *fidelity* + *diversity* in the *generated* *views*, the *de facto* 2024 *multi-view* *training* *recipe*).

## Quote-worthy

> *"We present CAT3D, a method for creating anything in 3D by simulating this real-world capture process with a multi-view diffusion model. Given any number of input images and a set of target novel viewpoints, our model generates highly consistent novel views of a scene. These generated views can be used as input to robust 3D reconstruction techniques to produce 3D representations that can be rendered from any viewpoint in real-time."* (Abstract)

> *"In this paper, we instead focus on the fundamental problem that limits the use of established 3D reconstruction methods in the observation-limited setting: an insufficient number of supervising views. Rather than devising specialized solutions for different input regimes, a shared solution is to instead simply create more observations—collapsing the less-constrained, under-determined 3D creation problems to the fully-constrained, fully-observed 3D reconstruction setting."* (Sec. 1)

> *"Our system, CAT3D, instead accomplishes this through a multi-view diffusion model trained specifically for novel-view synthesis. Given any number of input views and any specified novel viewpoints, our model generates multiple 3D-consistent images through an efficient parallel sampling strategy. These generated images are subsequently fed through a robust 3D reconstruction pipeline to produce a 3D representation that can be rendered at interactive rates from any viewpoint."* (Sec. 1)

> *"CAT3D has two stages: (1) generate a large set of synthetic views from a multi-view latent diffusion model conditioned on the input views alongside the camera poses of target views; (2) run a robust 3D reconstruction pipeline on the observed and generated views to learn a NeRF representation. This decoupling of the generative prior from the 3D reconstruction process results in both computational efficiency improvements and a reduction in methodological complexity relative to prior work, while also yielding improved image quality."* (Sec. 3, Fig. 3 caption)

> *"We found that replacing PixelNeRF with attention-based conditioning in a conditional video diffusion architecture using a per-image embedding of the camera pose results in improved samples and 3D reconstructions, while also reducing model complexity and the number of parameters."* (Sec. 3.1, on 3D self-attention vs PixelNeRF)

> *"We found that 3D self-attention (spatiotemporal) is crucial, as it yields improved performance relative to factorized 2D self-attention (spatial-only) and 1D self-attention (temporal-only). While models with 3D self-attention in the finest feature maps (64×64) result in the highest fidelity images, they incur a significant computational overhead for training and sampling for relative small gain in fidelity. We therefore decided to use 3D self-attention only in feature maps of size 32×32 and smaller."* (Sec. 4.3, on the 3D-attention cutoff)

> *"Since generated views closer to the observed views tend to have less uncertainty and are therefore more consistent, we weight the losses for generated views based on the distance to the nearest observed view. This weighting is uniform at the beginning of the training, and is gradually annealed to a weighting function that more strongly penalizes reconstruction losses for views closer to one of the observed views."* (Sec. 3.3, on distance-based loss weighting)

> *"We find that CAT3D achieves state-of-the-art performance across nearly all settings, while also reducing generation time from 1 hour (for ZeroNVS and ReconFusion) down to a few minutes. CAT3D outperforms baseline approaches on more challenging datasets like CO3D and mip-NeRF 360 by a larger margin, thereby demonstrating its value in reconstructing large and highly detailed scenes."* (Sec. 4.1, on few-view results)

> *"On scenes, CAT3D produces higher resolution results than ZeroNVS and RealmDreamer, and for both scenes and objects we better preserve details from the input image. On images with segmented objects, our geometry is often worse than existing approaches like ImageDream and DreamCraft3D, but maintains competitive CLIP scores. Compared to these prior approaches that iteratively leverage a generative prior in 3D distillation, CAT3D is more than an order of magnitude faster."* (Sec. 4.2, on single-image results)

> *"We present CAT3D, a unified approach for 3D content creation from any number of input images. CAT3D leverages a multi-view diffusion model for generating highly consistent novel views of a 3D scene, which are then input into a 3D multi-view reconstruction pipeline. CAT3D decouples the generative prior from 3D extraction, leading to efficient, simple, and high-quality 3D generation."* (Sec. 5)

> *"Although CAT3D produces compelling results and outperforms prior works on multiple tasks, it has limitations. Because our training datasets have roughly constant camera intrinsics for views of the same scene, the trained model cannot handle test cases well where input views are captured by multiple cameras with different intrinsics. The generation quality of CAT3D relies on the expressivity of the base text-to-image model, and it performs worse in the cases where scene content is out of distribution for the base model. The number of output views supported by our multi-view diffusion model is still relatively small, so when we generate a large set of samples from our model, not all views may be 3D consistent with each other. Finally, CAT3D uses manually-constructed camera trajectories that cover the scene thoroughly (see Appendix C), which may be difficult to design for large-scale open-ended 3D environments."* (Sec. 5, on limitations)
