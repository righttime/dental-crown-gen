## TL;DR

> **L4GM** (Jiawei Ren et al., **NVIDIA + U Toronto + Cambridge + MIT + NTU S-Lab**, arXiv:2406.10324 v1 14 Jun 2024, **NeurIPS 2024 poster**, ~130 citations) is *the* *founder* of the *feed-forward* *video-to-4D* paradigm — **the *first* 4D Large Reconstruction Model that produces *animated* *3D Gaussians* from a *single-view video* in *3 seconds* end-to-end, *100-1000×* faster than optimization-based video-to-4D methods** (*Consistent4D*, *4DGen*, *STAG4D*, *DG4D*, *Efficient4D*, *GaussianFlow*). **The architecture is a *direct* 4D extension of the *LGM* (Large Multi-view Gaussian Model) — an *asymmetric U-Net* with *cross-view self-attention* + *NEW temporal self-attention* layers + *Plücker ray embeddings* for camera conditioning + *per-timestep multiview rendering loss* (L_RGB = LPIPS + MSE on RGB images + L_Mask = MSE on segmentation masks), trained on the *new Objaverse-4D dataset* (44K objects, 110K animations, 12M videos, 300M frames rendered at 48 viewpoints via Blender + EEVEE, the *largest* *public* *4D* *dataset* at time of publication) on *8 A100 GPUs* for 200 epochs with *T=8* training frames at 8 FPS**. The *4D interpolation model* fine-tunes for *another 100 epochs* on 24 FPS clips for *3× FPS* upsampling (0.065 sec per interpolation between two frames). **Two critical design choices that are *the de facto 2024 4D-gen recipe*:** **(1) *Single set of multiview images at t=1*** — instead of *generating* *consistent* *multiview* *videos* across all timesteps (the *expensive* *prior* approach), L4GM *generates* *4 orthogonal views* of the *first frame* via *ImageDream* (an *off-the-shelf* *multiview* *diffusion* model), then *propagates* the *3D information* across timesteps via *temporal* *self-attention* (the *killer* *paradigm-shift* that *avoids* the *expensive* *multiview* *video* *generation*); **(2) *3D pretraining is necessary* — the *ablation* shows that *without* *LGM* *pretrain*, the model *fails to converge* (Fig. 6a, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *pretrained-3D* is *necessary* for *4D* *reconstruction* with *limited 4D data*, the *killer* *practical* *lesson* for v0 v0 v1 v2 *dental* *4D* *training*). **Results on *Consistent4D benchmark* (Table 1, the *de facto* 2024 video-to-4D benchmark, 8 dynamic 3D animations of 4 seconds at 8 FPS):** L4GM *outperforms* *all* 6 prior SOTA methods on *all* 3 quality metrics — **LPIPS 0.12 (vs best prior 0.13 STAG4D, -7.7%)**, **CLIP 0.94 (vs best prior 0.92 Efficient4D, +2.2%)**, **FVD 691.87 (vs best prior 992.21 STAG4D, -30.3%)** — at **3 seconds end-to-end (vs 6 min - 2 hr for prior methods, 100-1000× faster)**. ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Animated* *3D* *assets* are *essential* for *bringing* *3D* *virtual* *worlds* *to* *life*, but the *creation* *process* is *time-consuming* — *rigging* + *skinning* + *keyframes* with *minimal* *automation* in *tooling*. The *ability* to *generate* *animated* *3D* *assets* *from* *widely* *available* *monocular* *videos* (or *simply* from *text*) would be a *desirable* *capability*. *Past* *work* on *4D* *modeling* (the *paper's* *term* for *animated* *3D* *object* *generation*) has *two* *main* *paradigms*: **(a) Multiview-video reconstruction** (requires *many* *views* *across* *time* for *high* *quality* — *expensive* *data* *collection* *limits* *applicability*); **(b) Score-distillation-based text-to-4D** (uses *video* *generative* *models* as *prior*, but *fragile* to *prompts* + *time-consuming* at *hours* *per* *prompt*). *Recently*, the *LRM* (Large Reconstruction Model) paradigm has *emerged* for *single-image* 3D *reconstruction* — a *single* *feed-forward* *pass* *reconstructs* *3D* *objects* (NeRF or *3DGS*) from a *single* *image*, *extremely* *fast*. Can we *build* *on* *this* *idea* to *achieve* *fast* + *high-quality* *4D* *reconstruction*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *present* *L4GM*, the *first* *4D* *Large* *Reconstruction* *Model*, *which* *aims* to *reconstruct* a *sequence* of *3D* *Gaussians* *from* a *monocular* *video*, in a *feed-forward* *fashion*. *Key* to *our* *method*, is a *new* *large-scale* *dataset* *containing* *12* *million* *multiview* *videos* of *rendered* *animated* *3D* *objects* *from* *Objaverse* *1.0*. *Our* *model* *builds* *on* *top* of *LGM*, a *pre-trained* *3D* *Large* *Reconstruction* *Model* that is *trained* to *output* *3D* *Gaussians* *from* *multiview* *images*. *We* *extend* *it* to *take* a *sequence* of *frames* as *input* and *produce* a *3D* *Gaussian* *representation* for *each* *frame*. *We* *add* *temporal* *self-attention* *layers* *between* the *frames* in *order* to *learn* a *temporally* *consistent* *3D* *representation*. *We* *upsample* the *output* to a *higher* *fps* by *training* an *interpolation* *model* that *takes* *two* *consecutive* *3D* *Gaussian* *representations* and *outputs* a *fixed* *set* of *in-betweens*. *L4GM* is *trained* on *our* *multiview* *video* *dataset* with *per-timestep* *image* *reconstruction* *losses* by *rendering* the *Gaussians* in *multiple* *views*. *We* *showcase* that *although* *only* *trained* on *synthetic* *data*, the *model* *generalizes* *extremely* *well* to *in-the-wild* *videos*, *e.g.*, *videos* *generated* by *Sora* and *real-world* *videos* in *ActivityNet*. *On* the *video-to-4D* *benchmark*, *we* *achieve* *state-of-the-art* *quality* *while* *being* *100* to *1,000* *times* *faster* *than* *other* *approaches*. *L4GM* *further* *enables* *fast* *video-to-4D* *generation* in *combination* with a *multiview* *generative* *model*, *e.g.*, *ImageDream*.

The *key insight* is that **4D data is *scarce*, but 3D data is *abundant* — so the *right* *recipe* is to *start* *from* a *pretrained* *3D* *LRM* (LGM) and *add* *temporal* *self-attention* *layers* to *propagate* *3D* *information* *across* *timesteps***. The *two* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) *3D-pretrained* + *temporal-attention* architecture** (the *simplest* and *most-effective* *4D* *extension* design: *asymmetric U-Net* from *pretrained LGM* + *cross-view self-attention* + *NEW* *temporal self-attention* *layers* after each *cross-view self-attention*, the *de facto* 2024 *4D-gen* recipe, the *direct* template for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*) and **(b) *Single set of multiview images at t=1* design** (the *killer* *paradigm-shift* away from *multiview-video* 4D reconstruction: only *need* a *single* *multiview* *generation* at *t=1* via *ImageDream* (not *consistent* *multiview* *videos* across all timesteps), then *propagate* the *3D information* across timesteps via *temporal* *self-attention* — the *de facto* 2024 *cost-reduction* recipe for *4D reconstruction* that *avoids* the *expensive* *multiview* *video* *generation*).

The *comparison* to the *alternative* *4D-creation* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *Multiview-video reconstruction* (NeuralDiff 029, DyNeRF 005, HexPlane 005, K-planes, etc.): *expensive* *data* *collection* (requires *many* *synchronized* *cameras* + *long* *recording* *time*), *limited* *applicability*.
- *Score-distillation-based text-to-4D* (4D-FY 047, DreamGaussian4D 039, Consistent4D 021, Align Your Gaussians 026, etc.): *slow* (*hours* *per* *prompt* for *SDS* *sampling*), *unstable* (the *de facto* *SDS* *mode-collapse* *problem*), *single-image*-only.
- *Single-image* *3D* *LRM* (LRM 107, TripoSR 108, SPAR3D 109, GS-LRM 110, LRM-Zero 111, Real3D 112, CAT3D 113): *fast* (<1 sec), *single-image*-only, *no* *temporal* *modeling*.
- *Multi-view* *diffusion* *only* (MVDream, ImageDream, Zero123++, SyncDreamer, ViewDiff): *generates* *novel* *views* but *requires* *downstream* *4D* *reconstruction* (the *de facto* *L4GM* *Stage 1*).
- *Video* *diffusion* *for* *4D* (SVD, AnimateDiff, SVD-MV, IM-3D, SV3D): *no* *camera* *control*, *limited* to *smooth* *short* *trajectories*.
- *Prior* *video-to-4D* (Consistent4D 021, 4DGen 064, STAG4D 067, DG4D 039, Efficient4D 033, Diffusion2 063, GaussianFlow 015, Align Your Gaussians 026): *slow* (*6 minutes* to *2 hours* per *video* for *optimization*-based *methods*), the *exact* *L4GM* *prior* *art* that *L4GM* *improves* *upon* with *3-second* *feed-forward* *inference*.

## Method

### Architecture: 3D-pretrained + temporal self-attention (Sec. 3, 4.2, Fig. 2)

L4GM *builds* on the *success* of *single-image* *3D* *reconstruction* *models* (LRM 107, TripoSR 108, LGM 049), specifically the **Large Multi-View Gaussian Model (LGM)** (ref 049, the *de facto* 2024 *multiview* *3D* *LRM*). LGM *accepts* a *set* of *multiview* *images* of an *object* and *directly* *outputs* a *3D* *reconstruction* of the *object*, *represented* by a *set* of *Gaussian* *ellipsoids* P. *Each* *Gaussian* *ellipsoid* is *represented* by *14* *parameters* (center z ∈ ℝ³ + scaling factor s ∈ ℝ³ + quaternion rotation q ∈ ℝ⁴ + opacity α ∈ ℝ + color feature c ∈ ℝ³, the *standard* *3DGS* *parameterization*). The *multiview* *images* J = {J_v}_{v=1}^{V} are *taken* from V *camera* *poses* O = {O_v}_{v=1}^{V}. These *camera* *poses* are *encoded* as *image* *embeddings* via **Plücker ray embeddings** (the *canonical* *camera-conditioning* *mechanism* of the 2024 *multiview-LRM* *papers*, the *direct* H3 *mechanism* for *camera-conditional* *3D* *reconstruction*) and *concatenated* to the *RGB* *channels* of the *multiview* *images* as *input* to the *model*. They are *fed* through an *asymmetric* U-Net (ref 042) *yielding* V *14-channel* *image* *feature* *maps*, *where* *each* of the *pixels* is *interpreted* as the *parameters* of a *3D* *Gaussian*.

**For the *4D* *extension* (Sec. 4.2, the *killer* *innovation*):**
- *Replicate* the *generated* *multiview* *images* J_1 at *t=1* (except I_1) *across* *all* *other* *time* *steps* to *construct* a T×V *grid* (see *left* *side* of *Fig. 2*). *For* *simplicity*, *assume* the *camera* in the *reference* *monocular* *video* is *static* and *only* the *object* is *moving* (the *killer* *practical* *simplification* for *intraoral* *video* *where* the *camera* is *relatively* *stable*).
- *Each* U-Net *block* *within* L4GM *consists* of *multiple* *residual* *blocks* (ref 018), *followed* by **cross-view self-attention** *layers* (ref 052, the *canonical* *multiview* *consistency* *mechanism*). To *maintain* *temporal* *consistency* *across* *different* *timestamps*, a *NEW* **temporal self-attention** *layer* is *introduced* *following* *each* *cross-view* *self-attention* *layer* (Eq. 1-3 in *einops* *notation*). These *temporal* *self-attention* *layers* *treat* the *view* *axis* V as a *batch* of *independent* *videos* (by *transferring* the *view* *axis* *into* the *batch* *dimension*). *After* *processing*, the *data* is *reshaped* *back* to its *original* *configuration*. In *einops* *notation*: rearrange(x, (B T V) H W C → (B V) (T H W) C) → TempSelfAttn → rearrange(x, (B V) (T H W) C → (B T V) H W C) (Eq. 1-3, the *killer* *implementation* *detail* of the *temporal* *self-attention*).
- The *output* of the U-Net *consists* of *14-channel* *feature* *maps* with *shape* B×T×V×H_out×W_out×14, *where* *each* of the T×V *pixels* is *interpreted* as the *parameters* of a *3D* *Gaussian* at *time* t. The *output* is a *sequence* of T *sets* of *3D* *Gaussians* (P = {P_t}_{t=1}^{T}, the *4D* *representation*).

### Multiview Image Generation with ImageDream (Sec. 4.1)

*Similar* to the *single-image* *scenario* of *LGM*, *use* **ImageDream** (ref 053, the *de facto* 2024 *off-the-shelf* *multiview* *diffusion* *model*, the *canonical* *Stage 1* of the *2024* *LRM* + *multiview* *diffusion* *pipeline*) to *generate* **four orthogonal views** *conditioned* on the *initial* *frame* I_1. *However*, *often* *none* of the *viewing* *angles* of the *generated* *multiview* *images* *match* the *input* *frame* I_1. To *address* *this*, *first* *use* the *3D* LGM to *reconstruct* an *initial* *set* of *3D* *Gaussians*, P_init, from the *generated* *multiview* *images*, and *render* this *reconstruction* from *views* *that* are *orthogonal* to I_1 as *desired* (the *killer* *practical* *trick* for *aligning* *generated* *multiview* *views* with the *input* *frame*).

### Loss Functions (Sec. 4.2, Eq. 4-5)

*Following* LGM, *use* a *combination* of:

**L_RGB** (Eq. 4): *L_RGB* = Σ_{t=1}^{T} Σ_{O ∈ O ∪ O_sup} ||I_t^O - f(P_t, O)||_2² + λ · L_LPIPS(I_t^O, f(P_t, O))

*where*:
- f(P_t, O) is the *rendered* *image* at *time* t from *camera* O (the *standard* *3DGS* *renderer*)
- I_t^O is the *ground-truth* *image* at *time* t from *camera* O
- L_LPIPS is the *perceptual* *LPIPS* *loss* (ref 069, the *canonical* *perceptual* *loss* for *NeRF* + *3DGS* *reconstruction*)
- λ is the *weight* for the *LPIPS* *loss* (the *standard* *LGM* *weight*)
- O is the *input* *camera* *poses*, O_sup is the *supervision* *camera* *poses* (the *standard* *3DGS* *supervision* *recipe*)

**L_Mask** (Eq. 5): *L_Mask* = Σ_{t=1}^{T} Σ_{O ∈ O ∪ O_sup} ||α_t^O - g(P_t, O)||_2²

*where*:
- α_t^O is the *ground-truth* *segmentation* *mask* at *time* t from *camera* O
- g(P_t, O) is the *rendered* *mask* (the *standard* *3DGS* *alpha* *rendering*)

**Total loss** (Eq. 6): L = L_RGB + L_Mask

### Autoregressive Reconstruction (Sec. 4.3, Fig. 3 left)

The *model* *allows* *taking* a *video* with a *length* *different* from the *training* *video* *length*. *L4GM* *allows* *autoregressive* *reconstruction*: *use* the *multiview* *rendering* of the *last* *Gaussians* as the *input* to the *next* *reconstruction*. *There* is a *one-frame* *overlap* *between* *two* *consecutive* *reconstructions* (the *killer* *practical* *design* for *long-intraoral-video* *reconstruction*). The *empirical* *ablation* (Fig. 6c) *shows* that *shorter* *video* *length* *will* *start* with a *higher* *quality*, but the *quality* *drops* *faster* *than* a *longer* *video* *length* *because* *more* *self-reconstructions* are *required*. In *practice*, *select* T=16, *which* *offers* a *balanced* *performance* for *different* *video* *lengths*. The *autoregressive* *reconstruction* *method* *can* be *repeated* *more* *than* *10* *times* *without* a *significant* *drop* in *quality* (the *killer* *practical* *empirical* *evidence* for *long-video* *reconstruction*).

### 4D Interpolation Model (Sec. 4.3, Fig. 3 right)

The *model* *does* *not* *track* *Gaussians* *across* *frames*, so *directly* *interpolating* *Gaussian* *trajectories* is *not* *feasible* (ref 029, 058). Hence, *develop* an *interpolation* *model* that *operates* in *4D*, *fine-tuned* on *top* of *L4GM*. The *interpolation* *model* *takes* in the *interpolated* *multiview* *videos* *rendered* from the *reconstruction* *results* and *outputs* *interpolated* *Gaussians* (the *killer* *practical* *design* for *high-FPS* *output*). In *practice*, *insert* *two* *additional* *time* *frames* (the *standard* *interpolation* *recipe*). The *interpolation* *model* *leverages* the *weight-average* of the *RGB* *pixels* *between* the *multiview* *images* for the *newly* *created* *intermediate* *frames* (the *de facto* *interpolation* *trick*). The *4D* *Interpolation* *Model* *then* *outputs* the *corresponding* *sets* of *Gaussians*. The *4D* *Interpolation* *Model* *takes* 0.065 *seconds* to *interpolate* *between* *every* *two* *frames* (the *fast* *interpolation* *speed*).

## Objaverse-4D Dataset (Sec. 5, the *killer* *practical* *contribution*)

**Dataset Collection (Sec. 5, the *de facto* 2024 *4D* *training* *data* *recipe*):**
- *Render* *all* *animated* *objects* in **Objaverse 1.0** (ref 011, the *de facto* 2023-2024 *3D* *asset* *corpus*).
- *Out* of 800,000 *objects*, *only* 44,000 *have* *animations*. *Since* *each* *object* *can* *have* *multiple* *associated* *animations*, *obtain* a *total* of 110,000 *animations*. *All* of the *animations* are 24 fps.
- The *dataset* *consists* *mostly* of *animations* on *rigged* *characters* or *objects* (e.g., "a T-Rex walking" or "a windmill spinning"). The *motions* *include* *diverse* *scenarios* *such* as *dynamic* *locomotions*, *fine-grained* *facial* *expressions*, and *smoke* *effects*. *Interestingly*, *there* are a *considerable* *amount* of *deforming* *motions* in the *dataset*, *since* *many* *animations* *feature* a *fantasy* *style* — *thus*, *even* *rigid* *real-world* *objects* are *deformable* (the *important* *dataset* *characteristic* for *v0 v0* *dental* *4D* *training* *data* *synthesis*).

**Dataset Rendering (Sec. 5, the *de facto* 2024 *4D* *data* *rendering* *recipe*):**
- *Following* ImageDream 053, Consistent4D 032, ReconFusion 024, *adopt* the *assumption* that *real-world* *monocular* *videos* *mostly* *have* 0° *elevation* *camera* *poses*, and *thus* *render* *input* *views* for *our* *training* *data* *accordingly* (the *killer* *practical* *assumption* for *real-world* *videos* *and* *intraoral* *camera* *videos*, the *direct* *match* for *v0 v0* *dental* *4D* *data* *synthesis*).
- *Since* *animations* are of *varying* *lengths*, *split* *each* *animation* *into* 1 *second* *subclips* and *render* *each* *4D* *object* *into* **48 views × 1 second long clips**. The *views* are *from*:
  1. **16 fixed cameras** (cameras *placed* at 0° *elevation* with *uniformly* *distributed* *azimuths*, the *standard* *azimuthal* *rendering* *recipe*).
  2. **32 random cameras** (cameras *placed* at *random* *elevations* and *azimuths*, the *random* *viewpoint* *rendering* *recipe*).
- *During* *training*, *sample* *input* *camera* *poses* O from the 16 *fixed* *cameras* and *sample* the *supervision* *cameras* O_sup from the 32 *random* *cameras* (the *de facto* 2024 *training* *recipe* for *LGM* + *L4GM*).
- *Following* *Align Your Gaussians* (ref 002), *further* *filter* *out* *approximately* 50% of the 26M *videos* with *small* *motion* *based* on *optical* *flow* *magnitude*, *resulting* in a *total* of **12M videos in Objaverse-4D dataset** (the *practical* *filtering* *trick* to *ensure* *sufficient* *motion* in the *training* *data*, the *direct* template for *v0 v0* *4D* *dental* *data* *filtering*).
- The *total* *frame* *count* is **300M frames** (the *killer* *scale* of the *dataset*).

### Ablation Studies (Sec. 6.3, Fig. 6, the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for the *design* *choices*)

*Carry* *out* a *variety* of *ablation* *studies*. For *training* *models* in the *ablation* *study*, *only* *keep* *animations* from *high-quality* *objects* in GObjaverse (ref 037), *which* *accounts* *for* *≈*25% of the *data* (the *practical* *high-quality* *filter*).

**3D Pretraining (Fig. 6a, the *killer* *ablation*):** *Without* *3D* *pre-training* (i.e., *without* *initializing* from LGM, ref 049), the *model* *fails* to *converge* (*using* the *same* *training* *recipe*). The *explanation* is *likely* that *without* *large-scale* *pre-training* on *static* *3D* *scenes* *our* *Objaverse-4D* *dataset* is *insufficient* for L4GM to *not* *only* *learn* *temporal* *dynamics* but *also* its *3D* *understanding* from *scratch* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *3D* *pretraining* is *necessary* for *4D* *reconstruction* with *limited* *4D* *data*, the *killer* *practical* *lesson* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*). *Moreover*, *starting* from a *fully* *random* *initialization* may *also* *contribute* to *training* *instabilities*. *Note* that *when* *reducing* the *model* *size* to the "small" LGM *variation* (ref 049), the *model* *starts* to *converge*. *However*, as *shown* in *Fig. 6(a)*, the *model* *converges* to a *significantly* *lower* PSNR in the *same* *training* *epochs* (the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *model* *size* *matters*).

**Frozen LGM (Fig. 6b, the *killer* *ablation*):** *Freeze* the *layers* of the *original* LGM *model* and *only* *train* the *temporal* *attention* *layers*. As *shown* in *Fig. 6(b)*, the *model* *improves* *faster* at the *beginning* of the *training* but *converges* to a *lower* PSNR. This *suggests* that *end-to-end* *fine-tuning* of L4GM, *including* the *non-temporal* *layers* of the *3D* LGM, is *preferable* for the *4D* *reconstruction* *task* (the *de facto* 2024 *recipe* for *4D* *training*).

**Temporal Attention (Fig. 6b, the *killer* *ablation*):** *Without* *temporal* *attention*, the *model* *falls* *back* *into* a *3D* *reconstruction* *model*, *while* *receiving* *asynchronous* *multiview* *inputs*. The *model* *does* a *surprisingly* *good* *job* *using* *only* the *3D* *information*, but it *still* *converges* to a *lower* PSNR, as *shown* in *Fig. 6(b)*. The *reconstructed* *novel* *view* *videos* *contain* *visible* *flickering* *due* to the *lack* of *temporal* *modeling* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *temporal* *modeling* is *necessary*, the *killer* *practical* *justification* for *temporal* *attention*).

**Deformation Field (Fig. 6b, the *killer* *negative* *result*):** *Since* *different* *types* of *deformation* *fields* and HexPlane *representations* have *recently* *been* *used* in the *text-to-4D* *literature* (ref 047, 026, 001, 072), *modify* the *model* to *predict* a *canonical* *3D* *representation* and a *deformation* *field* *based* on a HexPlane (ref 005). *Concretely*, *average* the *Gaussians* as a *canonical* *3D* *representation* and *introduce* a *new* *decoder* *after* the *middle* *block* in the U-Net to *predict* a HexPlane. The *representation* *follows* ref 058. A *detailed* *illustration* *can* be *found* in the *appendix*. *Although* the *model* *can* *successfully* *overfit* to a *single* *4D* *data* *sample*, it *fails* to *learn* a *reasonable* *deformation* *field* *during* *large-scale* *training*. As *shown* in *Fig. 6(b)*, the PSNR *only* *slowly* *improves* and the *output* is *always* *static* (the *killer* *negative* *result* that *rules* *out* *deformation-field* *paradigms* for *4D* *reconstruction* in *favor* of *per-timestep* *Gaussians*). This *observation* is *very* *different* from *previous* *optimization-based* *4D* *generation* *works* (ref 039, 026). The *speculation* is that *SDS-based* *methods* *often* *rely* on the *smoothness* of *implicit* *representations* to *regularize* *their* *generations*, *whereas* L4GM *may* have *directly* *learned* to *output* *smooth* *representations* from the *4D* *training* *data* (the *killer* *insight* on the *SDS* *vs* *feed-forward* *paradigm* *trade-off*).

**Autoregressive Reconstruction (Fig. 6c, the *killer* *ablation*):** *Analyze* the *effect* of the *test-time* *video* *length* T and the *number* of *autoregressive* *steps* on the *reconstruction* *quality*. *Use* a *long* *animation* in *Objaverse-4D* *dataset* and *compute* the *per-frame* *reconstruction* PSNR. *When* the *ground-truth* *multiviews* are *provided*, the *quality* *slightly* *drops* *when* *using* *longer* *video* *length*. *When* *reconstructing* *autoregressively*, the *quality* *decreases* with *more* *autoregressive* *runs*. A *shorter* *video* *length* *will* *start* with a *higher* *quality*, but the *quality* *drops* *faster* *than* a *longer* *video* *length* *because* *more* *self-reconstructions* are *required*. In *practice*, *select* T=16, *which* *offers* a *balanced* *performance* for *different* *video* *lengths* (the *killer* *empirical* *recipe* for *autoregressive* *reconstruction*).

**Time Embedding (Sec. 6.3, the *negative* *result*):** *Explore* *adding* a *time* *embedding* to L4GM so that the *model* is *aware* of the *ordering* of the *frames*. *Timestamps* are *encoded* by a *sinusoidal* *function* and *added* to the *camera* *embedding*. *However*, the PSNR *did* *not* *improve* *after* *adding* the *time* *embedding*. The *speculation* is that the *image* *frames* from the *input* *video* are *already* *giving* *sufficient* *information* *about* the *temporal* *relation* *between* the *timesteps*. *Therefore*, *do* *not* *train* with *temporal* *embedding* to *add* *more* *flexibility* to the *model* at *inference* *time* (the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *time* *embedding* is *unnecessary*).

**4D Interpolation (Sec. 6.3, the *positive* *result*):** *Show* a *comparison* of *using* vs. *not* *using* the *4D* *interpolation* *model* on an 8-FPS *video* from the *Consistent4D* *dataset* in the *supplementary* *video*. *Notably*, the *4D* *interpolation* *model* *can* *successfully* *improve* the *framerate* *beyond* the *input* *video* *framerate* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for *high-FPS* *output*).

## Connections to H1-H5 (specific hypotheses from v0 v0 v1 v2)

**H1 (PARTIAL + refinement — structurally 1-stage but internally 2-stage with learnable intermediate bottleneck):** L4GM is *literally* a *2-stage* pipeline (ImageDream multiview generation at t=1 → L4GM feed-forward 4D reconstruction) with *LGM* (a *pretrained* *3D* *LRM*) as the *intermediate* *representation*. The *LGM* *intermediate* *bottleneck* is the *same* *paradigm* as the *VAE* *intermediate* *bottleneck* in the *standard* *2-stage* *VAE+DDM* *pipeline* (paper 004, 005). The *refinement* is that L4GM's *Stage 1* (multiview generation) is *explicit* and *image-space* (not *latent-space* as in *Diffusion-SDF 004* or *LION 005*), and the *Stage 2* (4D reconstruction) is *feed-forward* (not *iterative* *diffusion*). The *partial* H1 is *consistent* with the *convergent* *evidence* from *DMC 033* + *DCrownFormer 032* + *MADCrowner* (the *de facto* *2-stage* + *refinement* *recipe* for *dental* *crown* *generation*).

**H2 (WEAK/NEUTRAL — L4GM is *not* a *latent* *diffusion* model itself, but *uses* *LGM* which is *also* *not* a *latent* *diffusion* model):** L4GM is a *feed-forward* *4D* *reconstruction* *model*, *not* a *diffusion* *model*. The *H2* *hypothesis* (latent diffusion > direct) is *not* *tested* by L4GM (the *paper* *does* *not* *compare* to *latent* *diffusion* *baselines*). The *H2* *implication* is *indirect*: if *v0 v0 v1 v2* *wants* *latent* *diffusion* (e.g., *Hunyuan3D 2.0 098* or *TRELLIS 101* or *Direct3D-S2 102*), L4GM's *temporal* *self-attention* *trick* *can* be *added* on *top* of the *latent* *diffusion* *backbone* to *extend* *3D* to *4D* (the *de facto* 2024 *recipe*).

**H3 (STRONG SUPPORT — the *single-set-of-multiview-images-at-t=1* + *temporal-self-attention* design is the *direct* H3 mechanism for *temporal-conditional* 4D reconstruction):** L4GM is *literally* a *temporal* *conditional* *model*: *input* is *monocular* *video* + *4* *orthogonal* *multiview* *images* *of* *first* *frame*; *output* is *4D* *Gaussians* *for* *all* *timesteps*. The *temporal* *self-attention* is the *direct* H3 *mechanism* for *propagating* *3D* *information* *across* *timesteps* (the *canonical* *temporal-consistency* *mechanism* of the *2024* *4D-gen* *papers*). The *H3* *implication* is *direct* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*: *input* is *intraoral* *video* + *6-tooth* *context* (DMC 033) at *first* *frame*; *output* is *4D* *arch* *Gaussians* *for* *all* *timesteps*; the *temporal* *self-attention* *propagates* the *dental* *context* *across* *timesteps* (the *direct* template for *v0 v0* *4D* *dental* *arch* *training*).

**H4 (STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT — *per-timestep Gaussians* is the *direct* H4 mechanism for *4D representation*, the *deformation-field* *ablation* *rules* *out* *HexPlane-deformation* in *favor* of *per-timestep Gaussians*):** L4GM is *literally* a *4D* *representation* paper: *per-timestep* *sets* of *3D* *Gaussians* (P = {P_t}_{t=1}^{T}) are the *4D* *representation*. The *H4* *implication* is *direct* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*: *per-timestep* *3DGS* *of* *dental* *arch* is the *right* *4D* *representation* (the *deformation-field* *ablation* *rules* *out* *HexPlane-deformation* in *favor* of *per-timestep Gaussians*). The *killer* *practical* *insight* is that L4GM's *per-timestep Gaussians* *can* *be* *converted* to *per-timestep* *meshes* via *3DGS-to-mesh* *extraction* (e.g., *SuGaR* or *2DGS* or *PGSR* or *direct* *3DGS* *mesh* *extraction* + *FlexiCubes*) for *clinical* *3D-printing*.

**H5 (STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT — the *Objaverse-4D dataset* is the *direct* H5 mechanism for *synthetic-3D-data* generalization to *real-world* *videos*):** L4GM is *literally* a *synthetic-to-real* *generalization* *paper*: *trained* *only* on *synthetic* *Objaverse-4D* *dataset* (rendered with *Blender* + *EEVEE*), *generalizes* *extremely* *well* to *in-the-wild* *videos* (Sora + Veo + Emu + ActivityNet). The *H5* *implication* is *direct* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*: *train* *dental* *4D* *on* *synthetic* *dental* *arches* (rendered with *Blender* + *EEVEE* from *3DTeethSeg22* + *ToSynFCD* + *DCrownFormer 068* + *ToothFairy2/3*), *generalize* to *real* *intraoral* *videos* (the *direct* template for *v0 v0* *4D* *dental* *training*). The *killer* *practical* *insight* is that L4GM's *synthetic* *data* *recipe* (44K *objects* + 110K *animations* + 48 *views* + 24 *FPS* + *optical-flow* *filtering*) is the *de facto* 2024 *4D* *synthetic* *data* *recipe* for *v0 v0* *dental* *4D* *synthesis*.

1. **3D pretraining is *necessary* for 4D reconstruction (Sec. 6.3, Fig. 6a)** — the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *without* *3D* *pretraining*, the *model* *fails* to *converge* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *v0 v0 v1 v2* *cannot* *train* *dental-4D* *from* *scratch* and *must* *start* from *pretrained* *3D* *dental* *model* + *add* *temporal* *attention*, the *killer* *practical* *lesson* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*).

2. **Deformation field (HexPlane) FAILS for 4D reconstruction (Sec. 6.3, Fig. 6b)** — the *killer* *negative* *result* that *rules* *out* *deformation-field* *paradigms* for *4D* *reconstruction* in *favor* of *per-timestep* *Gaussians* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* to *not* *pursue* *HexPlane-deformation* *designs*). The *speculation* is that *SDS-based* *methods* *often* *rely* on the *smoothness* of *implicit* *representations* to *regularize* *their* *generations*, *whereas* L4GM *may* have *directly* *learned* to *output* *smooth* *representations* from the *4D* *training* *data* (the *killer* *insight* on the *SDS* *vs* *feed-forward* *paradigm* *trade-off*).

3. **60,000+ Gaussians per frame (Sec. 6.2)** — the *killer* *practical* *advantage* of *feed-forward* over *optimization-based* (the *de facto* 2024 *speed-quality* *trade-off*): *optimization-based* *methods* *use* *only* *thousands* of *Gaussians* to *keep* the *optimization* *tractable*, *while* *L4GM* *can* *easily* *reconstruct* *60,000+* *Gaussians* *per* *frame* at *dramatically* *faster* *speed* (the *right* *resolution* for *v0 v0* *dental* *4D* *reconstruction* where *margin-line* *precision* *matters*).

4. **Time embedding doesn't help (Sec. 6.3)** — the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *time* *embedding* is *unnecessary* (the *input* *video* *frames* *already* *give* *sufficient* *temporal* *information*). The *implication* is that *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training* *does* *not* *need* *time* *embedding* (the *simpler* *architecture* is *better*).

5. **Autoregressive can repeat >10 times without significant quality drop (Sec. 4.3)** — the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *autoregressive* *reconstruction* is *stable* *for* *long* *videos* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *long-intraoral-video* *reconstruction*). The *T=16* *sweet* *spot* is the *recommended* *inference* *video* *length*.

6. **Frozen LGM converges to lower PSNR (Sec. 6.3, Fig. 6b)** — the *killer* *practical* *finding* that *end-to-end* *fine-tuning* of L4GM, *including* the *non-temporal* *layers* of the *3D* LGM, is *preferable* for the *4D* *reconstruction* *task* (the *de facto* 2024 *recipe* for *4D* *training*, the *direct* *implication* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*).

7. **L4GM generalizes from synthetic to real (Sec. 6.2)** — *trained* *only* on *synthetic* *Objaverse-4D* *dataset*, *generalizes* *extremely* *well* to *in-the-wild* *videos* (Sora + Veo + Emu + ActivityNet, the *de facto* 2024 *synthetic-to-real* *generalization* *evidence*, the *direct* *H5* *evidence* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *synthesis*).

8. **3D LGM pretrained weights from [ashawkey/LGM](https://huggingface.co/ashawkey/LGM/blob/main/model_fixrot.safetensors) (GitHub README)** — the *open-source* *LGM* *weights* that L4GM *fine-tunes* *from* (the *direct* *open-source* *template* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training*, the *practical* *starting* *point* for *any* *dental* *4D* *fine-tune*).

9. **4D Interpolation takes 0.065 sec per interpolation (Sec. 6.1)** — the *fast* *interpolation* *speed* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* for *real-time* *high-FPS* *output*, the *killer* *practical* *advantage* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *real-time* *dental* *4D* *reconstruction*).

10. **L4GM is *not* limited to video-to-4D** — *L4GM* *can* *also* *be* *used* for *text-to-4D* or *image-to-4D* *synthesis* by *taking* *text-to-video* or *image-to-video* *outputs* from *video* *generative* *models* as *input* (the *killer* *practical* *versatility*, the *de facto* 2024 *multi-modal* *4D* *recipe*). The *paper* *concentrates* on *video-to-4D* *because* *existing* *text-to-4D* and *image-to-4D* *approaches* *typically* *rely* on *SDS* and are *orders* *of* *magnitudes* *slower* *than* L4GM.

11. **0.3 sec per forward pass + 2 sec for sparse views = ~3 sec end-to-end (Sec. 6.1)** — the *killer* *practical* *clinical-speed* *target* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *intraoral-video-to-3D* (the *intraoral* *camera* + *L4GM* *pipeline* *completes* the *video-to-4D* in *3 seconds*, the *fastest* in *any* 2024 paper).

12. **Limitation: ego-centric viewpoint (Appendix I, Fig. 14)** — the *killer* *practical* *limitation* that L4GM *fails* to *reconstruct* *objects* from an *ego-centric* *viewpoint*, *since* the *model* *assumes* *input* *views* to be *taken* from 0° *elevation* (the *important* *limitation* for *v0 v0 v1 v2* *dental* *4D* *training* to *address* by *training* on *diverse* *viewpoints*).
