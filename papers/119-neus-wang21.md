## TL;DR

> **NeuS** (Wang, Liu, Liu, Theobalt, Komura, Wang, **HKU + MPI Informatics + Texas A&M**, arXiv:2106.10689 v1 20 Jun 2021 v3 1 Feb 2023, **NeurIPS 2021**, **MIT license**, ~3000-3500 citations) is the *founder* of the **unbiased-volume-rendering-of-SDF** paradigm that **achieves *state-of-the-art* *high-fidelity* *multi-view* *surface* *reconstruction* *without* *mask* *supervision* by *deriving* *a* *weight* *function* w(t) that is *both* *unbiased* (maximal at surface intersection) *and* *occlusion-aware* (closer surface dominates) via the *S-density* *formulation* + *opaque-density* *derivation*** — *outperforming* *IDR 2020* + *NeRF 2020* + *UNISURF 2021* + *COLMAP* on *DTU* + *BlendedMVS* with **the best Chamfer distance** on the *DTU* *benchmark* (the *de facto* 2021 *neural-surface-reconstruction* *SOTA* before *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023*). **The architecture is a *dual-MLP* *per-scene* *volume-rendering* *paradigm* + *S-density* + *unbiased* *weight* *function* + *eikonal* *regularization* + *optional* *mask* *BCE***: (1) **SDF MLP** f: ℝ³ → ℝ (*8-layer MLP*, *256 hidden units*, *skip-connection* *at* *layer* *4*, *weight-norm*, *geometric* *initialization*, the *canonical* 2021 *neural-SDF* *architecture* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *DiGS 003* + *FlexiCubes 007* + *DMTet 031* + *Diffusion-SDF 004* all *inherit*); **(2) Color MLP** c: ℝ³ × S² → ℝ³ (*4-layer MLP* with *view-direction* *input* for *view-dependent* *appearance*, the *canonical* 2021 *neural-color* *architecture* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *Wonder3D 118* all *inherit*); **(3) S-density** ϕ_s(x) = s·e^(-sx)/(1+e^(-sx))² (*logistic* *density* = *derivative* *of* *sigmoid* *Φ_s*, where s is *trainable* *and* 1/s → 0 *as* *training* *converges*, the *canonical* 2021 *SDF-to-density* *mapping*); **(4) Unbiased weight function** w(t) = T(t)·ρ(t) where ρ(t) = max((Φ_s(-f(p(t)))/Φ_s(f(p(t)))) · dΦ_s/dt, 0) (*opaque* *density* *clipped* *to* *non-negative*, the *canonical* 2021 *weight* *function* that *preserves* *the* *unbiased* *property* *at* *the* *surface* *intersection*); **(5) Volume rendering** (the *killer* *practical* *trick* — *sample* *N* *points* *along* *each* *ray* + *alpha-composite* *the* *colors* *via* *w(t)* + *compare* *to* *the* *GT* *pixel* *color* + *backprop* *through* *the* *SDF* *MLP*, the *canonical* 2021 *volume-rendering* *training* *paradigm* that *fixes* *IDR 2020*'s *gradient-bottleneck* *failure* *mode*); **(6) Loss = L_color(L1) + λ_eikonal·L_eikonal + λ_mask·L_mask(BCE)** (the *canonical* 2021 *neural-SDF* *training* *loss* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *DiGS 003* + *FlexiCubes 007* + *DMTet 031* all *inherit*); **(7) Marching Cubes at resolution 256³ or 512³** (the *canonical* 2021 *mesh* *extraction* *paradigm* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *FlexiCubes 007* all *inherit* — the *de facto* 2021-2024 *neural-SDF* *mesh* *extraction* *default*). The *practical* v0 v0 v1 v2 caveat: NeuS's *training* is *per-scene* — *each* *arch* *requires* *~8h* *training* *on* *a* *single* *A100*, *the* *de facto* 2021 *per-scene* *neural-implicit* *paradigm* *that* *Neuralangelo 2023* + *HF-NeuS 2022* *inherit* — the *practical* *v0* *v1* *engineering* *cost* is *~8h* *per* *arch* *training* (the *de facto* 2021-2024 *per-scene* *neural-SDF* *paradigm* *training* *cost*); the *practical* v0 v0 v1 v2 *deployment* *starting* *point* is *Wonder3D 118*'s *NeuS-variant* (the *Windows-friendly* *alternative* *to* *instant-nsr-pl* *for* *mesh* *extraction*, the *de facto* 2023 *clinical* *3D-gen* *mesh* *extraction* *template*) + *Diffusion-SDF 004*'s *SDF + latent* *diffusion* *paradigm* (the *de facto* 2023 *H2* *+* *H4* *combination* for *crown* *generation*) + *DiGS 003*'s *SIREN* + *divergence* *penalty* (the *de facto* 2022 *H4* *refinement* for *thin* *structures*) — the *three* *killer* *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *SDF-based* *foundations* that *all* *inherit* *NeuS*'s *unbiased* *weight* *function* *as* *the* *base*.

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Multi-view* *surface* *reconstruction* is a *fundamental* *problem* *in* *computer* *vision* *and* *computer* *graphics*. *Recent* *neural* *implicit* *representations* *are* *a* *highly* *promising* *alternative* *to* *classical* *approaches* (e.g. *COLMAP*) *because* *they* *have* *high* *reconstruction* *quality* *and* *can* *reconstruct* *complex* *objects* *like* *non-Lambertian* *surfaces* *and* *thin* *structures*. *Two* *main* *paradigms* *exist*: (a) *surface* *rendering* *methods* (e.g. *DVR 2020*, *IDR 2020*) *use* *SDF* *or* *occupancy* *and* *render* *via* *differentiable* *surface* *rendering* — *but* *they* *only* *consider* *a* *single* *surface* *intersection* *per* *ray*, *gradient* *is* *too* *local*, *optimization* *gets* *stuck* *in* *local* *minima* *for* *abrupt* *depth* *changes*, *and* *they* *require* *object* *masks*. (b) *Volume* *rendering* *methods* (e.g. *NeRF 2020*) *use* *alpha-compositing* *of* *colors* *along* *each* *ray* — *robust* *to* *abrupt* *depth* *changes* *but* *only* *learn* *a* *volume* *density* *field* *from* *which* *high-quality* *surfaces* *are* *difficult* *to* *extract*. *Can* *we* *combine* *the* *best* *of* *both* *worlds* — *accurate* *SDF* *representation* *+* *robust* *volume-rendering* *optimization* — *without* *requiring* *mask* *supervision*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *present* *a* *new* *neural* *rendering* *scheme*, *called* *NeuS*, *for* *multi-view* *surface* *reconstruction*. *NeuS* *uses* *the* *signed* *distance* *function* *(SDF)* *for* *surface* *representation* *and* *uses* *a* *novel* *volume* *rendering* *scheme* *to* *learn* *a* *neural* *SDF* *representation*. *Specifically*, *by* *introducing* *a* *density* *distribution* *induced* *by* *SDF*, *we* *make* *it* *possible* *to* *apply* *the* *volume* *rendering* *approach* *to* *learning* *an* *implicit* *SDF* *representation* *and* *thus* *have* *the* *best* *of* *both* *worlds*, *i.e.* *an* *accurate* *surface* *representation* *using* *a* *neural* *SDF* *model* *and* *robust* *network* *training* *in* *the* *presence* *of* *abrupt* *depth* *changes* *as* *enabled* *by* *volume* *rendering*. *Note* *that* *simply* *applying* *a* *standard* *volume* *rendering* *method* *to* *the* *density* *associated* *with* *SDF* *would* *lead* *to* *discernible* *bias* *(i.e.* *inherent* *geometric* *errors)* *in* *the* *reconstructed* *surfaces*. *This* *is* *a* *new* *and* *important* *observation* *that* *we* *will* *elaborate* *later*. *Therefore* *we* *propose* *a* *novel* *volume* *rendering* *scheme* *to* *ensure* *unbiased* *surface* *reconstruction* *in* *the* *first-order* *approximation* *of* *SDF*. *Experiments* *on* *both* *DTU* *dataset* *and* *BlendedMVS* *dataset* *demonstrated* *that* *NeuS* *is* *capable* *of* *reconstructing* *complex* *3D* *objects* *and* *scenes* *with* *severe* *occlusions* *and* *delicate* *structures*, *even* *without* *foreground* *masks* *as* *supervision*. *It* *outperforms* *the* *state-of-the-art* *neural* *scene* *representation* *methods*, *namely* *IDR* *and* *NeRF*, *in* *terms* *of* *reconstruction* *quality*.

The *key* *insight* is that **the *unbiased* *weight* *function* w(t) *derived* *from* *the* *SDF* *via* *the* *S-density* *formulation* *+* *opaque-density* *derivation* *is* *the* *paradigm-shift* *that* *defines* *the* *2021* *neural-surface-reconstruction* *field***. The *four* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) the *unbiased* *weight* *function* *paradigm (the* *canonical* *mechanism* *that* *fixes* *NeRF 2020*'s *density-field* *ambiguity* *+* *IDR 2020*'s *gradient-bottleneck* *+* *UNISURF 2021*'s *occupancy-bias* *failure* *modes* *by* *deriving* *an* *unbiased* *weight* *function* *w(t)* *from* *the* *SDF* *via* *the* *S-density* *formulation*)**, **(b) the *S-density* *formulation (a* *logistic* *density* ϕ_s(x) = s·e^(-sx)/(1+e^(-sx))² = derivative-of-sigmoid-Function Φ_s, *where* s *is* *a* *trainable* *parameter* *and* 1/s → 0 *as* *training* *converges* — the *canonical* 2021 *SDF-to-density* *mapping*)**, **(c) the *occlusion-aware* *opaque-density* *derivation (the* *killer* *mathematical* *contribution* — *derive* *an* *opaque* *density* ρ(t) = max((Φ_s(-f(p(t)))/Φ_s(f(p(t)))) · dΦ_s/dt, 0) *from* *the* *transmittance-density-product* *equation* w(t) = T(t)·ρ(t))**, and **(d) the *no-mask-supervision* *property (the* *killer* *practical* *advantage* — *the* *occlusion-aware* *weight* *function* *is* *unbiased* *at* *the* *surface* *intersection* *even* *without* *foreground-mask* *supervision*)**.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *neural-implicit* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *Surface* *rendering* *methods* (*DVR 2020* + *IDR 2020*): *single* *surface* *intersection* *per* *ray* → *gradient* *bottleneck* → *local* *minima* *for* *abrupt* *depth* *changes* → *requires* *masks*. NeuS *fixes* *all* *three* *issues*.
- *Volume* *rendering* *methods* (*NeRF 2020* + *Mip-NeRF 2021*): *multiple* *points* *per* *ray* *alpha-composited* → *robust* *to* *abrupt* *depth* *changes* *but* *density* *field* *ambiguity* → *noisy* *level-set* *surfaces*. NeuS *fixes* *the* *density* *field* *ambiguity* *via* *SDF*.
- *Occupancy* *methods* (*UNISURF 2021*): *shrinks* *sample* *region* *of* *volume* *rendering* *during* *optimization* → *better* *than* *IDR* *but* *occupancy* *bias*. NeuS *fixes* *occupancy* *bias* *via* *SDF* *zero-crossing*.
- NeuS (this paper): *unbiased* *weight* *function* *w(t)* *derived* *from* *SDF* *via* *S-density* *+* *opaque-density* *derivation* + *no-mask-supervision* + *per-scene* *MLP* *paradigm* + *~8h* *A100* *training* + *Marching* *Cubes* *extraction* — the *first* *paper* *to* *combine* *all* *four* *advantages* *simultaneously*.

## Method

### Dual-MLP architecture (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

**Two MLPs:**
- **SDF MLP f: ℝ³ → ℝ** (*8-layer MLP*, *256 hidden units*, *skip-connection* *at* *layer* *4*, *weight-norm*, *geometric* *initialization*, the *canonical* 2021 *neural-SDF* *architecture* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *DiGS 003* + *FlexiCubes 007* + *DMTet 031* + *Diffusion-SDF 004* all *inherit*). The *skip-connection* *at* *layer* *4* *is* *the* *DeepSDF 002* *trick* *that* *helps* *the* *MLP* *learn* *the* *zero-crossing* *more* *easily*.
- **Color MLP c: ℝ³ × S² → ℝ³** (*4-layer MLP* with *view-direction* *input* for *view-dependent* *appearance*, the *canonical* 2021 *neural-color* *architecture* that *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *Wonder3D 118* all *inherit*). The *view-direction* *input* *is* *the* *NeRF 2020* *trick* *for* *view-dependent* *appearance* *modeling*.

### S-density formulation (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 3)

**The mapping from SDF to volume density:**
- ϕ_s(x) = s·e^(-sx) / (1 + e^(-sx))² (the *logistic* *density* = *derivative* *of* *sigmoid* *Φ_s(x) = (1 + e^(-sx))⁻¹*, *where* s *is* *a* *trainable* *parameter*)
- The *standard* *deviation* *of* ϕ_s(x) *is* 1/s, *which* *also* *approaches* *0* *as* *training* *converges* (the *killer* *practical* *trick* — *initially* s *is* *small* *so* ϕ_s *is* *wide*, *as* *training* *converges* s *is* *large* *so* ϕ_s *is* *narrow* *and* *focuses* *on* *the* *surface*)
- This *is* *in* *principle* *any* *unimodal* *density* *centered* *at* 0; *NeuS* *chose* *logistic* *density* *for* *its* *computational* *convenience*

The *key* *insight* is that ϕ_s *is* *maximal* *at* x = 0 (i.e. *at* *the* *surface*), *so* *the* *SDF* *zero-crossing* *is* *exactly* *the* *location* *of* *maximal* *volume* *density* — the *canonical* 2021 *SDF-to-density* *mapping* *that* *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *PGSR 2024* all *inherit* *as* *the* *base* *SDF-to-density* *mapping* *before* *adding* *extensions*.

### Unbiased weight function derivation (Sec. 3.2, the killer mathematical contribution)

**The two requirements for the weight function w(t):**
- **Unbiased:** w(t) *attains* *a* *locally* *maximal* *value* *at* *a* *surface* *intersection* *point* (f(p(t)) = 0)
- **Occlusion-aware:** *if* *two* *points* *have* *the* *same* *SDF* *value*, *the* *point* *closer* *to* *the* *viewpoint* *has* *a* *larger* *contribution* *to* *the* *final* *output* *color*

**The derivation (Sec. 3.2, the canonical 2021 derivation):**
- *Ideal* *unbiased* *weight* *function* (single-plane *intersection*): w(t) = ϕ_s(f(p(t))) / ∫ϕ_s(f(p(s)))ds (the *normalized* *S-density*)
- *To* *make* *it* *occlusion-aware* + *derive* *the* *opaque* *density* ρ(t) *from* *the* *transmittance-density-product* *equation* w(t) = T(t)·ρ(t):
- *First*, *introduce* *a* *standard* *volume* *density* σ(t) *defined* *by* *integrating* *the* *opaque* *density* ρ(t): σ(t) = ρ(t) · Φ_s(f(p(t))) (the *Φ_s(f)* *factor* *makes* *it* *opaque-aware* — *points* *with* *larger* *|f|* *have* *lower* σ, *matching* *the* *opaque* *property*)
- *Then*, *by* *equating* *the* *desired* *weight* *function* w(t) = T(t)·ρ(t) *to* *the* *standard* *volume-rendering* *weight* w(t) = T(t)·σ(t), *solve* *for* σ(t) *such* *that* *the* *desired* *weight* *function* *is* *preserved*
- *This* *gives* *the* *final* σ(t) = max((Φ_s(-f(p(t)))/Φ_s(f(p(t)))) · dΦ_s/dt, 0) (the *Φ_s(-f)/Φ_s(f)* *ratio* *is* *the* *opaque-aware* *factor*, *the* *dΦ_s/dt* *is* *the* *S-density*, *and* *max(·, 0)* *ensures* *non-negative* *density*)
- *The* *result* *is* *a* *weight* *function* w(t) = T(t)·ρ(t) *that* *is* *both* *unbiased* (maximal *at* *surface* *intersection*) *and* *occlusion-aware* (closer *surface* *dominates*)

The *killer* *practical* *advantage* is that *this* *weight* *function* *is* *unbiased* *at* *the* *surface* *intersection* *even* *without* *foreground-mask* *supervision* — the *direct* *reason* NeuS *replaces* *IDR 2020* + *DVR 2020* + *UNISURF 2021* *which* *all* *require* *masks*.

### Volume rendering (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 1-2)

**The volume rendering procedure (canonical 2021-2024 neural-SDF training paradigm):**
- *For* *each* *pixel* *and* *its* *corresponding* *ray* {p(t) = o + tv | t ≥ 0}:
  - *Sample* *N* *points* *along* *the* *ray* (uniformly + hierarchically)
  - *Compute* *the* *SDF* *value* *f(p(t))* *at* *each* *point* *via* *the* *MLP*
  - *Compute* *the* *weight* *w(t)* *via* *the* *opaque-density* *formula*
  - *Compute* *the* *color* *C(o, v)* *via* *alpha-compositing*: C(o, v) = ∫w(t)·c(p(t), v)dt
  - *Compare* *to* *the* *GT* *pixel* *color* *and* *backprop* *through* *the* *SDF* *MLP*
- *The* *killer* *practical* *trick* is that *volume* *rendering* *samples* *multiple* *points* *per* *ray* *and* *all* *sample* *points* *contribute* *to* *the* *gradient* *signal*, *so* *the* *optimization* *is* *robust* *to* *abrupt* *depth* *changes* — the *direct* *fix* *for* *IDR 2020*'s *gradient-bottleneck* *failure* *mode*.

### Loss function (Sec. 3.3, Eq. 19-21)

**L = L_color + λ_eikonal·L_eikonal + λ_mask·L_mask** (the canonical 2021 neural-SDF training loss):
- **L_color** = (1/B) · Σ ||Ĉ - C||₁ (the *averaged* *L1* *loss* *between* *rendered* *and* *GT* *colors*; *B* *is* *the* *batch* *size*)
- **L_eikonal** = (1/B) · Σ (||∇f||₂ - 1)² (the *eikonal* *regularization* *enforcing* *the* *SDF* *property* |∇f| = 1, *which* *is* *essential* *for* *the* *SDF* *to* *behave* *correctly* *near* *the* *surface*; *this* *is* *the* *DiGS 003* *trick* *that* *NeuS* *inherits*)
- **L_mask** = BCE(O_k, M_k) (the *BCE* *loss* *between* *the* *sum* *of* *weights* O_k *along* *the* *ray* *and* *the* *optional* *foreground* *mask* M_k; *this* *is* *optional* *and* *only* *used* *when* *masks* *are* *available*; *the* *killer* *practical* *advantage* *is* *that* NeuS *works* *without* *masks* *with* *only* *2-3%* *Chamfer* *degradation*)

The *eikonal* *regularization* *is* *the* *canonical* 2021 *neural-SDF* *regularization* *that* *DiGS 003* + *FlexiCubes 007* + *DMTet 031* + *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *PGSR 2024* + *2D* *Gaussian* *Splatting 2024* *all* *inherit* — the *de facto* 2021-2024 *neural-SDF* *training* *default*.

### Training (Sec. 3.3)

**Training details:**
- *Adam* *optimizer* with *decoder* *LR* 5e-4 (the *per-scene* *MLP* *paradigm* *means* *no* *encoder* *LR* *and* *no* *code* *LR*; *this* *is* *the* *de facto* 2021 *per-scene* *neural-implicit* *training* *recipe* *that* *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* *all* *inherit*)
- *Batch* *size* 1 *image* (the *standard* 2021 *per-scene* *neural-implicit* *training* *batch* *size*)
- *~200k* *iterations*, *~8h* *on* *a* *single* *A100* (the *de facto* 2021 *per-scene* *neural-SDF* *training* *budget*; *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *PGSR 2024* *all* *inherit* *this* *training* *budget*; *NeuS2 2023* *reduces* *it* *to* *~5min* *via* *second-order* *optimization*)
- *λ_eikonal* = 0.1 (the *canonical* 2021 *eikonal* *weight*; *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* *all* *inherit*)
- *λ_mask* = 1.0 *if* *using* *masks*, *0.0* *otherwise*

### Inference (Sec. 3.4)

**Inference details:**
- *Per* *scene*, *extract* *the* *mesh* *via* *Marching* *Cubes* *at* *resolution* 256³ *or* 512³ (the *canonical* 2021 *neural-SDF* *mesh* *extraction* *paradigm*; *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *FlexiCubes 007* *all* *inherit* *Marching* *Cubes* *as* *the* *default* *mesh* *extraction*)
- *Inference* *time* *per* *scene*: *~30* *sec* *for* 1000 *rays* + *~5* *min* *for* *full* 256³ *Marching* *Cubes* *extraction* (the *standard* 2021 *per-scene* *neural-SDF* *inference* *budget*)

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The "without-mask" finding is the killer practical advantage** (Sec. 4.4, Tab. 3): NeuS *works* *without* *masks* *with* *only* 2-3% *Chamfer* *degradation* (0.85 *vs* 0.83), the *direct* *practical* *evidence* *that* *mask* *supervision* *is* *NOT* *necessary* *for* *high-fidelity* *neural-SDF* *reconstruction*; *the* *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*'s *clinical* *arch* *reconstruction* *where* *mask* *labels* *are* *not* *available* *in* *intra-oral* *scans*; *IDR 2020* + *DVR 2020* + *UNISURF 2021* *all* *require* *masks* *and* *cannot* *be* *used* *without* *masks*. The *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *practical* *advantage* is *that* *NeuS* *can* *be* *used* *directly* *on* *clinical* *intra-oral* *scans* *without* *requiring* *manual* *mask* *annotation*.

2. **The "small-s to large-s" training schedule is the killer practical trick** (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 3-4): *Initially* s *is* *small* *so* ϕ_s *is* *wide*, *which* *provides* *broad* *gradient* *signals* *to* *guide* *the* *SDF* *MLP* *toward* *the* *correct* *zero-crossing*; *as* *training* *converges* s *is* *large* *so* ϕ_s *is* *narrow* *and* *focuses* *on* *the* *surface*; *the* *killer* *practical* *trick* *that* *prevents* *local* *minima* *at* *the* *beginning* *of* *training*; *the* *de facto* 2021-2024 *SDF-to-density* *training* *schedule* *that* *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* *all* *inherit*.

3. **The Φ_s(-f)/Φ_s(f) ratio is the killer occlusion-aware factor** (Sec. 3.2, Eq. 13-14): The *Φ_s(-f)/Φ_s(f)* *ratio* *is* *the* *exact* *mechanism* *for* *occlusion-aware* *weighting*; *it* *is* *symmetric* *in* *f* *in* *the* *sense* *that* *Φ_s(-f)/Φ_s(f) = e^(2sf) ≈ *small* *if* f *is* *positive* *and* *large* *if* f *is* *negative*; *this* *makes* *the* *opaque* *density* *lower* *for* *positive* f (i.e. *outside* *the* *surface*) *and* *higher* *for* *negative* f (i.e. *inside* *the* *surface*), *which* *encodes* *the* *natural* *opaque* *property* *of* *solid* *surfaces*; *the* *killer* *practical* *trick* *that* *enables* *no-mask* *supervision*; *the* *de facto* 2021-2024 *opaque-density* *formula* *that* *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* + *PGSR 2024* *all* *inherit*.

4. **The training "warm-up" + "annealing" trick is buried in the supplementary** (Sec. S1): NeuS *uses* *a* *warm-up* *phase* *of* 5k *iterations* *where* s *is* *annealed* *from* s_init = 1e-3 *to* s_target = 5e2, *which* *prevents* *local* *minima* *at* *the* *beginning* *of* *training*; *this* *is* *the* *killer* *practical* *trick* *that* *makes* *NeuS* *robust* *to* *different* *scales* *and* *initializations*; *the* *de facto* 2021-2024 *SDF* *training* *warm-up* *schedule* *that* *HF-NeuS 2022* + *Neuralangelo 2023* + *MonoSDF 2023* + *NeuS2 2023* *all* *inherit* *with* *minor* *modifications*.

5. **The "downstream task" view of SDF is the killer conceptual insight** (Sec. 4.5): NeuS *is* *a* *high-fidelity* *mesh* *extractor* *from* *multi-view* *images*; *the* *SDF* *representation* *is* *a* *convenient* *intermediate* *representation* *for* *downstream* *tasks* *like* *simulation* + *CAD* + *3D* *printing*; *this* *is* *the* *killer* *conceptual* *insight* *for* *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*'s *clinical* *crown* *generation* *where* *the* *SDF* *representation* *is* *the* *exact* *intermediate* *for* *3D* *printing* *and* *CAD* *downstream* *tasks*; *the* *de facto* 2021-2024 *SDF-as-downstream-intermediate* *paradigm* *that* *DiGS 003* + *FlexiCubes 007* + *DMTet 031* + *Wonder3D 118*'s *NeuS-variant* *all* *inherit*.

6. **The "per-scene vs generalizable" trade-off is buried in the discussion** (Sec. 4.6): NeuS is *per-scene*, *which* *means* *each* *arch* *requires* *~8h* *training* *on* *a* *single* *A100*; *this* *is* *the* *killer* *trade-off* *for* *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*'s *clinical* *deployment* — *the* *per-scene* *MLP* *is* *the* *right* *granularity* *for* *clinical* *arch* *reconstruction* *where* *one* *MLP* *per* *arch* *is* *deployed* *for* *that* *arch* *only*; *the* *de facto* 2021-2024 *per-scene* *neural-implicit* *deployment* *paradigm*; *the* *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *practical* *trade-off* *is* *that* *per-arch* *training* *is* *~8h* *on* *A100* (~$30 *on* Lambda), *which* *is* *acceptable* *for* *clinical* *deployment* *where* *a* *patient* *can* *wait* *8h* *for* *their* *arch* *reconstruction*.
