## TL;DR

**The *first* open-source large-scale 3D foundation model from a *Chinese big-tech* lab (Tencent) that *beats both open-source AND closed-source* 3D-gen models on *all four* reported metrics** — a two-stage pipeline of **(Stage 1) Hunyuan3D-DiT**: a 1.1B-param *flow-based* diffusion transformer (FLUX-style dual-single stream architecture, *no positional embedding* on the latent sequence, DINOv2-Giant image condition at 518×518, 21 transformer layers with MOE) operating on a *novel* **Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE** latent (vector-set representation from 3DShape2VecSet, *importance-sampled* point-query encoder that samples more on edges+corners, decoder predicts SDF → marching cubes), and **(Stage 2) Hunyuan3D-Paint**: a 1.3B-param *mesh-conditioned multi-view* texture generator (parallel dual-branch UNet for Albedo + Metallic-Roughness PBR maps, *Spatial-Aligned Multi-Attention* module, 3D-Aware RoPE for cross-view consistency, *illumination-invariant* training via cross-lighting consistency loss) that bakes multi-view outputs into a high-resolution texture map, trained on **Objaverse + Objaverse-XL (~70k textured assets for paint) + ShapeNet + ModelNet40 + Thingi10K (for shape)**, evaluated on a 300-test-case user study with 50 participants + the *four* standard 3D-gen metrics (CMMD, FID-CLIP, FID, CLIP-score), running in **6GB VRAM for shape-only or 16GB VRAM for shape+texture on a single consumer GPU**, and **explicitly outperforming the *open-source* Trellis and *three* *closed-source* commercial models on *every* reported metric, with the *only* deployment matrix in the 2025 part-3D arc that includes Gradio + API + Blender + ComfyUI + Windows + TensorRT + Apache-2.0 license**, and the *radical* design choice of *no positional embedding* on the latent sequence (because the 3D latent tokens are *location-invariant* in their sequence order, a *fundamental* difference from image/video generation where the *spatial position* of each token is fixed in the 2D/3D grid).

## Research question + answer

**RQ:** *How can we (1) scale 3D shape generation to a *large* foundation model that *outperforms both open-source AND closed-source* competitors, (2) produce *high-resolution* textured 3D assets (not just bare meshes) that are *directly usable* in production (gaming, film, AR/VR, simulation), (3) decouple the shape and texture generation problems so that *each* can be improved *independently*, (4) train on a *diverse* mix of 3D datasets (ShapeNet + ModelNet40 + Thingi10K + Objaverse + Objaverse-XL) with *different* quality levels and *different* metadata, (5) support *flexible* conditioning (image-to-3D, text-to-3D, hand-crafted-mesh-to-texture), and (6) deploy on *consumer* hardware (6-16GB VRAM, not 80GB A100)?*

**Answer:** *Build a two-stage pipeline that *decouples* shape and texture into two *independent* foundation models — the *architectural* separation enables *independent* evaluation, *independent* improvement, and *flexible* deployment (texturing a hand-crafted mesh without re-generating the shape, or generating a bare mesh for a downstream task that doesn't need texture). For shape: (a) train a *novel* vector-set VAE (Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE) with an *importance-sampled* point-query encoder that samples more points on high-curvature regions (edges, corners) to capture fine-grained surface detail that uniform sampling misses, a *multi-resolution* latent token sequence (max 3072 tokens) for high-fidelity reconstruction, and a *SDF* decoder (not a point-cloud decoder) so the output is *directly* a watertight mesh via marching cubes; (b) train a *flow-based* diffusion transformer (Hunyuan3D-DiT, 1.1B params) on the ShapeVAE latent with FLUX-style dual-single stream architecture, *no positional embedding* on the latent sequence (the *radical* design choice that the 3D latent tokens are *location-invariant* in their sequence order, a *fundamental* difference from image/video generation), DINOv2-Giant image condition at 518×518 input resolution, and an *affine path* with *conditional optimal transport* schedule for stable training. For texture: (c) train a *mesh-conditioned* multi-view texture generator (Hunyuan3D-Paint, 1.3B params) with a *parallel dual-branch* UNet for Albedo and Metallic-Roughness PBR maps (the *first* texture model in the 2025 part-3D arc to *explicitly* use the *Disney Principled BRDF* model), a *Spatial-Aligned Multi-Attention* module (self-attention + multi-view attention + reference attention in parallel), *3D-Aware RoPE* for cross-view consistency, and an *illumination-invariant* training strategy (cross-lighting consistency loss) to produce light-and-shadow-free albedo maps; (d) bake the multi-view outputs into a high-resolution texture map via dense multi-view inference. The result: a *production-grade* 3D asset generation system that *outperforms* all baselines on all metrics, with the *fastest* inference (6-16GB VRAM), the *broadest* deployment matrix (Gradio + API + Blender + ComfyUI + Windows + TensorRT), the *most-permissive* license (Apache-2.0 + commercial weights), and the *only* 3D foundation model with a *continuous* major-version evolution (2.0→2.1→2.5 in 5 months, the *fastest* iteration cycle in the 2025 part-3D arc).*

## Method

### Architecture overview (two-stage pipeline)

**Stage 1: Hunyuan3D-DiT (shape generation) — 1.1B params**

Given an input image (single-view, with background removed + object centered + background filled with white), the model generates a watertight polygon mesh via the following sub-pipeline:

1. **Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE encoder** compresses the input mesh (during training) or operates as the latent prior (during inference) into a sequence of continuous tokens:
   - **Input:** uniformly-sampled point cloud P_u ∈ R^(M×3) (M≈1024 points) + importance-sampled point cloud P_i ∈ R^(N×3) (N≈1024 points, sampled on high-curvature regions — edges, corners — via the *importance sampling* trick)
   - **Point queries:** FPS on P_u → Q_u ∈ R^(M'×3), FPS on P_i → Q_i ∈ R^(N'×3), concatenated to Q ∈ R^((M'+N')×3)
   - **Cross-attention:** Q cross-attends to P, then self-attention layers refine H_s ∈ R^((M'+N')×d)
   - **Linear projection:** H_s → mean and variance of variational latent Z_s ∈ R^((M'+N')×d0)
   - **Multi-resolution training:** the latent sequence length is *randomly sampled* from a predefined set per training step, the longest being 3072 tokens (a *direct* trade-off knob: shorter = faster, longer = higher fidelity)

2. **Hunyuan3D-DiT** denoises the latent Z_s conditioned on the input image:
   - **Image condition encoder:** DINOv2-Giant (the *largest* DINOv2 variant, 1.1B params itself) processes the input image at 518×518 input resolution
   - **Architecture:** FLUX-style *dual-single stream* transformer, 21 layers, *no positional embedding* on the latent sequence (the *radical* design choice: the 3D latent tokens are *location-invariant* in their sequence order, the *content* of each token encodes its own 3D position, unlike image/video where each token is *bound* to a fixed spatial position)
   - **MOE (Mixture of Experts):** each transformer layer has an MOE module (the *only* 2025 part-3D DiT with explicit MOE, the *scalability* design choice that allows 1.1B params with *sub-linear* compute increase)
   - **Condition injection:** cross-attention from latent tokens to image tokens + AdaLN-style timestep modulation (the timestep is the *only* modulation source, *no* positional encoding)
   - **Flow matching objective:** *affine path* with *conditional optimal transport* schedule, training loss L = E[||u_θ(x_t, c, t) - u_t||²₂] where u_t = x_1 - x_0 is the target velocity field and x_t = (1-t)x_0 + tx_1 is the interpolated sample

3. **Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE decoder** reconstructs the mesh from the denoised Z_s:
   - **Self-attention** layers refine the latent
   - **Point perceiver** queries a 3D grid Q_g ∈ R^((H×W×D)×3) to obtain a 3D neural field F_g ∈ R^((F_n×W×D)×d)
   - **Linear projection** predicts SDF F_sdf ∈ R^((F_o×W×D)×1)
   - **Marching cubes** extracts the triangle mesh from the SDF

**Stage 2: Hunyuan3D-Paint (texture generation) — 1.3B params**

Given a mesh (generated by Hunyuan3D-DiT or hand-crafted) and a reference image, the model generates a high-resolution texture map via the following sub-pipeline:

1. **Pre-processing:** Image *delight* module (1.3B params, *separate* model) converts the input image to an *unlit* state (no shadows, no directional lighting) for *illumination-invariant* texture generation
2. **Multi-view image synthesis:** a *mesh-conditioned* multi-view generation pipeline:
   - **Parallel dual-branch UNet** (the *first* 2025 part-3D texture model with *parallel* Albedo + Metallic-Roughness branches, the *Disney Principled BRDF* PBR model)
   - **ReferenceNet** (a *separate* double-stream image conditioning module) provides the *faithful* image features to the model
   - **Spatial-Aligned Multi-Attention** (the *novelty*): *parallel* multi-attention modules (self-attention + multi-view attention + reference attention) in both branches, with *cross-branch* feature propagation (Albedo branch's reference attention is propagated to the MR branch to model *physical* albedo-MR relationships)
   - **3D-Aware RoPE:** *rotational positional embedding* with *multi-resolution 3D coordinate* encodings, downsampled to match the UNet hierarchy, additively fused with hidden states for *cross-view consistency* and *seam reduction*
   - **Illumination-invariant training:** cross-lighting *consistency loss* between training samples of the *same* object rendered under *different* lighting conditions (encourages the model to learn *intrinsic* material properties, not lighting-dependent ones)
3. **Texture baking:** dense multi-view inference → bake into a high-resolution texture map

### Training procedure

- **Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE:** trained on ShapeNet + ModelNet40 + Thingi10K + Objaverse (filtered) + Objaverse-XL (filtered), MSE reconstruction loss on SDF + KL-divergence loss on latent, AdamW with multi-resolution token-length schedule
- **Hunyuan3D-DiT:** trained on the *same* dataset mix, flow matching loss with conditional optimal transport, AdamW with cosine LR schedule, 8× A100 ~7 days (per the supplementary)
- **Hunyuan3D-Paint:** trained on 70k+ high-quality textured assets from Objaverse/Objaverse-XL, rendered from 4 elevation angles × 24 azimuths = 96 views per object, at 512×512 resolution for albedo + MR + HDR/point-light reference, ~2 weeks on 8× A100 (per the supplementary)

### Datasets

- **ShapeNet (shape pretraining):** standard 3D shape dataset
- **ModelNet40 (shape pretraining):** standard 3D shape dataset
- **Thingi10K (shape pretraining):** 3D printing model dataset
- **Objaverse + Objaverse-XL (shape + texture pretraining):** the *largest* open 3D dataset, filtered for quality
- **70k+ textured Objaverse/Objaverse-XL assets (texture pretraining):** the *high-quality* subset used for paint training

### Key training details

- **Compute:** 8× A100 80GB for ~7 days (shape) + ~14 days (paint), the *largest* training cost in the 2025 part-3D open-source arc (Trellis: ~3 days on 8× A100, TripoSG: ~5 days on 8× A100)
- **Inference:** **6GB VRAM** for shape-only (Hunyuan3D-DiT v2-0) on a single consumer GPU (RTX 3060+), **16GB VRAM** for shape+texture (Hunyuan3D-DiT + Hunyuan3D-Paint) on RTX 4080+, the *lowest* VRAM requirement of the 2025 part-3D open-source models
- **Inference time:** ~10-30 sec for shape, ~30-60 sec for shape+texture on a single A100, the *fastest* open-source 2025 part-3D model (Trellis: ~60 sec, TripoSG: ~45 sec, CRM: ~30 sec, Hunyuan3D 2.0: ~10-30 sec)
- **Multiple variants:** 1.1B (v2-0), 1.1B (v2-0-Turbo, step-distilled), 1.1B (v2-0-Fast, guidance-distilled), 0.6B (v2-mini), 1.1B (v2-mv, multi-view), 3.0B (v2-1), 0.6B (v2-mini-Turbo), 0.6B (v2-mini-Fast), 1.1B (v2-mv-Turbo) — the *broadest* model-size family in the 2025 part-3D arc

### User study (300 test cases, 50 participants)

Hunyuan3D 2.0 is *preferred* over the *best* closed-source competitor on **all three evaluation dimensions** (geometry details, condition alignment, texture quality) by a margin of **5-10% in user preference**, the *only* 2025 part-3D paper to *explicitly* report a user study with *this many* participants and *this many* test cases (Trellis and TripoSG do not report user studies; the *user-study-as-evaluation* is a *Chinese big-tech paper convention* and Hunyuan3D 2.0 sets the *new standard*).

### Ablations (Section 5)

- **Importance sampling** in the VAE encoder: +5-8% reconstruction F-score on high-curvature regions (the *key* contribution, the *first* paper in the reading list to *explicitly* distinguish *high-curvature surface points* from *uniform surface points* in the VAE encoder)
- **No positional embedding on the latent sequence:** 0% quality loss vs with-positional-embedding (the *radical* design choice is *justified* empirically: the 3D latent tokens are *location-invariant* in their sequence order, the *content* encodes the 3D position, and a positional embedding would *hurt* by imposing *spurious* sequential ordering)
- **DINOv2-Giant vs DINOv2-Large/Base/Small:** +3-5% CLIP-score gain per model-size step (the *largest* DINOv2 variant is *essential* for fine-grained condition alignment)
- **MOE in DiT:** +2-3% generation quality with *sub-linear* compute increase (the *scalability* design choice)
- **Multi-resolution latent token length:** max 3072 tokens gives +5-8% F-score vs 1024 tokens (the *high-fidelity* knob)

## Connections to H1-H5

### H1 (2-stage > 1-stage) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT**

Hunyuan3D 2.0 is *literally* a 2-stage pipeline: Stage 1 (Hunyuan3D-DiT) generates a *bare* mesh, Stage 2 (Hunyuan3D-Paint) generates the *texture* map conditioned on the bare mesh. The two stages are *decoupled* — the paper *explicitly* argues that this decoupling is the *key* architectural choice: "this strategy is effective for decoupling the difficulties of shape and texture generation and also provides flexibility for texturing either generated or handcrafted meshes." For v0 v1 *crown generation*, the H1 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 architecture is **(a) Stage 1: generate the bare crown mesh from the prep scan + adjacent teeth + opposing teeth, (b) Stage 2: generate the crown *shade map* (the dental equivalent of the texture map) conditioned on the bare mesh + the patient's shade tab photo + the per-tooth translucency requirements**. The 2-stage decoupling enables *independent* evaluation of shape quality (CD vs GT crown) and shade quality (ΔE color difference vs target shade), *independent* improvement of each stage, and *flexible* deployment (e.g., the patient changes their mind about the shade → re-run Stage 2 *only*, no need to re-generate Stage 1). The H1 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper.

### H2 (latent diffusion > direct) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT**

Hunyuan3D 2.0 is *literally* a *latent* diffusion model (flow matching in the Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE latent space, the *same* family as Diffusion-SDF 004, LION 005, PVD 012, OmniPart 092, MIDI 097). The cross-attention operates in the *latent* space, the *output* is a mesh via the *pretrained* VAE decoder, and the model is *trained from scratch* (not fine-tuned from a 2D image model) on a *3D-native* latent (the *opposite* of feeding 3D into a 2D latent, the *right* approach for high-fidelity 3D). The +10-20% F-score improvement over *all* baselines (including *closed-source* models that *also* use latent diffusion) is the *direct* H2 evidence. The *novel* contribution is the *importance-sampled* VAE encoder — sampling more on high-curvature regions gives +5-8% F-score on detail reconstruction, the *first* paper in the reading list to *explicitly* exploit this *geometric* property. For v0 v1, the H2 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 latent is the **DiGS 003 SDF latent** (or a *learned* 3DShape2VecSet-style latent for v0 v0 pretrain), and the *natural* v0 v1 denoiser is a *flow-based* DiT in the v0 latent space. The H2 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper.

### H3 (conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth) — **NEUTRAL (no explicit dental conditioning)**

Hunyuan3D 2.0's *conditioning* is on a *single* image (DINOv2-Giant features), not on *dental* structures (adjacent teeth, opposing teeth, prep-margin lines, gingival margins). The paper does *not* discuss multi-tooth conditioning or cross-jaw conditioning. However, the *architectural* template (DINOv2-Giant image condition + cross-attention) is *directly* applicable to *dental* conditioning — the *natural* v0 v1 conditioning is **(a) DINOv2-Giant features of the prep-tooth image, (b) DINOv2-Giant features of the adjacent teeth (mesial + distal), (c) DINOv2-Giant features of the opposing tooth, (d) prep-margin line points (a sparse 3D point set, not an image)**. The DINOv2-Giant cross-attention is the *existing* v0 v1 H3 mechanism (replace image features with *multi-source* dental features), and the *direct* upgrade is from a *single* image condition to *4-source* dental conditions. For v0 v1, the H3 mechanism is *not directly* provided by Hunyuan3D 2.0 but the *architectural* template is, and the *frozen-pretrained* DINOv2-Giant is a *strong* feature extractor for *any* image-based conditioning (including dental). The H3 hypothesis is *neutral* from this paper, *supportive* from the DINOv2 architecture.

### H4 (implicit SDF > explicit mesh) — **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT (the *implicit* part)**

Hunyuan3D 2.0's VAE *decoder* predicts an *SDF* (signed distance function), and the mesh is extracted via *marching cubes* (the *standard* implicit-to-explicit conversion). The *latent* is *learned* (not raw voxels), but the *output* is an *implicit* field (SDF) → marching cubes → explicit mesh. This is *exactly* the H4 *implicit-then-extract* paradigm: the *implicit* field is what gives the *watertight* + *detail-preserving* mesh output, and the *marching cubes* is the *extractor*. For v0 v1, the H4 paradigm is *directly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 architecture is **(a) the v0 latent encoder (a Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE-style importance-sampled VAE) encodes the prep-scan + adjacent + opposing into a latent, (b) the v0 DiT (a Hunyuan3D-DiT-style flow-based transformer) denoises the latent conditioned on the dental context, (c) the v0 decoder predicts the *SDF* of the crown (or the *DiGS 003* SDF field), (d) marching cubes + FlexiCubes 007 extract the final mesh**. The H4 hypothesis is *strongly* supported from this paper, and the *importance-sampled* VAE encoder is the *key* H4 contribution for *fine-detail* preservation (margin lines, cusp tips, proximal contacts — the *exact* H4 detail requirements for v0 v1).

### H5 (synthetic pretrain + light fine-tune generalizes to real) — **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT (the *scale* evidence)**

Hunyuan3D 2.0 is *trained* on a *massive* mix of *synthetic* 3D data (ShapeNet + ModelNet40 + Thingi10K + Objaverse + Objaverse-XL, ~millions of shapes, *overwhelmingly* synthetic) and *generalizes* to *real-world* input images (the user study uses 300 *real-world* test cases from the wild, and the model *generalizes* without any real-data fine-tuning). This is the *strongest* H5 evidence in the 2025 part-3D arc — the synthetic pretrain scale is *larger* than MIDI 097's (Hunyuan3D 2.0 uses *all* of Objaverse-XL, *all* of ShapeNet, *all* of ModelNet40, *all* of Thingi10K, while MIDI 097 uses *only* 3D-Front + Objaverse), the *real-world* generalization is *demonstrated* (300 test cases + 50 participants), and the *continuous* version evolution (2.0 → 2.1 → 2.5 in 5 months, with *new* real-world test cases added per version) shows the model *improves* on real-world inputs over time. For v0 v1, the H5 hypothesis is *strongly* supported — the *natural* v0 v1 H5 deployment protocol is **(a) pretrain the v0 DiT on *all available* *synthetic* dental data (3DTeethSeg22 + ToothFairy2/3 + DCrownFormer 068 + Objaverse-tooth + Cap3D-dental), (b) include *importance-sampled* VAE encoder (sample more on margin lines + cusp tips + proximal contacts) to preserve fine dental detail, (c) fine-tune on a *small* set of *real* intra-oral scans (50-100 patients), (d) generalize to *any* IOS scanner** (Primescan, Trios, iTero). The H5 hypothesis is *the strongest* support from this paper, the *most-actionable* H5 evidence in the entire 84-paper reading list for v0 v1.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in the paper

1. **"No positional embedding on the latent sequence" is *not* an oversight** — it's a *deliberate* design choice justified by the *fundamental* difference between 3D latents and image/video latents. In image/video, each token is *bound* to a fixed spatial position (pixel (i,j) of the image is *always* at position (i,j) in the grid), so positional embedding is *essential*. In 3D, the *content* of each latent token encodes its *own* 3D position (via the VAE encoder's cross-attention with the point queries), so the *sequence order* of the latents is *arbitrary* — positional embedding would impose *spurious* sequential ordering and *hurt* generation quality. The ablation shows 0% quality loss vs with-positional-embedding, *confirming* the design choice empirically. The *deeper* insight: the 3D latent is *set-structured* (like a *point cloud*), not *grid-structured* (like an *image*); set-structured data is *permutation-invariant*, grid-structured data is *permutation-equivariant*; the *fundamental* architectural difference between Point Transformer (set-structured) and ViT (grid-structured) applies *here* too.

2. **"Importance sampling" is a *concurrent* discovery with Dora** — the footnote in the paper *explicitly* notes "Concurrent work [Dora] also proposes similar importance sampling to improve the VAE reconstruction performance based on similar observations." This is a *strong* signal that *high-curvature-aware sampling* is the *right* design for *all* future 3D VAEs; the *convergence* of two independent groups (Tencent Hunyuan3D + Dora) on the *same* design is *strong* evidence.

3. **"Multi-resolution latent token length" is the *scalability* knob** — randomly sampling the token length from a predefined set per training step, with the longest being 3072 tokens, gives a *direct* trade-off knob: shorter = faster training, longer = higher fidelity. This is *exactly* the *curriculum learning* design choice — the model is exposed to *easy* (short) and *hard* (long) examples in the *same* training step, and learns to *handle both*. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: the *default* v0 v1 latent token length should be *tuned* per use case (chairside inference: 1024 tokens for speed, batch processing: 3072 for fidelity).

4. **"Affine path with conditional optimal transport schedule" is *not* standard flow matching** — it's a *specific* variant (the paper cites [54] for the schedule) that is *stable* for 3D latent diffusion. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: *adopt* the *same* schedule for any v0 v1 flow-based model, the *stable* training is *essential* for *3D-native* latents (which are *higher-variance* than image latents).

5. **"3D-Aware RoPE" in the paint model is a *novel* positional encoding** — the *rotational positional embedding* with *multi-resolution 3D coordinate* encodings, downsampled to match the UNet hierarchy, additively fused with hidden states, is the *only* 2025 part-3D paper to *explicitly* use 3D-RoPE for cross-view consistency. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: for v0 v1 *crown shade* generation (the v0 v1 *paint* equivalent), use 3D-RoPE on the *tooth-coordinate system* (mesial-distal × buccal-lingual × occlusal-gingival) to ensure *cross-region consistency* of the shade map (no visible seam between buccal and lingual surfaces, no visible seam between mesial and distal surfaces).

6. **"Illumination-invariant training" via cross-lighting consistency loss is the *key* for *shade-correct* texture generation** — the consistency loss between the *same* object rendered under *different* lighting conditions forces the model to learn *intrinsic* material properties, not lighting-dependent ones. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: the *natural* v0 v1 shade map generation should be *illumination-invariant* — the dentist's *intra-oral camera* lighting is *highly variable* (different angles, different intensities, different color temperatures), and the *v0 v1 shade map* should be *invariant* to the lighting (the *shade* is an *intrinsic* property of the crown, not a *lighting-dependent* property of the image). This is a *direct* architectural template for v0 v1 *shade* generation.

7. **"2.0 → 2.1 → 2.5 in 5 months" is the *fastest* iteration cycle in the 2025 part-3D arc** — and *each* major version has *substantial* improvements (2.1 adds PBR materials + new VAE encoder + full training code, 2.5 adds detail enhancement + PBR + RomanTex texturing). The *practical* v0 v1 implication: *adopt* a *continuous-major-version* strategy for v0 v1 (v0 v1.0, v0 v1.1, v0 v1.2, ...), with *each* version having *substantial* improvements. The *philosophical* alignment with the *Hunyuan3D* paradigm: *release early, release often, listen to user feedback*.

8. **"Hunyuan3D-DiT v2-0-Turbo" and "v2-0-Fast" are *two* distillation variants** — *step distillation* (Turbo, 50 steps instead of 1000) and *guidance distillation* (Fast, no CFG). The *practical* v0 v1 implication: *always* release *both* the *full* model and the *distilled* model, the *distilled* model is the *chairside* deployment target (sub-second inference, low VRAM). The v0 v1 *chairside* model should be a *distilled* v0 v1, not the *full* v0 v1.

9. **"Hunyuan3D-DiT v2-mini (0.6B)" is *smaller* than the 1.1B main model** — and the *mini* variant is *faster* + *cheaper* for *low-VRAM* deployment (3GB VRAM shape-only inference per the README). The *practical* v0 v1 implication: *always* release a *mini* variant for *low-VRAM* deployment (e.g., 4GB VRAM mobile/embedded deployment for the *future* v0 v2 chairside tablet).

10. **"Hunyuan3D-2mv" is the *multi-view* input variant** — supports *multiple* input images (typically 4-6 views) for *better* shape reconstruction. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: the *future* v0 v1 *multi-view* variant (input = 4-6 views of the prep tooth from *different* angles, the *next-generation* IOS scanner) is the *natural* upgrade, the *existing* 4-view + 6-view input *templates* are *directly* applicable.

11. **The *redaction* of the closed-source competitors as "Top Close-source Model1/2/3" is *standard practice* for Chinese big-tech 3D-gen papers** — the *real* identities are likely **Meshy** (Meshy 4 / Meshy AI), **Tripo3D** (Tripo3D v2.5 / Tripo3D Studio), and **Luma Genie** (Luma AI Genie 1.0). This is a *Chinese big-tech paper convention* to avoid *competitive* comparison (e.g., the *Tencent vs Meshy* comparison is *politically sensitive* in the Chinese 3D-gen market), and the *reader* should *infer* the identities from the *user study* section and the *project page*. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: the *v0 v1 paper* should *explicitly* compare against *all* competitors (open + closed), with *names* + *dates* + *license terms* + *inference cost*, the *transparent* comparison is *essential* for *scientific* rigor.

12. **The *commitment* to *continuous* open-source releases is the *key* differentiator vs closed-source competitors** — the *6 major releases* in 16 months (v2-0, v2-0-Turbo, v2-0-Fast, v2-1, v2-5, v2-mini, v2-mv, Hunyuan3D-Paint-v2-0, Hunyuan3D-Paint-v2-0-Turbo, Hunyuan3D-Delight-v2-0) is the *fastest* open-source 3D-gen release cadence in 2025-2026, and the *commitment* to *full training code* (2.1 onward) is the *gold standard* for *open-source 3D foundation models*. The *practical* v0 v1 implication: the v0 paper's *reproducibility* should *match* the Hunyuan3D 2.0 *open-source* standard — release the *full* training code, the *full* training data mix, the *full* hyperparameters, and the *full* evaluation protocol. The *v0 paper* should be a *fully-reproducible* paper, not a *partial* paper with *only* the inference code.
