## TL;DR

> **Real3D** (Hanwen Jiang, Qixing Huang, Georgios Pavlakos, **UT Austin**, arXiv:2406.08479 v1 12 Jun 2024, **ICCV 2025** camera-ready 27 Sep 2025) is the *first* *Large Reconstruction Model* that *trains* on *single-view* *in-the-wild* *real* *images* (no 3D / no multi-view ground truth needed) — the *killer* *real-image* *self-training* *paradigm-shift* paper that *combines* *unsupervised* *losses* (*cycle-consistency* *pixel-level* *loss* + *CLIP-based* *semantic-level* *loss* with *hard* *negative* *mining* + *stop-gradient* + *pose-sampling* *curriculum*) with *automatic* *data* *curation* (*DECOLA* *instance* *segmentation* + *Depth* *Anything* *monocular* *depth* + *Sobel* *boundary* *analysis* + *D_inner/D_outer* *occlusion* *voting*) to *leverage* *300K* *uncurated* *in-the-wild* *real* *images* (filtered from *3M+* *initial* *instances*) for *LRM* *training* (the *de facto* *real-image* *LRM* *training* *paradigm* of 2024-2026). The *architecture* is *literally* *TripoSR* 108 (a *fine-tuned* *TripoSR* 108 as the *base* *model*, the *practical* *MIT-licensed* *production-grade* *LRM* *starting* *point* — the *killer* *engineering* *decision* to *start* *from* *the* *MIT-licensed* *TripoSR* 108 instead of *LRM* 107's *not-released* *code*) with *no* *architectural* *changes* — the *only* *innovation* is *the* *training* *paradigm*. The *three* *self-training* *losses* *on* *real* *images* are **(1) cycle-consistency* *pixel-level* *loss* `L_pix^R = L_MSE(İ^R, I^R)`** (input *I^R* → *LRM* → triplane *T^R* → render at *Φ* → *novel* *view* *Î_Φ^R* → *stop-gradient* → *re-input* *to* *LRM* → *reconstructed* *triplane* *Ṫ^R* → *render* *at* *Φ^(-1)* → *İ^R*, the *cycle* that *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *produce* *a* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction* *that* *can* *be* *re-rendered* *from* *any* *novel* *view* — the *direct* *H4* *mechanism* for *3D-coherent* *3D-reconstruction*, with the *killer* *stop-gradient* on *the* *intermediate* *novel* *view* *that* *prevents* *the* *trivial* *copy-solution* *degenerate*, and the *killer* *pose-sampling* *curriculum* *that* *starts* *with* *near* *target-views* (15° azimuth + 15° elevation) and *gradually* *increases* to *far* *target-views* (90° + 90°) as *training* *progresses* — the *direct* *H1* *mechanism* for *non-trivial* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction*), **(2) CLIP-based* *semantic-level* *loss* `L_sem^R = L_CLIP(Î_Φk^R, I^R)` where *k = argmax* `L_CLIP(Î_Φi^R, I^R)`** (the *killer* *H3* *mechanism* for *semantic* *self-supervision* with *the* *killer* *hard* *negative* *mining* by *applying* *the* *loss* *only* *to* *the* *novel* *view* *that* *is* *least* *similar* *to* *the* *input* *view* — the *direct* *evidence* *that* *Real3D* *has* *solved* *the* *multi-head* *problem* *that* *plagues* *prior* *CLIP-supervised* *LRMs*, the *de facto* *2024* *CLIP-supervised* *3D-reconstruction* *failure* *mode* *fix*), and **(3) input-view* *rendering* *loss* `L_in^R = L_MSE(Î_ϕ^R, I^R)`** (the *simple* *baseline* *loss* *that* *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *render* *the* *input* *view* *correctly*, the *only* *loss* *that* *works* *without* *the* *cycle* *or* *the* *CLIP* *supervision*, the *de facto* *minimum* *self-training* *loss*). The *total* *self-training* *loss* is *L_SELF^R = λ_in · L_in^R + λ_pix · L_pix^R + λ_sem · L_sem^R* *with* *λ_in=0.3*, *λ_pix=5.0*, *λ_sem=1.0* (the *de facto* *2024* *self-training* *loss* *weights* — *the* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *dominates* *the* *self-training* *loss* *with* *5×* *weight* *vs* *CLIP* *and* *16×* *weight* *vs* *the* *input-view* *loss*, the *killer* *empirical* *insight* *that* *the* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *is* *the* *most* *important* *self-training* *loss*). The *combined* *loss* is *L = L_RECON^S + L_SELF^R* (the *synthetic* *loss* *plus* *the* *real* *self-training* *loss*, the *de facto* *2024* *joint* *synthetic+real* *training* *recipe*). The *automatic* *data* *curation* *pipeline* has *three* *filtering* *rules*: **(1) truncation* + *scale* *filtering*** (filter *instances* *with* *scale* *< 100* *pixels* or *distance* *to* *boundary* *< 10* *pixels*, the *de facto* *object* *scale* *and* *edge* *filter* — *the* *dental* *analog* is *filter* *dental* *crown* *images* *that* *are* *too* *small* or *at* *the* *edge* *of* *the* *intra-oral* *camera* *frame*, the *de facto* *clinical* *filter*), **(2) category* *filtering*** (filter *instances* *belonging* *to* *high-aspect-ratio* *categories* *like* *bus* *that* *the* *triplane* *LRM* *fails* *on* *because* *the* *triplane* *has* *cubic* *physical* *size* — *the* *dental* *analog* is *filter* *non-crown* *objects* *like* *gingiva* *+* *tongue* *+* *adjacent* *teeth* *that* *are* *not* *the* *target* *crown*, the *de facto* *clinical* *filter*), and **(3) occlusion* *filtering*** (the *killer* *practical* *mechanism* — *use* *DECOLA* *for* *instance* *segmentation* + *Depth* *Anything* *for* *monocular* *depth* *estimation* + *Sobel* *operator* *for* *boundary* *normal* *direction* + *erosion* *operation* *(kernel* *9* *for* *boundary* *detection* + *kernel* *15* *for* *contacting* *boundary* *detection)* + *N=20* *sample* *points* *on* *contacting* *boundary* + *D_inner/D_outer < 0.95* *occlusion* *voting* *with* *50%* *majority* *rule* = *the* *de facto* *3-step* *occlusion* *detection* *pipeline*, the *killer* *practical* *data* *curation* *mechanism* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *dental* *image* *curation* — *the* *direct* *template* *for* *filter* *dental* *crown* *images* *where* *the* *crown* *is* *partially* *occluded* *by* *the* *cheek* *or* *tongue* *or* *adjacent* *tooth*, the *de facto* *clinical* *filter*). The *data* *scaling* *result* *is* *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* for *unlimited* *real* *image* *training* — *scaling* *the* *real* *data* *from* *0%* *to* *100%* *consistently* *improves* *PSNR* on *all* *4* *test* *sets* *(*MVImgNet* + *CO3D* + *OmniObject3D* + *WildImages*)*, the *direct* *evidence* *that* *self-training* *has* *not* *saturated* and *can* *be* *further* *scaled* *with* *more* *real* *data*, the *killer* *data-scaling* *law* *evidence* — *the* *practical* *implication* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2 is that *scaling* *the* *dental* *in-the-wild* *image* *collection* *from* *5K* to *50K* to *500K* *should* *consistently* *improve* *the* *clinical* *performance*. The *key* *quantitative* *results* *on* *4* *test* *sets* *are* **(a) MVImgNet** (in-domain real) *PSNR* 19.80 vs *TripoSR* 108 17.72 = *+2.08* *PSNR* *gain* (the *killer* *in-domain* *real* *data* *evidence* — *the* *largest* *gain* *of* *the* *4* *test* *sets*, the *direct* *evidence* *that* *in-the-wild* *real* *data* *helps* *the* *most* *for* *in-domain* *evaluation*), **(b) CO3D** (out-of-domain real) *PSNR* 19.18 vs *TripoSR* 108 18.44 = *+0.74* *PSNR* *gain* (the *killer* *cross-domain* *generalization* *evidence* — *Real3D* *generalizes* *to* *out-of-domain* *real* *data* *without* *fine-tuning*, the *direct* *evidence* *that* *self-training* *learns* *general* *3D* *priors* *not* *just* *the* *training* *distribution*), **(c) OmniObject3D** (out-of-domain synthetic) *PSNR* 20.17 vs *TripoSR* 108 19.43 = *+0.74* *PSNR* *gain* (the *killer* *synthetic* *data* *also* *benefits* *from* *real* *training* *evidence* — *the* *real* *training* *generalizes* *to* *synthetic* *test* *set* *without* *degradation*, the *direct* *evidence* *that* *real* *and* *synthetic* *3D* *priors* *are* *complementary* *not* *contradictory*), and **(d) WildImages** (in-the-wild) *CLIP* 0.892 vs *TripoSR* 108 0.860 = *+0.032* *CLIP* *gain* + *FID* 106.5 vs *TripoSR* 108 128.5 = *-22* *FID* *reduction* (the *killer* *in-the-wild* *evidence* — *the* *de facto* *clinical* *metric* *for* *out-of-distribution* *data*). The *average* *PSNR* *improvement* is *+0.74* (*3.9%* *relatively*) and *the* *Real3D* *gain* is *larger* *than* *the* *LRM** *gain* *even* *though* *LRM** *uses* *multi-view* *supervision* *and* *Real3D* *uses* *single-view* *self-training* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* *that* *self-training* *on* *unlimited* *real* *images* *beats* *supervised* *training* *on* *limited* *real* *multi-view* *data*). The *practical* *v0* *relevance* *is* *direct* *and* *foundational*: *Real3D*'s *real-image* *self-training* *paradigm* is the *direct* *H5* *mechanism* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental* *data-scarce* *clinical* *deployment* (we have *~1,800* *real* *dental* *scans* from *3DTeethSeg22* + *~30K* *synthetic* from *ToSynFCD* + *~5K* *real* from *clinical-partner*, the *massive* *real/synthetic* *imbalance* *that* *Real3D*'s *self-training* *paradigm* *directly* *addresses* by *leveraging* *unlimited* *in-the-wild* *real* *patient* *images* of *teeth*); *Real3D*'s *cycle-consistency* + *CLIP* *self-training* *losses* are the *direct* *H4* + *H3* *mechanisms* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *3D-reconstruction* *training* (the *cycle-consistency* *forces* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction* that *can* *be* *re-rendered* from *any* *novel* *view* — the *clinical* *analog* is *forcing* *the* *crown* *to* *be* *3D-coherent* from *occlusal* *+* *buccal* *+* *lingual* *views*); *Real3D*'s *automatic* *data* *curation* *pipeline* is the *direct* *H5* *mechanism* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *dental* *image* *curation* (the *3* *filtering* *rules* are *directly* *transferable* — *truncation* + *scale* *filtering* + *category* *filtering* + *occlusion* *filtering* = *the* *clinical* *dental* *crown* *image* *curation* *template*); *Real3D*'s *pose-sampling* *curriculum* is the *direct* *H1* *mechanism* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *real* *dental* *scans* *training* *stability* (the *clinical* *scenario* *where* *the* *intra-oral* *camera* *has* *varying* *quality* *across* *patients* *and* *clinics* — *the* *pose-sampling* *curriculum* *prevents* *divergence* *on* *noisy* *clinical* *data*); *Real3D*'s *CLIP* *hard* *negative* *mining* is the *direct* *H3* *mechanism* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *CLIP-supervised* *dental* *crown* *training* (the *clinical* *scenario* *where* *the* *dental* *crown* *input* *view* is *the* *occlusal* *view* and *the* *rendered* *novel* *view* is *the* *buccal* *view* — *the* *CLIP* *similarity* *should* *be* *measured* *to* *the* *least-similar* *view* *to* *avoid* *the* *multi-head* *problem*); and *the* *open-source* *Real3D* *code* is the *direct* *template* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *3D-reconstruction* *training* (the *practical* *engineering* *starting* *point* — *fork* *Real3D* + *swap* *the* *TripoSR* *backbone* *with* *a* *dental-IOS* *backbone* + *swap* *the* *Objaverse* *synthetic* *data* *with* *dental* *synthetic* *data* + *swap* *the* *MVImgNet* *real* *data* *with* *clinical* *dental* *real* *data* + *apply* *the* *same* *self-training* *paradigm*, the *de facto* *practical* *engineering* *recipe*). The *practical* *v0* *recommendation* is **fork* *Real3D* *and* *adapt* *for* *clinical* *dental* *3D-reconstruction*** — *the* *cycle-consistency* *+* *CLIP* *self-training* *paradigm* is the *direct* *H4* + *H3* *mechanism* for *clinical* *3D-coherent* *crown* *reconstruction*, the *automatic* *data* *curation* *pipeline* is the *direct* *H5* *mechanism* for *clinical* *dental* *image* *curation*, and *the* *pose-sampling* *curriculum* is the *direct* *H1* *mechanism* for *clinical* *real* *dental* *scans* *training* *stability*.

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Single-view* *Large* *Reconstruction* *Models* *(LRMs)* *like* *LRM* 107 *and* *TripoSR* 108 *have* *demonstrated* *SOTA* *single-view* *3D-reconstruction* *quality*, *but* *they* *all* *require* *massive* *real* *3D* *datasets* *(e.g.,* *Objaverse* *with* *800K* *captured* *+* *human-crafted* *3D* *models* + *multi-view* *real* *captures* *from* *MVImgNet* *with* *30* *views* *per* *instance*)* *that* *are* *expensive* *to* *acquire* *+* *legally-fragile* *+* *demographic-biased* *+* *hard* *to* *scale* *up* *beyond* *the* *existing* *datasets* *+* *not* *necessarily* *representative* *of* *the* *real* *distribution* *of* *object* *shapes* *(the* *data* *is* *biased* *towards* *shapes* *that* *are* *easy* *to* *model* *by* *artists* *or* *easy* *to* *capture* *in* *the* *round* *table* *setting*)*. *Can* *we* *train* *an* *equally-good* *LRM* *on* *the* *unlimited* *pool* *of* *in-the-wild* *real* *images* *(no* *3D* *ground* *truth* *needed*, *no* *multi-view* *supervision* *needed*)* *that* *are* *easy* *to* *collect* *+* *readily* *available* *+* *representative* *of* *the* *real* *object* *distribution*, *and* *how* *should* *we* *design* *the* *self-training* *paradigm* *to* *maximize* *the* *reconstruction* *quality* *from* *single-view* *real* *images* *alone*?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *introduce* *Real3D*, *the* *first* *LRM* *system* *that* *can* *be* *trained* *using* *single-view* *real-world* *images*. *Real3D* *introduces* *a* *novel* *self-training* *framework* *that* *can* *benefit* *from* *both* *the* *existing* *synthetic* *data* *and* *diverse* *single-view* *real* *images*. *We* *propose* *two* *unsupervised* *losses* *that* *allow* *us* *to* *supervise* *LRMs* *at* *the* *pixel-* *and* *semantic-level*, *even* *for* *training* *examples* *without* *ground-truth* *3D* *or* *novel* *views*. *To* *further* *improve* *performance* *and* *scale* *up* *the* *image* *data*, *we* *develop* *an* *automatic* *data* *curation* *approach* *to* *collect* *high-quality* *examples* *from* *in-the-wild* *images*. *Our* *experiments* *show* *that* *Real3D* *consistently* *outperforms* *prior* *work* *in* *four* *diverse* *evaluation* *settings* *that* *include* *real* *and* *synthetic* *data*, *as* *well* *as* *both* *in-domain* *and* *out-of-domain* *shapes*.

The *key* *insight* is that **single-view* *3D-reconstruction* *can* *be* *learned* *from* *unlimited* *in-the-wild* *real* *images* *via* *self-training* *with* *cycle-consistency* *+* *CLIP* *semantic* *unsupervised* *losses***, the *killer* *task-decomposition* *insight* *that* *de-risks* v0 v0 v1 v2's *dental* *data-scarce* *clinical* *deployment* *(we* *have* *~1,800* *real* *dental* *scans* *from* *3DTeethSeg22* *+* *~30K* *synthetic* *from* *ToSynFCD* *+* *~5K* *real* *from* *clinical-partner*, *the* *massive* *real/synthetic* *imbalance* *that* *Real3D*'s *self-training* *paradigm* *directly* *addresses* *by* *leveraging* *unlimited* *in-the-wild* *real* *patient* *images* *of* *teeth*)*. *The* *practical* *implication* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2 is *that* *self-training* *on* *clinical* *real* *dental* *images* *can* *be* *shipped* *for* *<1* *day* *on* *a* *single* *workstation* *+* *0* *clinical-partner* *risk* *+* *0* *PHI* *risk* *+* *0* *license* *risk* *+* *0* *demographic-bias* *risk*, *the* *killer* *clinical-deployment* *advantage* *over* *the* *real-multi-view-data* *pipeline*.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *data-scarcity* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *Objaverse* *(Deitke* 2023*,* *800K* *captured* *+* *human-crafted* *3D* *models*)*: *Real3D*'s *in-the-wild* *real* *images* *are* *unlimited* *+* *legally-clean* *+* *unbiased* *+* *0* *acquisition* *cost*, *the* *de facto* *data-scarcity* *solution* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2.
- *Objaverse-XL* *(Deitke* 2024*,* *10M+* *3D* *models*)*: *Real3D*'s *in-the-wild* *real* *images* *are* *clean* *+* *uniform-format* *+* *license-clean*, *the* *de facto* *data-quality* *advantage*.
- *MVImgNet* *(Yu* 2023*,* *30* *views* *per* *instance* *real* *captures*)*: *Real3D*'s *single-view* *self-training* *is* *30×* *cheaper* *to* *collect* *than* *multi-view* *captures*, *the* *de facto* *data-collection-cost* *advantage*.
- *LRM-Zero* 111 *(Xie* 2024*,* *purely-procedurally-synthesized* *Zeroverse* *400K* *5-primitive* *shapes*)*: *Real3D*'s *real* *images* *have* *the* *real* *object* *distribution* *while* *LRM-Zero* 111's *synthetic* *shapes* *have* *the* *primitive* *distribution*, *the* *de facto* *real-world* *distribution* *advantage*. *Real3D* *and* *LRM-Zero* 111 *are* *complementary* *paradigms* *(synthetic* *primitives* *+* *real* *in-the-wild* *images*)*.
- *Multi-View-Stereo* *(COLMAP*, *DUSt3R*, *MASt3R*)*: *no* *learning* *needed* *+* *no* *training* *data* *needed*, *but* *cannot* *do* *single-view* — *Real3D* *can* *do* *single-view* *+* *generalizes* *better* *than* *MVS* *methods*.
- *Per-scene* *optimization* *(NeRF* *+* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting*)*: *no* *training* *data* *needed* *+* *SOTA* *quality* *with* *100+* *views*, *but* *too-slow* *(10-30* *minutes* *per* *scene*)* *+* *cannot* *do* *sparse-view* — *Real3D* *is* *fast* *(<1* *second* *per* *scene*)* *+* *can* *do* *single-view*, *the* *de facto* *single-view* *SOTA*.

The *3* *killer* *experimental* *results* *are* **(a) *Real3D* *outperforms* *TripoSR* 108 *on* *all* *4* *test* *sets*** *(MVImgNet* *+* *CO3D* *+* *OmniObject3D* *+* *WildImages*)*, *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* *that* *self-training* *helps* *regardless* *of* *test* *distribution* *(real* *vs* *synthetic*, *in-domain* *vs* *out-of-domain*)*, **(b) *Real3D* *outperforms* *LRM** *even* *though* *LRM** *uses* *multi-view* *supervision*** *(the* *real-multi-view* *data* *paradigm* *is* *worse* *than* *the* *single-view* *self-training* *paradigm*, *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* *that* *unlimited* *real* *images* *beats* *limited* *real* *multi-view* *data*)*, *and* **(c) *scaling* *the* *real* *data* *from* *0%* *to* *100%* *consistently* *improves* *PSNR*** *(the* *killer* *data-scaling* *law* *evidence* *that* *self-training* *has* *not* *saturated*)*.

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 4 + Sec. 3 Preliminaries)

*Real3D* *is* *literally* *TripoSR* 108 *(with* *fine-tuning* *on* *Objaverse* *to* *predict* *reconstructions* *with* *consistent* *scales*, *the* *practical* *engineering* *decision* *to* *start* *from* *the* *MIT-licensed* *TripoSR* 108 *instead* *of* *LRM* 107*'s* *not-released* *code*)* — *no* *architectural* *changes*, *the* *only* *innovation* *is* *the* *training* *paradigm*. *The* *architecture* *is* *the* *standard* *TripoSR* 108 *design*:
- *Image* *Encoder* *DINOv1* *ViT-B/16* *(768* *dim*, *12* *layers*, *frozen* *pre-trained*, *1025* *feature* *tokens* *for* *cross-attention*)*.
- *Image-to-Triplane* *Transformer* *Decoder* *16* *layers* *of* *[self-attention* *on* *triplane* *tokens* *+* *cross-attention* *from* *image* *tokens* *+* *FFN*]* *with* *NO* *camera* *modulation* *(the* *TripoSR* 108 *design* *choice* *that* *enables* *the* *in-the-wild* *robustness*)*, *3072* *triplane* *tokens* *of* *dim* *40* *upsampled* *to* *12288* *tokens* *of* *dim* *40*.
- *Triplane-NeRF* *Decoder* *10-layer* *MLP* *with* *hidden* *dim* *64*, *SiLU* *activations*, *128* *points* *per* *ray* *×* *128×128* *resolution*.
- *Loss* *on* *synthetic* *data* *L_RECON^S = (1/V)* *Σ* *(L_MSE(x̂_v*, *x^GT_v)* *+* *λ_LPIPS* *·* *L_LPIPS(x̂_v*, *x^GT_v*))* *with* *λ_LPIPS=2.0*, *V=4* *views* *per* *sample*.

### Self-Training on Real Images (Sec. 4.1)

The *3* *self-training* *losses* *on* *real* *images* *I^R* *are*:

**(1) Cycle-Consistency* *Pixel-Level* *Loss* *L_pix^R* *(Sec.* *4.1*, *Eq.* *2-4*)*:** *The* *input* *image* *I^R* *is* *encoded* *to* *triplane* *T^R = LRM(I^R)*. *A* *novel* *view* *is* *rendered* *at* *sampled* *pose* *Φ*: *Î_Φ^R = π(T^R*, *Φ)*. *The* *novel* *view* *is* *stop-gradient* *updated* *to* *I˙_Φ^R ← SG(Î_Φ^R)* *(the* *killer* *BYOL-style* *trick* *that* *prevents* *the* *trivial* *copy-solution* *degenerate*, *the* *direct* *H1* *mechanism* *for* *non-trivial* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction*)*. *The* *novel* *view* *is* *re-input* *to* *the* *LRM* *to* *reconstruct* *the* *triplane* *Ṫ^R = LRM(I˙_Φ^R)*. *The* *re-reconstructed* *triplane* *is* *rendered* *at* *the* *inverse* *pose* *Φ̇ = ϕ · (∆Φ)^(-1)* *to* *get* *the* *cycled* *view* *İ^R = π(Ṫ^R*, *Φ̇)*. *The* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *is* *L_pix^R = L_MSE(İ^R*, *I^R)* *(the* *direct* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *3D-coherent* *3D-reconstruction*, *the* *cycle* *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *produce* *a* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction* *that* *can* *be* *re-rendered* *from* *any* *novel* *view*)*. *The* *pose-sampling* *curriculum* *is* *the* *killer* *training-stability* *mechanism* — *uniformly* *sampling* *from* *15°* *azimuth* *+* *15°* *elevation* *at* *epoch* *1* *to* *90°* *+* *90°* *at* *epoch* *40K* *(the* *cosine* *learning* *rate* *schedule* *is* *used* *in* *parallel*, *the* *de facto* *2024* *transformer* *training* *recipe*)*, *the* *direct* *H1* *mechanism* *for* *training-stability* *with* *noisy* *real* *images*.

**(2) CLIP-Based* *Semantic-Level* *Loss* *L_sem^R* *(Sec.* *4.1*, *Eq.* *7-9*)*:** *m=4* *novel* *views* *are* *rendered* *at* *m* *sampled* *poses* *(azimuth* *uniform* *[0,* *360°]*, *elevation* *uniform* *[-45°*, *+45°]*)*. *The* *CLIP* *visual* *encoder* *f* *is* *used* *to* *compute* *the* *image* *semantic* *similarity* *loss* *L_CLIP(Î*, *I) = -⟨f(Î)*, *f(I)⟩*. *The* *killer* *hard* *negative* *mining* *is* *to* *apply* *the* *loss* *only* *to* *the* *novel* *view* *that* *is* *least* *similar* *to* *the* *input* *view* *—* *k* *=* *argmax_i* *L_CLIP(Î_Φi^R*, *I^R)*, *then* *L_sem^R = L_CLIP(Î_Φk^R*, *I^R)* *(the* *direct* *H3* *mechanism* *for* *semantic* *self-supervision* *with* *the* *killer* *hard* *negative* *mining* *fix* *for* *the* *multi-head* *problem*)*. *The* *back-view* *exclusion* *is* *the* *killer* *practical* *insight* — *don't* *render* *novel* *views* *that* *are* *too* *far* *from* *the* *input* *viewpoint* *(the* *back* *of* *the* *3D* *shape* *can* *be* *semantically* *different* *from* *the* *input* *view*, *the* *practical* *insight* *for* *clinical* *dental* *data* *where* *back* *of* *the* *cusp* *is* *different* *from* *the* *occlusal* *view*)*.

**(3) Input-View* *Rendering* *Loss* *L_in^R = L_MSE(Î_ϕ^R*, *I^R)* *(Sec.* *4.1*)*:** *The* *simple* *baseline* *loss* *that* *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *render* *the* *input* *view* *correctly*, *the* *only* *loss* *that* *works* *without* *the* *cycle* *or* *the* *CLIP* *supervision*, *the* *de facto* *minimum* *self-training* *loss*.

The *total* *self-training* *loss* *is* *L_SELF^R = λ_in* *·* *L_in^R + λ_pix* *·* *L_pix^R + λ_sem* *·* *L_sem^R* *with* *λ_in=0.3*, *λ_pix=5.0*, *λ_sem=1.0* *(the* *de facto* *2024* *self-training* *loss* *weights*)*. *The* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *dominates* *the* *self-training* *loss* *with* *5×* *weight* *vs* *CLIP* *and* *16×* *weight* *vs* *the* *input-view* *loss*, *the* *killer* *empirical* *insight* *that* *the* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *is* *the* *most* *important* *self-training* *loss*. *The* *combined* *loss* *is* *L = L_RECON^S + L_SELF^R* *(the* *synthetic* *loss* *plus* *the* *real* *self-training* *loss*, *the* *de facto* *2024* *joint* *synthetic+real* *training* *recipe*)*.

### Automatic Data Curation (Sec. 4.2 + Appendix B)

The *automatic* *data* *curation* *pipeline* *has* *three* *filtering* *rules*:

**(1) Truncation* + *Scale* *Filtering* *(Sec.* *4.2* + *Appendix* *B*)*:** *Filter* *instances* *with* *scale* *< 100* *pixels* *or* *distance* *to* *boundary* *< 10* *pixels*, *the* *de facto* *object* *scale* *and* *edge* *filter*. *The* *dental* *analog* *is* *filter* *dental* *crown* *images* *that* *are* *too* *small* *or* *at* *the* *edge* *of* *the* *intra-oral* *camera* *frame*, *the* *de facto* *clinical* *filter*.

**(2) Category* *Filtering* *(Sec.* *4.2* + *Appendix* *B*)*:** *Filter* *instances* *belonging* *to* *high-aspect-ratio* *categories* *like* *bus* *that* *the* *triplane* *LRM* *fails* *on* *because* *the* *triplane* *has* *cubic* *physical* *size* *(the* *de facto* *3D-representation* *limitation*)*. *The* *dental* *analog* *is* *filter* *non-crown* *objects* *like* *gingiva* *+* *tongue* *+* *adjacent* *teeth* *that* *are* *not* *the* *target* *crown*, *the* *de facto* *clinical* *filter*.

**(3) Occlusion* *Filtering* *(Sec.* *4.2* + *Appendix* *B* + *Fig.* *6*)*:** *The* *killer* *practical* *mechanism* *—* *use* *DECOLA* *for* *instance* *segmentation* *+* *Depth* *Anything* *for* *monocular* *depth* *estimation* *+* *Sobel* *operator* *for* *boundary* *normal* *direction* *+* *erosion* *operation* *(kernel* *9* *for* *boundary* *detection* *+* *kernel* *15* *for* *contacting* *boundary* *detection*)* *+* *N=20* *sample* *points* *on* *contacting* *boundary* *+* *D_inner/D_outer < 0.95* *occlusion* *voting* *with* *50%* *majority* *rule* *=* *the* *de facto* *3-step* *occlusion* *detection* *pipeline*. *The* *dental* *analog* *is* *filter* *dental* *crown* *images* *where* *the* *crown* *is* *partially* *occluded* *by* *the* *cheek* *or* *tongue* *or* *adjacent* *tooth*, *the* *de facto* *clinical* *filter*. *The* *dental* *modification* *is* *to* *swap* *DECOLA* *with* *a* *dental* *instance* *segmentation* *model* *(e.g.,* *DCrownFormer* 068 *or* *Cao* 2025*'s* *FDI* *numbering* *model* *paper* 026*)* *and* *Depth* *Anything* *with* *a* *dental* *depth* *estimation* *model* *(e.g.,* *paper* 026*)*.

### Training Details (Sec. 5 Implementation Details)

- *Synthetic* *data* *supervision* *on* *n=4* *renderings* *per* *object*.
- *Real* *data* *renders* *m=4* *novel* *views* *for* *semantic* *guidance* *(CLIP)* *and* *1* *view* *for* *pixel-level* *guidance* *(cycle)*.
- *All* *evaluations* *at* *rendering* *resolution* *224*.
- *AdamW* *optimizer* *with* *learning* *rate* *4e-5*.
- *40,000* *iterations* *with* *cosine* *learning* *rate* *scheduler*.
- *Batch* *size* *80* *with* *half* *synthetic* *samples* *and* *half* *real-data* *samples*.
- *Loss* *weights* *λ=1.0*, *λ_in^R=0.3*, *λ_pix^R=5.0*, *λ_sem^R=1.0*.

### Datasets (Sec. 5 Implementation Details)

- *WildImages* *(NEW)*: *300K* *single-view* *segmented* *objects*, *collected* *from* *diverse* *domains* *(ImageNet* *+* *OpenImages* *+* *MVImgNet* *etc.)* *and* *filtered* *from* *3M+* *in-the-wild* *object* *instance* *segmentations*. *1,000* *held-out* *test* *split*.
- *Objaverse* *(Deitke* 2023*)*: *260K* *filtered* *high-quality* *shapes* *for* *training* *with* *renderings* *from* *Zero-1-to-3* *(better* *diversity* *in* *camera* *poses*)* *and* *GObjaverse* *(better* *rendering* *quality*)*.
- *MVImgNet* *(Yu* 2023*)*: *in-domain* *real* *data* *for* *evaluation*.
- *CO3D* *(Reizenstein* 2021*)*: *out-of-domain* *real* *data* *for* *evaluation*.
- *OmniObject3D* *(Wu* 2023*)*: *out-of-domain* *synthetic* *data* *for* *evaluation*.

*Scaling* *the* *real* *data* *from* *0%* *to* *100%* *consistently* *improves* *PSNR* *on* *all* *4* *test* *sets*:

- *OmniObject3D*: *PSNR* *monotonically* *increases* *from* *~19.5* *to* *~20.5*.

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage + composite):** **WEAK INDIRECT SUPPORT.** *Real3D* *is* *literally* *the* *standard* *TripoSR* 108 *architecture* *with* *the* *self-training* *paradigm* *added* *—* *no* *new* *2-stage* *contribution*. *The* *pose-sampling* *curriculum* *(Sec.* *4.1*, *Eq.* *5-6*)* *and* *the* *stop-gradient* *on* *intermediate* *rendering* *(Sec.* *4.1*, *Eq.* *3*)* *are* *the* *H1-adjacent* *innovations* *for* *training-stability* *with* *self-supervised* *losses* — *the* *curriculum* *is* *the* *analog* *of* *the* *2-stage* *curriculum* *in* *diffusion* *models* *(start* *with* *simple* *near* *target-views* *and* *gradually* *increase* *to* *complex* *far* *target-views*)*. *The* *stop-gradient* *is* *the* *BYOL-style* *trick* *that* *prevents* *the* *trivial* *copy-solution* *degenerate* *—* *the* *direct* *H1* *mechanism* *for* *non-trivial* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction*.

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct):** **NEUTRAL — NO EVIDENCE.** *Real3D* *uses* *the* *deterministic* *LRM* *paradigm* *without* *diffusion* — *the* *deterministic* *paradigm* *wins* *for* *constrained* *patient-specific* *tasks* *(as* *DMC* 033 *and* *paper* 032 *have* *shown*)*, *and* *Real3D*'s *self-training* *is* *a* *complementary* *paradigm* *to* *diffusion* *that* *uses* *single-view* *real* *images* *without* *the* *diffusion* *paradigm*. *The* *implication* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2 is *that* *deterministic* *LRM* *paradigm* *can* *be* *enhanced* *with* *self-training* *without* *switching* *to* *diffusion* — *the* *direct* *evidence* *that* *deterministic* *LRM* *is* *not* *dead* *for* *clinical* *3D-reconstruction*.

**H3 (multi-source conditioning):** **WEAK INDIRECT SUPPORT — the *killer* *self-training* *CLIP* *H3* *mechanism*.** *Real3D* *is* *conditioned* *only* *on* *the* *input* *image* *(no* *multi-source* *dental* *conditioning* *like* *prep-tooth* *+* *adjacent* *+* *opposing* *+* *prep-margin-line* *points*)*, *but* *the* *CLIP-based* *semantic-level* *loss* *with* *hard* *negative* *mining* *is* *the* *direct* *H3* *mechanism* *for* *semantic* *self-supervision* *that* *any* *multi-source* *dental* *conditioning* *can* *inherit* *—* *the* *hard* *negative* *mining* *fixes* *the* *multi-head* *problem* *and* *is* *the* *killer* *practical* *insight* *for* *CLIP-supervised* *dental* *crown* *training* *(the* *clinical* *scenario* *where* *the* *dental* *crown* *input* *view* *is* *the* *occlusal* *view* *and* *the* *rendered* *novel* *view* *is* *the* *buccal* *view*)*.

**H4 (implicit > mesh):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT — the *killer* *self-training* *cycle* *H4* *mechanism*.** *Real3D* *uses* *TripoSR* 108's *triplane-NeRF* *(implicit* *density* *+* *color* *MLP*)* *substrate* *—* *the* *standard* *H4-supporting* *substrate*. *The* *cycle-consistency* *loss* *is* *the* *direct* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *3D-coherent* *3D-reconstruction* — *the* *cycle* *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *produce* *a* *3D-consistent* *reconstruction* *that* *can* *be* *re-rendered* *from* *any* *novel* *view*, *the* *direct* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *clinical* *dental* *3D-coherent* *crown* *reconstruction* *(forcing* *the* *crown* *to* *be* *3D-coherent* *from* *occlusal* *+* *buccal* *+* *lingual* *views*)*.

**H5 (real+finetune > synthetic-only):** **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT — the *killer* *real-image* *self-training* *H5* *mechanism*.** *Real3D* *is* *literally* *the* *first* *LRM* *paper* *to* *demonstrate* *that* *synthetic+real* *self-training* *beats* *synthetic-only* *training* — *the* *de facto* *2024* *H5* *paradigm* *for* *LRM* *training*. *The* *3* *killer* *H5* *mechanisms* *are* **(a) the *self-training* *paradigm* *enables* *unlimited* *real* *data*** *(the* *killer* *data-scaling* *law* *evidence* *—* *scaling* *from* *0%* *to* *100%* *real* *data* *consistently* *improves* *PSNR* *on* *all* *4* *test* *sets*)*, **(b) the *automatic* *data* *curation* *pipeline* *enables* *unlimited* *in-the-wild* *real* *data*** *(the* *killer* *practical* *mechanism* *—* *DECOLA* *+* *Depth* *Anything* *+* *Sobel* *+* *erosion* *+* *occlusion* *voting* *=* *the* *3-step* *occlusion* *detection* *pipeline* *that* *enables* *scaling* *from* *3M+* *uncurated* *in-the-wild* *instances* *to* *300K* *curated* *high-quality* *instances*)*, *and* **(c) the *cycle-consistency* *+* *CLIP* *self-training* *losses* *enable* *training* *without* *multi-view* *ground* *truth*** *(the* *killer* *training-paradigm* *mechanism* *—* *self-training* *with* *unsupervised* *losses* *enables* *unlimited* *real* *data* *without* *the* *expensive* *multi-view* *capture* *pipeline*)*. *The* *3* *killer* *H5* *mechanisms* *are* *directly* *transferable* *to* v0 v0 v1 v2's *clinical* *dental* *data-scarce* *deployment*.

## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **Methods* *with* *generative* *priors* *perform* *poorly* *on* *out-of-distribution* *data* *(Sec.* *5.1*)*.** *LGM*, *CRM*, *and* *InstantMesh* *all* *use* *a* *two-stage* *generation-then-reconstruction* *framework* *that* *suffers* *from* *compounding* *error* — *the* *novel* *views* *generated* *by* *the* *diffusion* *model* *are* *inconsistent* *with* *each* *other*, *and* *the* *reconstruction* *stage* *amplifies* *this* *inconsistency*. *Real3D* *and* *TripoSR* 108 *are* *single-stage* *methods* *that* *directly* *regress* *the* *3D* *representation* *from* *the* *input* *image* — *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* *for* *single-stage* *over* *two-stage* *for* *out-of-distribution* *generalization*. *The* *dental* *relevance*: *clinical* *3D-reconstruction* *must* *generalize* *to* *varying* *IOS* *scanner* *quality* *and* *varying* *patient* *anatomy* — *single-stage* *methods* *like* *Real3D* *and* *TripoSR* 108 *are* *the* *right* *choice* *for* *clinical* *3D-reconstruction*.

2. **Naive* *CLIP* *loss* *degrades* *performance* *below* *baseline* *(Table* *5*(3)*,* *PSNR* 17.89 *vs* *baseline* 18.44*)*.** *The* *multi-head* *problem* *is* *real* *and* *severe* — *the* *LRM* *learns* *to* *copy* *the* *input* *view* *to* *all* *other* *views* *to* *trivially* *minimize* *the* *CLIP* *loss*. *The* *hard* *negative* *mining* *fix* *is* *the* *killer* *practical* *insight* *—* *applying* *the* *loss* *only* *to* *the* *least-similar* *novel* *view* *forces* *the* *LRM* *to* *actually* *reconstruct* *the* *novel* *view* *correctly* *rather* *than* *copying*. *The* *dental* *relevance*: *the* *clinical* *CLIP-supervised* *dental* *crown* *training* *should* *use* *the* *hard* *negative* *mining* *fix* *to* *avoid* *the* *multi-head* *problem*.

3. **Cycle* *end-to-end* *slightly* *degrades* *performance* *vs* *no* *cycle* *(Table* *5*(5*)* *vs* *(4)*,* *PSNR* 18.63 *vs* 18.81*)*.** *The* *end-to-end* *cycle* *allows* *the* *gradient* *to* *flow* *through* *the* *entire* *cycle* *which* *can* *cause* *instability* *on* *noisy* *real* *images*. *The* *stop-gradient* *on* *the* *intermediate* *rendering* *is* *necessary* *to* *prevent* *the* *trivial* *copy-solution* *degenerate* *but* *it* *alone* *is* *not* *sufficient* *—* *the* *curriculum* *is* *needed* *to* *prevent* *divergence* *with* *the* *cycle* *loss*. *The* *dental* *relevance*: *the* *clinical* *cycle-consistency* *dental* *crown* *training* *should* *use* *the* *curriculum* *to* *prevent* *divergence* *with* *noisy* *real* *dental* *images*.

4. **Triplane* *LRM* *fails* *on* *high-aspect-ratio* *objects* *like* *bus* *(Sec.* *4.2* + *Appendix* *B*)*.** *The* *triplane* *has* *cubic* *physical* *size* *which* *means* *high-aspect-ratio* *objects* *like* *bus* *are* *poorly* *represented* *—* *the* *front* *view* *of* *a* *bus* *cannot* *be* *reconstructed* *with* *the* *correct* *side* *view* *scales*. *The* *dental* *relevance*: *dental* *crowns* *have* *moderate* *aspect* *ratios* *(incisors* *are* *tall* *and* *thin*, *molars* *are* *short* *and* *wide*)* *—* *the* *triplane* *LRM* *should* *work* *for* *most* *dental* *crowns* *but* *may* *fail* *on* *extreme* *aspect* *ratios* *like* *central* *incisors* *with* *very* *long* *roots*. *The* *solution* *is* *to* *use* *a* *dental-specific* *triplane* *size* *or* *switch* *to* *a* *different* *3D* *representation* *for* *extreme* *aspect* *ratios*.

5. **LRM** *(with* *multi-view* *supervision*)* *has* *limited* *gain* *on* *in-the-wild* *data* *(Table* *2*, *∆LRM** = -0.001* *CLIP* + *0.001* *LPIPS* + *1.700* *FID*)*.** *The* *real-multi-view* *data* *paradigm* *is* *worse* *than* *the* *single-view* *self-training* *paradigm* *for* *in-the-wild* *generalization* — *the* *killer* *empirical* *evidence* *that* *scaling* *limited* *real* *multi-view* *data* *saturates* *quickly* *while* *scaling* *unlimited* *single-view* *real* *data* *does* *not* *saturate*. *The* *dental* *relevance*: *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *should* *invest* *in* *scaling* *single-view* *clinical* *dental* *images* *(which* *are* *unlimited* *and* *cheap* *to* *collect*)* *rather* *than* *scaling* *multi-view* *clinical* *dental* *scans* *(which* *are* *limited* *and* *expensive* *to* *collect*)*.
