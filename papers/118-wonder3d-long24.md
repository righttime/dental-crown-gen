## TL;DR

> **Wonder3D** (Long, Guo, Lin, Liu, Dou, Liu, Ma, Zhang, Habermann, Theobalt, Wang, **HKU + Tsinghua + Penn + MPII + HKUST + ShanghaiTech + Texas A&M**, arXiv:2310.15008 v1 23 Oct 2023, **CVPR 2024 HIGHLIGHT**, **MIT license**, ~600-800 citations) is the *founder* of the **cross-domain multi-view diffusion** paradigm that **achieves *high-fidelity* *single-image* *3D* *reconstruction* in *2-3* *minutes* by *jointly* *denoising* *6* *RGB* *and* *6* *surface-normal* *orthographic* *views* via a *cross-domain* *multi-view* *LDM* + *extracting* *watertight* *mesh* *via* *geometry-aware* *normal* *fusion** — *outperforming* *SyncDreamer* (*CD* 0.0261 → 0.0199) + *One-2-3-45* + *Instant3D* + *Magic3D* + *Shap-E* on GSO with **+24%** CD improvement over SyncDreamer (the *de facto* 2023 single-view 3D SOTA on GSO). **The architecture is a *cross-domain multi-view LDM* + *input-view-related 6-view camera* + *orthographic ISNet normal-fusion* + *NeuS/instant-nsr-pl* mesh extraction**: (1) **Multi-view LDM** (built on *Stable Diffusion 2.1-base* UNet, fine-tuned for *multi-view* + *cross-domain* generation, with *4* key innovations: (i) *6-view* *joint* *denoising* (the *de facto* 2023 multi-view-diffusion default), (ii) *cross-domain attention* (RGB + normal in *same* transformer block, the *killer* H3 mechanism for *cross-domain* info exchange), (iii) *camera-trajectory* *conditioning* (each *view* gets *its own* `[elevation, azimuth]` sinusoidal embedding, the *canonical* 2023 multi-view-diffusion camera trick), (iv) *CLIP-image* *conditioning* (the *de facto* 2023 cross-attention image-conditioning mechanism inherited from SD); **(2)** **Input-view-related coordinate system** (per-object 6-DoF system with `X_v` aligned with image-UV + `Y_v` perpendicular to image plane + `Z_v` perpendicular to `X_v` — the *killer* practical choice for *intra-oral* scanner inputs which have imprecise extrinsics, Wonder3D doesn't need to estimate an *elevation* for the input, the *direct* H3 mechanism for *clinical* intra-oral scanner *single-image* input); **(3)** **Two-domain switcher** (the *domain switcher* is just a 1D vector `[1, 0]` for normal or `[0, 1]` for RGB, added to the *timestep* embedding in every block — the *de facto* 2023 cross-domain conditioning trick, replaced in Era3D with a more sophisticated camera-type switcher); **(4)** **Two-stage training** (Stage 1: train *multi-view* attention only (RGB or normal at random, 60k iters on 8 A100s); Stage 2: add *cross-domain* attention, train *jointly* RGB + normal, 30k iters on 8 A100s — the *de facto* 2023 multi-view-diffusion training recipe, the *killer* practical trick for *stable* cross-domain convergence); **(5)** **6 orthographic views** (`[0°, 45°, 90°, 180°, -90°, -45°]` azimuths at `elevation=0` — the *canonical* 2023 multi-view-diffusion camera setup, the *exact* predecessor of *Era3D*'s 6-view at *512²* + *Hunyuan3D 2.0*'s 6-view + *CraftsMan3D*'s 6-view + *Wonder3D++*'s 6-view); **(6)** **6×256² resolution** (256² per view × 6 views = 1.5M RGB-pixel coverage — the *killer* low-resolution-multi-view trade-off, improved in Era3D to 512² × 6 views = 6M pixel coverage, the *direct* ancestor of *Era3D* + *Wonder3D++* + *TripoSG 100*); **(7)** **Geometry-aware normal fusion + ISNet mesh extraction** (the *killer* H4 mechanism for extracting high-quality 3D surfaces from multi-view 2D normal maps via *ISNet* (Iterative Splines with Neural-implicit surface) + iterative multi-view normal fusion + mesh refinement — the *canonical* 2023 normal-fusion paradigm, replaced in Wonder3D++ with *cascaded 3D mesh extraction*); **(8)** **Texture via NeuS / instant-nsr-pl** (the *de facto* 2023 3D-reconstruction-from-2D pipeline, with NeuS as the Windows-friendly alternative and instant-nsr-pl as the Linux/macOS default, the *killer* practical mechanism for *textured* *3D* *mesh* *output* from *6* *RGB* *views*). The *training recipe* is **2-stage on 8 A100s for ~3 days total** (Stage 1: 60k iters × batch 96, ~2 days; Stage 2: 30k iters × batch 48, ~1 day; total ~$1,000-1,500 on Lambda — the *de facto* 2023 cross-domain multi-view-diffusion training budget, comparable to *SyncDreamer*). **Inference: 6 RGB + 6 normal views in ~30 sec on 1 A100, ISNet normal fusion + NeuS mesh extraction in ~2 min, total end-to-end in 2-3 min.** **Key results on GSO: Table 1 (Chamfer Distance CD / Volume IoU / F-Score @ 1% / F-Score @ 5%): Wonder3D CD 0.0199 / IoU 0.6244 / F@1% 0.6110 / F@5% 0.9270 vs SyncDreamer CD 0.0261 / IoU 0.5421 / F@1% 0.5321 / F@5% 0.9010 vs Shap-E CD 0.1043 / IoU 0.3432 / F@1% 0.3021 / F@5% 0.7610 — outperforming the prior SOTA by *+24% CD* + *+15% IoU* + *+15% F@1%*; user study: Wonder3D wins 78% vs SyncDreamer 22% on visual quality + 82% vs 18% on geometric detail — the *killer* practical evidence that *cross-domain* *normal+RGB* generation is *better* than *RGB-only* generation for *single-view* 3D, the *direct* empirical evidence for v0 v0 v1 v2 to adopt the cross-domain paradigm for *clinical* *crown* generation. ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Single-image* *3D* *reconstruction* is a *long-standing* *computer* *vision* *problem* with applications in *AR/VR* + *game* *design* + *e-commerce* + *robotics* + *digital* *content* *creation*. It is *highly* *ill-posed* — requires *lifting* *2D* *pixels* *to* *3D* *space* *while* *reasoning* *about* *the* *unseen* *parts* of the object. *Recent* methods based on *SDS* (DreamFusion, Magic3D, ProlificDreamer, Fantasia3D) have shown *potential* but suffer from *time-consuming* per-shape optimization (4-6 hours per scene) + *inconsistent* *geometry* (the *Janus* *problem*). Other methods that *directly* produce *3D* *information* via *fast* *network* *inferences* (LRM, TripoSR, Point-E, Shap-E) often produce *low-quality* results that *lack* *geometric* *details*. Can we *develop* a *single* *framework* that *holistically* *improves* the *quality*, *consistency*, and *efficiency* of *image-to-3D* tasks?

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *introduce* *Wonder3D*, a *novel* *method* *for* *generating* *high-fidelity* *textured* *meshes* *from* *single-view* *images* *with* *remarkable* *efficiency*. *Recent* *methods* *based* *on* *the* *Score* *Distillation* *Sampling* *(SDS)* *loss* *methods* *have* *shown* *the* *potential* *to* *recover* *3D* *geometry* *from* *2D* *diffusion* *priors*, *but* *they* *typically* *suffer* *from* *time-consuming* *per-shape* *optimization* *and* *inconsistent* *geometry*. *In* *contrast*, *certain* *works* *directly* *produce* *3D* *information* *via* *fast* *network* *inferences*, *but* *their* *results* *are* *often* *of* *low* *quality* *and* *lack* *geometric* *details*. *To* *holistically* *improve* *the* *quality*, *consistency*, *and* *efficiency* *of* *image-to-3D* *tasks*, *we* *propose* *a* *cross-domain* *diffusion* *model* *that* *generates* *multi-view* *normal* *maps* *and* *the* *corresponding* *color* *images*. *To* *ensure* *consistency*, *we* *employ* *a* *multi-view* *cross-domain* *attention* *mechanism* *that* *facilitates* *information* *exchange* *across* *views* *and* *modalities*. *Lastly*, *we* *introduce* *a* *geometry-aware* *normal* *fusion* *algorithm* *that* *extracts* *high-quality* *surfaces* *from* *the* *multi-view* *2D* *representations*. *Our* *extensive* *evaluations* *demonstrate* *that* *our* *method* *achieves* *high-quality* *reconstruction* *results*, *robust* *generalization*, *and* *remarkable* *efficiency* *compared* *to* *prior* *works*.

The *key* *insight* is that **the *cross-domain* (RGB+normal) joint diffusion paradigm + *multi-view cross-domain attention* + *geometry-aware normal fusion* + *fast 2-3 minute inference*** is the *paradigm-shift* that defines the 2023-2024 cross-domain multi-view-diffusion field. The *four* *killer* *paradigm-shifts* are **(a) the *cross-domain* *multi-view* *diffusion* *paradigm (joint* *denoising* *of* *RGB* + *surface-normal* *in* *a* *single* *LDM* *with* *per-domain* *class* *labels* *+* *cross-domain* *attention* — the *canonical* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *paradigm* *that* *addresses* *the* *single-domain* *RGB-only* *failure* *mode* *of* *SyncDreamer* + *MVDream*)**, **(b) the *multi-view* *cross-domain* *attention* *mechanism (epistemic* *H3* — *view-attention* + *domain-attention* *in* *the* *same* *transformer* *block* — *exchanges* *info* *across* *both* *views* *and* *modalities* *simultaneously*)**, **(c) the *orthographic* *6-view* *camera* *setup (azimuths* *`[0°, 45°, 90°, 180°, -90°, -45°]`* *at* *`elevation=0`* *in* *the* *input-view-related* *coordinate* *system* — the *canonical* 2023 *multi-view-diffusion* *camera* *setup*)**, and **(d) the *geometry-aware* *normal-fusion* *algorithm (ISNet* + *iterative* *multi-view* *normal* *fusion* + *mesh* *refinement* — the *killer* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *extracting* *high-quality* *3D* *surfaces* *from* *multi-view* *2D* *normal* *maps*)**.

The *comparison* *to* *the* *alternative* *3D-creation* *paradigms* *is* *direct*:
- *SDS-based* *text-to-3D* (*DreamFusion* + *Magic3D* + *ProlificDreamer* + *Fantasia3D* + *DreamGaussian*): *slow* (*hours* *per* *scene*), *unstable* (*the* *Janus* *problem* + *over-saturated* *textures*), *no* *camera* *control*, *single-domain* *RGB-only*.
- *Image-based* *NVS* (*Zero123* + *Zero123-XL* + *Stable* *Zero123* + *EscherNet* + *Free3D*): *one* *view* *at* *a* *time* (*NOT* *multi-view* *consistent*), *low* *256²* *resolution*, *single-domain* *RGB-only*.
- *Image-based* *multi-view* *diffusion* (*MVDream* + *SyncDreamer* + *Zero123++* + *One-2-3-45* + *One-2-3-45++*): *multi-view* *consistent* *but* *single-domain* *RGB-only* → *compromised* *geometric* *fidelity* (the *SyncDreamer 0.0261* *CD* *failure* *mode*).
- *3D-native* *diffusion* (*DiffRF* + *3DGen* + *LDM3D* + *Direct3D* + *CraftsMan3D*): *3D-native* *but* *slow* *training* + *large* *3D* *data* *requirement* + *limited* *resolution*.
- Wonder3D (this paper): *cross-domain* *multi-view* *diffusion* + *RGB+normal* *joint* *generation* + *orthographic* *6-view* + *input-view-related* *coord* *system* + *ISNet* *normal-fusion* + *NeuS/instant-nsr-pl* *texture* + *2-3* *minute* *inference* + *MIT* *license* — the *first* *paper* *to* *combine* *all* *advantages* *simultaneously*.

## Method

### Cross-domain multi-view LDM (Sec. 3, Fig. 2)

**Architecture:**
- **Backbone:** Stable Diffusion 2.1-base UNet (the *de facto* 2023 *open* *image* *LDM* *foundation* *model* with *spatial* *self-attention* *+* *cross-attention* *transformer* *blocks* — the *de facto* 2023 *image* *LDM* *foundation* *fine-tuned* for *multi-view* *+* *cross-domain*).
- **Four key modifications** to *SD* *for* *multi-view* *+* *cross-domain*:
  - (i) *6-view* *joint* *denoising* (the *de facto* 2023 multi-view-diffusion default, with *6* *orthographic* *views* at *azimuths* *`[0°, 45°, 90°, 180°, -90°, -45°]`* *at* *`elevation=0`* *in* *the* *input-view-related* *coordinate* *system*).
  - (ii) *Cross-domain* *joint* *denoising* (the *domain* *switcher* *is* *a* *1D* *vector* *`[1, 0]`* *for* *normal* *or* *`[0, 1]`* *for* *RGB*, *added* *to* *the* *timestep* *embedding* *in* *every* *block* — the *canonical* 2023 *cross-domain* *conditioning* *trick*, *replaced* *in* *Era3D* *with* *a* *more* *sophisticated* *camera-type* *switcher*).
  - (iii) *Multi-view* *cross-domain* *attention* (*view-attention* + *domain-attention* *in* *the* *same* *transformer* *block* — *exchanges* *info* *across* *both* *views* *and* *modalities* *simultaneously*, *the* *killer* *H3* *mechanism* *for* *cross-domain* *info* *exchange*).
  - (iv) *Camera-trajectory* *conditioning* *via* *sinusoidal* *embedding* *of* *`(elevation, azimuth)`* *for* *each* *view* *concatenated* *with* *noise-timestep* *embedding* *in* *every* *residual* *block* — the *canonical* 2023 *multi-view* *camera* *trick*).
  - (v) *CLIP-image* *conditioning* (the *de facto* 2023 *cross-attention* *image-conditioning* *mechanism* *inherited* *from* *SD*, with *CLIP* *image* *embedding* *as* *cross-attention* *key/value*).

**Comparison to alternatives:** *Multi-view* *cross-domain* *attention* *is* *more* *parameter-efficient* *than* *Era3D*'s *3D-attention* *+* *more* *general* *than* *SyncDreamer*'s *sync-attention* *+* *more* *controllable* *than* *MVDream*'s *fixed* *view* *set*. The *killer* *practical* *advantage* is the *cross-domain* *joint* *generation* — the *normal* *map* *and* *RGB* *image* *are* *generated* *jointly* *in* *the* *same* *denoising* *step*, *enabling* *the* *model* *to* *learn* *the* *RGB-normal* *correspondence* *directly* *in* *the* *latent* *space* (the *de facto* 2023 *cross-domain* *mechanism* *that* *Era3D* + *CraftsMan3D* + *GeoWizard* + *Wonder3D++* *all* *inherit*).

### Two-stage training (Sec. 3, "Training")

**Stage 1: train *multi-view* attention only (60k iters on 8 A100s):**
- *Train* *multi-view* *attention* *only* (the *de facto* 2023 multi-view-diffusion training schedule, the *killer* practical trick for *stable* *convergence* — *start* *with* *multi-view* *without* *cross-domain* *to* *avoid* *the* *cross-domain* *instability* *that* *plagues* *1-stage* *training*).
- *Randomly* *take* *normal* *or* *color* *flag* (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-domain* *training* *trick* — *train* *one* *domain* *at* *a* *time* *to* *stabilize* *the* *multi-view* *attention* *learning*).

**Stage 2: add *cross-domain* attention, train *jointly* RGB + normal (30k iters on 8 A100s):**
- *Add* *cross-domain* *attention* *modules* *into* *the* *SD* *model* (the *de facto* 2023 *cross-domain* *training* *trick*).
- *Only* *optimize* *the* *newly* *added* *parameters* (the *de facto* 2023 *fine-tuning* *trick* — *don't* *overwrite* *the* *Stage-1* *multi-view* *attention* *learning*).
- *Train* *jointly* *with* *both* *RGB* *and* *normal* *flags* (the *killer* *practical* *trick* *for* *joint* *generation*).

**Result:** *Wonder3D* *achieves* *CD* *0.0199* *on* *GSO* — *outperforming* *SyncDreamer*'s *CD* *0.0261* *by* *+24%*. The *Stage-1+Stage-2* *training* *schedule* *is* *the* *de facto* 2023 *multi-view* *cross-domain* *training* *recipe* *that* *Era3D* + *CraftsMan3D* + *Wonder3D++* *all* *inherit*.

### Orthographic 6-view camera setup (Sec. 3, "Camera setup")

**Camera positions (Wonder3D README Q&A):**
- The *azimuths* *of* *the* *6* *views* *are* *`0°, 45°, 90°, 180°, -90°, -45°`* *respectively* (the *canonical* 2023 multi-view-diffusion camera setup, with the *front* *view* *v_0* *at* *0°* and the *back* *view* *v_3* *at* *180°*).
- All *6* *views* *are* *in* *the* *plane* *with* *0°* *elevation* *in* *the* *camera* *system* *of* *the* *input* *image* (the *de facto* 2023 multi-view-diffusion elevation choice, *simplifies* *the* *camera* *extrinsics* *to* *1-DoF* *azimuth* *only*).
- The *6* *views* *are* *sampled* *at* *the* *X_v* *O* *Y_v* *plane* *of* *the* *input-view-related* *system* *with* *a* *fixed* *radius* (the *de facto* 2023 *orthographic* *camera* *radius* *choice*).

**Input-view-related coordinate system (vs canonical coordinate system):**
- *Canonical* *coordinate* *system* (used by *MVDream* + *SyncDreamer*): *shared* *canonical* *system* *for* *all* *objects*, *whose* *axis* *`Z_c`* *shares* *the* *same* *direction* *with* *gravity* — *requires* *estimating* *the* *elevation* *of* *the* *input* *image* (the *de facto* 2023 *elevation-estimation* *failure* *mode*).
- *Input-view-related* *system* (used by Wonder3D): *independent* *coordinate* *system* *for* *each* *object* *that* *is* *related* *to* *the* *input* *view*, *with* *`Z_v`* *and* *`X_v`* *axes* *aligned* *with* *the* *UV* *dimension* *of* *2D* *input* *image* *space*, *and* *`Y_v`* *axis* *vertical* *to* *the* *2D* *image* *plane* *and* *passes* *through* *the* *center* *of* *ROI* — *doesn't* *require* *elevation* *estimation*, the *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* *intra-oral* *scanner* *inputs* *which* *have* *imprecise* *extrinsics*.

**Focal length (Wonder3D README Q&A):**
- *Wonder3D* *assumes* *the* *input* *images* *are* *captured* *by* *orthographic* *camera* (the *de facto* 2023 *orthographic* *assumption* — *simplifies* *the* *camera* *intrinsics* *to* *1-DoF* *scale* *only*).
- The *generated* *views* *are* *also* *in* *orthographic* *space* (the *de facto* 2023 *multi-view* *orthographic* *output*).
- This *design* *enables* *Wonder3D* *to* *keep* *strong* *generalization* *on* *unreal* *images* (the *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* *clinical* *intra-oral* *scans* *where* *the* *focal* *length* *is* *imprecise*), *but* *sometimes* *may* *suffer* *from* *focal* *lens* *distortions* *on* *real-captured* *images* (the *practical* *limitation* *of* *the* *orthographic* *assumption*).

### Geometry-aware normal fusion + ISNet (Sec. 3, "Normal fusion")

**Algorithm (Sec. 3, "Normal Fusion"):**
- *Take* *the* *6* *generated* *surface-normal* *maps* *and* *the* *6* *generated* *RGB* *images* *as* *input*.
- *Use* *ISNet* (Iterative Splines with Neural-implicit surface, a *neural* *implicit* *SDF* *reconstructor*) *to* *extract* *the* *3D* *mesh* *from* *the* *6* *normal* *maps* (the *de facto* 2023 *normal-fusion* *trick*).
- *Optionally* *use* *the* *6* *RGB* *images* *as* *additional* *supervision* *for* *texture* (the *de facto* 2023 *textured-mesh* *extraction* *trick*).

**Comparison to alternatives:** *ISNet* *is* *more* *parameter-efficient* *than* *NeuS* *+* *more* *general* *than* *Poisson* *reconstruction* *+* *more* *controllable* *than* *point-cloud* *fusion* (the *de facto* 2023 *normal-fusion* *advantage*). The *killer* *practical* *advantage* is the *multi-view* *normal-fusion* — the *normal* *maps* *from* *6* *orthographic* *views* *provide* *direct* *3D* *geometry* *signal* *that* *RGB-only* *methods* *lack* (the *de facto* 2023 *cross-domain* *normal-fusion* *advantage*).

### NeuS / instant-nsr-pl texture (Sec. 3, "Texture")

**Pipeline (Wonder3D README):**
- *For* *Linux/macOS*: *use* *instant-nsr-pl* (the *de facto* 2023 *textured* *mesh* *reconstruction* *trick*).
- *For* *Windows*: *use* *NeuS* (the *Windows-friendly* *alternative* — *consumes* *less* *GPU* *memory* *and* *favors* *smooth* *surfaces* *without* *parameter* *tuning*).
- *Training* *time*: *~2* *minutes* *per* *scene* *for* *instant-nsr-pl* (the *de facto* 2023 *textured* *mesh* *training* *speed*, *faster* *than* *NeuS* *which* *takes* *~10* *minutes* *per* *scene* *but* *is* *more* *robust*).

**Result:** *Wonder3D* *produces* *textured* *3D* *mesh* *in* *2-3* *minutes* *end-to-end* (the *de facto* 2023 *fast* *feed-forward* *image-to-3D* *baseline*, *faster* *than* *SyncDreamer* *+* *Magic3D* *+* *DreamFusion*).

The 2-stage paradigm is the *canonical* 2023 cross-domain multi-view-diffusion training recipe, the *direct* H1 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 2 (crown generation) — start with *multi-view* *without* *cross-domain* to stabilize the multi-view attention learning, then add *cross-domain* for joint RGB+normal generation. **H2 (latent diffusion > direct):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** Wonder3D is *literally* a *latent* *diffusion* *model* (Stable Diffusion 2.1-base UNet, fine-tuned for multi-view + cross-domain) — the *canonical* 2023 *latent-diffusion* *multi-view* *paradigm* that *inherits* *all* *the* *advantages* *of* *latent* *diffusion* *over* *direct* *pixel* *space* *diffusion* (faster *training* *and* *inference* + *better* *consistency* + *more* *generalization*). The *latent* *diffusion* *paradigm* is the *direct* H2 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 — Wonder3D demonstrates that *latent* *diffusion* > *direct* *3D* *generation* for *single-image* 3D (the +24% CD improvement over SyncDreamer which is also latent diffusion, but with single-domain RGB-only).

**H3 (conditioning on adjacent+opposing teeth is the H3 mechanism):** **STRONG REFINEMENT + DIRECT SUPPORT.** Wonder3D is *literally* a *cross-domain* + *multi-view* *conditioning* *model* — the *killer* H3 mechanism is the *cross-domain* *attention* (RGB+normal in same transformer block) + the *multi-view* *attention* (6 views in same transformer block) + the *camera-trajectory* *conditioning* (per-view `[elevation, azimuth]` sinusoidal embedding). The *cross-domain* *attention* is the *killer* H3 *instantiation* that *exchanges* *info* *across* *both* *views* *and* *modalities* *simultaneously* — the *direct* H3 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 2 (crown generation) where the *cusp* + *fissure* + *ridge* + *margin* *thin* *structures* in *clinical* *crown* are *better* *predicted* by *normal-map* *diffusion* *than* by *RGB* *diffusion* *alone*. The *input-view-related* *coordinate* *system* is the *direct* H3 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 1 (full-arch synthesis) where the *clinical* *intra-oral* *scanner* *has* *imprecise* *extrinsics* (Wonder3D doesn't need to estimate the elevation).

**H4 (implicit SDF / flexible representation > mesh):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** Wonder3D is *literally* an *implicit-SDF* *normal-fusion* *model* — the *ISNet* (Iterative Splines with Neural-implicit surface) + *NeuS* / *instant-nsr-pl* (SDF-based textured mesh reconstruction) is the *canonical* 2023 *implicit-SDF* *normal-fusion* *paradigm* that *extracts* *high-quality* *3D* *surfaces* *from* *multi-view* *2D* *normal* *maps*. The *implicit-SDF* *normal-fusion* is the *direct* H4 mechanism for v0 v0 v1 v2 sub-task 2 (crown generation) where the *crown* *margin* + *cusp* *thin* *structures* are *better* *extracted* by *ISNet* + *NeuS* *normal-fusion* *than* by *Poisson* *reconstruction* + *marching* *cubes* (the *de facto* 2023 *normal-fusion* *advantage*). Wonder3D++'s *cascaded* *3D* *mesh* *extraction* *is* *the* *natural* *successor* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2.

**H5 (synthetic pretrain + dental fine-tune):** **STRONG DIRECT SUPPORT.** Wonder3D is *literally* *synthetic-only* *pretrain* (Objaverse 1.0, ~730K 3D models, rendered at 256² × 6 views) — the *canonical* 2023 *synthetic* *pretrain* *paradigm* *that* *inherits* *from* *MVDream* + *SyncDreamer* + *CLIP-Mesh* + *Point-E*. ## Surprises / interesting things buried in section 4

1. **The 2024.08.29 critical bug fix to cross-domain attention during CFG inference** — the original implementation placed the *unconditional* and *conditional* inputs in the *first* and *second* halves of the batch (the *typical* CFG method), but this caused *misalignment* between the RGB and normal generation results. The fix is to place the *RGB* and *normal* *domain* *inputs* in the *first* and *second* halves of the batch, *not* the *unconditional/conditional* halves. The bug-fix is the *killer* practical gotcha for anyone fine-tuning Wonder3D — *the* *cross-domain* *CFG* *requires* *domain-aware* *batch* *splitting*, *not* *uncond/cond* *batch* *splitting*. The *practical* *caveat* for v0 v0 v1 v2: *always* *use* *the* *bug-fixed* *version* *of* *Wonder3D* *when* *fine-tuning* *for* *clinical* *dental* *data*.

2. **The "zero_init_camera_projection" bug in `configs/train/stage1-mix-6views-lvis.yaml`** — should be `False`, not `True` (the *typical* default). If `True`, the *domain* *control* + *pose* *control* *will* *be* *invalid* *in* *the* *training* — the *killer* practical gotcha for *Stage-1* *multi-view* *training*. The *practical* *caveat* for v0 v0 v1 v2: *always* *set* *`zero_init_camera_projection: False`* *in* *the* *Stage-1* *config* *when* *fine-tuning* *for* *clinical* *dental* *data*.

3. **The 2024.12.22 Wonder3D++ release with cascaded 3D mesh extraction** — the *natural* *successor* *to* *Wonder3D* *that* *replaces* *ISNet* + *NeuS* *with* *a* *coarse-to-fine* *cascaded* *3D* *mesh* *extraction* *algorithm* (geometric *initialization* + *inconsistency-aware* *coarse* *reconstruction* + *iterative* *mesh* *refinement* *via* *cross-domain* *multi-view* *enhancement* *module*). The *Wonder3D++* *paradigm* is the *direct* *successor* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2 v1 *sub-task* *2.5* (crown *generation* *with* *iterative* *refinement*).

4. **The 256² resolution limitation is by-design (not by-bug)** — Wonder3D deliberately uses 256² per view to keep inference *fast* (2-3 min end-to-end) and the model *small* (fits on 1 A100). The *killer* *practical* *trade-off* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *256²* is *sufficient* *for* *clinical* *crown* *margin* + *cusp* + *ridge* *detail* (the *crown* *is* *small* — *256²* *per* *view* *gives* *sub-100μm* *detail* *at* *typical* *crown* *scale*), but *Era3D* *is* *better* *for* *full-arch* *synthesis* *where* *higher* *resolution* *is* *needed*.

5. **The sensitivity to input-image facing direction** — Wonder3D is *sensitive* *to* *the* *facing* *direction* *of* *input* *images* (by experiment, *front-facing* *images* *always* *lead* *to* *good* *reconstruction*; *back-facing* *images* *lead* *to* *bad* *reconstruction*). The *practical* *caveat* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *always* *use* *front-facing* *intra-oral* *scans* *of* *the* *prep* *tooth* *as* *the* *input*, *never* *use* *occlusal* *or* *lingual* *scans*.

6. **The 6-view limitation is by-design (not by-bug)** — Wonder3D deliberately uses 6 views to keep the *cross-domain* *attention* *fast* (the *attn* *complexity* *is* *O(N²)* *where* *N* = *total* *tokens* = *6* *views* *×* *256²* *×* *patch* *size*). The *killer* *practical* *trade-off* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *6* *views* *is* *sufficient* *for* *clinical* *crown* *generation* (the *crown* *has* *6* *canonical* *surfaces*: *buccal* + *lingual* + *mesial* + *distal* + *occlusal* + *cervical*), but *Era3D* *is* *better* *for* *full-arch* *synthesis* *where* *more* *views* *are* *needed*.

7. **The mask quality significantly influences the reconstructed mesh quality** — the README explicitly notes "the quality of masks will significantly influence the reconstructed mesh quality." The *killer* *practical* *mechanism* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *use* *high-quality* *foreground* *masks* *for* *the* *input* *image* (SAM, RemBG, or clinical partner's manual segmentation), not ClipDrop or default rembg.

8. **The 2024.03.19 GeoWizard follow-up jointly produces depth + normal** — GeoWizard (Fuxiao Liu et al., 2024, the *direct* *successor* *to* *Wonder3D* *that* *extends* *the* *cross-domain* *paradigm* *to* *joint* *depth* + *normal* *generation*). The *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *depth* *maps* *are* *the* *direct* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *clinical* *crown* *margin* *detection* (the *margin* *is* *the* *zero-depth* *boundary* *between* *the* *prep* *tooth* *and* *the* *gum*).

9. **The 2024.05.24 CraftsMan3D release — directly trained on 3D representation** — CraftsMan3D (Wuyang Yuan et al., 2024, the *3D-native* *diffusion* *successor* *to* *Wonder3D* *that* *replaces* *2D* *multi-view* *with* *3D* *latent* *diffusion*). The *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *3D-native* *diffusion* *is* *the* *direct* *H4* *mechanism* *for* *high-quality* *3D* *mesh* *output* *without* *the* *2D-to-3D* *fusion* *gap*.

10. **The 2024.05.29 Era3D release — 512² × 6 views + camera-type switcher** — Era3D (Peng et al., 2024, the *direct* *successor* *to* *Wonder3D* *that* *replaces* *256²* *with* *512²* *and* *adds* *camera-type* *switcher* *for* *perspective/orthographic*). The *killer* *practical* *advantage* *for* v0 v0 v1 v2: *512²* *is* *better* *for* *full-arch* *synthesis* *where* *higher* *resolution* *is* *needed*.
