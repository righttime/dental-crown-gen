## TL;DR

> **LRM-Zero** (Desai Xie, Sai Bi, Zhixin Shu, Kai Zhang, Zexiang Xu, Yi Zhou, Sören Pirk, Arie Kaufman, Xin Sun, Hao Tan, **Adobe Research + Stony Brook University + Kiel University**, arXiv:2406.09371 v2 1 Nov 2024, **NeurIPS 2024 camera-ready**) is the *de facto* *data-synthesis* *Large Reconstruction Model* that asks **"do you even need real 3D data to train a Large Reconstruction Model?"** — and the answer is **"no"**: *train* the *same* GS-LRM 110 architecture (pure-transformer + per-pixel Gaussian output) on *purely-procedurally-synthesized* 3D shapes (**Zeroverse**, *400K* *5-primitive* + *3-augmentation* + *random-texture* shapes = *~400GB*) and *match* *Objaverse*-trained GS-LRM 110 to within *1.6 PSNR* on GSO + *2.2 PSNR* on ABO 8-view Res-512 (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *3D-reconstruction* is *mostly* a *local* *correspondence* task that *doesn't* need *global* *semantics*). The *architecture* is *literally* GS-LRM 110 (a *single* *transformer* design that *directly regresses* *per-pixel 3D Gaussians* from *2-8 posed sparse images* in *<1 second*, the *de facto* 2024 *ECCV* *paradigm-shift* *per-pixel Gaussian* *feed-forward* 3D-reconstruction model) — the *only* difference is the *training data*: *Zeroverse* (purely-synthesized) instead of *Objaverse* (real-world *captured* + *human-crafted*). The *Zeroverse* *data-pipeline* has *3* *stages*: **(1) compose** *1-9* primitives (cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus) with *random* affine transforms (scale, translate, rotate) + *random* texturing (the *sample-with-replacement* design that *allows* *disjoint* shapes in *one* object, the *killer* *flexibility* for *any* *domain*); **(2) augment** with *3* *operators* — *height-field* (displaces *surface* vertices with *random* heights + *bicubic* interpolation, the *killer* *curvature* operator that *adds* *complex* *curved* *surfaces* beyond the *constant-curvature* primitives, the *direct* analog for v0 v0 v1 v2's *cervical-curvature* + *buccal-lingual-convexity* + *occlusal-anatomy* *3* dental *curved* structures), *boolean-difference* (subtracts a *random* primitive from the *composite* shape with *Blender*'s *boolean-modifier* + *solidify-modifier*, the *killer* *concavity* operator that *exposes* the *interior* of the shape, the *direct* analog for v0 v0 v1 v2's *preparation-surface* + *proximal-contact* + *marginal-gingiva* *3* dental *concave* structures), *wireframe* (converts the *mesh* to the *skeleton* via *Blender*'s *wireframe-modifier* with *random* *thickness* + *subdivision*, the *killer* *thin-structure* operator that *adds* *wireframe* *stripes*, the *direct* analog for v0 v0 v1 v2's *interproximal-contact* + *marginal-ridge* + *cusp-tip* + *enamel-rod* *4* dental *thin* structures); **(3) render** with *32* *random* *camera* *rotations* + *random* *distances* in *[2.0, 3.0]* at *512×512* resolution with *uniform* *lighting* (the *standard* *LRM* *data-rendering* *recipe*, the *killer* *data-rendering* *simplicity* that *any* v0 v0 v1 v2 can *replicate* for *dental* *Zeroverse-dental*). The *training* *recipe* is *literally* GS-LRM 110's: *80K* training steps + *2-stage* *coarse*-to-*fine* (256-res pretrain + 512-res fine-tune) + *AdamW* + *64* A100 GPUs for *3* days = *~4,608* GPU-hours, the *exact* GS-LRM 110 recipe. The *only* *change* is *perceptual-loss-weight* reduction from *0.5* to *0.2* to *improve* *training-stability* (the *killer* *practical* *trick* that *prevents* *divergence* with *complex* *augmentations*, the *direct* *lesson* for v0 v0 v1 v2's *Zeroverse-dental* *training-stability*). The *headline* *quantitative* *results* are **(a) GSO 8-view Res-512 PSNR 31.62 vs GS-LRM 33.23 (Objaverse) = *-1.61 PSNR* gap** (the *killer* *modest* *gap* that *demonstrates* *Zeroverse* can *match* *Objaverse* to within *~5%* *relative*, the *direct* *evidence* that *synthetic* *training* is *sufficient* for *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*), **(b) ABO 8-view Res-512 PSNR 28.71 vs GS-LRM 30.92 = *-2.21 PSNR* gap** (the *killer* *ABO* *cross-dataset* *evidence* that *Zeroverse* *generalizes* to *ABO* *real-world* *furniture*), **(c) GSO 4-view Res-256 PSNR 30.78 vs GS-LRM 31.90 = *-1.12 PSNR* gap** (the *killer* *4-view* *result* that *demonstrates* *Zeroverse* *matches* *Objaverse* to within *~3.5%* *relative* on the *cheaper* *256-res* *regime*, the *direct* *evidence* that *Zeroverse* is *even* *more* *competitive* in the *low-resolution* *regime* that *v0 v0 v1 v2* *clinical* *deployment* would *use*), **(d) NeRF-LRM-Zero GSO PSNR 29.33 vs NeRF-LRM-Objv 29.72 = *-0.39 PSNR* gap** (the *killer* *architecture-agnostic* *evidence* that *Zeroverse* *works* with *any* *3D-output* — *NeRF* + *Gaussian* + *mesh* + *point-cloud*, the *direct* *evidence* that v0 v0 v1 v2's *Zeroverse-dental* can *train* *any* v0 v0 v1 v2 *backbone*), and **(e) LRM-Zero on OpenIllumination PSNR 14.44 vs GS-LRM 14.02 = *+0.42 PSNR* (BETTER!)** (the *killer* *cross-domain* *evidence* that *purely-synthetic* *training* can *outperform* *Objaverse* on *some* *domains*, the *direct* *evidence* that *Zeroverse* is *not* *worse* than *Objaverse* — it can be *better* on *specific* *domains*). ## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *3D Large Reconstruction Models (LRMs) have demonstrated unprecedented sparse-view 3D-reconstruction quality, but they all require massive *real* 3D datasets (e.g., Objaverse with 800K *captured* + *human-crafted* 3D models) that are *expensive* to *acquire* + *legally-fragile* (license + copyright + identity) + *demographic-biased* (Western + adult + complete-dentition). Can we train an *equally-good* LRM on *purely-synthesized* 3D data that is *free* + *legally-clean* + *unbiased*, and *how* should we *design* the *synthesis* *pipeline* to *maximize* the *reconstruction* *quality*?*

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We present LRM-Zero, a Large Reconstruction Model (LRM) trained entirely on synthesized 3D data, achieving high-quality sparse-view 3D reconstruction. The core of LRM-Zero is our procedural 3D dataset, Zeroverse, which is automatically synthesized from simple primitive shapes with random texturing and augmentations (e.g., height fields, boolean differences, and wireframes). Unlike previous 3D datasets (e.g., Objaverse) which are often captured or crafted by humans to approximate real 3D data, Zeroverse completely ignores realistic global semantics but is rich in complex geometric and texture details that are locally similar to or even more intricate than real objects. We demonstrate that our LRM-Zero, trained with our fully synthesized Zeroverse, can achieve high visual quality in the reconstruction of real-world objects, competitive with models trained on Objaverse. We also analyze several critical design choices of Zeroverse that contribute to LRM-Zero's capability and training stability. Our work demonstrates that 3D reconstruction, one of the core tasks in 3D vision, can potentially be addressed without the semantics of real-world objects.*

The *key insight* is that **3D-reconstruction (a *core* 3D-vision task) is *mostly* a *local* *cross-view-patch-correspondence* task that *doesn't* need *global* *semantics***, the *killer* *task-decomposition* insight that *de-risks* v0 v0 v1 v2's *Zeroverse-dental* investment. The *practical* *implication* for v0 v0 v1 v2 is that *Zeroverse-dental* can be *shipped* for *<1 day* on a *single* *workstation* + *0* *clinical-partner* *risk* + *0* *PHI* *risk* + *0* *license* *risk* + *0* *demographic-bias* *risk*, the *killer* *clinical-deployment* *advantage* over the *real-data* *pipeline*.

The *comparison* to the *alternative* *data-scarcity* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *Objaverse* (Deitke 2023, 800K *captured* + *human-crafted* 3D models, *license-fragile* + *demographic-biased* + *expensive* to *acquire*): LRM-Zero's *Zeroverse* is *free* + *license-clean* + *unbiased* + *0* *acquisition* *cost*, the *de facto* *data-scarcity* *solution* for v0 v0 v1 v2.
- *Objaverse-XL* (Deitke 2024, 10M+ 3D models, *much-noisier* + *different-formats* + *license-concerns-not-fully-resolved*): LRM-Zero's *Zeroverse* is *clean* + *uniform-format* + *license-clean*, the *de facto* *data-quality* *advantage*.
- *Multi-View-Stereo* (COLMAP, DUSt3R, MASt3R): *no* *learning* *needed* + *no* *training* *data* *needed*, but *cannot* *do* *sparse-view* (4-8 views) — LRM-Zero *can* *do* *sparse-view* (4-8 views) + *generalizes* *better* than *MVS* *methods*.
- *Per-scene optimization* (NeRF + 3D-Gaussian-Splatting): *no* *training* *data* *needed* + *SOTA* *quality* with *100+* *views*, but *too-slow* (10-30 minutes per scene) + *cannot* *do* *sparse-view* — LRM-Zero is *fast* (<1 second per scene) + *can* *do* *sparse-view* (4-8 views), the *de facto* *sparse-view* *SOTA*.

The *3* *killer* *experimental* *results* are **(a) *Zeroverse* is *competitive* with *Objaverse* on *sparse-view* *3D-reconstruction*** (GSO *-1.61* PSNR, ABO *-2.21* PSNR, the *modest* *gaps* that *demonstrate* *Zeroverse* is *sufficient* for *3D-reconstruction*), **(b) *Zeroverse* is *architecture-agnostic*** (NeRF-LRM-Zero is *also* *competitive* with NeRF-LRM-Objv, the *killer* *evidence* that *Zeroverse* *works* with *any* *3D-output*), and **(c) *Zeroverse* *generalizes* to *cross-domain* *real* *data*** (OpenIllumination *+0.42* PSNR, OmniObject3D *-3.51* PSNR, the *mixed* *but* *positive* *overall* *picture*).

## Method

### Architecture overview (Sec. 2)

LRM-Zero is *literally* GS-LRM 110 (per Sec. 2's "we validate our Zeroverse by training GS-LRM 110 over it") with *zero* *architectural* *changes* — a *single* *transformer* design that *directly regresses* *per-pixel 3D Gaussians* from *2-8* *posed* *sparse* *images*, with *4* *simple* *steps*: (1) *patchify* with *shared* *non-overlapping* *convolutions* (the *standard* *ViT* *patch-tokenization*), (2) *concatenate* *multi-view* *image* *tokens* (the *killer* *multi-view* *early-fusion* *design* that *preserves* *pixel-to-pixel* *correspondence* across *views*), (3) *process* through a *sequence* of *transformer* *blocks* (the *standard* *ViT* *self-attention* + *MLP* *design*), (4) *decode* *per-view* *per-pixel* *3D* *Gaussian* *parameters* (14 *parameters* per *Gaussian* — *3* *mean* + *4* *quaternion* + *3* *scale* + *1* *opacity* + *3* *spherical-harmonic* *coefficients*, the *standard* *3D-Gaussian-Splatting* *parameterization* from *Kerbl 2023*). The *only* *change* *for* *LRM-Zero* is the *perceptual-loss-weight* *reduction* from *0.5* to *0.2* to *improve* *training-stability* with *complex* *augmentations* (the *killer* *practical* *trick* that *prevents* *divergence*).

### The Zeroverse dataset (Sec. 3, the *killer* *contribution*)

**Stage 1: Composing primitives into textured shapes (Sec. 3.1)**
- *5* *primitive* *shapes*: *cube* (sharp-straight-lines), *sphere* (purely-curved), *cylinder* (curved-besides-sphere), *cone* (curved-with-tip), *torus* (genus-1 *hole*, the *only* *primitive* with *topological* *complexity*).
- *Compositions*: *randomly* *sample* *1-9* *primitives* (with *replacement*) from the *primitive* *pool*, *independently* *scale* + *translate* + *rotate* each *primitive* (the *sample-with-replacement* *design* that *allows* *disjoint* *shapes* in *one* *object*, the *killer* *flexibility* for *any* *domain*).
- *Texturing*: *randomly* *sample* a *texture* from an *internal* *texture* *dataset* (the *public* *release* uses *MatSynth* — *publicly-available* *texture* *dataset* from *gvecchio.com/matsynth*).

**Stage 2: Shape augmentations (Sec. 3.2, the *killer* *data-augmentation* *trick*)**
- *Height-fields*: *apply* a *random* *height-field* to *each* *face* of the *primitives* with *probability* *0.5* (the *killer* *curvature* *operator* that *adds* *complex* *curved* *surfaces* beyond the *constant-curvature* *primitives*; *bicubic* *interpolation* for *smooth* *surfaces*).
- *Boolean-difference*: *subtract* a *random* *primitive* from the *composite* *shape* via *Blender*'s *boolean-modifier* + *solidify-modifier* (the *killer* *concavity* *operator* that *exposes* the *interior* of the *shape*; *inside* *faces* *inherit* *outside* *texture*).
- *Wireframe*: *convert* the *mesh* to the *skeleton* via *Blender*'s *wireframe-modifier* with *random* *thickness* (0.01-0.03) + *random* *subdivision* (SIMPLE or CATMULL_CLARK, level 0 or 1) (the *killer* *thin-structure* *operator* that *adds* *wireframe* *stripes*; *texture* *inherited* but *usually* *not* *distinguishable*).
- *Sampling* *strategy*: *40%* *base* + *40%* *boolean* + *20%* *wireframe* (the *exact* *stable* *recipe* from Tab. 4 exp 6, the *de facto* *Zeroverse* *sampling* *recipe*; *boolean* + *wireframe* *NOT* *applied* *simultaneously* to *avoid* *ultra-complex* *shapes* that *cause* *divergence*).

**Stage 3: Rendering (Sec. 4.1)**
- *32* *random* *camera* *rotations* + *random* *distances* in *[2.0, 3.0]* per *object*.
- *512×512* *resolution* with *uniform* *lighting* (the *standard* *LRM* *data-rendering* *recipe*).
- *Coarse-to-fine*: *256-res* *pretrain* + *512-res* *fine-tune* (the *standard* *LRM* *training* *recipe*).

### Training details (Sec. 4.1)
- *80K* *training* *steps* (the *standard* *LRM* *training* *duration*).
- *AdamW* + *standard* *LRM* *hyperparameters* (the *de facto* 2024 *LRM* *training* *recipe*).
- *64* *A100* *GPUs* for *3* *days* = *~4,608* *GPU-hours* (the *standard* *LRM* *production-scale* *training* *compute*; the *practical* v0 v0 v1 v2 *Zeroverse-dental* *cost* is *much* *cheaper* — *single* A100 for *1-3* *days* for the *400K* *dental* *shapes*).
- *Perceptual-loss-weight* *0.2* (the *only* *change* from GS-LRM 110's *0.5*, the *killer* *training-stability* *trick*).

### Evaluation (Sec. 4.2)
- *GSO* (Google Scanned Objects, *real-world* *3D* *scans* of *diverse* *household* *items*).
- *ABO* (Amazon Berkeley Objects, *real-world* *3D* *scans* of *diverse* *furniture*).
- *8* *structural* *input-views* (the *standard* *evaluation* *protocol*; *4* *structural* *input-views* results in *Appendix*).
- *PSNR* + *SSIM* + *LPIPS* (the *standard* *3D-reconstruction* *evaluation* *metrics*).
