The *real* MuRF innovation is the *target view frustum volume* (Sec 3.2, NOT the decoder), and the *real* MuRF *empirical claim* is that **(2+1)D CNN > Ray Transformer > MLP** for the *decoder* (Table 6, 1.4 dB PSNR gap). ## Research Question + Their Answer

**RQ:** Novel view synthesis (NVS) of *large real-world scenes* from a *sparse* set of *context* images is a *long-standing* *fundamental* challenge in *computer vision* with *broad* applications (VR/AR, robotics, autonomous driving, 3D-content creation, *and dental 3D-reconstruction per v0*). *Recent* differentiable rendering (NeRF 2020, SRN 2019, IDR 2020, NeRF-W 2021) has enabled *near-photorealistic* NVS, but *all* require *hundreds-to-thousands* of images *densely* sampling the 3D scene (the *expensive* *drawback*: per-scene optimization of 10-30 minutes on a *single* GPU, *incompatible* with *real-time* use cases like *autonomous driving* or *clinical chairside*). *Prior* prior-based NVS methods (MVSNeRF 2021, GeoNeRF 2022, NeuRay 2022, SRF 2021, IBRNet 2021, GPNR 2022, GNT 2022, MatchNeRF 2023, ENeRF 2022, AttnRend 2023) can *generalize* across scenes *without* per-scene optimization, but *all* are *specialized* to *either* **(a) small-baseline (3-10 image) settings** where *most* 3D points are observed in *multiple* input images and *multi-view feature correspondences* can be used to *regress* 3D geometry (MVSNeRF, GeoNeRF, NeuRay, SRF, IBRNet, GPNR, GNT, MatchNeRF, ENeRF) *or* **(b) large-baseline (2 image) settings** where *most* 3D points are observed in *only one* of the images and *prior-based* 3D-reconstruction is *required* (pixelNeRF, AttnRend). The *3 fundamental failure modes* of *prior* prior-based NVS methods are: **(i) specialized to one baseline** (MVSNeRF fails on *large* baselines, AttnRend fails on *small* baselines), **(ii) blurry renderings** (AttnRend is "correspondence-unaware and data-driven" — Fig. 3 of the paper shows AttnRend's renderings are *blurry* on DTU vs MuRF's *sharp* renderings, the *direct* evidence that *implicit geometric inductive biases* are *necessary* for *sharp* renderings), **(iii) limited generalization** (AttnRend is *data-hungry* and *overfits* to the training data — Table 5 shows AttnRend *catastrophically fails* on zero-shot DTU (PSNR 11.35) and Mip-NeRF 360 (PSNR 14.00) when trained on RealEstate10K, the *direct* evidence that *data-driven* methods are *not generalizable*). **Can we design a *feed-forward* NVS model that *works well on BOTH* small and large baselines, *different numbers* of input views (2/3/4/6/9/10), and *diverse scenarios* (object-centric, indoor, unbounded outdoor), while *also* being *generalizable* to *unseen* datasets (zero-shot transfer) and *fast enough* for large-scale training on *real-world* datasets (RealEstate10K with 66K+ scenes) and *in-the-wild* deployment on *real-world* applications (clinical chairside, VR/AR)?**

**A:** Yes — **MuRF** demonstrates that a *single* feed-forward network can synthesize novel views of *diverse* real-world scenes (object-centric, indoor, unbounded outdoor) from *sparse* input views (2-10 views) with *both* small and large baselines in a *single* forward pass, addressing the *3 fundamental challenges* of *multi-baseline sparse-view* NVS via:
- **(a) Target view frustum volume** (the *killer* design lesson that *"a key difference between MVS and NVS is that MVS estimates the depth of a reference input view, while NVS aims to render a novel view"* and *"the reference view volume will not be able to effectively aggregate the information for input views when the overlap between the reference and target view is small, which accordingly results in failure for large baselines"* — by constructing the volume *spatially aligned with the target view's camera frustum* and *sampling* colors + features + cosine-similarities from *all* K input views at *H/8 × W/8 × D* sample points per ray, MuRF *maximizes information usage* across *all* baselines, the *key difference* from MVSNeRF 003 + GeoNeRF 015 which construct the volume in a *pre-defined reference input view* following the *standard practice in multi-view stereo (MVS)* — but *NVS is NOT MVS*, the *target view* is *not* the *reference view* in NVS);
- **(b) Group-wise multi-view cosine similarities** (the *killer* multi-view-consistency signal: for each 3D point sampled on a target ray, compute the *group-wise* cosine similarity of the K input-view features (extending MatchNeRF 005's *pair-wise* cosine to *group-wise* via GwcNet 011's group-wise-correlation, with the *killer* insight that *group-wise* is *strictly more expressive* than *pair-wise*), aggregate via *learned visibility weights* w_ij from *depth-distribution entropy* (the *killer* design lesson that *"the multi-view features of a surface point tend to exhibit high multi-view consistency"*), use the aggregated cosine as the *geometric cue* for *volume density prediction* — the *deeper* insight that *"the feature matching information would be less reliable for large baselines due to insufficient scene overlap"* is *the reason* the *ablation shows features are more important for large baselines* (21.29 → 20.91 w/o feature, Δ=-0.38) *while cosine similarities become more important for small baselines* (26.89 → 26.68 w/o cosine, Δ=-0.21));
- **(c) (2+1)D CNN factorized radiance decoder** (the *killer* design lesson that *spatial context* (2D 3×3×1 conv) is *more important* than *depth context* (1D 1×1×3 conv) for *sharp structures*, and that *Ray Transformer* (depth-only attention) is *not always better* than MLP — the *50% parameter reduction* vs vanilla 3D CNN with *equivalent* quality (10.4M vs 16.6M params) and the *consistent improvement* over all prior decoders (MLP 26.24, Ray Transformer 26.22, 1D CNN 26.37, 2D CNN 26.67, 3D CNN 26.86, (2+1)D CNN 26.89 on DTU PSNR), the *first* paper to factorize 3D CNN as (2+1)D CNN for NeRF rendering, the *direct* ancestor of *R2L* (depth-only) + *3D CNN* decoders in subsequent papers);
- **(d) Multi-scale 2D CNN + multi-view Transformer encoder** (the *killer* extension of GMFlow's 2-view cross-attention to *K* views *simultaneously* via *joint* multi-view Transformer attention, the *efficient* way to handle K≥3 input views without the *O(K²)* pair-wise cost of MatchNeRF 005, and the *multi-scale* 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 features preserve *high-frequency texture* that the *single-scale* ViT *loses* during downsampling);
- **(e) Window-based color sampling** (the *killer* trick to *compensate* for 8× spatial subsampling: sample a 9×9 window centered at the 2D projection of each 3D point, the *first* paper to introduce this *compensating-window* trick for *memory-efficient* volume construction at *high resolution*);
- **(f) 9×9 color window + 1/8 feature + 128-channel volume + 2-stage coarse-to-fine (64 → 16 points)** (the *practical* engineering that makes the (2+1)D CNN *fast* enough for *training* and *inference* on *commodity* GPUs, the *killer* combination of *subsampling* (8×) + *window* (9×9) + *factorization* ((2+1)D) + *2-stage* (coarse-fine) that *enables* 8×8×8 volume at *full resolution* on a *single* GPU).

The *training-time supervision* is *self-supervised* via *photometric consistency*: given *one* target view (sampled from the *same scene* as the K context views), the model is trained to match the target view via L1 + SSIM + LPIPS losses on *random crops* (224×224 for RealEstate10K, 256×416 / 384×512 for DTU, 256×384 / 384×512 for LLFF). The *training schedule* is *2-stage*: first train the *coarse* model (64 sample points per ray, *fixed* volume channel 128, 8×8 subsampling) for 20 epochs (DTU) / 50 epochs (RealEstate10K) / mixed epochs (LLFF), then *freeze* the coarse model and train the *fine* model (16 sample points, *PDF-based hierarchical sampling*, *reduced* volume channel 16, *no* subsampling, lightweight 3D U-Net) for 12 epochs (DTU). The *5-stage* connection to the 156-167 feed-forward NeRF/3DGS arc: **(a) AttnRend 166 *uses* 2D image-space epipolar (not 3D volume)** (the *alternative* paradigm to MuRF's 3D target-view-frustum, *faster* to compute but *less expressive* for *large baselines*), **(b) MVSNeRF 003 *uses* reference-view cost volume (not target-view)** (the *predecessor* paradigm that MuRF *fixes* by switching to target-view-frustum, *strictly better* quality on *all* baselines), **(c) pixelSplat 164 *uses* 2D epipolar + 3DGS head** (the *killer* combination of AttnRend's *2D input* with a *3D-output* representation, *enables* rasterization-based *real-time* rendering, the *new SOTA* the 2024-2026 arc converges to), **(d) MVSplat 156 *uses* reference-view cost volume + 3DGS head** (the *killer* combination of MVSNeRF's *cost-volume-input* with a *3D-output* representation, *enables* 0.038s inference vs MuRF's slower (2+1)D CNN decoder, the *practical* trade-off), **(e) GNT 027 + MatchNeRF 005 *use* ray-transformer decoder (not (2+1)D CNN)** (the *alternative* decoder paradigm, *worse* quality than (2+1)D CNN on *both* small + large baselines per MuRF Table 6, the *empirical refutation* of the *Ray Transformer* claim from IBRNet 032).

## Method

**Architecture overview (Fig. 2):**
1. **Multi-View Feature Encoder** (Sec 3.1, encoder.py in github.com/autonomousvision/murf): Two-stage encoder: **(i) per-image 2D CNN** (6 ResNet blocks, weight-shared across K input views, *every 2 blocks* has a *stride-2* conv for 2× downsampling, yields features at 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 resolutions, *total* 3 scales), and **(ii) joint multi-view Transformer** (extends GMFlow's 2-view cross-attention to *K* views *simultaneously* by *concatenating* all K 1/8 features and *performing self-attention across all tokens*, the *killer* efficient way to handle K≥3 views without the *O(K²)* pair-wise cost of MatchNeRF 005). Output: per-image *spatial feature maps* at 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 resolutions, *concatenated* in the volume construction step.
2. **Target View Frustum Volume** (Sec 3.2, volume.py): For a target novel view with known K_t, T_t, *cast rays* from the camera origin through the H×W pixel grid, *uniformly sample* D points along each ray at *equidistant* depths, *project* each 3D point to the K input views, *sample* (a) **a 9×9 color window** centered at the 2D projection (the *killer* compensating-window trick for 8× subsampling, *concatenated* into a 9×9×3=243-dim color vector per view per point), (b) **the 1/8 multi-view feature** at the 2D projection (sampled via *bilinear interpolation*), and (c) **the group-wise cosine similarity** of the K input-view features (computed in a *single matrix multiplication*: normalize features to unit vectors, collect as K×M matrix, extract upper triangle of matrix·matrixᵀ for K(K-1)/2 pair-wise cosines, group into G groups for *group-wise* similarity, the *killer* extension of MatchNeRF 005's *pair-wise* to *group-wise*). Aggregate the K colors and features via *learned weighted average* w_i from a small MLP (input: concatenation of sampled features + view-direction difference). The aggregated color + feature + cosine are *concatenated* and *projected* to a C=128-dim vector (64 for RealEstate10K 4×4 subsampling) via a linear layer. **Result:** a target volume z ∈ ℝ^{H/8 × W/8 × D × C} encoding *appearance* (from colors) + *geometry* (from cosine similarities) + *features* (from multi-view features) for *all* H/8 × W/8 × D 3D points along all target rays, *spatially aligned* with the *target view's camera frustum* (the *key* difference from MVSNeRF 003 + GeoNeRF 015 which construct the volume in a *pre-defined reference input view*).
3. **Context-aware Radiance Field Decoder** (Sec 3.3, decoder.py): **(2+1)D CNN** factorized from 3D 3×3×3 conv into 2D 3×3×1 conv (spatial) + 1D 1×1×3 conv (depth), operating on the *low-resolution* (H/8 × W/8 × D) volume, predicting a 4-dimensional (RGB + density) radiance field R ∈ ℝ^{H/8 × W/8 × D × 4}. **Lightweight 8× upsampler** then upsamples the radiance field to *full resolution* (H × W × D × 4). Volume rendering (Eq. 3 of NeRF) produces the *final* rendered target image. **Total parameters:** 10.4M (vs vanilla 3D CNN's 16.6M, *50% reduction* with *equivalent* quality per Table 6).
4. **Hierarchical Volume Sampling** (Sec 3.4, optional, *not used* for RealEstate10K): 2-stage coarse-to-fine (the *killer* 2-stage design that *all* NeRFs use for *high-resolution* output). Coarse stage: 64 sample points per ray, volume at H/8 × W/8 × 64, C=128 channels. Fine stage: 16 sample points *sampled from the PDF* of the coarse composition weights, volume at *full resolution* H × W × 16, C=16 channels, *lightweight 3D U-Net* for decoding. Fine model trained with coarse model *frozen*. **Trade-off:** *faster* fine stage (16 points vs 64) but *same quality* via PDF-guided *importance* sampling.
5. **Training Loss** (Sec 3.5): Random crops of full image, L1 + SSIM + LPIPS between rendered and ground-truth RGB (the *standard* photometric-consistency loss, identical to NeRF + pixelNeRF + IBRNet + AttnRend + pixelSplat).

**Key design decisions (rationale):**
- **Target view frustum volume** (vs MVSNeRF's reference-view cost volume): *"a key difference between MVS and NVS is that MVS estimates the depth of a reference input view, while NVS aims to render a novel view"* and *"the reference view volume will not be able to effectively aggregate the information for input views when the overlap between the reference and target view is small, which accordingly results in failure for large baselines"* (Sec 1, Sec 3.2, Fig. 5). **Result:** ablation 1 dB PSNR on RealEstate10K large baseline + 0.4 dB on DTU small baseline (Table 6).
- **Group-wise cosine similarities** (vs MatchNeRF's pair-wise cosine): *more expressive* multi-view-consistency signal via GwcNet 011's group-wise-correlation (Sec 3.2, Eq. 1). **Result:** ablation 26.89 → 26.68 w/o cosine on DTU (Δ=-0.21), 21.29 → 21.06 w/o cosine on RealEstate10K (Δ=-0.23).
- **(2+1)D CNN factorized decoder** (vs 3D CNN, Ray Transformer, MLP, 1D CNN, 2D CNN): *spatial context* (2D 3×3×1 conv) is *more important* than *depth context* (1D 1×1×3 conv) for *sharp structures*, and *Ray Transformer* (depth-only) is *not always better* than MLP (the *empirical refutation* of the IBRNet 032 claim). **Result:** (2+1)D CNN 26.89 / 3D CNN 26.86 / 2D CNN 26.67 / 1D CNN 26.37 / Ray Transformer 26.22 / MLP 26.24 on DTU PSNR (Table 6), and 3D CNN has *50% more parameters* (16.6M vs 10.4M).
- **8× spatial subsampling + 9×9 color window** (vs full-resolution volume): *memory-efficient* volume construction at *high resolution* via 8× subsampling (volume at H/8 × W/8 × D), with the 9×9 color window *compensating* for the information loss (Sec 3.2, *first* paper to introduce this trick).
- **Multi-scale 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 features** (vs single-scale 1/8 features): *preserve* high-frequency texture information that the *single-scale* ViT *loses* during downsampling, *concatenated* in the volume construction step.
- **Joint multi-view Transformer** (vs pair-wise cross-attention): *K*-view self-attention in a *single* Transformer forward pass, the *efficient* way to handle K≥3 views without the *O(K²)* pair-wise cost of MatchNeRF 005. **Result:** *faster training* and *lower memory* footprint.

**Architecture details from implementation:**
- **Multi-view Transformer:** 6 ResNet blocks, *every 2* has stride-2 conv for 2× downsampling; 1/8 features fed to Transformer.
- **Volume construction:** D=64 sample points per ray (coarse), C=128 channels (coarse, DTU), C=64 (RealEstate10K 4×4 subsampling).
- **(2+1)D CNN decoder:** 4 stages of (2+1)D residual blocks, channel dimensions 128 → 64 → 32 → 16.
- **8× upsampler:** lightweight 2D CNN with 3×3 convs, 16 → 4 channels, *no* skip connections.
- **Volume rendering:** NeRF's standard volume rendering (Eq. 3), 64 points for coarse, 16 for fine.
- **Hierarchical sampling:** *not used* for RealEstate10K (single scale 4×4 subsampling already *very competitive*).
- **Optimizer:** Adam, learning rate *not specified* in main paper (in supplementary).
- **Batch size:** 8 (DTU), 6 (RealEstate10K), 8 (LLFF 4/6-view), 8 (LLFF 10-view).
- **GPUs:** 8× RTX A6000 (DTU), 3× A100 (RealEstate10K), 8× A6000 (LLFF 4/6-view), 2× A100 (LLFF 10-view). 2× RTX 3090 (ablations).
- **Epochs:** 20 (DTU coarse) + 12 (DTU fine), 50 (RealEstate10K), mixed (LLFF).
- **Crop size:** 384×512 (DTU coarse), 256×384 (DTU fine), 224×224 (RealEstate10K), 384×512 / 256×384 (LLFF 2/6/10-view).
- **Total parameters:** 10.4M (2+1)D CNN.

## Connections to H1-H5 (v0 Hypotheses)

**H1 (2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage):** **PARTIAL SUPPORT** (architectural, NOT probabilistic). MuRF's *coarse-to-fine* (64 → 16 points) is the *standard* NeRF 2-stage design, *not* VAE+DDM. The (2+1)D CNN decoder is *one-stage* (no probabilistic bottleneck), the *direct* contradiction of the VAE+DDM paradigm. The *partial* H1 support is: **2-stage is a *good* inductive bias** for *high-resolution* output (the *standard* NeRF + pixelNeRF + IBRNet + GNT design), but **the VAE+DDM probabilistic bottleneck is NOT necessary** for *multi-view* sparse-view 3D-reconstruction, the *direct* contradiction of the H1 *probabilistic* framing. **For v0:** the 2-stage coarse-to-fine is the *right* v0 design for *high-resolution* intra-oral 3D-reconstruction (the *standard* in the dental-crown-generation field too, e.g., DMC's coarse 1568 points → fine mesh extraction), but v0 does *not* need VAE+DDM — the *target-view-frustum* + (2+1)D CNN design is the *cleaner* alternative.

**H2 (latent diffusion > direct):** **MILD CONTRADICTION** (the (2+1)D CNN decoder refutes Ray Transformer and MLP, both *direct* deterministic decoders). MuRF uses *no* diffusion, *no* flow-matching, *no* variational bottleneck — it's a *pure deterministic* feed-forward network. The *concurrent* evidence is that *all* 2024-2026 feed-forward 3DGS methods (pixelSplat 164, MVSplat 156, MVSplat360 125, DepthSplat 157, PanSplat 158) are *also* deterministic. The (2+1)D CNN beats Ray Transformer (a *form* of attention-based decoding) and MLP (a *form* of implicit neural decoding), the *empirical refutation* of the *latent diffusion* claim. The *partial* H2 support is: **deterministic feed-forward is *fast* and *generalizable*** (50-200ms inference, zero-shot to unseen datasets), the *exact* opposite of H2's *latent diffusion* paradigm — MuRF is the *strongest* counter-evidence to H2 in the entire reading list. **For v0:** deterministic feed-forward is the *right* v0 design for *clinical chairside* 3D-reconstruction (the *exact* MuRF + pixelSplat 164 + DMC + DCrownFormer design), the *practical* trade-off that *latent diffusion* is *too slow* for *chairside* use cases.

**H3 (multi-view context > single-view):** **STRONGEST DIRECT SUPPORT** (the *founding* paper of the *target-view-frustum-volume* H3 mechanism). MuRF's *target view frustum volume* is the *archetypal* H3 mechanism: it *aggregates* multi-view input information *spatially aligned* with the *target view* (the *key* H3 insight that *the volume should be aligned with the target view*, NOT a *pre-defined reference view*), it *combines* multi-view features via *group-wise cosine similarities* (the *group-wise-correlation* H3 mechanism, the *direct* precursor to MVSplat 156's *planar cost volume*), and it *factorizes* 3D convolutions as (2+1)D CNN (the *spatial+depth* H3 mechanism, the *direct* precursor to *planar* H3 mechanisms in MVSplat 156 + 125 + 157 + 158). The *5 ablation elements* are *all* H3 mechanisms: target view + feature aggregation + cosine similarity + color window + (2+1)D CNN. **For v0:** the target-view-frustum-volume + group-wise-cosine + (2+1)D CNN is the *v0 paper's* H3 toolkit (the *most* comprehensive in the reading list), the *direct* adoption of MuRF's H3 mechanisms for *multi-view* 3D-reconstruction.

**H4 (implicit SDF > mesh):** **MILD CONTRADICTION** (the (2+1)D CNN decoder outputs RGB + density, NOT SDF). MuRF uses *volumetric rendering* (NeRF-style alpha compositing), *not* SDF + sphere tracing. The *density* prediction is *implicit* (the *output of the (2+1)D CNN*), but the *rendering* is *volumetric*, *not* SDF-based. The *practical* trade-off: volumetric rendering is *slower* (D=64 forward passes per ray) but *more flexible* (the density can be *any* non-negative scalar, not constrained to be a *signed distance*), the *reason* the feed-forward NeRF/3DGS arc converged to *volumetric + 3DGS* rendering, NOT *SDF + sphere tracing*. **For v0:** the *dental-crown-generation* field uses *implicit* (e.g., DMC's indicator-grid, MADCrowner's margin segmentation) but *NOT* SDF (e.g., TeethGenerator's VQ-VAE+diffusion, ToothCraft's SDF diffusion are *exceptions*; the *majority* of the field uses *point cloud + indicator function* or *mesh extraction*). The MuRF + pixelSplat 164 + MVSplat 156 design is *consistent* with the *dental-crown-generation* field's *non-SDF* approach.

**H5 (synthetic+finetune > direct):** **MILD CONTRADICTION** (MuRF is *pure* feed-forward, *no* synthetic data, *no* finetuning). MuRF is trained on *real* RealEstate10K + DTU + LLFF, *no* synthetic pretraining, *no* finetuning. The *zero-shot generalization* to *unseen* DTU + Mip-NeRF 360 is *pure* generalization, *no* synthetic + finetune. ## Surprises / Interesting Things Buried in Section 4

1. **Volume Orientation is the most important single architecture choice (Sec 4.2, Table 6):** Target view beats reference view by +1.09 dB on RealEstate10K *large* baseline (20.20 → 21.29) and +0.39 dB on DTU *small* baseline (26.50 → 26.89). The *killer* insight: **the choice of volume orientation is more impactful than the choice of decoder (2+1)D vs 3D CNN, 3D CNN is barely better than 2D CNN on RealEstate10K (21.39 vs 21.06 = +0.33) and 2D CNN barely beats 1D CNN (21.06 vs 20.78 = +0.28)**. This is the *direct* evidence that **target-view-frustum-volume is the *key* architectural choice**, and **(2+1)D CNN is the *right* decoder for *parameter efficiency***. **For v0:** this is a *killer* design lesson for *any* volume-based 3D-reconstruction — the *spatial alignment* with the target view is *more important* than the *decoder architecture*.

2. **Color is the most important volume element (Sec 4.2, Table 6):** W/o color: 20.04 (large) / 25.80 (small), Δ=-1.25 / Δ=-1.09 from the full model. W/o feature: 20.91 (large) / 26.79 (small), Δ=-0.38 / Δ=-0.10. W/o cosine: 21.06 (large) / 26.68 (small), Δ=-0.23 / Δ=-0.21. The *killer* insight: **color is the *most* important volume element for *both* small and large baselines**, not feature or cosine. This *contradicts* the intuition that *geometric cues* (cosine) are *more important* for *large* baselines — actually, *appearance* (color) is *always* the *most* important. **For v0:** this is a *killer* design lesson for *any* multi-view 3D-reconstruction — *appearance* (color, texture) is *more important* than *geometry* (cosine, feature) for *all* baselines.

3. **Ray Transformer is NOT always better than MLP (Sec 4.2, Table 6, Fig. 6):** Ray Transformer (depth-only attention): 20.57 (large) / 26.22 (small). MLP (point only): 20.41 (large) / 26.24 (small). Ray Transformer is *barely* better than MLP on small baseline (+0.02 dB) and *slightly worse* on large baseline (-0.16 dB). This is the *empirical refutation* of the IBRNet 032 + GNT 027 + MatchNeRF 005 claim that *Ray Transformer is the right decoder for multi-view 3D-reconstruction*. **For v0:** the *dental-crown-generation* field's *consensus* decoder is the *point cloud + indicator function* (DMC, MADCrowner, ToothCraft) or *MLP* (Cao25, DuoDent), *not* Ray Transformer — MuRF's *empirical refutation* of Ray Transformer is *consistent* with the *dental-crown-generation* field's choice.

4. **3D CNN barely beats (2+1)D CNN with 50% more parameters (Sec 4.2, Table 6, Fig. 7):** 3D CNN: 21.39 (large) / 26.86 (small), Δ=+0.10 / Δ=-0.03 from (2+1)D CNN. **3D CNN is *barely* better on large baseline and *slightly worse* on small baseline, despite having 50% more parameters (16.6M vs 10.4M)**. This is the *killer* efficiency claim: **(2+1)D CNN is the *right* factorization** for *parameter-efficient* 3D-rendering, the *direct* precursor to *depth-only* (1D CNN) decoders in subsequent *speed-critical* papers. **For v0:** the *parameter efficiency* is *critical* for *clinical chairside* inference — (2+1)D CNN is the *right* v0 design for *edge* deployment.

5. **MuRF 4-view ALREADY beats all 10-view methods on LLFF (Sec 4.1, Table 5):** MuRF 4-view: 25.95 vs GPNR 10-view: 25.72, +0.23 dB. The *killer* data-efficiency claim: **MuRF needs 60% fewer input views to beat the prior SOTA**, the *direct* evidence that **the target-view-frustum-volume + (2+1)D CNN design is *much more data-efficient* than prior methods**. **For v0:** this is a *killer* design lesson for *clinical* 3D-reconstruction where *intra-oral scans* provide *limited* input views (typically 1-3 per arch) — MuRF-style design is the *right* v0 design for *few-view* 3D-reconstruction.

6. **MuRF is the FIRST feed-forward NeRF to MATCH per-scene-optimization SOTA on DTU 3-view (Sec 4.1, Table 4):** MuRF 3-view: 21.31 vs SPARF 3-view: 21.01, +0.30 dB. The *killer* data-efficiency claim: **feed-forward is *not necessarily worse* than per-scene-optimization for *sufficient* data + *good* inductive biases**. The 9-view MuRF (25.28) is *better than* the 9-view Mip-NeRF (23.58) and DietNeRF (23.83), the *direct* evidence that **feed-forward is *strictly better* than per-scene-optimization for *large* data + *good* design**. **For v0:** this is a *killer* design lesson for *clinical* 3D-reconstruction where *per-scene-optimization* is *infeasible* (10-30 minutes per patient vs 50-200ms for feed-forward).

7. **Zero-shot generalization to Mip-NeRF 360 (Sec 4.1, Table 5):** MuRF Mip-NeRF 360: 23.98 / 0.800 / 0.293 vs AttnRend: 14.00 / 0.474 / 0.712. The *killer* result: **MuRF is the FIRST feed-forward NeRF to *plausibly* render *unbounded* outdoor scenes zero-shot**, the *direct* evidence that **geometric inductive biases (target view frustum + (2+1)D CNN) are *necessary* for *generalization*, NOT *data-hungry* data-driven features alone**. **For v0:** this is a *killer* design lesson for *clinical* 3D-reconstruction where *patient diversity* requires *generalization* to *unseen* anatomies.

8. **The *renaming* of AttnRend in MuRF's footnote (Sec 1, footnote 1):** *"We name the previously unnamed method of Du et al. [8] as AttnRend, according its GitHub repository name."* This is a *subtle attribution* detail: the paper *"Learning to Render Novel Views from Wide-Baseline Stereo Pairs"* (Du 2023) was *not* explicitly named "AttnRend" in the original paper, the name was *coined* by MuRF's authors based on the *GitHub repo name* `cross_attention_renderer`. The 166-note already documented this; this is the *confirmation* that **AttnRend is the *community* name, not the *paper* name**. **For v0:** the *convention* is to *cite* by the *paper* title + *paper* authors + *arXiv ID* (Du 2023, arXiv:2304.08463), and *use* the *community* name (AttnRend) in *tables* and *text* for *brevity*.

9. **The (2+1)D CNN is borrowed from video recognition (Sec 1, Sec 3.3, citations [9, 28]):** *"we use a computationally more efficient 3D CNN alternative by factorizing the 3D 3×3×3 convolution to a 2D 3×3×1 convolution in the spatial dimension and a 1D 1×1×3 convolution in the depth dimension, i.e., (2+1)D CNN, a popular strategy in video recognition works"*. The *killer* insight: **the (2+1)D CNN factorization is *NOT new* to NeRF — it comes from *video recognition* (Feichtenhofer 2019 SlowFast, Tran 2018 R(2+1)D)**, the *direct* evidence that **NeRF benefits from *cross-domain* knowledge transfer (video recognition → 3D rendering)**. **For v0:** the *dental-crown-generation* field could benefit from *video-recognition* tricks (e.g., SlowFast for *multi-scale feature aggregation* in *3DTeethSeg22 + ToSynFCD* training) for *faster* training and *better* multi-scale features.

10. **The *group-wise cosine similarity* is borrowed from stereo (Sec 3.2, Eq. 1, citation [11, 5]):** *"we use group-wise cosine similarities [11, 5] for further improved expressiveness, where the same computation still applies"*. The *killer* insight: **the group-wise-correlation trick is *NOT new* to multi-view NeRF — it comes from *stereo depth estimation* (Guo 2019 GwcNet, the *founding* paper of *group-wise correlation* for stereo) and *MatchNeRF 005* (Chen 2023, the *MuRF 3rd-author's own paper*)**, the *direct* evidence that **multi-view NeRF benefits from *stereo* knowledge transfer (stereo depth estimation → 3D rendering)**. **For v0:** the *dental-crown-generation* field could benefit from *stereo* tricks (e.g., group-wise correlation for *multi-tooth feature aggregation* in *3DTeethSeg22* segmentation) for *better* multi-tooth features.
