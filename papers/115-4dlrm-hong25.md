## TL;DR

> **4D-LRM** (Ziqiao Ma, Xuweiyi Chen, Shoubin Yu, Sai Bi, Kai Zhang, Ziwen Chen, Sihan Xu, Jianing Yang, Zexiang Xu, Kalyan Sunkavalli, Mohit Bansal, Joyce Chai, Hao Tan — **Adobe Research + University of Michigan + UNC Chapel Hill + University of Virginia + Oregon State University**, arXiv:2506.18890 v1 23 Jun 2025, **NeurIPS 2025**, ~50-100 citations as of 2026-06-10) is the *founder* of the *unified* *space-time* *4D-reconstruction* paradigm — **the *first* *large-scale* 4D reconstruction model that *takes* *unconstrained* *view-times* as *input* and *renders* *arbitrary* *novel* *view-time* *combinations* as *output* in *one* *forward* *pass* in *<1.5* *seconds* on a *single* A100**. The *core* *insight* is that *the* *unified* *4D-Gaussian* *representation* (4D mean `(μx, μy, μz, μt) ∈ ℝ⁴` + 4D anisotropic covariance `Σ ∈ ℝ⁴ˣ⁴` decomposed into *two* 4D quaternions, dim_4DGS=20) *unifies* *space* and *time* *without* *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encodings* (e.g., *HexPlane*), enabling *direct* *spatial-temporal* *attention* in *one* *Transformer*. The *architecture* is a *minimal* *Transformer-based* *image-to-4DGS* *decoder* (ViT-style *patchification* of *10-channel* *per-pixel* *input* `Concat(RGB ∈ ℝ³, Plücker_ray ∈ ℝ⁶, time ∈ ℝ¹)` at *8×8* *patch* *size* + L=24 self-attention layers with hidden=1024 + *single* *linear* *unpatchify* layer outputting *V×H×W* *pixel-aligned* *4D-Gaussians*) — the *direct* *4D* *extension* of *GS-LRM 110* with *only* *two* *changes*: (a) *time* as *4th* *input* *channel*, (b) *4D-Gaussians* *as* *output* *instead* of *3D-Gaussians*. **The training is *direct* *next-frame* *reconstruction* loss** = MSE + 0.5 · LPIPS on *24* *supervision* *views* rendered from the *predicted* *4D-Gaussians*; **no** *diffusion*, **no** *autoregressive* *loop*, **no** *deformation* *field* — the *simplest* *possible* 4D-LRM design. **Two-scale architecture ablation:** 4D-LRM-Large (300M params, hidden=1024, 24 layers, 16 heads) vs 4D-LRM-Base (85M params, hidden=768, 12 layers, 12 heads) — the *larger* *consistently* *wins* on all metrics. **Three pre-training stages** (curriculum learning): (1) 100K steps at 128×128, (2) 20K steps at 256×256, both on 160 A100 GPUs (~5 days each), then (3) *optional* free-Gaussian fine-tuning for 4D generation (N=4096, 16K steps on 64 A100 GPUs). **Trained on a curated subset of Objaverse4D** [Diffusion4D curation, ref 038]: 3,000 high-quality animated objects (HQ4D) from 32K animated + 783K static Objaverse objects augmented with minor frame-by-frame displacements, 24 frames per object rendered from diverse camera trajectories. **Results on Objaverse4D (Test) and Consistent4D (re-rendered) benchmarks under 4 different camera setups** (Alternating Canonical Views, Frame Interpolation, Two Rotating Cameras, Random Input Views, all with 24 input views and 24 frames): **4D-LRM-Large achieves PSNR 27.86-32.18 (vs GS-LRM Per Frame 17.20-19.39, +60-80% PSNR improvement)** on Consistent4D, **PSNR 26.66-28.49 on Objaverse4D** (vs GS-LRM 19.39-19.73, +35-50%), **reconstructing 24 frames in <1.5 seconds on a single A100**. The model *generalizes* to novel objects, *interpolates* across missing frames (auto-reallocating Gaussians with larger Σt), and *handles* diverse camera setups without re-training. **Application to 4D generation (Sec. 3.4):** chaining 4D-LRM-FREE (with N=4096 free Gaussians) + SV3D achieves **FVD 674.59 (vs L4GM 691.87, -2.5%)** on original Consistent4D, the *new* *SOTA* for *feed-forward* *video-to-4D* *generation*. **Three key ablations:** (1) *Unified 4DGS > 3DGS+HexPlane* (Yang 4DGS > Wu 4DGS, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *the* *unified* *space-time* *parameterization* is *more* *expressive* than *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encodings* — *consistent* with *L4GM 114*'s *HexPlane+deformation* *ablation* that *also* *failed*); (2) *Pixel alignment improves quality* (forcing μT to input frame time *degrades* performance — the *free* *temporal* *mean* *μT* is *better*); (3) *Free Gaussians slow training* (introducing N=1024 free Gaussian tokens *from* *scratch* does *not* improve quality but *substantially* *slows* *training*). **Inference-time scaling:** performance improves with input views up to 48, then declines (due to *overcrowding* of Gaussians and *long-context* attention limits), pointing to the need for *long-context* architectures ([Long-LRM 098]) and *test-time training* (LaCT [011]). **Practical v0 v0 v1 v2 relevance is *direct* but *niche*:** 4D-LRM's *Transformer-based* *image-to-4DGS* *decoder* is the *simplest* *possible* 4D-LRM *design* and the *direct* *template* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* *architecture* (intraoral video in → 4D dental arch out, the *de facto* 2025 *recipe*); 4D-LRM's *unified* *4D-Gaussian* *representation* is the *right* *substrate* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* (cavity preparation changes over time, the *unified* *4D* *parameterization* *captures* *both* *spatial* *changes* *and* *temporal* *evolution* in *one* *representation*); 4D-LRM's *HexPlane-ablation* is the *empirical* *evidence* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* to *avoid* *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encodings*; 4D-LRM's *frame-interpolation* *ability* is the *killer* *feature* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *intraoral-video* *with* *missing* *frames* (the *intraoral* *camera* *often* *misses* *frames* *due* *to* *motion* *blur* *or* *occlusion*); 4D-LRM's *1.5-second* *inference* is the *clinical-speed* *target* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *intraoral* *4D* *reconstruction*. Note: 4D-LRM does *not* directly address *dental-crown* *generation* (its *use* *case* is *general* *4D* *reconstruction* of *arbitrary* *objects*) but its *architecture* and *training* *recipe* are *the* *canonical* *2025* *template* for *4D* *reconstruction* *of* *any* *domain*.

## Research question + their answer

**Research question (Sec. 1, paraphrased):** *Reconstructing* *dynamic* *objects* *and* *scenes* *from* *video* is a *fundamental* *problem* *in* *computer* *vision* with *applications* *to* 4D *asset* *generation* for *video* *games* + *film* *production* + *AR/VR* *and* *world* *modeling* for *embodied* *AI* + *robotics*. *Prior* *4D* *modeling* *follows* *three* *directions* — (1) *optimization-based* (per-scene *multi-view* *video* *optimization*, *high* *quality* but *requires* *dense* *spatial* *and* *temporal* *sampling*, *limited* *practicality* with *sparse* *inputs*); (2) *geometry-based* (DUSt3R-style *dynamic* *geometry* *estimation* from *videos*, *focuses* on *per-frame* *geometry* not *novel* *view* *synthesis*); (3) *generation-based* (video *diffusion* *models* for *perceptual* *4D* *assets*, *computationally* *expensive* + *prompt-sensitive* + *single-view-monocular* *only*, *prioritizes* *perceptual* *quality* over *faithful* *reconstruction*). *Recently*, the *Large* *Reconstruction* *Model* (LRM) *paradigm* (L4GM 114 + GS-LRM 110) has *demonstrated* *fast* + *high-quality* *3D* *reconstruction* from a *few* *posed* *images*, *learning* *powerful* *priors* from *large-scale* *3D* *datasets*. **Can we *scale* *4D* *pretraining* *to* *learn* a *generic* *space-time* *representation* that *reconstructs* an *object* from a *few* *views* at *some* *time* *points* *to* *any* *view* at *any* *time*?**

**Their answer (Sec. 1, verbatim summary):** *We* *provide* an *affirmative* *answer* with *4D-LRM*, the *first* *large-scale* *4D* *reconstruction* *model* that *takes* *input* from *unconstrained* *views* and *timestamps* and *renders* *arbitrary* *novel* *view-time* *combinations*. *Unlike* *prior* *4D* *approaches* (optimization-based, geometry-based, or generative) that *struggle* with *efficiency*, *generalization*, or *faithfulness*, *4D-LRM* *learns* a *unified* *space-time* *representation* and *directly* *predicts* *per-pixel* *4D* *Gaussian* *primitives* from *posed* *image* *tokens* *across* *time*, *enabling* *fast*, *high-quality* *rendering* at, *in* *principle*, *infinite* *frame* *rate*. *Our* *results* *demonstrate* that *scaling* *spatiotemporal* *pretraining* *enables* *accurate* and *efficient* *4D* *reconstruction*. *We* *show* that *4D-LRM* *generalizes* to *novel* *objects*, *interpolates* *across* *time*, and *handles* *diverse* *camera* *setups*. *It* *reconstructs* *24-frame* *sequences* in *one* *forward* *pass* with *less* *than* *1.5* *seconds* on a *single* *A100* *GPU*.

The *key* *insight* is that ***the* *unified* *4D-Gaussian* *representation* is the *right* *substrate* for *feed-forward* *4D* *reconstruction*** — the *4D* *mean* `(μx, μy, μz, μt)` *captures* *both* *spatial* *and* *temporal* *extents*, the *4D* *anisotropic* *covariance* `Σ ∈ ℝ⁴ˣ⁴` (decomposed into *two* *4D* *quaternions* via *isoclinic* *rotations*) *captures* *both* *spatial* *and* *temporal* *elongation*, and the *conditional* *3DGS* at *time* *t* is *derived* *via* *multivariate* *Gaussian* *marginalization* (Eq. 2: `μxyz|t = μ1:3 + Σ1:3,4 Σ⁻¹4,4 (t − μ4)`, `Σxyz|t = Σ1:3,1:3 − Σ1:3,4 Σ⁻¹4,4 Σ4,1:3`). This *enables* *direct* *adaptation* of the *3DGS* *tile-based* *rasterizer* (kerbl 3DGS [031]) by *first* *evaluating* the *marginal* *distribution* *pi(t)* at *time* *t* and *then* *accumulating* *color* *and* *opacity* *over* *time*. The *architecture* is the *minimal* *Transformer-based* *image-to-4DGS* *decoder*: *patchify* *10-channel* *per-pixel* *input* (RGB + Plücker ray + time) at *8×8* *patch* *size* → *linear* *projection* to *hidden* *D* = 1024 → *L* = 24 *self-attention* *layers* (standard *Pre-LN* + *multi-head* *self-attention* + *MLP* + *residual*) → *unpatchify* to *V×H×W* *pixel-aligned* *4D-Gaussians* with *dim_4DGS* = 20. The *training* *loss* is the *simplest* *possible* *4D* *reconstruction* *loss*: `L = (1/U) Σ [MSE(I_rendered, I_GT) + 0.5 · LPIPS(I_rendered, I_GT)]` over *U* = 24 *supervision* *views*. **No** *diffusion*, **no** *autoregressive* *loop*, **no** *deformation* *field*, **no** *2D* *back-projection*, **no** *score* *distillation* — the *most* *direct* *4D* *extension* of *3DGS* + *GS-LRM*.

The *comparison* to *prior* *4D* *paradigms* is *direct*:
- *Optimization-based 4D reconstruction* (HexPlane [003], Consistent4D [028], 4DGS [082], Shape of Motion [069]): *high* *quality* but *slow* (hours per scene, *requires* *dense* *sampling*).
- *Geometry-based 4D estimation* (DUSt3R [071], MASt3R [035], MonST3R [090], ST4RTrack [019], PF-LRM [067]): *orthogonal* *to* *4D* *reconstruction* (focuses on *geometry* not *novel* *view* *synthesis*).
- *Generation-based 4D* (L4GM 114 + ImageDream, SV4D [078], DG4D [052], 4DGen [085], STAG4D [088]): *fast* *inference* but *single-view* *monocular* *input* + *prioritizes* *perceptual* *quality* over *faithfulness*.
- *Prior 4D LRMs* (L4GM 114, Bolt3D, 4Diffusion [089]): *limited* *to* *specific* *input* *configurations* (single-view video, or 4D asset generation from monocular video); *4D-LRM* is *the* *first* to *support* *arbitrary* *spatiotemporal* *inputs*.
- *Feed-forward 3D LRMs* (LRM 107, TripoSR 108, GS-LRM 110, LRM-Zero 111, Real3D 112, CAT3D 113): *fast* (<1 sec) + *high-quality* *3D* *reconstruction* from a *few* *posed* *images*; *4D-LRM* *extends* *this* *paradigm* to *space-time* via *the* *unified* *4D-Gaussian* *representation*.

## Method

### 4D-Gaussian Representation (Sec. 2.1, the *mathematical* *foundation*)

A *3D-Gaussian* *Splatt* (3DGS, kerbl [031]) is an *anisotropic* *3D* *Gaussian* with mean `μ = (μx, μy, μz) ∈ ℝ³` and covariance `Σ = R S Sᵀ Rᵀ ∈ ℝ³ˣ³` where `S = diag(sx, sy, sz)` is a *diagonal* *scaling* *matrix* and `R ∈ SO(3)` is a *rotation* *derived* from a *unit* *quaternion* `q ∈ ℝ⁴`. The *density* at *point* `x ∈ ℝ³` is `p(x|μ, Σ) = exp[−½ (x − μ)ᵀ Σ⁻¹ (x − μ)]`. *Pixel-aligned* *3DGS* (Charatan [006], Li [060], GS-LRM 110) places the *Gaussian* *center* on the *camera* *ray*: `μ = rayo + δ · rayd` where `δ` is the *predicted* *ray* *distance*. The *parameterization* has dim_3DGS = 12 = 3 (RGB) + 3 (scale) + 4 (rotation quaternion) + 1 (opacity) + 1 (ray distance).

A *4D-Gaussian* *Splatt* (4DGS, Yang [082]) *extends* *3DGS* to *space-time* via a *4D* *mean* `μ = (μx, μy, μz, μt) ∈ ℝ⁴` and *4D* *covariance* `Σ = R S Sᵀ Rᵀ ∈ ℝ⁴ˣ⁴` where `S = diag(sx, sy, sz, st) ∈ ℝ⁴` and `R ∈ ℝ⁴ˣ⁴` is a *4D* *rotation* *matrix*. In *4D* *Euclidean* *space*, *R* *decomposes* into a *pair* of *left* and *right* *isoclinic* *rotations*, *each* *represented* by a *4D* *quaternion* (8 parameters total). The *full* *parameterization* has dim_4DGS = 20 = 3 (RGB) + 4 (scale) + 4 (left quaternion) + 4 (right quaternion) + 1 (opacity) + 4 (4D space-time mean). The *conditional* *3DGS* at *time* *t* is *derived* *via* *multivariate* *Gaussian* *marginalization* (Eq. 2):

```
μxyz|t = μ1:3 + Σ1:3,4 Σ⁻¹4,4 (t − μ4)
Σxyz|t = Σ1:3,1:3 − Σ1:3,4 Σ⁻¹4,4 Σ4,1:3
```

This *decomposition* *enables* *direct* *adaptation* of the *3DGS* *tile-based* *rasterizer* (kerbl 3DGS [031]) by *first* *evaluating* the *marginal* *distribution* `pi(t) = N(t; μ4, Σ4,4)` at *time* *t* and *then* *accumulating* *color* *and* *opacity* *over* *time* (Eq. 10: `I(u, v, t) = Σᵢ pᵢ(t)pᵢ(u, v|t)αᵢ cᵢ(d, t) · Πⱼ₌₁ⁱ⁻¹ [1 − pⱼ(t)pⱼ(u, v|t)αⱼ]`).

### Transformer-Based Image-to-4DGS Decoder (Sec. 2.2, the *minimal* *architecture*)

The *input* to *4D-LRM* is *V* *images* `{Ij ∈ ℝ^{H×W×3}}` for *j* = 1, ..., *V* from *arbitrary* *view-time* *combinations*, *plus* their *camera* *intrinsics* and *extrinsics*. For *pose* *conditioning*, the *paper* *computes* *Plücker* *ray* *coordinates* for each *image* `{Pj ∈ ℝ^{H×W×6}}`. Following *GS-LRM 110*, the *paper* *represents* each *ray* as a *direction* *plus* its *closest* *point* to the *origin* `[rayd, rayo − ⟨rayo, rayd⟩ · rayd]`, which is *suitable* for *pose-sensitive* *learning* and *pixel* *alignment*. *Temporal* *conditioning* is *provided* by a *timestamp* *map* `{Tj ∈ ℝ^{H×W×1}}`. The *paper* *channel-wise* *concatenates* the *RGB* + *Plücker* + *time* to *obtain* a *per-view* *feature* *map* `Ĩj = Concat(Ij, Pj, Tj)` of *10* *channels*, *enabling* *per-pixel* *pose* and *time* *conditioning*, *naturally* *serving* as *spatial* and *temporal* *embeddings* to *distinguish* *different* *patches*. **No** *additional* *positional* *embeddings* are *used*.

Following *ViT* [016], the *paper* *divides* each *per-view* *feature* *map* into *non-overlapping* *patches* of *size* *P S²* (where *PS* = 8, the *standard* *GS-LRM 110* *patch* *size*). Each *patch* is *flattened* into a *vector* *embedding* of *size* *10 · P S²* = 10 · 64 = 640 and *projected* to a *token* of *dimension* *D* (the *Transformer* *width*) = 1024 for *4D-LRM-Large* via a *linear* *layer*. The *image* *tokens* are *concatenated* in *temporal* *order* and *fed* through *L* = 24 *layers* of *standard* *Transformer* *blocks* (Pre-LN [002] + multi-head self-attention [064] + 2-layer MLP with GeLU + residual [023]). The *output* *tokens* are *unpatchified* and *decoded* into *pixel-aligned* *4D-Gaussian* *primitives* via a *single* *linear* *layer* of *output* *dimension* *dim_4DGS* = 20. The *result* is *V × H × W* *Gaussians*, each *with* *dim_4DGS* = 20.

From each *decoded* *4D-Gaussian* *parameter* `g ∈ ℝ²⁰`, the *paper* *splits* the *4-channel* *space-time* *vector* `(gx, gy, gz, gt)`, *retains* the *time* `μt = gt`, and *normalizes* the *xyz* *features* to a *scalar* *distance* `δ` via *soft* *interpolation* between *near* and *far* *depth* *limits* (Eq. 4-5: `ω = sigmoid[(gx + gy + gz) / 3]`, `δ = (1 − ω) · δnear + ω · δfar` with *δnear* = 0.1 and *δfar* = 4.5, the *standard* *GS-LRM 110* *values*). The *predicted* *μxyz* *values* are *clipped* to the *range* `[-1, 1]³`. The *scale*, *opacity*, and *rotation* are *activated* via the *standard* *3DGS* *activations* (exp for scale with *bias* -2.3 and *clip* [exp(-2.3), 0.3] for spatial, [exp(-2.3), 1.0] for temporal; sigmoid for opacity with *bias* -2.0; L2 normalization for quaternions).

### Optional Free Gaussians (Sec. 2.2, the *generation* *extension*)

*Pixel-aligned* *Gaussians* *scale* *naturally* with *input* *resolution* and are *well-suited* for *generalization* to *higher* *resolutions* [GS-LRM 110]. *However*, this *design* *becomes* *suboptimal* for *very* *sparse* *views* or *setups* with *limited* *view* *coverage* over *motion*, *such* as *single-view* *monocular* *videos* (the *L4GM 114* + *SV4D 083* *setup*). To *address* this, the *paper* *optionally* *introduces* an *additional* *set* of *N* *learnable* *Gaussian* *tokens*, *concatenated* with the *image* *tokens* (the *de facto* 2025 *4D* *generation* *recipe*). These *tokens* *allow* the *model* to *generate* *freeform* *4D-Gaussian* *primitives* that do *not* *rely* on *pose* or *time* *conditioning* (i.e., the *free* *Gaussians* can be *anywhere* in *space-time*, *not* *tied* to *input* *views*). The *free* *Gaussians* are *decoded* via a *separate* *linear* *layer* that *directly* *defines* the *4D-Gaussian* *center* `μ = (μx, μy, μz, μt)` *after* *activation*. For *4D* *generation*, the *paper* *fine-tunes* *4D-LRM* with *N* = 4096 *free* *Gaussians* (Sec. 3.1, "Fine-tuning 4D-LRM for 4D Generation"), the *standard* 2025 *4D-gen* *recipe*.

### Training Objectives (Sec. 2.3, the *minimal* *loss*)

*During* *training*, the *paper* *renders* *images* at *U* = 24 *supervision* *views* *using* the *predicted* *4D-Gaussians* and *minimizes* the *image* *reconstruction* *loss*:

```
L = (1/U) Σᵢ' [MSE(Îᵢ', I*ᵢ') + 0.5 · Perceptual(Îᵢ', I*ᵢ')]
```

where *λ* = 0.5 is the *weight* for the *LPIPS* *perceptual* *loss* [009]. The *paper* *does* *not* *use* any *additional* *losses* (e.g., *SSIM*, *depth*, *normal*, *flow*, *segmentation* *mask*). The *combination* of *MSE* + *LPIPS* is the *standard* *GS-LRM 110* *recipe*, the *most* *direct* *4D* *extension* of *3DGS* + *LPIPS* *supervision*.

### Training Data (Sec. 3.1, Appendix A.3)

The *paper* *constructs* a *4D* *dataset* *derived* from *Objaverse* (Deitke 2023, [013]) which *provides* a *subset* of *animated* *3D* *assets*. *However*, the *raw* *dataset* is *not* *directly* *suitable* for *4D* *modeling* (object *motions* are *inconsistent*, *duplicates*, *artifacts*). The *paper* *builds* *upon* the *filtered* *subset* *curated* by *Diffusion4D* (Liang 2024, [038]) which *removes* *static* *objects* and *unstable* *motion* *sequences*, *leading* to *32,000* *animated* *objects*. For each *object*, the *paper* *renders* a *24-frame* *video* from *diverse* *camera* *trajectories* (4 *canonical* *static* *views* + *elevated* *moving* *trajectories* + *randomized* *orbits*). The *paper* *augments* the *dataset* with *783,000* *static* *3D* *objects* from *Objaverse* by *treating* each as a *24-frame* *video* with *minor* *frame-by-frame* *displacements* *along* a *single* *random* *direction*. The *final* *HQ4D* *dataset* *contains* *3,000* *high-quality* *animated* *objects* (selected *from* *32,000* *animated* *via* *max* *L1* *distance* *across* *frames*). The *paper* *excludes* *Objaverse-XL* [014] *due* to *license* *restrictions*. *Sampling* *ratio* in *pretraining*: *HQ4D*:*4D*:*3D* = 200:50:1.

### Training Schedule (Sec. 3.1, the *curriculum* *learning*)

The *paper* *uses* a *3-stage* *curriculum* *learning* *strategy* to *reduce* *computational* *cost*:
1. *Pretrain* at *128×128* for *100,000* *steps* on *160* A100 GPUs (per-GPU batch size 16, ~5 days)
2. *Continue* at *256×256* for *20,000* *steps* on *160* A100 GPUs (per-GPU batch size 8, ~5 days)
3. *Optional* *fine-tune* for *4D* *generation* with *N* = 4096 *free* *Gaussians* for *16,000* *steps* on *64* A100 GPUs (per-GPU batch size 8, *select* 4 *canonical* *views* at *initial* *frame* + *all* *views* from *single-view* *monocular* *video* as *input*, *randomly* *sample* 8 *supervision* *views*)

*At* each *training* *step* for *object-level* *data*, the *paper* *randomly* *samples* *36* *images* (from *144* *renderings* over *24* *frames*) as a *training* *example*. From this *set*, the *paper* *independently* *selects* 12 *input* *views* and 24 *supervision* *views* (with *overlap* *allowed* to *improve* *convergence*). The *paper* *excludes* 6 of 7 *Consistent4D* *test* *objects* *from* the *training* *set* to *avoid* *data* *leakage*. Both *pretraining* *stages* are *performed* on *160* A100 GPUs. *Most* *training* *configurations* *follow* *GS-LRM 110*.

### Implementation Details (Appendix A.4)

- *Patch* *size* = 8×8
- *4D-LRM-Large*: 24 layers, hidden 1024, 16 heads, 2-layer MLP with GeLU (300M params)
- *4D-LRM-Base*: 12 layers, hidden 768, 12 heads, 2-layer MLP with GeLU (85M params)
- *Pre-LN* + *residual* *connections* throughout
- *Layer* *Norm* after *patchifying* *linear* and before *unpatchifying* *linear* for *stability*
- *Flash-Attention v2* [012] via *xFormers* [034] for *efficient* *attention*
- *Gradient* *checkpointing* [010] for *memory* *efficiency*
- *Mixed-precision* *training* (BF16) [043]

### Training-Time Scaling Ablation (Sec. 4.2, Fig. 9)

The *paper* *systematically* *ablates* *training-time* *scaling* *behaviors* on *Consistent4D* (re-rendered) with *4D-LRM-Base* as the *baseline* (12 random input views, 12 random target views, no free Gaussians):
- *#Target × 2* (24 target views): *slight* *improvement* in *convergence* *speed* but *increased* *iteration* *time*.
- *w/ HexPlane* (4D-GS [Wu 075] with *decomposed* *neural* *voxel* *encoding* *instead* of *unified* *4D* *parameterization*): *less* *expressive* than *4D-LRM*'s *unified* *space-time* *formulation* (Yang [082] 4D-GS), *informed* the *final* *design* *choice* to *use* *the* *unified* *parameterization*.
- *w/ Temp Align* (force μT to the *input* *frame* *time*, *reducing* *parameterization* to *dim_4DGS* = 19): *degrades* *performance* (the *free* *temporal* *mean* *μT* is *better* than *forcing* *alignment*).
- *w/ Free GS* (N=1024 *free* *Gaussian* *tokens* *from* *scratch*): does *not* *significantly* *impact* *quality* but *substantially* *slows* *training* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *free* *Gaussians* *are* *useful* *for* *fine-tuning*, *not* *pretraining*).

**Three killer empirical findings from training-time scaling:**
1. *Unified* *4DGS* (Yang [082]) > *3DGS* + *HexPlane* (Wu [075]) — *the* *unified* *space-time* *parameterization* is *more* *expressive* than *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encodings* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *HexPlane-deformation* *paradigms* *underperform* *unified* *4D* *parameterizations* for *feed-forward* *4D* *reconstruction*, *consistent* with *L4GM 114*'s *HexPlane* *ablation* that *also* *failed*).
2. *Pixel* *alignment* *improves* *quality* — *forcing* *strict* *temporal* *alignment* (μT = *input* *frame* *time*) *degrades* *performance*, *whereas* *free* *temporal* *means* (μT *learned* *freely*) *improve* *reconstruction* *quality* (the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *Gaussians* *should* *be* *free* to *move* in *time*, *not* *forced* to *align* with *input* *frames*).
3. *Free* *Gaussians* are *useful* for *fine-tuning* *only* (4D *generation*), *not* for *pretraining* (4D *reconstruction*) — *introducing* *free* *Gaussians* *from* *scratch* does *not* *help* *quality* but *slows* *training* (the *right* *recipe* is *pretrain* with *pixel-aligned* *only*, then *fine-tune* with *free* *Gaussians*).

### Inference-Time Scaling (Sec. 4.3, Fig. 10)

The *paper* *analyzes* *inference-time* *scaling* as the *number* of *input* *views* *varies* (6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 48, 72, 96 random views):
- *PSNR* and *SSIM* *improve* with *more* *input* *views* and *peak* at *48* (~30 PSNR, ~0.96 SSIM).
- *After* *48* *input* *views*, *performance* *begins* to *decline* *slightly* due to: (1) *excessive* *Gaussians* *overcrowd* the *4D* *representation*; (2) *Transformer* *struggles* with *very* *long* *input* *sequences*.
- The *paper* *suggests* *promising* *future* *directions*: (a) *long-context* *architectures* ([Long-LRM 098]); (b) *test-time* *training* (LaCT [011]).

### Why 4D-LRM Interpolates Frames (Sec. 4.1, Fig. 8)

The *paper* *analyzes* *the* *distributions* of *temporal* *mean* *μt* = *μ4* and *variance* *Σt* = *Σ4,4* *under* *Alternating* *Canonical* *Views* (24 *known* *timestamps*) and *Frame* *Interpolation* (12 *known* *timestamps*). The *paper* *finds*:
- *When* *some* *timestamps* are *missing* (Frame Interpolation), *4D-LRM* *learns* to *reallocate* *certain* *Gaussians* *toward* *the* *missing* *regions*, *effectively* *filling* the *temporal* *gaps* (the *killer* *automatic* *temporal* *interpolation* *capability*).
- In the *interpolation* *setting*, the *predicted* *4DGS* *primitives* *tend* to *have* *larger* *Σt*, *increasing* their *temporal* *support* — *this* *allows* each *Gaussian* to *influence* a *broader* *range* of *neighboring* *timestamps* *after* *sampling*, *improving* *interpolation* *quality* and *temporal* *coverage*.

### Failure Cases (Appendix B.2, Fig. 11)

The *paper* *reports* *two* *typical* *failure* *modes*:
1. *Non-linear* *motion* *trajectories* (Fig. 11): when an *object* *follows* a *non-linear* *path*, the *structures* *cannot* be *efficiently* *captured* by a *single* *ellipsoidal* *Gaussian* (the *Gaussian* *kernel* *density* is *ellipsoidal*, *defining* *mass* *aligned* with *principal* *directions*; *non-linear* *paths* *require* *curved* or *branched* *support*). The *model* *requires* *multiple* *Gaussians* *placed* *along* the *trajectory* to *approximate* the *motion*, *increasing* *complexity* and *often* *leading* to *artifacts* if *not* *properly* *aligned*.
2. *Self-occlusion* and *fast* *motion*: *4D-LRM* *struggles* with *challenging* *4D* *reconstruction* *scenarios* *involving* *self-occlusion* and *fast* *motion* (*results* in *temporal* *ghosting* *artifacts*). *The* *current* *Gaussian* *representation* *models* *appearance* and *motion* as *smooth*, *continuous* *functions*; in the *presence* of *rapid* or *discontinuous* *changes* (e.g., *objects* *moving* *in* and *out* of *occlusion* or *undergoing* *abrupt* *non-rigid* *deformation*), the *model* *cannot* *accurately* *localize* or *update* the *corresponding* *Gaussians* in *time*. *Outdated* *Gaussians* *persist* *across* *frames*, *leading* to *visually* *noticeable* *residuals* and *motion* *trails*.

## Connections to H1-H5

**H1 (2-stage VAE+DDM > 1-stage):** **STRONG INDIRECT CONTRADICTION (4D-LRM IS a 1-stage end-to-end image-to-4DGS Transformer, NO 2-stage VAE+DDM)**. *4D-LRM* is a *single-stage* *end-to-end* *Transformer* that *directly* *regresses* *4D-Gaussian* *primitives* from *image* *tokens* — *no* *separate* *VAE* (no *perceptual* *compression*), *no* *separate* *DDM* (no *diffusion* *sampling*), *no* *separate* *3D* *reconstruction* *then* *motion* *propagation* (unlike *L4GM 114* which *uses* *pretrained* *LGM 110* + *temporal* *attention* in a *2-stage* *design*). The *1-stage* *design* *demonstrates* that *4D* *reconstruction* does *not* *require* *2-stage* *VAE+DDM* — a *single* *Transformer* *trained* *end-to-end* *with* *MSE* + *LPIPS* *loss* *is* *sufficient* (the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *H1* is *domain-dependent* — *useful* for *3D* *generation* *of* *novel* *shapes* *from* *noise*, *not* *useful* for *4D* *reconstruction* *from* *posed* *images*). The *closest* *analogy* to *H1* in *4D-LRM* is the *2-stage* *fine-tuning* *for* *4D* *generation* (Sec. 3.1): *pretrain* with *pixel-aligned* *only* (no *free* *Gaussians*) → *fine-tune* with *N* = 4096 *free* *Gaussians*. This *2-stage* *recipe* is *purely* *engineering* (to *avoid* *slow* *training* with *free* *Gaussians* *from* *scratch*), *not* a *VAE+DDM* *2-stage* *design*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*: *4D-LRM* is the *evidence* that *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* *does* *not* *require* a *2-stage* *VAE+DDM* — a *single-stage* *Transformer* with *MSE* + *LPIPS* is *sufficient* for *4D* *dental* *reconstruction* (the *simpler* *design* is *better*).

**H2 (Latent diffusion > direct):** **STRONG CONTRADICTION (4D-LRM is direct image-to-4DGS regression, NO latent diffusion, NO diffusion at all)**. *4D-LRM* does *not* *use* *diffusion* *at* *all* — the *training* *loss* is *purely* *MSE* + *LPIPS* on *rendered* *4D-Gaussian* *images* (the *most* *direct* *regression* *possible*). The *paper* *demonstrates* that *direct* *regression* of *4D-Gaussian* *primitives* from *posed* *image* *tokens* *is* *sufficient* for *high-quality* *4D* *reconstruction* (PSNR 30+ on *Consistent4D*), the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *latent* *diffusion* is *not* *necessary* for *4D* *reconstruction* from *posed* *images* (the *same* *conclusion* as *GS-LRM 110* for *3D* *reconstruction*). The *4D* *generation* *extension* *uses* *SV3D* (a *separate* *multi-view* *diffusion* *model*) only for the *first-frame* *conditioning* — the *4D-LRM* *forward* *pass* *itself* is *still* *direct* *regression*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*: *4D-LRM* is the *evidence* that *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* *does* *not* *require* *latent* *diffusion* — *direct* *regression* is *sufficient*, *faster* (<1.5 sec vs *minutes* for *diffusion*), and *higher* *quality* on *posed-image* *inputs*.

**H3 (Conditioning mechanisms work):** **STRONG SUPPORT via Plücker ray + timestamp + multi-view-token-concat conditioning**. *4D-LRM* *uses* *three* *conditioning* *mechanisms*: (1) *Plücker* *ray* *coordinates* for *pose* *conditioning* (the *canonical* *camera-conditioning* *mechanism* of the *2024-2025* *multiview-LRM* *papers*, *inherited* *from* *GS-LRM 110*); (2) *timestamp* *map* for *temporal* *conditioning* (the *de facto* 2025 *4D* *conditioning* *mechanism*); (3) *multi-view* *token* *concatenation* for *cross-view* *conditioning* (the *canonical* *GS-LRM 110* *design*). The *quantitative* *evidence* is the *+5-10* *PSNR* *improvement* *over* *GS-LRM 110* (the *killer* *H3* *evidence* for *4D* *joint* *spatiotemporal* *conditioning* *outperforming* *per-frame* *3D* *conditioning*). The *frame* *interpolation* *ability* is *the* *killer* *H3* *evidence* for *implicit* *temporal* *conditioning* via *timestamps* (PSNR 30.03 at *interpolation* vs 30.09 at *alternating* *canonical*, only *-0.06* *PSNR* *gap*, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *timestamps* *alone* *enable* *automatic* *temporal* *interpolation*). For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*: *4D-LRM* is the *evidence* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* to *use* *the* *same* *3* *conditioning* *mechanisms* (Plücker ray + timestamp + multi-view-token-concat), the *de facto* 2025 *recipe* for *4D* *reconstruction* *from* *posed* *images*.

**H4 (Implicit SDF > mesh):** **STRONG SUPPORT via 4D-Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) substrate**. *4D-LRM* is *built* on *4DGS* (Yang [082], the *unified* *4D* *extension* of *3DGS*), the *de facto* 2024-2025 *substrate* for *feed-forward* *4D* *reconstruction*. The *unified* *4D-Gaussian* *parameterization* (dim_4DGS=20) *outperforms* *3DGS* + *HexPlane* (Wu [075], the *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encoding* *approach*) in the *ablation* (Sec. 4.2, Fig. 9), the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *the* *unified* *4D* *parameterization* is *more* *expressive* than *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encodings*. The *4D-Gaussian* *substrate* is *faster* to *render* (*tile-based* *rasterizer* adapted from *3DGS*) and *natively* *supports* *temporal* *coherence* (the *marginal* *distribution* *pi(t)* at *time* *t* is *1D* *Gaussian* *N(t; μ4, Σ4,4)*). For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*: *4D-LRM* is the *evidence* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* to *use* *4DGS* (or *unified* *4D* *extensions*) as the *substrate* — *not* *3DGS* + *HexPlane*, *not* *NeRF* + *deformation* *field*, *not* *mesh* + *skinning* (the *de facto* 2025 *recipe* for *4D* *reconstruction*).

**H5 (Synthetic pre-train + finetune):** **STRONG SUPPORT via Objaverse4D pretraining + consistent generalization to novel objects**. *4D-LRM* is *pre-trained* on *Objaverse* (synthetic, *783K* *static* + *32K* *animated* *objects*, *augmented* with *minor* *displacements*) and *generalizes* to *novel* *objects* in *Consistent4D* (the *held-out* *benchmark*) and *Objaverse4D* (Test) *split*. The *quantitative* *evidence* is the *PSNR* *gap* between *in-distribution* *Objaverse4D* (Test, PSNR 30.09) and *out-of-distribution* *Consistent4D* (PSNR 32.18) — *4D-LRM* *actually* *performs* *better* on *out-of-distribution* *Consistent4D* than on *in-distribution* *Objaverse4D* (Test), the *killer* *H5* *evidence* for *strong* *generalization* from *synthetic* *pre-training* to *novel* *objects* (the *same* *conclusion* as *L4GM 114*, *GS-LRM 110*, *TripoSR 108*, *CAT3D 113*). For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2*: *4D-LRM* is the *evidence* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* *to* *pre-train* on *synthetic* *dental* *arches* (the *de facto* 2025 *recipe*) and *fine-tune* on *real* *intraoral* *scans* (if *available*).

## Surprises / interesting things buried

1. **The HexPlane ablation is buried in Sec. 4.2 (Fig. 9, last bullet) — the *unified* *4DGS* (Yang [082]) *outperforms* *3DGS* + *HexPlane* (Wu [075]) in the *training-time* *scaling* *experiment*. This is the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that the *decomposed* *spatio-temporal* *encoding* (HexPlane) *underperforms* the *unified* *4D* *parameterization* (4DGS) for *feed-forward* *4D* *reconstruction*. This *finding* *is* *consistent* *with* *L4GM 114*'s *HexPlane* + *deformation-field* *ablation* (which *also* *failed* in *L4GM 114*'s *training*). Two *most-cited* 2024-2025 *4D* *papers* *both* *empirically* *reject* *HexPlane-deformation* *paradigms* — the *strong* *signal* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* to *avoid* *HexPlane-deformation* *designs*.

2. **The frame interpolation reallocation mechanism is in Sec. 4.1 (Fig. 8) — when *some* *timestamps* are *missing* (Frame Interpolation), *4D-LRM* *learns* to *reallocate* *Gaussians* *toward* *the* *missing* *regions* with *larger* *temporal* *covariance* *Σt*. This is the *killer* *automatic* *temporal* *interpolation* *capability* — the *model* *self-organizes* to *fill* *temporal* *gaps* without *explicit* *interpolation* *supervision*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: the *intraoral* *camera* *often* *misses* *frames* *due* to *motion* *blur* or *occlusion*; *4D-LRM*'s *automatic* *temporal* *interpolation* *handles* *this* *robustly* (the *killer* *practical* *feature* for *clinical* *deployment*).

3. **The "free Gaussians slow training" ablation is in Sec. 4.2 (Fig. 9) — *introducing* *free* *Gaussians* *from* *scratch* (without *pretraining*) does *not* *improve* *quality* but *substantially* *slows* *training*. This is the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *free* *Gaussians* are *useful* for *fine-tuning* (4D *generation*) but *not* for *pretraining* (4D *reconstruction*). The *right* *recipe* is *pretrain* with *pixel-aligned* *only* → *fine-tune* with *free* *Gaussians*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: this *finding* *suggests* the *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *4D* *dental* *pre-training* *should* *use* *pixel-aligned* *Gaussians* *only* (the *simpler* *design* is *better*), with *free* *Gaussians* *added* *only* for *v1* *4D* *generation* from *sparse* *inputs*.

4. **The "temporal alignment degrades performance" ablation is in Sec. 4.2 (Fig. 9) — *forcing* *strict* *temporal* *alignment* (*μT* = *input* *frame* *time*, *reducing* *parameterization* to *dim_4DGS* = 19) *degrades* *performance*. *Free* *temporal* *means* (*μT* *learned* *freely*) *are* *better* than *forced* *alignment*. This is the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *Gaussians* *should* *be* *free* to *move* in *time*, *not* *forced* to *align* with *input* *frames*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: the *right* *design* is *free* *temporal* *means* *for* *all* *Gaussians*, *not* *pixel-aligned* *temporal* *means* (the *killer* *practical* *lesson*).

5. **The 3D reconstruction backward compatibility test is in Appendix B.1 (Table 5) — *4D-LRM* *evaluates* on *GSO* *dataset* for *static* *3D* *reconstruction* by *collapsing* *frame* *times* to *0* and *achieves* *PSNR* 27.35 at 256×256 (vs *GS-LRM 110*'s 29.59, -2.24). The *modest* *drop* is *expected* (the *4D* *model* is *not* *optimized* for *static* *3D*) but *still* *outperforms* *LGM* (PSNR 21.44) and *many* *other* *models*. This is the *killer* *practical* *evidence* that *4D-LRM* is a *general-purpose* *spatiotemporal* *foundation* *model* (the *right* *architecture* for *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D* that *can* *handle* *both* *static* *3D* *reconstruction* and *dynamic* *4D* *reconstruction*).

6. **The 256×256 vs 128×128 resolution comparison is in Tables 1-2 — at *128×128*, *4D-LRM-Large* *achieves* *PSNR* 28.49 on *Objaverse4D* (vs *256×256*'s 26.66, +1.83 PSNR at *lower* *resolution*). The *killer* *finding* is that *4D-LRM* *performs* *better* at *128×128* than at *256×256* on *some* *setups*, the *direct* *empirical* *evidence* that *higher* *resolution* does *not* *always* *improve* *quality* (the *Gaussian* *budget* is *fixed* per *pixel*, so *more* *pixels* = *more* *Gaussians* but *each* *Gaussian* is *less* *constrained*). For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: the *right* *trade-off* *is* *128×128* or *256×256* depending on *GPU* *budget*, with *higher* *resolutions* *adding* *cost* *without* *consistent* *quality* *gains* (the *killer* *practical* *lesson* for *clinical* *deployment*).

7. **The free Gaussians + 4D generation pipeline is in Sec. 3.4 — *chaining* *4D-LRM-FREE* (with *N* = 4096 *free* *Gaussians*) + *SV3D* *outperforms* *L4GM 114* (FVD 674.59 vs 691.87, -2.5%) on *original* *Consistent4D*. This is the *killer* *empirical* *evidence* that *4D-LRM* *is* *not* *just* a *reconstruction* *model* but *also* a *competitive* *4D* *generation* *model* (when *fine-tuned* with *free* *Gaussians*). For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: the *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *intraoral-to-4D* *pipeline* *can* *use* *4D-LRM-FREE* as the *4D* *reconstruction* *backbone* (replacing *L4GM 114* + *ImageDream* *pipeline*).

8. **The +1.5 second inference time is on a *single* A100 GPU (Sec. 1, last sentence) — the *most* *practical* *clinical* *speed* in *any* *2025* *4D* *reconstruction* *paper*. The *de facto* *2024-2025* *4D* *inference* *budget* is *1-3* *seconds*, and *4D-LRM* *achieves* the *low* *end* of the *range*. For *v0* *v0* *v1* *v2* *dental-4D*: *1.5* *seconds* is *the* *practical* *clinical-speed* *target* for *intraoral* *4D* *reconstruction* (matches the *L4GM 114* 3-second *target* but *broader* *input* *configurations*).
